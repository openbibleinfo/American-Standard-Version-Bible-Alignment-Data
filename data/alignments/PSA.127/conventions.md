# Psalms 127 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **PSA.127.3** — Both verbless cola are read predicate-first as the ASV construes them: the copula joins נַחֲלַת (*are a heritage*) and שָׂכָר (*is reward*), with בָּנִים and פְּרִי הַבָּטֶן as subjects, on the strength of the chiastic parallel (children ∥ fruit of the womb; heritage ∥ reward). The competing reading, which rule 49's definite-construct-chain sentence would suggest, puts *are* on בָּנִים as the indefinite predicate; it was considered and not taken.
