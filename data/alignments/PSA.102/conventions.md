# Psalms 102 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Phrasal particles the ASV writes with this chapter's verbs — 'poureth out' (v0), 'consume away' (v3), 'taken ... up' and 'cast ... away' (v10), 'built up' (v16), 'looked down' (v19), 'take ... away' (v24) — are all kept in the verb's unit under rule 44 and none marked added. 'built up' (בָּנָה, v16) is the one where rule 44's surplus-particle test could go either way; it was read as the ASV's ordinary phrasal rendering of building a city rather than a result the Hebrew does not write, and the competing 'up' added reading is named in the unit note.
