# Mark 5 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- ἀρχισυνάγωγος is one source token carrying the whole English phrase the ASV builds for it, kept in a single unit and headed on ruler/rulers, the word glossing the compound's ἀρχ- element: `τῶν ἀρχισυναγώγων` → *rulers of the synagogue* (5.22), `τῷ ἀρχισυναγώγῳ` → *ruler of the synagogue* (5.36), `τοῦ ἀρχισυναγώγου` → *ruler of the synagogue* (5.38). A governing article keeps only its own determiner and case preposition — *unto the* at 5.36, *of the* at 5.38, *the* at 5.35 — and never part of the phrase. At 5.35 the elliptical genitive `ἀπὸ τοῦ ἀρχισυναγώγου` → *from the ruler of the synagogue's house* folds the ASV's italicized *house* into the same unit as the completion of the ellipsis, on rule 52's pattern, no other token standing for it to belong to; marking it `added` under rule 103 remains defensible and is recorded in that verse's note.
- An article governing a substantivized improper preposition takes the English determiner itself, and the adverb keeps only the phrase rendering the adverb: `εἰς τὸ πέραν τῆς θαλάσσης` → *to the other side of the sea* (5.1) and `εἰς τὸ πέραν` → *unto the other side* (5.21) give *the* to `τό` and *other side* to `πέραν` headed on *side* under rule 71, with the governed noun's own article taking *of the* under rules 21 and 42 and the expressed `εἰς` keeping its own word. `ἔξω τῆς χώρας` → *out of the country* (5.10) is the same division with the adverb rendered by a single word, so *out* is the head and *of the* is the article's. Rule 42 speaks of the article of the governed noun and does not name an article standing over the adverb itself; this chapter reads that article as an ordinary rule 19 determiner.
- The compound ἀρχισυνάγωγος is one unit taking the whole English phrase 'ruler of the synagogue', headed on 'ruler' (MRK.5.22 'rulers of the synagogue', 5.35, 5.36, 5.38). Both English nouns gloss an element of the compound, so rule 66's gloss-versus-generic-completion test does not decide it; 'ruler' is the element the word names and 'synagogue' the one that qualifies it, exactly as 'priest' is what ἀρχιερεύς names. The supplied 'of the' is a rule 7 helper inside that unit, no source token answering to it. At 5.35 the italicized 'house' is folded in as the completion of the elliptical genitive 'the ruler of the synagogue's', its English being incomplete without it. Do not read rule 104's prefix-family analogy as putting the head on the stem word here.
- In εἰς τὸ πέραν (MRK.5.1, 5.21) the expressed preposition takes its own English word ('to', 'unto') and the substantivizing article takes the determiner 'the', leaving the adverb 'other side' headed on 'side' per rule 71. Rule 42's 'of the' on the article is a different token — the article of the governed noun, which at 5.1 is τῆς θαλάσσης and does take 'of the'. So an article standing before an improper preposition is not the article rule 42 speaks of, and rules 2 and 26 forbid absorbing an expressed εἰς or τό into the adverb's phrase.
