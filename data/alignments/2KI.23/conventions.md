# 2 Kings 23 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Construct chains whose rectum carries an article and is rendered anarthrously — צְבָא הַשָּׁמָיִם → the host of heaven, הַר־הַמַּשְׁחִית → the mount of corruption, אִישׁ הָאֱלֹהִים → the man of God — give the phrase's single 'the' to the rectum's article (rule 29 fronting) and leave the construct its bare word; the article is not unrepresented. Verses 4, 5, 13, 16, 17 were corrected to this at reconciliation. Arthrous chains (the book of the covenant, the governor of the city) are counted the/the as before.
- מִחוּץ לְ before a place name → 'without' is one three-morpheme aligned unit headed on חוּץ (rule 30); the trailing לְ is part of the compound's single rendering and is not marked unrepresented (vv. 4, 6).
- Hiphil דקק → 'beat ... to dust': the verb takes 'beat', לְ takes 'to', עָפָר takes 'dust', and the ASV's object pronoun 'it' is added under rule 44, since the Hebrew writes no object (vv. 6, 15).
