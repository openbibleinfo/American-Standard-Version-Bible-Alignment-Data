# Psalms 87 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Refrain זֶה יֻלַּד־שָׁם (vv. 4, 6) → 'This one was born there': זֶה takes the supplied 'one' headed on 'This' (rule 31), the Pual perfect takes 'was born', שָׁם 'there'. The distributive אִישׁ וְאִישׁ of v. 5 is read the same way, each noun taking its own 'This one' / 'that one' with the contrastive determiner inside the noun's unit (rule 15) and nothing unrepresented. The predicate בְּ of vv. 1 and 7 carries the copula (rule 49); the בְּ of מְדֻבָּר בָּךְ in v. 3 is the participle's complement and takes 'of' — the candidate table's three treatments of בְּ are contexts, not a split.
