# Ezekiel 5 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Oath formula חַי־אָנִי: the ASV writes 'As I live', and the supplied 'As' belongs to the predicate adjective חַי, whose unit is the discontinuous 'As ... live' headed on 'live'; the explicit pronoun אָנִי takes only 'I'. The corpus reads it this way in six chapters, EZK.16.48, EZK.20.3 and EZK.20.31 among them. Treating the 'As' as a subordinator supplied for a conjunctionless clause, and so 'added' under rule 55, was tried in this chapter's verse pass and reversed at reconciliation.
- אִם־לֹא introducing an oath's content and rendered by the single word 'surely' is 'noncompositional', source head on אִם as the deterministic anchor and target head on 'surely': the polarity reversal leaves neither morpheme's own sense in the English. Compositional fusions keep 'aligned' status — לָכֵן → 'Therefore'/'Wherefore', מִשָּׁם → 'thereof', מִמֶּנּוּ → 'therefrom' — because each morpheme's sense survives in the one English word. Read which kind of fusion is in hand before choosing the status.
- אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה is rendered 'the Lord Jehovah': the supplied 'the' goes with אֲדֹנָי as the nominal expression it determines (rule 15), and the word's 1cs suffix is 'unrepresented', the ASV writing no possessive for it (5.5, 5.7, 5.8, 5.11). All four Sp1cs disagreements BSB reports on this chapter are this one decision.
- סְבִיבוֹת plus its suffix is one English rendering 'round about', headed on 'round' as the lexeme-carrying word. Where the phrase is the predicate of a verbless clause the construction-supplied copula joins it — 'countries are round about her', 'that are round about you' (5.5, 5.6, 5.7, 5.14, 5.15) — and where it is adverbial to a written verb no copula is written and none is placed (5.2, 5.12). The suffix keeps its own pronoun in every case.
