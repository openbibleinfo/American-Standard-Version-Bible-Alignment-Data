# Psalms 38 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **PSA.38.6** — כָּל־הַיּוֹם rendered 'all the day long' (vv.6, 12): 'long' is read as part of the ASV's fixed rendering of the noun and joins יוֹם's unit, headed on 'day'; כֹּל takes 'all' and the article 'the'. Marking 'long' added was the alternative and is named in both notes.
- **PSA.38.6** — עַד־מְאֹד rendered by a single English adverb ('greatly' v.6, 'sore' v.8) fuses as one unit under rule 30, headed on מְאֹד, which carries the lexeme; עַד is not marked unrepresented beside it.
- **PSA.38.15** — In the vocative pairings אֲדֹנָי אֱלֹהָי → 'O Lord my God' (v.15) and אֲדֹנָי תְּשׁוּעָתִי → 'O Lord my salvation' (v.22), the single written 'my' stands before the second noun and answers to that noun's own suffix; the suffix of the title אֲדֹנָי is unrepresented under rule 57, as it is in the bare 'Lord' of v.9. The 'O' joins אֲדֹנָ under rule 36.
