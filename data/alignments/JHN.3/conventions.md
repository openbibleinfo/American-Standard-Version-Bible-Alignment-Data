# John 3 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- A fused particle idiom — two Greek function words the ASV renders with one English word that realizes neither separately (ἐὰν μή / εἰ μή / ἵνα μή -> except, but, lest) — is one noncompositional unit headed on the first particle. Contrast rule 35: where the single English word visibly carries both elements (οὐ δύναται -> 'cannot', οὐδὲ ἕν -> 'nothing'), the unit stays aligned.
- A supplied English preposition that marks a bare Greek accusative object recast as a prepositional object rides with the object, not with the verb: 'bear witness of that which' gives 'of' to ὃ (v. 11) as 'of that he beareth witness' gives it to τοῦτο (v. 32).
