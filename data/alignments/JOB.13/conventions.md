# Job 13 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **JOB.13.8** — נָשָׂא פָּנִים → 'show partiality' (13.8, 13.10) is one noncompositional unit on פָּנֶה + the verb: the idiom is rendered by words of its own writing neither lexeme, so the source head is the first token פָּנֶה under rule 6's fallback (the governing verb is the competing head) and the target head is 'partiality' by rule 44's subtraction. The verb's subject pronoun and auxiliary join the unit, as does the 'to' the English verb needs to reach a suffix's 'him' (rule 44); the suffix keeps its own pronoun.
- **JOB.13.5** — חָרֵשׁ hiphil → 'hold your peace' / 'hold my peace' (13.5, 13.13, 13.19) is one unit headed on 'peace' (rule 44); the possessive the English idiom writes on the complement stays inside the verb's unit and is not rule 18's added possessive. Likewise גָּוַע → 'give up the ghost' (13.19) keeps 'the' inside.
- **JOB.13.14** — A preposition fused with מָה into one English interrogative — עַל־מָה and לָמָּה → 'Wherefore' (13.14, 13.24), כַּמָּה → 'How many' (13.23) — is one aligned unit headed on מָה as the morpheme carrying the interrogative content (rule 23); the preposition as first-token syntactic head is the competing reading, named in each note. Where the fused pair is the clause's predicate the construction copula joins it (13.23 'How many are').
- **JOB.13.4** — וְאוּלָם → 'But' (13.4): one English word for two morphemes, so 'But' goes to אוּלָם, whose own lexeme it writes, and the waw is unrepresented (rule 14's וְעַתָּה count); giving 'But' to the waw is the competing reading. Bare אוּלָם → 'Surely' (13.3) takes its word alone.
