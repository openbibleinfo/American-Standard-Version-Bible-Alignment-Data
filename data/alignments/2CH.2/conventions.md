# 2 Chronicles 2 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The labour counts of 2.2 and 2.18 divide alike: שִׁבְעִים → threescore and ten is one numeral unit headed on threescore (ten the competing head); סַבָּל → to bear burdens is one unit headed on burdens, the ASV having recast the agent noun as an infinitive phrase; חֹצֵב → that were hewers keeps the supplied relative and copula with the participle whether a noun precedes it (2.2, rule 41) or not (2.18, rule 46).
- יוֹדֵעַ שֵׂכֶל/בִּינָה → endued with discretion/understanding (2.12, 2.13): the with is the English verb's valency preposition reaching the participle's bare object and stays with the participle under rule 44, not with the noun under rule 33; the head is endued.
- In the provision list of 2.10 the supplied of before each commodity (of wheat, of barley, of wine, of oil) belongs to the commodity noun as a bare nominal under rule 33, not to the measure noun (כֹּר → measures, בַּת → baths) it stands after; in חִטִּים מַכּוֹת → of beaten wheat the of goes to חִטִּים, discontinuous around beaten.
