# Titus 3 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **TIT.3.11** — An article governing a substantival demonstrative is unrepresented, and the demonstrative takes the whole English expression including any generic noun the ASV supplies: ὁ τοιοῦτος → “such a one” gives all three words to τοιοῦτος headed on “such” and nothing to ὁ. This matches TIT.3.8's ταῦτα → “These things” and τούτων → “these things,” and the corpus, where an article immediately before a demonstrative is unrepresented in all six occurrences (3JN.1.8, EPH.5.27, JHN.5.47, JHN.8.5, PHP.2.29, REV.18.17). The contrast is a substantival article before an adjective, which does supply the nominal word: TIT.3.14's οἱ ἡμέτεροι → “our people” keeps “people” on the article. Promoted to the Greek contract as rule 49 in nt-greek-verse-v26.
- **TIT.3.4** — A supplied English possessive falls to the article of the phrase it stands in unless a source word inside that phrase realizes it. In ἡ χρηστότης καὶ ἡ φιλανθρωπία … τοῦ σωτῆρος ἡμῶν θεοῦ → “the kindness of God our Saviour, and his love toward man,” θεοῦ is already spent on “of God” in the first conjunct, so it does not displace the second conjunct's article: ἡ takes “his” and θεοῦ keeps “of God” alone. Rule 20's displacement is reserved for the cases it names, where the possessive is the displacing word's own realization—as it is for ἡμῶν in this same verse and in TIT.3.6, for αὐτοῦ in TIT.3.5, and for ἐκείνου in TIT.3.7, all four leaving their articles unrepresented. Corpus support: 31 aligned article→possessive units across 18 chapters.
