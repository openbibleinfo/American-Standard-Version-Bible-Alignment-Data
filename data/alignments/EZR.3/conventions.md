# Ezra 3 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- יָסַד in every stem (Pual 3.6, Piel 3.10, Hophal 3.11, Qal infinitive construct 3.12) is rendered by the ASV as 'the foundation ... was laid' or 'laid the foundation'; the whole phrase, including the supplied 'the', belongs to the verb's one discontinuous unit headed on 'foundation' (rule 44, light verb plus the complement carrying the lexeme), and the Hebrew subject or object noun the recasting demotes (הֵיכַל, בַּיִת) takes the 'of' the ASV writes before it under rule 33 only where no independent source relation licenses it, with its own supplied 'the' under rule 29. In 3.10, the present object marker אֵת instead owns 'of' connecting the retained verbal rendering 'laid the foundation' to its marked object under rules 11, 16 and 44; הֵיכַל retains 'the temple'. The 'yet' of 3.6 'was not yet laid' is added; the competing 'not yet' on לֹא was noted and not taken.
- כַּכָּתוּב → 'as it is written' (3.2, 3.4): the assimilated article on the passive participle takes the supplied 'it' under rule 41 as the substantival article's pronoun, and the participle keeps 'is written' with its copula under rule 20; the competing reading that marks 'it' added as an impersonal dummy was noted in both verses and not taken.
