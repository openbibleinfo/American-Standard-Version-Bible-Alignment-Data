# 1 Samuel 6 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The compound place names BEIT SHEMESH and QIRYAT YEARIM arrive as two Np tokens sharing one lemma. Align each to the English word written for it -- Beth and shemesh, Kiriath and jearim -- rather than fusing them under rule 30, and give any relational or directional word the ASV writes for the whole name to the first token, which is the word that English stands before: the of of a construct relation under rule 19 (6.12, 6.15, 6.19, 6.20, 6.21) and the supplied to of rule 33 (6.9, with Ekron at 6.16 read on the same terms). Where the ASV writes the name for its inhabitants and supplies a noun phrase around it -- u-veit shemesh qotzrim -> they of Beth shemesh were reaping (6.13) -- the supplied they of joins the first token's unit under rule 46, on the ground that the written name is the source nominal the English noun phrase realizes; the competing reading, which marks they of added under rules 17 and 30, is named in that verse's note.
- The gentilic beit-ha-shimshi (6.14, 6.18) arrives as two Ngmsa tokens whose second carries the lemma ha. Read the surface and morphology rather than the lemma, under rule 39: the first token takes Beth and the second takes the written the together with shemite, and the article is not treated as a separate determiner morpheme to be counted against the phrase's the under rules 29 and 47.
