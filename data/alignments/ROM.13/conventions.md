# Romans 13 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- ROM.13 aligns the ASV's text and never its marginal alternatives, naming the margin in the affected unit's note instead. This decides real edges rather than presentation: 13:4's three 'Or, it' notes leave the supplied subject pronouns of ἐστιν as 'he', and 13:11's 'Or, our salvation nearer than when etc.' is declined, so ἡμῶν is aligned as the word governed by ἐγγύτερον and not as a possessive on σωτηρία. Rule 11 makes the ASV's own addition markup evidence rather than a verdict; a marginal rendering is weaker still, being a translation the ASV did not adopt in its text.
