# John 18 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Name + article + participle (Ἰούδας ὁ παραδιδοὺς αὐτόν, JHN.18.2 and JHN.18.5): the article governs an expressed head noun, so under rule 31 it is unrepresented and the supplied relativizer 'who' is the participle's helper. Rule 32's split — article takes the pronoun, participle takes 'that ...' — applies only where no head noun is expressed (JHN.18.4, 18.14, 18.21, 18.37).
- Partitive ἐκ in 'Art thou also one of ... disciples?' (JHN.18.17, 18.25) takes the supplied 'one of', headed on 'of', and is recorded at medium confidence in both verses because reading 'one' as added remains defensible (rules 7, 11, 16).
- Three articles that look alike were decided by what their modifier is. Before a participle with an expressed head noun the article is unrepresented and the relativizer is the participle's (rule 31: JHN.18.2, 18.5). Before an attributive adjective there is no verb to carry the relativizer, so the article keeps 'who' and the adjective takes the supplied copula under rule 53 (ὁ μαθητὴς ὁ ἄλλος ὁ γνωστός -> 'the other disciple, who was known', JHN.18.16). Where a second attributive article's noun is itself rendered as a finite clause the article is unrepresented and 'that' is the noun's helper (ἡ παιδίσκη ἡ θυρωρός -> 'The maid ... that kept the door', JHN.18.17); a bare article + θυρωρῷ with no head noun keeps the pronoun instead (JHN.18.16).
