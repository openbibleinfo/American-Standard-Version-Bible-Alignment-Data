# Revelation 4 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in `AGENTS.md` once the chapter is finished.

- **REV.4.4** — In ASV 'four and twenty' for Greek εἴκοσι τέσσαρες, keep the recoverable numerals separate despite reordering: τέσσαρες aligns to 'four', while εἴκοσι aligns to 'and twenty', headed on 'twenty'.
