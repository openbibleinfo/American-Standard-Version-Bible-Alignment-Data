# Joshua 18 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- In the border gazetteer, the lieth of a verbless relative clause with a prepositional predicate is the construction's linking verb under rules 20 and 49, not a supplied lexical verb under rule 20's added clause: it joins the preposition heading the predicate and the unit heads on that preposition. So מִנֶּגֶב → lieth on (18.13) and עַל־פְּנֵי → lieth before (18.14, 18.16), read exactly as the bare copula of בְּ → is in (18.16) and נֹכַח → is over against (18.17). Subtraction decides it: the Hebrew preposition has already made the locative predication, so what lieth adds is the link English writes for it, not a predication the Hebrew never wrote.
- A compound cardinal whose two Hebrew morphemes the ASV writes as one English word is one fused unit under rule 30, and neither morpheme is unrepresented: שְׁתֵּים עֶשְׂרֵה → twelve (18.24) and אַרְבַּע עֶשְׂרֵה → fourteen (18.28). Head on the first numeral morpheme as rule 6's deterministic anchor, since the one English word writes no morpheme's lexeme in particular. Rule 42's count of waws against ands does not reach these: no waw stands between the two morphemes and no source item is coordinated.
- מִן before a direction noun is read off the ASV and not off the lemma pair: מִצָּפוֹן → northward is one fused unit at 18.17 because a single English word carries both morphemes, while מִצָּפוֹן → on the north (18.12) and מִנֶּגֶב → on the south (18.5) are split, each morpheme holding a word of its own.
