# Exodus 34 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **EXO.34.3** — Where an explicit demonstrative follows an article and ASV writes one demonstrative determiner, the demonstrative takes it and the article is unrepresented.
- **EXO.34.15** — In construct chains, the relational of belongs to the nomen rectum, while a present rectum article takes ASV the, including when English fronts that article before the construct head.
- **EXO.34.16** — Renderings of zanah as play the harlot or make play the harlot head on harlot, the lexical complement, rather than on the light verbs play or make.
