# Isaiah 62 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **ISA.62.2** — The chapter's recurring naming formula (קרא + לְ + suffix, 'and thou shalt be called') is recast in the ASV as a personal passive rather than an impersonal active. Treat it the same way throughout: leave the לְ preposition unrepresented under the recasting rule, and align its pronominal suffix to the new English subject (thou/they), at medium confidence.
