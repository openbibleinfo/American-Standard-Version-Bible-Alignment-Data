# Jeremiah 48 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **JER.48.31** — Treat עַל־כֵּן rendered by the single English connective therefore as one noncompositional unit, headed on the first source token and therefore.
- **JER.48.13** — Split a compound Hebrew place name when the ASV spends distinct transliterated English tokens on its distinct source tokens; proper-name status alone does not make the correspondence noncompositional.
- **JER.48.2** — In the repeated privative construction rendered from being a nation or people, assign the supplied being frame to מִן together with from, headed on from.
