# 2 Samuel 24 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- goren is rendered by the two-word 'threshing floor' throughout the chapter (24.16, 24.18, 24.21, 24.24) and heads on 'threshing', since 'floor' alone is any floor (rule 31). morag rendered 'threshing instruments' and shelem rendered 'peace offerings' head the same way, on 'threshing' and on 'peace'.
- na takes whatever the ASV writes for it and nothing more: the whole entreaty formula where one is written ('I beseech thee' at 24.10, 'I pray thee' at 24.17, rule 30), and a bare 'now' where that is all the ASV writes (24.2, 24.14). A written attah takes the 'now' itself wherever in the clause it stands (24.10, 24.13, 24.16), so count the clause's adverbs against the particles before placing one.
