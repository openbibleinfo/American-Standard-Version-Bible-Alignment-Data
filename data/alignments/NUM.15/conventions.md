# Numbers 15 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Determiners in this chapter's distributive phrases are counted morpheme by morpheme. Where a definite noun is followed by הָאֶחָד and the ASV writes a single distributive word for the whole phrase, the numeral takes it and every article in the phrase is unrepresented: לַכֶּבֶשׂ הָאֶחָד → for each lamb (v.5), לַשּׁוֹר הָאֶחָד and לָאַיִל הָאֶחָד → for each bullock, for each ram (v.11). Where the ASV writes a determiner and a numeral word, each morpheme takes the word written for it: לָאֶחָד → to every one (v.12) gives every to the assimilated article and one to אֶחָד. Where no numeral stands in the phrase the article takes the determiner, as הַכָּנָף → of each border (v.38) does. And לַשֶּׂה בַּכְּבָשִׂים → for each of the he lambs (v.11) gives each to שֶׂה, the single head of small cattle, leaving the assimilated article unrepresented: there the word names the animal rather than determining a noun the ASV has otherwise written, and giving it to the article under rule 41 would leave a lexical noun with no English at all.
- בְּ or לְ plus שְׁגָגָה is carried by the single word unwittingly and is one unit throughout the chapter (vv.24, 26, 27, 28, 29). Where the clause writes a verb, that verb takes the English verb — נֶעֶשְׂתָה לִשְׁגָגָה → it be done unwittingly (v.24). Where it writes none, the construction's copula joins the fused unit under rules 20 and 49 while a supplied lexical verb standing beside it is its own added unit: כִּי לְכָל־הָעָם בִּשְׁגָגָה → it was done unwittingly (v.26) gives it was to בִּשְׁגָגָה and leaves done added.
- A preposition the ASV supplies before a nominal that writes none joins that nominal's own unit under rule 33, and this chapter places it on the word carrying the English nominal head: on the noun where the noun does — הַקָּהָל → For the assembly (v.15), רֵאשִׁית → Of the first (v.20), עֹלָה → for a burnt offering (v.8), שְׁלֹשָׁה → of three (v.9) — and on a substantival article's supplied pronoun where that pronoun is the head, הָאֶזְרָח → for him that is home born (v.29), whose unit heads on him under rule 41 while אֶזְרָח keeps is home born. Giving that for to אֶזְרָח instead, discontinuous around him that, was the competing reading and is what rule 33's parenthetical 'the noun or numeral opening that phrase' would suggest; it was not taken because the article's pronoun, not the substantive, is the word the preposition stands before.
