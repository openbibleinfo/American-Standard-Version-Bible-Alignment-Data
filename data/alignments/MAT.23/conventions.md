# Matthew 23 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- An article standing after an expressed anarthrous vocative and governing a participle in apposition to it is `unrepresented`, and the supplied address word rides on the vocative noun under rule 89's anarthrous arm: 23:16 and 23:24 hodegoi typhloi, hoi legontes / hoi diylizontes -> *ye blind guides, that say* and *that strain out*, and 23:37 Ierousalem Ierousalem, he apokteinousa -> *O Jerusalem, Jerusalem, that killeth*. Rule 89's closing clause about a following participle reaches only an article the ASV writes the address word for; rules 31 and 32 empty this one, and 23:37 confirms the line by aligning tous before apestalmenous to *them* in the same verse. BSB reports 23:16 hoi and 23:37 he as represented, and that is not a correction.
- epano autou is one `aligned` unit on *thereon* headed on the pronoun at 23:20 and 23:22, where the ASV writes one solid word, and splits at 23:18 into to -> *that*, epano -> *is upon*, autou -> *it*, where it writes four. The count of English words decides (rules 35, 81, 94); the identical Greek phrase read two ways in one chapter is rule 24's habit and not an inconsistency to level. 23:21 auton -> *therein* takes the whole solid word on the pronoun though no preposition is expressed, rule 106's accusative arm having no separate particle to place.
