# 2 Kings 8 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Compound proper names that MACULA segments as two Np tokens (בֶן־הֲדַד 8.7, 8.9; רָמֹת גִּלְעָד 8.28) are two units, each token taking the ASV's own half of the name (Ben / hadad, Ramoth / gilead); they are not fused under rule 30, since the ASV spends a word on each.
