# 1 Samuel 18 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Verb + bə'eyney: fused into the ASV's single verb only where the morphemes stand together — v. 8 ra' bə'eynaw → *displeased*, one unit headed on the verb, suffix keeping *him*. The previous v. 20 judgment remains recorded: where the subject intervenes, the verb alone holds *pleased* and bə and eyney are each unrepresented under the predicate-contiguity limit. At v. 26, the intervening subject still prevents fusion with the earlier predicate, but the contiguous experiencer nominal is fused separately under rule 6; this supersedes only the former declaration that bə and eyney are unrepresented at v. 26. Where bə'eyney stands under a predicate with no verb to fuse into, it fuses to the ASV's one word (v. 23 → *to*, headed on eyney); where the ASV writes *in the sight of* (v. 5) each morpheme takes its own word.
- hithatten bammelekh → *be the king's son in law* (vv. 22, 23, 26, 27): bə is unrepresented (recast as possessive, rule 40), the assimilated article takes the *the* standing before the possessor's word (rule 29), melekh takes *king's*, and the verb's unit holds *be son in law* headed on *son*, discontinuous around the possessive. A preceding lə takes whatever the ASV writes for it (*to* v. 26, *that* v. 27) and the bare infinitive of v. 23 takes its own *to* (rule 52).
- Periphrastic hayah + participle: where the ASV writes one finite verb for the pair and the subject stands between them (v. 9 → *eyed*, v. 14 → *behaved himself wisely*), the participle holds the verb and hayah is unrepresented rather than fused (rule 30 contiguity); where the ASV writes copula + noun (v. 29 → *was David's enemy*) hayah takes *was* and the participle the noun.
