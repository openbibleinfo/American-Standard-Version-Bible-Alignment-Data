# Psalms 107 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- When ASV renders the chapter's bare jussive praise refrain as 'Oh that men would praise,' the verb takes the optative frame 'Oh that ... would praise' headed on 'praise,' while the unsupported lexical subject 'men' remains added.
