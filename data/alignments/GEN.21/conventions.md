# Genesis 21 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **GEN.21.16** — In an arthrous construct chain, assign of to the nomen rectum and the to its article; do not assign of to the article.
- **GEN.21.17** — A wholly supplied lexical verb in a verbless clause is added; a neighboring preposition with no English relation word remains unrepresented.
