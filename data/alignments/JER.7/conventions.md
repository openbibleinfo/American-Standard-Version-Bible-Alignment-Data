# Jeremiah 7 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- לְמִן is one unit. Where the ASV renders the compound preposition לְ + מִן by a single English word — Since (7.25), from (7.7) — the two morphemes fuse as one noncompositional unit headed on מִן, rather than giving the word to מִן and marking the לְ unrepresented. 7.7 was corrected to match 7.25.
- A demonstrative phrase standing as nomen rectum: in נִבְלַת הָעָם הַזֶּה → the dead bodies of this people (7.33) the chain's single written the goes to the article morpheme on הָעָם, fronted to the phrase's left edge under rule 29, while זֶה keeps this and its agreement article is unrepresented. This is the chain case and does not disturb the chapter's eleven bare demonstrative phrases (הַמָּקוֹם הַזֶּה → this place and the like), where the ASV writes no the for the phrase at all and both articles go unrepresented. Read whether a the is written for the chain before deciding.
- The formula אֲשֶׁר נִקְרָא שְׁמִי עָלָיו → which is called by my name (7.10, 7.11, 7.14, 7.30) is divided the same way every time: עַל takes by, שֵׁם and its suffix take my name, and the resumptive 3ms suffix on עָלָיו is unrepresented under rule 22, the ASV writing no second pronoun for it. BSB represents that suffix at all four; the difference is conventional.
- The ethical dative לָכֶם after a verb of trusting (אַל־תִּבְטְחוּ לָכֶם 7.4, בֹּטְחִים לָכֶם 7.8) is wholly unrendered: the preposition and its suffix are each their own unrepresented unit, since the ASV writes nothing for either. Where the ASV instead recasts לָכֶם as a possessive (your own hurt 7.6, your God 7.23) the suffix takes that pronoun and only the preposition is unrepresented, rule 40.
