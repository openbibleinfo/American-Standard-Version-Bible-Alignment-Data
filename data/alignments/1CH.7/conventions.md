# 1 Chronicles 7 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Census totals: the construction-supplied copula goes on the predicate noun where the Hebrew writes one (7.40 מִסְפָּרָם אֲנָשִׁים ... → 'was ... men', discontinuous around the numerals) and otherwise on the first Hebrew numeral of the figure (7.2 'was twenty', 7.5 'were fourscore'); where the ASV instead writes 'were reckoned by genealogy' the 'were' is the infinitive's (7.7, 7.9).
- The infinitive הִתְיַחְשָׂם → 'reckoned by genealogy' (with 'were' where the ASV writes it) is one unit on the infinitive in 7.5, 7.7, 7.9 and 7.40, discontinuous around 'in all' in 7.5; its subject suffix takes 'they' where the ASV writes a pronoun (7.7, 7.9) and is unrepresented where it writes none (7.5) or spends 'of them' on the suffix of מִסְפָּר instead (7.40).
- Relative recastings: a present pronominal suffix takes the ASV's relative and the waw of its clause is unrepresented (7.15 'whose' on the suffix of אֲחֹתוֹ, 7.21 'whom' on the object suffix of הֲרָגוּם); a waw plus finite verb with no pronoun takes 'who' (7.24 וַתִּבֶן); an explicit הוּא takes 'who' with the copula on the predicate (7.31). The competing reading giving the relative to the waw is named in each verse note and was not taken.
- בֵּית אָבוֹת chains: written in full after a construct 'heads' (7.7, 7.9, 7.40) בַּיִת takes 'of houses' and אָב the possessive 'fathers'; under a לְ that holds its own English (7.2 'of', 7.4 'after') בַּיִת takes bare 'houses'; the elliptical רָאשֵׁי הָאָבוֹת of 7.11 gives 'of fathers houses' to אָב (rule 31) and 'their' to the article (rule 41). The article of the full chain takes the 'the' standing before 'fathers' (7.40).
