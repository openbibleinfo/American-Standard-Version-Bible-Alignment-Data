# Psalms 145 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The ASV reaches the bare objects of this chapter's verbs of proclamation through a supplied preposition — יֹאמֵרוּ → 'shall speak of' (vv. 6, 11), יְרַנֵּנוּ → 'shall sing of' (v. 7), יְדַבֵּרוּ → 'talk of' (v. 11), אָשִׂיחָה → 'Of ... will I meditate' (v. 5). Every such word is given to the verb's unit under rule 44 and never to the object noun under rule 33, and a second occurrence written over a coordinated member joins that same discontinuous unit under rule 25, which is why both 'Of's of v. 5 are the verb's. Where a preposition morpheme does stand before the object, rule 11's priority governs instead and that morpheme takes the word: לְכָל־חַי → 'of every living thing' (v. 16) puts the 'of' on the לְ.
- דּוֹר is read twice, by what the ASV writes. In v. 4 דּוֹר לְדוֹר → 'One generation ... to another' spends an English word on each of the three morphemes: the supplied 'One' joins the first noun as the determiner of its nominal expression under rule 15, the לְ takes 'to' discontinuously under rule 25, and the second, repeated דּוֹר takes the correlative 'another' as its own rendering, no אַחֵר standing in the phrase for it — marking that noun unrepresented and 'another' added would leave a written lexical noun holding nothing against rule 2. In v. 13 בְּכָל־דּוֹר וָדֹר → 'throughout all generations' is rule 22's distributive repetition instead: the first occurrence takes 'generations', the waw and the repeated occurrence are unrepresented, and the determiner slot before the noun is filled by the written כֹּל, which takes 'all'.
- A construct chain the ASV renders attributively writes no relational word between its two nouns, so rule 19 has nothing to place there and an 'of' standing before the whole phrase is the phrase-level word rule 33 gives to the noun opening it: גְדָל־חָסֶד → 'of great lovingkindness' (v. 8) puts 'of great' on גְדָל and leaves חֶסֶד its bare 'lovingkindness'. Where the ASV does write the relation between the two nouns, rule 19 governs as usual — אֶרֶךְ אַפַּיִם → 'Slow to anger' (v. 8) puts 'to anger' on the rectum. In v. 7 the two readings meet in one phrase: זֵכֶר רַב־טוּבְךָ → 'the memory of thy great goodness' gives the 'of' to רַב as the rectum of זֵכֶר under rule 19, while the inner רַב־טוּב chain is attributive and writes no second relation for טוּב.
