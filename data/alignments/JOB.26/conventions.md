# Job 26 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The hymnic participles of vv.7–9 (נֹטֶה, תֹּלֶה, צֹרֵר, מְאַחֵז) carry no subject morpheme, and the divine referent is written in this chapter only as the suffix of נֶגְדּוֹ in v.6, which the ASV renders 'God'. Rule 10 keeps that nominal inside v.6, so the ASV's supplied 'He' is its own added unit in each of vv.7, 8 and 9 (rule 25's מֵקִים example), while the finite verbs of vv.10 and 12 take their 'He' under rule 17. The supplied 'And' before the second, asyndetic participle in vv.7 and 9 is likewise added under rule 14.
- The rhetorical 'him that hath no wisdom' / 'him that is without power' / 'the arm that hath no strength' of vv.2–3 render לְלֹא + noun and a construct chain with לֹא + noun. The supplied antecedent and relative (with a supplied 'hath' where the ASV writes one) are one added unit under rule 2's נָצְרוּ דָעַת pattern; the dative לְ is unrepresented under rule 40; the negative takes the word the ASV writes for it ('no', or 'is without' with the construction copula under rule 20); and the noun keeps only its own word. The rule 46 headless-clause reading was considered and not taken, since the ASV wrote a lexical antecedent (him, the arm) rather than a bare noun-phrase frame.
