# Genesis 5 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **GEN.5.5** — Doubled year-noun in the age formula: Hebrew gives one counted noun (שָׁנָה/שָׁנִים) per numeral group where the ASV writes a single 'years'. The rendered occurrence is the LAST year token of the Hebrew compound numeral; the earlier one is its own unrepresented unit (rule 22). Both units are high confidence with an empty note - the convention settles the tie, so it is no longer a competing analysis. Do not decide this by plural/singular agreement: Hebrew writes שָׁנִים after numerals 2-10 and שָׁנָה after 11+ regardless of the total, while English 'years' is plural because the sum is, so the morphological match is an accident of two unrelated agreement systems and is not evidence. Do not decide it by English adjacency either - English forces the counted noun to the end of the numeral phrase whatever order the ASV uses. Tail-to-tail is what survives, and it is stable across both Hebrew group orders (hundreds-first in v5, small-group-first in v8) and across the ASV's reordering.
- **GEN.5.17** — Doubled waw inside a compound numeral: the ASV's single 'and' goes to the waw joining the same two numerals English joins (the units-to-tens link, as in 'ninety and five'), and the waw at the numeral-group boundary, where the ASV writes nothing, is unrepresented. Unlike the year-noun above this is determined rather than a tie-break, since the ASV's 'and' stands between specific numerals; all ten doubled cases in the chapter read this way at high confidence.
