# Psalms 99 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Supplied subject pronoun of a participial (verbless) clause: where a single nominal or explicit pronoun standing earlier in the verse denotes the referent, the pronoun joins that morpheme's discontinuous unit under rule 25 and the participle keeps only its verb (v1 'He sitteth' → יְהוָה; v8 'thou tookest vengeance' → the earlier אַתָּה). Where the referent is a coordination of several nominals that no single morpheme denotes (v6 'They called' for Moses, Aaron and Samuel), the pronoun stays with the participle rendered as a finite verb under rule 17 rather than being marked added.
