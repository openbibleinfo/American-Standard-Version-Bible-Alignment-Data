# Psalms 32 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- סָבַב rendered 'compass ... about' keeps 'about' inside the verb's unit, discontinuous around the object (vv.7, 10): 'compass' alone writes the encircling, so the particle restates what the verb writes rather than supplying a direction the Hebrew never wrote, and rule 44 marks a particle surplus only where both of its conditions hold.
