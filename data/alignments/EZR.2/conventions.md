# Ezra 2 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Rule 29's fronting clause applies to a rectum that is a proper name exactly as to a common noun. `bene ha-ramah` -> *The children of Ramah* (2.26) and `anshe bet-el we-ha-ay` -> *The men of Beth el and Ai* (2.28) give the ASV's single written *the* to the article morpheme and leave the construct noun bare; the article is not set aside as lexicalized in the toponym. Both verse passes corrected an earlier `unrepresented` reading and recorded the competitor in the unit note, 2.28's being the stronger because its article stands on the second conjunct of a compound rectum whose first member is an anarthrous name. Where the ASV instead transliterates the article into the name - `bene has-sophereth` -> *the children of Hassophereth* (2.55) - the article is already rendered inside *Hassophereth*, shares that name's unit, and the written *the* is a supplied one on the construct. Read the transliteration before counting the ASV's *the*s.
- Where an inner construct relation is realized by an English possessive, the possessive is that noun's whole rendering and the chain's *of* stays with the noun whose relation it marks, discontinuously across the fronted possessive: `bene abde shelomoh` -> *The children of Solomon's servants* (2.55, 2.58) gives *of* ... *servants* to abde and *Solomon's* alone to shelomoh. Do not hand the *of* to the word it stands before, which here is the possessive; rule 29's read-which-word-it-stands-before test governs supplied *the*s, not the chain's *of*.
