# Numbers 7 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **NUM.7.15** — In the repeated בן/בני שנה age formula rendered “a year old,” בן/בני takes “old” and שנה takes “a year”; the indefinite article belongs to the count noun year.
- **NUM.7.49** — In the repeated מלא + substance formula rendered “full of” a substance, מלא takes “full of”; סלת takes “fine flour” headed on flour, and קטרת takes “incense.”
- **NUM.7.17** — In זבח השלמים rendered “the sacrifice of peace offerings,” the article on the nomen rectum takes the fronted English “the,” while the construct זבח takes only “sacrifice.”
- **NUM.7.48** — In the repeated definite day-plus-ordinal phrase with two article morphemes but one English “the,” the first article on day takes “the” and the ordinal article is unrepresented.
