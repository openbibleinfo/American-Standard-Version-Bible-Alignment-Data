# 2 Kings 3 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- In the repeated lament 'Jehovah hath called these three kings together' (3.10, 3.13) the 'together' is a surplus particle answering to no morpheme and is `added` under rule 44; it is not the promoted-object לְ (unrepresented under rule 40) nor the demonstrative's. חֶלְקָה → 'piece of land' (3.19, 3.25) is one unit headed on 'piece', 'land' the competing head.
