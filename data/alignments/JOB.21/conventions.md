# Job 21 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The ASV's list of the wicked's fate (vv. 17–18, 30) opens each colon with 'That'. Only the waw of v.17b (וְיָבֹא) licenses one and takes it under rule 14; the 'That' of v.17c and v.18a stands over asyndetic clauses and is added under rule 55. In v.30, the opening כִּי (`s001`) governs both parallel disaster-day propositions and takes both occurrences of 'That' (`t001`, `t012`) in one discontinuous aligned unit headed on `s001`/`t001` (rules 25, 55); the asyndesis of v.30b does not terminate that scope. The cleft 'is it that' of v.17a is likewise added (rule 20), and the speech frame 'Ye say' of v.19 is one added unit (rule 2).
