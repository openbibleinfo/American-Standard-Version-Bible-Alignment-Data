# John 19 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Where a compound verb of motion is followed by a pleonastic ἔξω and the ASV writes one 'out', the verb takes the motion word and ἔξω takes 'out', recorded medium: leaving ἔξω unrepresented as the compound's own particle stays defensible (JHN.19.4, JHN.19.5).
- A supplied English object pronoun on a verb with no expressed Greek object is a rule 7 helper on that verb, recorded high, not 'added': rule 11 keeps the ASV's italics from deciding it (JHN.19.1, JHN.19.6, JHN.19.15, JHN.19.19).
