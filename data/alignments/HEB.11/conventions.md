# Hebrews 11 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Where no Greek preposition stands, an English preposition the verb governs rides with the verb and its object stands bare: seek after him (11.6), looked for the city (11.10), seeking after a country (11.14), is ashamed of them (11.16), passed through the Red sea (11.29), as the pure particles of went out (11.8, 11.15), fell down (11.30), receive back (11.19), went about (11.30, 11.37), sawn asunder (11.37) do. A supplied preposition realizing a genitive or dative case is the contrast and stays with the noun or with the article that takes the determiner under rule 21: to God (11.6), for them (11.16), of house (11.7), than the (11.26).
- A συν- compound governing a bare dative keeps the English with that renders its prefix inside its own unit, and the governed dative keeps its own English: heirs with him (συγκληρονόμων, 11.9), to share ill treatment with (συγκακουχεῖσθαι, 11.25), perished ... with (συναπώλετο, 11.31, the article τοῖς taking them). The with is not added and is not given to the dative.
- A substantival article before a bare adjective, with no head noun expressed and no participle following, keeps both the supplied pronoun and the supplied relativizer, and the adjective takes the supplied copula: τὸν ... ἀόρατον → him who / is invisible (11.27). This is rule 31's carve-out for a modifier with no verb to carry the relativizer, reaching an adjective, with rule 53 placing the copula. The participle cases are the contrast and go the other way throughout the chapter, the article keeping only the pronoun: 11.6, 11.11, 11.14, 11.17, 11.31.
- Where the ASV compresses a Greek noun and its dependent genitive into a single English noun, the light governing noun is its own unrepresented unit and the surviving English word belongs to the genitive: παρὰ καιρὸν ἡλικίας → when she was past age leaves καιρόν empty (11.11), and ἐν φόνῳ μαχαίρης → with the sword leaves φόνῳ empty (11.37). Do not stretch the surviving noun back over both. A reference comparison reports both as disagreements.
- An English function word the ASV repeats from a token expressed earlier in the same sentence rides with the word it introduces under rule 86, and is neither added nor stretched back to the first token: the second that at 11.6 (licensed by ὅτι) rides with γίνεται, and the second of at 11.32 (licensed by περί) rides with Δαυίδ. Rule 86 names a conjunction; this chapter reads a preposition the same way.
- καί and τε are decided on the English alone, as rule 65 has it: even (11.19, 11.20), yea (11.17), also (11.12, 11.19), and where the ASV writes no word the particle is its own unrepresented unit. That covers the second intensifying καί of 11.11 and the τε of τε καί at 11.32, where the single English and goes to καί and τε realizes nothing; do not join the pair to give τε a share of the one word.
- A light-verb pair is decided by the ASV's word count and never by the Greek idiom. πεῖραν λαβόντες → assaying to do is one collapsed rule 73 unit at 11.29, while the same collocation at 11.36 (had / trial), καιρὸν ... εἶχον at 11.15 (they have had / opportunity) and ἔχειν ... ἀπόλαυσιν at 11.25 (to enjoy / the pleasures) split under rule 2, the ASV having spelled both elements out. The lemma table shows this as three treatments of one lemma pair; it is one analysis.
- The chapter records no added unit. Every supplied English word was folded onto a source token that licenses it, under rules 7, 11, 86 and 105: country (11.9, 11.15, 11.16), son (11.17), of God (11.7), leaning (11.21), even and he (11.18), so many (11.12), is (11.19), unto him (11.6), things (11.1), that (11.16). added was reserved for a word no token licenses, and no unit needed it.
