# Luke 12 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Supplied existential 'there' is a rule 7 helper inside the verb's own unit, headed on the verb, and is never its own unit and never `added`: ἐστίν → 'there is' (12:2), ἔσονται → 'there shall be' (12:52), ἔρχεται → 'There cometh' (12:54), ἔσται → 'There will be' (12:55). No source token answers to it and the verb is what licenses it.
- διαφέρω rendered by the periphrasis 'be of more value' is one unit on the verb headed on *value* — 12:7 'ye are of more value', 12:24 'value are ye' — with the supplied 'of' and 'more' riding inside it, no comparative source word standing. The genitive of comparison keeps only 'than' with its noun (12:7 στρουθίων) or with its article under rule 21 (12:24 τῶν).
- Where one Greek verb is rendered by an English light-verb phrase the chapter heads on the word carrying the lexeme's sense under rules 17 and 91, never on the finite verb: Ὁρᾶτε → 'Take heed' on *heed* (12:15), ἀναπαύου → 'take thine ease' on *ease* (12:19), θησαυρίζων → 'layeth up treasure' on *treasure* (12:21), μετεωρίζεσθε → 'be ye of doubtful mind' on *doubtful* (12:29), ἐγγίζει → 'draweth near' on *near* (12:33), ἑτοιμάσας → 'made ready' on *ready* (12:47). ἀληθῶς → 'Of a truth' on *truth* (12:44) is the same test for an adverb rendered by a phrase.
- Supplied English is `added` only where no source token licenses it: the resumed verb at 12:28 ('shall he clothe', ἀμφιέζει already spent, rule 103); the apodosis clause at 12:39 ('he would have watched and', rule 97, SBLGNT.LUK.12.39.v034 marked FALLBACK EXCLUDED BY EVIDENCE with r01 and r02 remaining); the asyndetic seam at 12:42 ('and' between two attributive adjectives, rule 98); and the supplied verb of perceiving at 12:55 ('ye see', its Greek standing and already spent in 12:54, and not rule 37 since 12:54's own English is present). Where a token does license it the chapter folds instead: the repeated possessive 'my … my' onto the one μου at 12:18, which is rule 103's pronoun contrast, and the elliptical complement 'to do' onto the modal δύνασθε at 12:26, the modal being what requires it in English.
