# Joshua 17 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- A `כִּי` the ASV renders by a coordinator plus a subordinator — *for though*, *and though* — is one rule 55 unit headed on the subordinating word, not on the coordinator. Rule 55's 'head on the coordinator' is written for a waw, whose own nature is coordinating; a `כִּי` is a subordinator, and JOS.17.18 writes a bare `כִּי` -> *though* beside the two pairs, which identifies the word realizing its lexeme.
- Possessive `לְ` divides by what the source writes, and the chapter writes all three shapes. A written `הָיָה` takes the *had*, *shall have* or *shall be* itself and leaves its lamed `unrepresented` (vv. 1, 3, 11, 17, 18); a verbless possessive clause puts the *hast* or *have* on the preposition, `בְּ` as readily as `לְ` (vv. 16, 17, 18); and where the ASV writes the relation inside an English genitive and spends no word on the preposition, the lamed fuses with the following name under rule 49's closing paragraph and the construction's copula joins that unit (v. 10, *was Ephraim's*, *was Manasseh's*). A suffix rendered by a bare possessive — v. 18's *thine* — is not such a genitive, since that is the word a bare suffix takes in any case, so the lamed is `unrepresented` there.
- Supplied English subjects: *this* in v. 1 is the subject pronoun of the written wayyiqtol under rule 17, while the two *it*s of v. 10 stand in verbless clauses where no morpheme can carry them and are `added` under rule 20's impersonal-*it* sentence. What decides it is whether a verb morpheme stands in the clause. A supplied lexical subject nominal is `added` either way — *the lot* (v. 2), *the inhabitants of* (v. 12).
- The chapter's two *even*s are rule 16's own two cases. *even to Ephraim* (v. 17) is the expanded rendering of the `לְ` standing beside it, headed on *to*; *even the three heights* (v. 11) stands before a bare nominal with no marker and no preposition, and the verse's single object marker stands on `אֶת־יֹשְׁבֵי דֹאר`, so it is `added` and is not reached across the clause for.
