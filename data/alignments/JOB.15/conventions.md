# Job 15 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **JOB.15.22** — A bare infinitive construct (no preposition) that the ASV recasts as a finite complement clause — שׁוּב → that he shall return — takes the complementizer, the supplied subject pronoun and the modal into the infinitive's own unit, on the footing rule 52 gives a bare infinitive its to and its for … to frame; the that is not marked added under rule 55, which governs a clause the Hebrew writes finite. Competing reading named in the unit's note.
