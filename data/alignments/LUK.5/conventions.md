# Luke 5 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- A Greek word the ASV renders by a hyphenated English compound that tokenizes as two words forms one unit with both tokens, headed on the compound's English head element: askos -> 'wine skins' headed on skins (5.37, 5.38), nymphon -> 'bride chamber' on chamber (5.34), semeron -> 'to day' on day (5.26). Where the ASV instead writes a genuine phrase the question is rule 66's and the content word heads, which is why telonion -> 'place of toll' heads on toll (5.27) rather than on the generic place.
- A periphrastic en/esan plus participle splits into two units wherever the ASV writes a two-word English verb phrase for it (5.1 'was standing', 5.17 'was teaching' and 'there were ... sitting by', 5.29 'were ... sitting at meat'), and is one aligned unit only where the ASV collapses the pair into a single English verb, as 5.16 en hypochoron -> 'withdrew himself' does, headed on the participle and on that verb. This is rule 73's collapse test carried from a Greek light-verb pair to a periphrasis; leaving the copula unrepresented there is the competing reading.
- The apodotic kai of the egeneto frame is decided on the English alone: aligned to the 'that' the ASV supplies (5.1, 5.17) and its own unrepresented unit where the ASV supplies nothing before the apodosis (5.12, before 'behold'). Do not read the aligned instances as licensing a supplied word in 5.12, and do not read 5.12 as evidence that the conjunction is empty elsewhere.
- A supplied English 'and' that joins a converted circumstantial participle to the verb following it rides with that verb under rule 88 even where the following verb is non-finite: 5.7 tou elthontas syllabesthai -> 'that they should come and help them' gives 'and help' to the infinitive, as 5.24, 5.25 and 5.28 give it to their finite verbs. Read 5.7's note as citing rule 88; rule 40, which it names, governs a repeated infinitival marker under an expressed kai and does not apply. Rule 98 does not reach this seam, the two constituents being a participle and an infinitive rather than two like constituents rendered alike.
