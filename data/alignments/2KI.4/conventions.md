# 2 Kings 4 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **2KI.4.5** — Treat contiguous מן + את (H854) with one undivided ASV prepositional rendering as one fused unit; 4:3, 4:5, and 4:28 follow this analysis.
- **2KI.4.23** — In the written neither ... nor pairs at 4:23 and 4:31, the opening negative takes neither and the closing nor fuses the waw with the repeated negative, headed on that negative.
- **2KI.4.43** — The bare יתר verbs at 4:43 and 4:44 take leave/left only; ASV thereof is an unsupported object and is added, as with the parallel unsupported thereof at 4:40.
