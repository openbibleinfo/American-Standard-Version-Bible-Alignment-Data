# Genesis 30 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **GEN.30.35** — When ASV writes a lexical possessive verb alongside a separately rendered Hebrew preposition, the lexical verb is added; in 'had white in it,' בְּ takes only 'in.'
- **GEN.30.36** — In 'betwixt X and Y,' the waw takes 'and' and the repeated second בֵּין is unrepresented because ASV renders the between relation once.
- **GEN.30.41** — בְּכָל rendered by the single word 'whensoever' is one fused unit; in the presentative frame, an apodosis waw takes the ASV's 'that.'
