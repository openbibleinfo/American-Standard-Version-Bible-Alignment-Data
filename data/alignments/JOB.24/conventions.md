# Job 24 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The chapter describes the wicked distributively in the singular (הוּא, 3ms suffixes on יִתֶּן־לוֹ, בְּלִילוֹ, אֵינֶנּוּ, קַל הוּא) while the ASV writes the class in the plural ('they', 'them', 'their'). Align the pronoun the ASV wrote to the morpheme that denotes it (rule 18) and do not mark the number mismatch: 24.5 s016 → 'them', 24.6 s005 → 'their', 24.18 s002 → 'they', 24.23 s003 → 'them', 24.24 s005 → 'they'.
- The ASV opens 24.2 and 24.9 with an existential-relative frame 'There are that' around a bare finite verb with an unwritten indefinite subject. No morpheme licenses the dummy, the copula or the relative, so the three words are one added unit doing one office (rule 2), headed on 'are'; the finite verb keeps only its lexical verb. Both verses are aligned that way.
