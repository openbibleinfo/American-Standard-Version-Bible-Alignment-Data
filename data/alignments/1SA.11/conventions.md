# 1 Samuel 11 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- הַגִּלְגָּל (vv.14, 15) is written bare 'Gilgal' by the ASV: the toponym's article is its own unrepresented unit (rule 29), and the supplied directional 'to' of 'go to Gilgal' / 'went to Gilgal' joins the name as an adverbial accusative (rule 33); the בַּגִּלְגָּל of v.15 gives 'in' to the preposition with the assimilated article still unrepresented.
