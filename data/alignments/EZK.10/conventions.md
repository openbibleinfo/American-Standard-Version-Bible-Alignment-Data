# Ezekiel 10 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- A supplied *as for* or appositional *even* goes to a morpheme standing on the phrase it introduces, and is `added` where none stands there. In this chapter the fronting לְ of לָאוֹפַנִּים takes *As for* (10:13) and the אֶל standing on *under the cherub* takes *even* (10:2), while the *as for* of 10:10 and 10:22 and the *even* of 10:12 stand before bare nouns carrying no marker and no preposition and are each their own `added` unit. The fronted noun does not take the expression: rule 33 gives a supplied function word to a noun whose own case-function licenses it, and topicalization is not a case-function.
- בֵּינוֹת לַ and תַּחַת לַ are split, not fused: בֵּינוֹת and תַּחַת take *between* and *under*, the assimilated article takes the written *the* (rule 12), and the לְ is its own `unrepresented` unit (rule 40) — the shape rule 27 already carves out for the לְ of לֵאמֹר. All six occurrences (10:2 ×3, 10:6 ×2, 10:7) go that way. What fuses under rule 30 here is a compound whose single English word no member's own lexeme supplies: מֵעַל → *from* (10:4, 10:16), בְּתוֹךְ → *within* (10:10), לְעֻמַּת → *beside* and מִלְמָעְלָה → *above* (10:19), כְּעֵין → *like unto* (10:9). Where the ASV spends a word on each member the pair splits instead, as *from over* does in 10:18.
- The two occurrences of אוֹתָם are read differently on the ASV's evidence, though MACULA tags both `To`: in 10:17 it is the preposition אֵת *with* (H854), which the ASV renders *with them* after *mounted up*, so the preposition takes *with* under rule 11; in 10:22 it is the object marker H853, whose suffix alone is realized by *themselves* while the marker itself goes `unrepresented` under rule 16.
