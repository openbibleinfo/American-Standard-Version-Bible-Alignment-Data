# 1 Corinthians 8 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- εἰδωλόθυτον is analysed by whether an article governs it, not by the English phrasing. Where one does (8:1, 8:4, 8:10) the article takes the supplied nominal things — with any case preposition riding along, as of things at 8:4 — and the adjective takes sacrificed to idols headed on sacrificed (rules 22, 102). Where the Greek is anarthrous (8:7) no article competes and the whole supplied expression a thing sacrificed to an idol belongs to the adjective, its determiners being rule 51 helpers and thing the generic completion, headed on the same word (rule 17).
- A supplied English existential there is rides with the predicate noun of a verbless clause and never with an attributive quantifier or numeral standing in front of it: εἷς θεός → there is one God (8:6) and οὐδεὶς θεός → there is no God (8:4) both give the discontinuous there is … God to θεός and leave the quantifier its own bare word. A negative quantifier is not an exception; carrying the clause's negation does not make it the carrier of a supplied verb. Where the negative is itself the predicate, as οὐδέν is in 8:4's first clause, rule 63 governs instead and the copula is its helper.
