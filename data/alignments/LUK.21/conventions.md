# Luke 21 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- ἐγγίζω and ἐγγύς head on the word that glosses the ἐγγυ- root, never on the supplied copula or the motion verb: ἤγγικεν → 'is at hand' heads on 'hand' at 21:8 and 21:20, ἐγγίζει → 'draweth nigh' heads on 'nigh' at 21:28, and the adverb ἐγγύς → 'nigh' heads on itself at 21:30 and 21:31. ἐγγίζω is denominal from ἐγγύς, so 'draweth' answers to no element of the lexeme and is the empty support word under rules 66 and 91, rule 104's φλογίζω → 'setteth on fire' being the model and rule 71's ἐγγύς → 'at hand' on 'hand' the confirmation. Heading ἐγγίζει on 'draweth', on the ground that 'draw nigh' reads as an ordinary verb phrase, remains the competing reading; 21:28 records a correction made in that direction.
- An article the ASV renders by a supplied distributive determiner is aligned to that determiner and headed on it, exactly as rules 51 and 60 align one to a supplied indefinite or demonstrative and rule 89 to a supplied address word: the accusatives of time at 21:37, τὰς ἡμέρας → 'every day' and τὰς νύκτας → 'every night', give 'every' to the article and a bare 'day' and 'night' to the nouns. The test is rules 51 and 60's own — whether the determiner is the article's own to take — and no other source token competes for the slot here, no πᾶς standing in either phrase. Do not reach for rule 23 or for 'added' merely because the supplied word is quantificational rather than 'the'.
- ὡς παγίς stands at the head of LUK.21.35 in the projection and the ASV translates it at the end of 21:34, so under rule 37 'as a snare' is added at 21:34 and both source tokens are unrepresented at 21:35, each verse's note naming the other. This is a versification difference and not a textual one: rule 97 is ruled out by name, LUK.21.34.v033 covering only s017–s020 and neither of its readings bearing on ὡς παγίς. The ASV's italicized 'so' at 21:35 is separately added under rule 5, the comparison it resumes being ὡς's and ὡς having already spent its English in the neighbouring verse. A reference comparison will report all of this as disagreement, and that report is not a correction.
