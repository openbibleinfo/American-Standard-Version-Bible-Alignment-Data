# Isaiah 6 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **ISA.6.1** — In ISA.6.1 and ISA.6.10, the single English “the” fronted to a construct head belongs to the Hebrew article on the nomen rectum; the construct noun keeps only “year” or “heart.”
- **ISA.6.7** — In ISA.6.7, both עַל phrases govern “mouth” or “lips” and are unrepresented when the ASV promotes that complement to a bare direct object; the supplied instrument phrase “with it” refers to the coal and is added as a whole.
- **ISA.6.13** — At the opening of ISA.6.13, contiguous “And if” is one rendering of the waw, headed on “And”; “if” is not added.
