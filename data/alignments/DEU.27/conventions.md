# Deuteronomy 27 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Determiner count in demonstrative phrases. Where a demonstrative pronoun morpheme stands in the phrase (`זֹאת`, `זֶה`, `אֵלֶּה`, `הוּא`), that pronoun takes the written *this*, *these*, *This* or *same*, and both the noun's own article and the agreement article on the demonstrative are `unrepresented` (27.3, 27.4, 27.8, 27.9, 27.26). Rule 41's construct-chain paragraph, which gives a demonstrative standing before the rectum's word to the rectum's article, does not reach these: the pronoun is a morpheme that licenses the determiner, so rule 41's later limit governs instead, and the corpus reading of `הֶעָרִים הָאֵלֶּה` → *these cities* agrees. Where no demonstrative pronoun is written, the article itself takes the demonstrative — `הַיּוֹם` → *this day* (27.1, 27.4, 27.10). Where the ASV writes two determiners, each morpheme takes the one standing before its own word — `בַּיּוֹם הַהוּא` → *the same day* (27.11) gives *the* to the present article and *same* to `הוּא`. In a construct chain the construct noun still takes a supplied *the* of its own: `כָּל־דִּבְרֵי הַתּוֹרָה הַזֹּאת` → *all the words of this law* (27.3, 27.8, 27.26) puts *the words* on `דִּבְרֵי`, *of law* on the rectum, and *this* on `זֹאת`.
- The twelvefold curse formula `אָרוּר` + anarthrous participle (27.15-27.25) is divided one way throughout: `אָרוּר` takes *Cursed* with the construction-supplied copula *be* (rule 20), and the participle takes the supplied *he that* together with its own English, headed on the word carrying its lexeme (rule 46) — on the complement where the ASV writes a light verb, as *setteth light by* (27.16) and *maketh ... to wander* (27.18) do. 27.26 is not a departure: the Hebrew writes `אֲשֶׁר` there, so the relative takes *he that* whole and heads on *that* under rule 46's first rider, a present morpheme taking its English before a bare participle does.
