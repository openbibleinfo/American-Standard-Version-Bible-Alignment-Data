# 2 Kings 1 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **2KI.1.3** — הֲמִבְּלִי אֵין אֱלֹהִים בְּיִשְׂרָאֵל → 'Is it because there is no God in Israel' (vv. 3, 6, 16): הַ takes the fronted copula Is (rules 17, 20); the cleft it is added, and the cleft that of vv. 3 and 6 is added too (rule 20); the privative מִן takes because (rule 48); the existential אֵין takes there is no, headed on no (rule 50); and בְּלִי, the second negative morpheme, is unrepresented, since the ASV writes one negation word and rule 50 keeps it with the existential. The competing reading, fusing מִבְּלִי → because, was set aside in every verse.
- **2KI.1.10** — שַׂר הַחֲמִשִּׁים → 'the captain of fifty' (vv. 10, 13, 14): the phrase's single the is the rectum article's, fronted under rule 29 because fifty is rendered anarthrously; the construct keeps the bare captain(s). Where an agreement article on a following ordinal or adjective repeats it (הַשְּׁלִישִׁי v. 13, הָרִאשֹׁנִים v. 14) that article is unrepresented under rule 47. Where no article morpheme stands (שַׂר־חֲמִשִּׁים, vv. 9, 11, 13a) the ASV's a or the is supplied on שַׂר, and the a of 'a third fifty' goes to the substantival numeral (rule 15).
- **2KI.1.9** — רֵדָה → 'Come down' (vv. 9, 11): the final he is the lengthened imperative's paragogic he, tagged Sh, and is its own unrepresented unit (rule 32 first sentence); down belongs to יָרַד's own rendering (rule 44), exactly as in the bare רֵד of v. 15. The MACULA gloss 'down' on the he is a display defect (rule 39), and BSB's aligning down to it is not followed.
- **2KI.1.4** — הַמִּטָּה אֲשֶׁר־עָלִיתָ שָּׁם לֹא־תֵרֵד מִמֶּנָּה → 'Thou shalt not come down from the bed whither thou art gone up' (vv. 4, 6, 16): אֲשֶׁר and the resumptive שָׁם share whither (rule 22), מִן takes from, the fronted הַמִּטָּה takes its own the bed, and the resumptive suffix on מִמֶּנָּה is unrepresented because the ASV writes the noun itself after from and no pronoun (rule 22).
