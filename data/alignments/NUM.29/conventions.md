# Numbers 29 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Sacrificial compound nouns head on the distinguishing word and never on *offering*: `מִנְחָה` → *meal*, `נֶסֶךְ` → *drink*, `חַטָּאת` → *sin*, `עֹלָה` → *burnt*, `שֶׁלֶם` → *peace*, `נְדָבָה` → *freewill*. Rule 31 already fixes `עֹלָה` → *burnt offerings* that way, and the ASV never writes *offering* alone for any of them in this chapter, so rule 31's subtraction test carries the rest. The corpus is split for `מִנְחָה` — 23 units headed on *meal* against 16 headed on *offering* — so this chapter takes one reading throughout and names the competing one at NUM.29.3.
- `מִלְּבַד` is one fused unit for *besides* under rule 30, headed on `בַּד` (rule 23), in all ten places, and the `לְבַד … מִן` of NUM.29.39 is read the same way. The morphemes are contiguous, the one English word carries the sense of each, and no morpheme owns a part of it, so the large-source-unit flag on these eleven units is expected rather than a defect.
- `בֶּן־בָּקָר` is split rather than fused: `בֵּן` takes the ASV's *young* and `בָּקָר` is its own `unrepresented` unit (NUM.29.2, 8, 13, 17), because *young* does not carry the herd noun's sense and rule 30's one-word condition therefore fails; `פַּר` keeps *bullock*. BSB attaches `בָּקָר` to its phrase and disagrees on all four.
- `שָׂעִיר`: where `עִזִּים` is written the pair splits — `שָׂעִיר` → *he*, `עִזִּים` → *goat* (vv.5, 11, 16, 19, 25); where no `עִזִּים` stands, `שָׂעִיר` alone carries the whole *he goat*, headed on *goat*, since the ASV writes *goat* alone for the class elsewhere in the chapter and *he* is therefore the specifier (rule 31; vv.22, 28, 31, 34, 38).
- `X יִהְיֶה לָכֶם` is read off the English, not the Hebrew shape. Where the ASV writes *ye shall have*, rule 43 governs: `הָיָה` takes the *have*, the `לְ` is `unrepresented`, and the suffix takes the subject pronoun (vv.1, 7, 12, 35). Where it writes *it is … unto you* or *they shall be unto you*, the copula and any dummy subject stay with `הָיָה` and the `לְ` takes its written *unto* (vv.1, 8). NUM.29.1 writes both clauses.
- In the daily formula the ASV's `the` is placed by counting what the Hebrew writes: `כַּמִּשְׁפָּט` (vv.18, 21, 24, 27, 30, 37) has an article morpheme, which takes it, while `כְּמִשְׁפָּטָם` (v.33) has a suffix and no article, so the supplied *the* joins `מִשְׁפָּט` under rule 15 and the suffix is `unrepresented` (rules 18, 22); v.6 writes *their* for that same suffix and the suffix takes it. The same count runs through the animal lists: an article present takes the written *the* (vv.4, 9, 10, 14 rams), and where none stands the supplied *the* joins the noun (vv.14 bullocks, 15 lambs).
