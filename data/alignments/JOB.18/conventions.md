# Job 18 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Impersonal 3mp verbs with a 3ms object suffix that the ASV recasts as an English passive (18.14 תַצְעִדֵהוּ → 'he shall be brought', 18.18 יֶהְדְּפֻהוּ … יְנִדֻּהוּ → 'He shall be driven … and chased'): the verb takes the passive frame and lexical verb, the object suffix takes the promoted subject pronoun, and where two suffixed verbs share one written English subject the second suffix is its own unrepresented unit (rules 17, 18, 22). BSB attaches that second suffix to its verb; the disagreement is convention.
