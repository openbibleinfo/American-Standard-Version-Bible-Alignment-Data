# 2 Chronicles 27 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Regnal age formula `בֶּן` + numeral + `שָׁנָה`: the construct `בֶּן` carries the English *old* in every verse, and the copula follows what the Hebrew writes. 27:1 has no verb, so the construction-supplied copula joins `בֶּן` (*was ... old*, discontinuous around the numeral phrase under rules 20 and 25) and the explicit subject `יוֹתָם` takes only its own name; 27:8 writes `הָיָה`, which takes *He was* with its subject pronoun under rule 17 and leaves `בֶּן` the bare *old*. The two verses are not a split — read the clause for a written `הָיָה` before placing the copula.
- `בַּשָּׁנָה הַהִיא` → *the same year* (27:5) writes two determiners for three candidate morphemes, so rule 41's count gives the noun's own article the *the*, gives the demonstrative pronoun the *same*, and leaves only the agreement article on the demonstrative `unrepresented`. Rule 41's own example `הַמָּקוֹם הַהוּא` → *that place* is the one-determiner case, where both articles are left with nothing; what separates them is how many determiners the ASV wrote, not that a demonstrative pronoun stands in the phrase.
