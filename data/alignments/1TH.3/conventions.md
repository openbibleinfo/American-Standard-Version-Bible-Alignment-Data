# 1 Thessalonians 3 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Elliptical comparative clauses: the chapter has two and analyses them differently because the Greek differs, not the English. 3:12's καθάπερ καὶ ἡμεῖς εἰς ὑμᾶς has a prepositional predicate, so the supplied lexical do rides with εἰς under rule 62, headed on the prepositional word. 3:6's καθάπερ καὶ ἡμεῖς ὑμᾶς has no preposition, and the supplied to see repeats an ἰδεῖν already spent earlier in the same verse, so it is added under rules 5 and 103. Rule 62's reading of rule 56 does not carry a supplied lexical verb onto the subject pronoun where rule 103 has already spent the token that would license it: rule 7's list does not include a full lexical verb, so the absence of a competitor licenses no fold.
- Comparative with an answering καί: the ASV's own wording decides the arm, and both arms stand in this chapter under one analysis. Where the ASV writes also for the particle (3:6, 3:12 καθάπερ καί), also is the καί's under rule 65 and the comparative keeps even as whole, headed on as. Where it writes no word of the particle's own (3:4 καθὼς καί → even as it came to pass), the particle takes even and the comparative a bare as, as rule 82's bare-even arm and 2CO.11.12 have it. Do not stretch a καί discontinuously over even … also.
