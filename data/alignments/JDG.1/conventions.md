# Judges 1 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- A supplied English of, or a supplied the inhabitants of, that continues a head noun the ASV has written for an earlier member of a list and then gapped is `added` wherever the Hebrew noun it stands before is an אֵת-marked direct object: JDG.1.19 (the hill country), JDG.1.27 (Beth-shean, Taanach) and JDG.1.31 (Ahlab, Achzib, Helbah, Aphik, Rehob). Rule 33 does not reach such a noun, since a marker stands on it, and none of rule 16's four families covers a word written for a relation to a head noun the Hebrew never wrote. Where instead a written construct governs the name, the of is the rectum's under rule 19: JDG.1.31 for Acco and Sidon, and JDG.1.33 for both Beth-shemesh and the coordinated Beth-anath, which carries no marker and stands as a second rectum under the one written יֹשְׁבֵי. The two shapes read alike in English and are told apart only by whether a marker or a construct head stands in the Hebrew.
- בְּהֹנוֹת with a following יָד is one rule 30 unit on the ASV's single word thumbs, headed on בְּהֹנוֹת, because English writes thumbs of hands only exactly as it writes great toes of feet only, so the one word carries both morphemes; the suffix keeps its own pronoun under rule 18, and a gapped בֹּהֶן leaves great toes whole with רֶגֶל, headed on toes. So read at JDG.1.6 and JDG.1.7, correcting an earlier reading that marked יָד unrepresented under rule 22, which governs a repeated or resumptive element and does not reach it.
- מִשָּׁם is read off the number of English words the ASV spends on it: split at JDG.1.11, where from goes to the preposition and thence to the adverb, and fused on the single word thence at JDG.1.20, headed on שָׁם. The difference is rule 30's condition and not an inconsistency.
