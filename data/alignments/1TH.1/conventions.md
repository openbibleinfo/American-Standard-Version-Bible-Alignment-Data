# 1 Thessalonians 1 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Versification split between 1TH.1.2 and 1TH.1.3 under rule 37: the projection carries ἀδιαλείπτως at the end of 1:2 (s015) and the ASV translates it at the head of 1:3 as *without ceasing*. 1:2 leaves the adverb `unrepresented`, 1:3 marks the two English words `added`, and neither verse reaches across. Do not fold *without ceasing* onto μνημονεύοντες, and do not stretch ἀδιαλείπτως onto ποιούμενοι. A reference comparison reports the adverb as a disagreement, and that report is not a correction.
- 1TH.1.5 source-projection correction (documented 2026-09-07): commit `f9480c8fa` corrected apparatus anchoring to the inline TEI position. The selected projection now has s014 ἐν followed by s015 δυνάμει, with no duplicated ἐν at that location. The former account of adjacent s014/s015 prepositions created by overlapping textual units, and of s015 as an unrepresented surplus preposition, no longer describes this projection. This corrects the historical source premise without introducing an alignment rule or replacing independent review. The original convention and its rationale are preserved in `data/audits/corpus-qa-bookkeeping/1TH.1.before-conventions.md`.
