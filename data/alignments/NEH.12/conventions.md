# Nehemiah 12 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **NEH.12.24** — In construct chains with a present article on the rectum and only one ASV the fronted before the construct, the article owns that the and the construct keeps only its lexical noun (NEH.12.24, .36, .40).
