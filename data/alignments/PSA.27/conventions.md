# Psalms 27 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- English phrasal particles in this psalm — eat up (27.2), lift up (27.5, 27.6), take up (27.10), put away and cast off (27.9), deliver over and risen up (27.12) — are kept inside the verb's unit under rule 44, discontinuous around the object where the ASV interposes it, on the reading that the bare English verb does not write the source verb's whole lexeme (אכל as consuming, אסף as gathering in, נטה hiphil as removing, רום polel/qal as exalting, נתן as delivering over). The one close call is 27.2's 'eat up', where marking 'up' added under rule 44's surplus-particle sentence was the competing reading and is named in the unit note; the chapter treats the whole family one way.
- The two 'Even's are deliberately different: 27.2 'Even mine adversaries' stands before an asyndetic appositive with no waw and no marker on the phrase and is added (rules 14, 16), while 27.3 'Even then' is the ASV's one adverbial rendering of the written בְּזֹאת and is aligned to those two morphemes under rules 30/40, headed on זֹאת, with the alternative ('then' on זֹאת, 'Even' added, בְּ unrepresented) named in the note. A later reader should not read 27.3 as folding an unlicensed 'Even' to avoid an added unit.
