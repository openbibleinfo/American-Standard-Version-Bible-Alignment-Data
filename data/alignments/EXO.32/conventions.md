# Exodus 32 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **EXO.32.10** — In the repeated phrases ‘a great nation’ and ‘a great sin,’ the supplied indefinite article belongs to the noun, while the adjective takes only ‘great’.
- **EXO.32.26** — In ‘the gate of the camp,’ the construct noun takes the supplied first ‘the,’ the Hebrew article takes the second ‘the,’ and the nomen rectum carries ‘of’.
