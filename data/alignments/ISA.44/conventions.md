# Isaiah 44 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **ISA.44.19** — In ISA.44, the lamed of le'mor is unrepresented; when ASV writes 'to say', the English infinitival marker belongs to the infinitive.
- **ISA.44.8** — In ISA.44 presentative questions, interrogative he has no separate English word; the supplied 'Is there' frame belongs to the existential or nominal predicate.
- **ISA.44.20** — In ISA.44, an English indefinite article belongs to the explicit noun it determines, including discontinuously in 'a deceived heart'.
