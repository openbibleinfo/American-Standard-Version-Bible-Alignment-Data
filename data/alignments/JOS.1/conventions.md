# Joshua 1 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- A universal relative that the ASV fuses into one -soever word is headed on the relative wherever one stands in the unit: bekol 'asher (1.7, 1.9) and 'el kol 'asher (1.16) -> *whithersoever* take 'asher as source head, with kol, which carries the -soever, named as the competing reading in each note under rule 8. Where no relative stands in the unit the quantifier heads it instead, as kol 'ish -> *Whosoever he be* does at 1.18. All four are `aligned` fusions under rule 30 rather than `noncompositional`, since each has a defensible internal head; the corpus records both statuses for kol 'asher -> *whatsoever* and neither is wrong.
- The correlative *so* answering a ka'asher clause is not read as one connective's correlative pair. Rule 14's *both ... and* sentence governs a connective morpheme rendered by a pair, and a comparative *as ... so* is not that. Where ken stands in the apodosis it takes the *so* (1.17); where none stands the *so* is its own `added` unit (1.5), which is what rule 14's own added-connective sentence directs for an apodosis connective no morpheme answers to. One chapter writes both shapes.
- An *even* standing before an appositional phrase is placed by which morpheme is present. At 1.4 a waw stands before 'ad, so the bare *even* is the connective's and only *unto* is the preposition's (rule 16's first limit). At 1.2 neither a connective nor an object marker stands, and the appositive's own le- takes the whole *even to*, on the reasoning by which rule 16 gives a fronting le- the *as for* of a topicalized phrase. The competing reading, marking that *even* `added` under rule 16's appositional limit, is named in the unit's note.
