# Job 4 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Where the ASV renders one Hebrew noun by a specifier plus an English class noun, and writes that same class noun alone for another Hebrew word of the same class in the context, the head goes on the class noun and the specifier is read as the ASV's disambiguation: JOB.4.10-11's שַׁחַל, כְּפִיר and לַיִשׁ head on 'lion(s)' beside the bare 'lion' of אַרְיֵה, JOB.4.17's אֱנוֹשׁ heads on 'man' beside the bare 'a man' of גֶּבֶר, and JOB.4.21's יֶתֶר heads on 'cord'. The specifier keeps the head only where the English noun is a generic the ASV never writes alone for that word — תַּרְדֵּמָה to 'deep sleep' at JOB.4.13, which is rule 31's 'high place' family.
- מִן + בְּלִי is fused into one unit at JOB.4.20 ('without') and split at JOB.4.11 ('for lack'), and כְּ + אֲשֶׁר is split at JOB.4.8 ('According as'). Each follows rule 30's one-rendering condition read off the ASV, so the same morpheme pair analysed two ways in one chapter is not a split where the ASV writes a single English word in one place and a word per morpheme in the other.
- JOB.4.18: where the ASV swaps roles — promoting the object of a written preposition to its verb's direct object and reaching the Hebrew's bare accusative through a preposition it supplies — the written preposition is unrepresented under rule 40 and the supplied one joins the verb's unit under rule 44. 'his angels he chargeth with folly' leaves בְ on מַלְאָכָיו with nothing to say and gives 'with' to יָשִׂים, whose object תָּהֳלָה carries no marker and no preposition of its own. BSB, which attaches a preposition to its own phrase, disagrees here by convention, not by a defect in the alignment.
