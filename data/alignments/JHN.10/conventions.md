# John 10 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **JHN.10.10** — A supplied English object pronoun ('may have it', 'scattereth them', 'hath given them', 'to snatch them', 'I do them') is a helper on the verb that licenses it, not an 'added' unit, and the judgment is high confidence: the ASV's italics are evidence only (rules 7, 11). JHN.10.10, 12, 29 (twice), 38.
- **JHN.10.3** — `ἴδιος` and the article divide the supplied possessive by configuration, per rule 47, which the contract gained (v24) after this chapter's verse passes ran. Attributive with an expressed head noun, the adjective takes the whole possessive and the article is `unrepresented`: τὰ ἴδια πρόβατα → 'his own sheep' gives 'his own' to ἴδια, as JHN.4.44 and TIT.2.5 do. Substantival with no head noun, the article supplies the pronoun and the adjective keeps 'own': πάντα τὰ ἴδια → 'all his own' in JHN.10.4, as ἐκ τῶν ἰδίων → 'of his own' in JHN.8.44 does. JHN.10.3 was corrected in reconciliation; JHN.10.4 already read this way.
- **JHN.10.33** — A supplied English indefinite article belongs to the noun it determines, not to an attributive adjective standing between it and that noun: Περὶ καλοῦ ἔργου → 'For a good work' gives the discontinuous 'a … work' to ἔργου and 'good' to καλοῦ (rules 7, 15). Adjacent cases follow the same rule ('a thief', 'a hireling', 'a demon', 'a man'); an adjective takes the article only where it is substantival with no expressed noun, as ἀλλοτρίῳ → 'a stranger' in JHN.10.5.
- **JHN.10.13** — The ASV's italicized 'he fleeth' opening JHN.10.13 renders Ὁ δὲ μισθωτὸς φεύγει, a reading the chapter's textual decision left out of the projection, so it is 'added' under rules 5 and 12. It is not rule 37: JHN.10.12's own φεύγει is aligned to that verse's 'fleeth'. The chapter's single rule 37 case is χειμὼν ἦν, which stands at the end of JHN.10.22 and is rendered at the head of JHN.10.23.
- **JHN.10.28** — Where the ASV compresses οὐ μή … εἰς τὸν αἰῶνα into the single negation 'never', rule 33 keeps that word whole on οὐ μή and every token of the duration phrase — the preposition, the article and αἰῶνα — is its own 'unrepresented' unit. This follows JHN.4.14, JHN.8.51 and JHN.8.52; a reference comparison reports the three tokens as disagreements and that report is not a correction.
- **JHN.10.7** — Recitative ὅτι introducing direct speech is its own 'unrepresented' unit (JHN.10.7, 34, 36, 41), while the ὅτι the ASV renders with an expressed complementizer is aligned to that word ('that' in JHN.10.38, 'because'/'because that'/'for' elsewhere). The chapter does both, as rule 43 read with rule 24 requires.
