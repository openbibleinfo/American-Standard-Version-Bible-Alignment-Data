# 1 Corinthians 15 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Verbless clause, adverbial predicate: the supplied copula goes to the adverb, not to the subject noun. houtos takes 'So ... is' at 15.42, proton 'is first' at 15.46, pou 'where is' at 15.55. Rule 96 is not the carrier rule here: it speaks of a determiner-carrying article standing inside the predicate phrase (2CO.1.7, where ye is the supplied subject), and an article in the subject phrase leaves the predicate to carry the copula under rule 53.
- Repeated elliptical material, three dispositions in one chapter. Where no source token governs the slot the repetition is added under rule 103: flesh at 15.39, body at 15.44, became at 15.45. Where a substantival article governs it, the repetition is that article's own supplied nominal under rule 102 and is not added: the second glory at 15.40, as the part at 15.6 and kind at 15.37 are. Where the supplied English is the cognate subject an impersonal verb requires, it rides with the verb under rule 9: the trumpet at 15.52. 15.40 and 15.52 each record the added reading as the competing one.
- pas with an article versus without. Articular ta panta splits, the article taking the supplied nominal things under rule 102 and the quantifier taking all (15.27 s020/s021, 15.28 s005/s006 and s016/s017); anarthrous panta keeps all things whole headed on All under rule 17 (15.27 s001 and s012). The same test governs nekron: articular at 15.29, 15.35, 15.42 and 15.52, anarthrous under rule 58 at 15.12, 15.13, 15.16, 15.20, 15.21 and 15.32. pas with a negator is split at 15.39 and 15.51 under rule 87's two-word arm, the ASV writing All and not separately in both.
- Head placement for the chapter's recurring light-verb renderings, decided by rule 91's operational test rather than English syntax: hypotasso heads on subjection where the ASV writes put in subjection (15.27 twice, 15.28) and on the verb word where it writes a plain subject (15.27 s018, 15.28 s014); zoiopoieo heads on the zoio- element, alive at 15.22 and life at 15.45, and on the verb word where the ASV writes quickened (15.36); kindyneuo heads on jeopardy (15.30) and eknepho on soberness (15.34), the latter recorded as arguable.
