# Matthew 26 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Coordinators: an expressed KAI or DE takes the English *and* standing at its own position in the clause, and an *and* standing before a constituent the Greek conjunction does not precede is rule 88's supplied juncture on the verb it introduces. Where the ASV writes no coordinator at the conjunction's own position, that conjunction is its own unrepresented unit rather than being stretched across an intervening verb. MAT.26.26 writes both halves in one verse; MAT.26.39 was corrected to this reading in reconciliation.
- AP' ARTI rendered by the single word *henceforth* is one noncompositional unit headed on ARTI (MAT.26.29, MAT.26.64), on the model of rule 93's APO TOU NYN: the corpus spends *now* on this adverb and never *henceforth*, so no part of the English answers to either token separately.
- EK with an ordinal rendered by a multiword English phrase leaves the preposition its own unrepresented unit, the ASV writing a bare *a second time* and *a third time* (MAT.26.42, MAT.26.44); rule 94 forbids joining the pair because more than one English word stands. The contrast in the same chapter is KATH' HEMERAN -> *daily* at MAT.26.55, one English word, joined and aligned under rule 39.
- EN governing the temporal phrase *this night* is its own unrepresented unit at MAT.26.31 and MAT.26.34: the ASV writes the phrase bare with no preposition, so nothing realizes it (rule 36).
- An ordinal or adjective with a supplied generic noun is decided by whether a Greek article stands and whether the ASV writes a determiner for it. Article plus determiner: the article takes both determiner and supplied noun and heads there, TEI PROTEI -> *on the ... day* at MAT.26.17 (rule 102). Anarthrous: the adjective takes the whole and heads itself, *a second time*, *a third time*, *the right hand*, *a little while*, *another maid* (rules 17, 58). Article with no determiner written: rule 23 empties the article and the supplied noun goes to the adjective, TON AZYMON -> *of unleavened bread* at MAT.26.17.
- The verb ENGIZO rendered *is at hand* heads on *hand* (MAT.26.45, MAT.26.46), by parity with the adverb ENGYS -> *at hand* at MAT.26.18, which rule 71 settles on *hand*. The initial *is at* carries no part of the lexeme.
