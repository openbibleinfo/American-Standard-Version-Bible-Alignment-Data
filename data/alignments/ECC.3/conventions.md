# Ecclesiastes 3 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The `עֵת` refrain of ECC.3.1-8 divides three ways, and which one applies is read off the morphemes that are there. `עֵת` takes *a time* with the supplied indefinite article joining it under rule 15. A written `לְ` before the infinitive takes the *to* under rule 52. Where the construct governs a bare infinitive with no preposition — `עֵת סְפוֹד`, `עֵת רְקוֹד`, `עֵת כְּנוֹס` — the infinitive takes its own *to* under rule 52's closing sentence, which puts it on the same unit rule 19 would have. Where the rectum is a noun — `עֵת מִלְחָמָה` → *a time for war*, `עֵת שָׁלוֹם` → *a time for peace* — the recast relation *for* belongs to the rectum under rule 19. The construct `עֵת` takes only its own word in every one of the eight verses.
- `אָמַרְתִּי אֲנִי` (ECC.3.17, ECC.3.18): the ASV writes one *I* where two morphemes could carry it, and the explicit pronoun `אֲנִי` takes it while the finite verb keeps only *said*. Both verses record the competing reading, which gives *I said* whole to the verb under rule 17 and marks the pronoun `unrepresented`.
- Tripartite verbless clauses are settled by counting the ASV's predicative words against the morphemes standing in the clause. At ECC.3.13 one *is* is written and the predicate noun `מַתַּת` is free to take it, so rule 20's ruling applies and the resuming `הִיא` is its own `unrepresented` unit. At ECC.3.15 every predicate morpheme already holds English of its own — `הָיָה` → *hath been*, `כְּבָר` → *long ago* — so the further *is* of *That which is* falls to `הוּא`, the only morpheme left, exactly as the parallel second half of the verse puts its *is* on the written `לְ` under rule 49. Rule 20's tripartite sentence is not a direction to leave a resumptive pronoun unrendered wherever one stands; it governs the case where a predicate morpheme is available for the copula.
- ECC.3.21 renders the attributive articles of `הָעֹלָה` and `הַיֹּרֶדֶת` by *whether*, the ASV recasting the attributive as an indirect question after *Who knoweth*. The article takes that word and heads on it under rule 41, since *whether* does the work rule 41 gives a relative; it is not `added` under rule 55 and the article is not left `unrepresented`. Rule 41's list of determiners was read as the words the ASV commonly writes rather than as a closed set, and the margin's *Or, that goeth* corroborates without deciding (rule 38).
- Verb particles across the chapter are decided by one subtraction: *away* (3.5, 3.6), *up* (3.2, 3.3), *down* (3.3), *out* (3.11), *together* (3.5) and the *again* of `שׁוּב` → *turn ... again* (3.20) all join their verb's unit, because the bare English verb does not write the source lexeme without them; the italicized *back* of `יְבִיאֶנּוּ` → *bring him back* (3.22) is `added`, because `בּוֹא` hiphil is the bringing and *bring* has already written it. Rule 44 was amended on this chapter's evidence to carry that test.
