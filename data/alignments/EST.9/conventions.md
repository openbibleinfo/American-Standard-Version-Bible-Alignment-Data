# Esther 9 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **EST.9.1** — In the recurring phrase rendered ‘them that hated them,’ the supplied antecedent and relative belong to the anarthrous substantival participle; the pronominal suffix takes only the final object pronoun.
- **EST.9.25** — A clause-level ‘that’ following a recast speech verb is added when no Hebrew connective licenses it; a later ‘and that’ remains the written waw’s rendering.
