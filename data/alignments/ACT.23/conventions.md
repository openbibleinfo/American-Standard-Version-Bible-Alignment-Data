# Acts 23 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Ἄνδρες ἀδελφοί → Brethren: the ASV writes one English word for the pair and it is ἀδελφοί's, ἀδελφοί alone carrying it. Ἄνδρες is its own unrepresented unit and is not joined to the noun (ACT.23.1, ACT.23.6).
- ἕως οὗ → till and until is one aligned unit taking the single English word, headed on ἕως, as rule 50 reads ἕως ἄν → till. The relative genitive is not left unrepresented and the one word is not split across the pair (ACT.23.12, ACT.23.14, ACT.23.21).
- Where the ASV writes one English word for μὲν οὖν it is So, and it is οὖν's under rule 70's operational test, μέν standing unrepresented (ACT.23.18, ACT.23.22, ACT.23.31). A μέν with no οὖν beside it and no word of its own is empty for the same reason (ACT.23.8).
- Recurring lexical heads in this chapter: χιλίαρχος → chief captain heads on captain, as ἀρχιερεύς → high priest heads on priest (rule 66), and not on chief; νεανίας and νεανίσκος → young man head on young, man being the generic completion; ἀναθεματίζω → bound under a curse heads on curse, the verb being denominal from ἀνάθεμα while bound glosses no element of it; ἐνέδρα → lying in wait and ἐνεδρεύω → lie in wait for both head on wait.
- τὰ περὶ + genitive, and an expressed preposition generally: where the ASV writes an English preposition for the phrase it belongs to the Greek preposition and not to the governing verb, even where the verb reads as phrasal and its object is the accusative article — judge of his case (ACT.23.15), inquire concerning him (ACT.23.20), testified concerning me (ACT.23.11), asked of what province on ἐκ (ACT.23.34). Rule 106's accusative arm reaches only an object with no preposition of its own, as heard of the ambush does at ACT.23.16; περί is unrepresented only where the ASV compresses the phrase into a bare possessive and writes no preposition at all.
