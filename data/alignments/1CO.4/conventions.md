# 1 Corinthians 4 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- A support frame's preposition and its repetition are placed differently in this chapter, and the difference is principled. The occurrence the frame licenses stays inside the verb's unit and the governed word stands bare where that word is accusative: 4:1 gives *Let ... account of* to λογιζέσθω with a bare *us*, and 4:17 gives *shall put ... in remembrance of* to ἀναμνήσει with a bare *ways* (rules 21 and 106). Where the ASV repeats that preposition over a second complement of the same frame, the repetition goes to the member it introduces and is neither stretched back nor marked `added`: 4:1 gives the second *of* to ὑπηρέτας though it too is accusative (rule 103). A dative governed word is the contrast and takes the preposition itself, as 4:8 συμβασιλεύσωμεν ὑμῖν -> *with you* does.
- A supplied intensifying *even* standing in front of a temporal preposition or conjunction is folded onto that token as a rule 7 helper and is not marked `added`: ἄχρι -> *Even unto* at 4:11 and ἕως -> *even until* at 4:13, each headed on the word rendering the source token. No appositive follows either, so rule 105's supplied appositional *even* is not in question, and no other source token stands to license the word.
- φυσιόω is not a prefixed compound. The *up* of *puffed up* answers to no element of the Greek word; it is an English phrasal particle and a rule 7 helper riding inside the verb's own unit, as 4:6, 4:18 and 4:19 all have it. Do not cite rule 106's preverb arm for this lexeme, and do not read the corpus rows for it as evidence that a preverb is realized.
