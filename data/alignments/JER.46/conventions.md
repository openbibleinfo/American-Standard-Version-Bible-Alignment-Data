# Jeremiah 46 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- When Hiphil רבה is rendered by a light-verb phrase containing many, many belongs to רבה and is the target head; an adjacent participle keeps its own verbal lexeme.
- In אין להם מספר rendered are innumerable, align the compositional negative-plus-number fusion and leave the possessive lamed and resumptive suffix unrepresented when English elides their subject relation.
