# James 1 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- A supplied English case preposition on a bare oblique noun stays with that noun, even where the governing Greek verb is a compound whose English reads as phrasal. JAS.1.2 `πειρασμοῖς περιπέσητε` → *ye fall into manifold temptations* gives *into* to the dative noun and a bare *ye fall* to the verb, on JHN.19.29's precedent (`περιθέντες ὑσσώπῳ` → *upon hyssop*, the corpus's one other anarthrous dative under a περι- compound, conceding nothing) and 2PE.2.5's. The corpus gives a compound verb an English particle only where that particle answers to the prefix itself — PHP.1.25 `παραμενῶ` → *abide with*, 2CO.8.22 `συνεπέμψαμεν` → *we have sent with* — and περι- means *around*, not *into*. Rule 36 does not settle this on its own, being scoped to an expressed Greek preposition. The chapter applies this uniformly: JAS.1.5 *to all*, 1.12 *of life*, 1.17 *by turning*, 1.18 *by the word*, 1.20 *of man*, 1.23 *unto a man*, 1.25 *of liberty*, and JAS.1.23 `ἔοικεν` → *is like* leaving *unto* to `ἀνδρί`. A verb still keeps a genuine particle: JAS.1.18 `ἀπεκύησεν` → *he brought forth*.
