# Deuteronomy 5 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **DEU.5.25** — Iterative יָסַף and עוֹד standing in one clause: where the ASV writes a single *any more* for both, DEU.5.25 gives it to עוֹד, headed on *more*, and leaves the participle יֹסְפִים `unrepresented`. The two morphemes are separated by the subject pronoun, the infinitive and its whole object phrase, so rule 30 cannot fuse them and one of the two must go unrendered; the tie was decided for the adverb whose own lexeme the English words carry, and the verse note records the reverse reading. This is the chapter's only unrepresented lexical verb, and it is not a split with DEU.5.22, where no עוֹד stands and יָסַף therefore holds *added ... more* itself.
- **DEU.5.22** — עֲרָפֶל → *thick darkness* heads on *darkness*, not on *thick*. Rule 31's subtraction test ties here, and the tie is broken by the chapter's own usage: DEU.5.23 writes a bare *darkness* for חֹשֶׁךְ, so *darkness* is a class noun the ASV writes alone for a word of that class and *thick* is its specifier. A later reader auditing head choices should not move it to the modifier on the strength of בָּמָה → *high place* and the like, where the ASV never writes the generic alone.
