# Ezekiel 40 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **EZK.40.48** — When the ASV supplies a distinguishing or distributive modifier such as 'one', 'other', or 'each' and no source morpheme licenses it, the modifier is an added unit and the source noun keeps only its lexical rendering.
- **EZK.40.10** — For the recurring min + poh rendered on this/that side, retain separate aligned units: min takes on, and poh takes this/that side, headed on the demonstrative under rule 24. The ASV preserves a spatial relation and an identifiable deictic location; contextual recasting from an ablative form to static English does not erase that distinction. Side is the generic English nominal completion. This adjudicates the retrospective audit disagreement; count alone did not decide it.
