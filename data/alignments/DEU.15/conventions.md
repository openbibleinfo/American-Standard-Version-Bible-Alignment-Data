# Deuteronomy 15 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- הַיּוֹם is aligned two ways in this chapter and neither is a split, because the ASV writes two different English expressions for it. At DEU.15.5 it writes *this day*: a determiner stands in the article's place, so the article takes *this* under rule 41 and יוֹם takes *day*. At DEU.15.15 it writes *to day*: one adverbial rendering of which no morpheme owns a part, so the article and the noun share one unit under rule 30, headed on יוֹם and on *day*, which is how the corpus reads all five of its הַ+יוֹם → *to day* units. Read which English the ASV wrote before choosing, and do not treat the pair as an inconsistency.
- The dative לְ with a pronominal suffix is placed by what the ASV spends on the relation, not by the lemma or by the BSB comparison. Where the ASV writes a preposition it takes it — *unto him* at 15.8, 15.10 and 15.14 — and where the ASV writes a bare English indirect object, object or subject it is unrepresented under rule 40, the suffix taking the pronoun that realizes it: *give him*, *furnish him* against *giveth thee*, *promised thee*, *he wanteth*, *it is well with him*, *he shall be thy servant*. Both shapes occur within one verse at 15.10 and at 15.14. This division accounts for twelve of the chapter's thirty-three BSB disagreements and is a documented convention difference rather than an error.
- The chapter's six preposition-plus-noun fusions divide rule 23 as follows and are consistent: where one morpheme carries content the other lacks, that morpheme heads — לִפְנֵי on פָּנֶה (15.20), מִקֶּרֶב on קֶרֶב (15.11), בִּגְלַל on גָּלָל (15.10), and the idiomatic בְעֵינֶיךָ → *unto* on עַיִן (15.18, under rule 40's closing paragraph, since the governed noun keeps no English of its own). Where neither does, the morpheme standing first heads — מֵעִם → *from* on מִן at 15.12, 15.13, 15.16 and 15.18, and likewise עַל־כֵּן on עַל (15.11, 15.15), כַּאֲשֶׁר on כְּ (15.6) and אֶפֶס כִּי on אֶפֶס (15.4).
