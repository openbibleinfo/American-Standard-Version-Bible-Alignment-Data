# Exodus 12 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **EXO.12.20** — In EXO.12, כֹּל plus discontinuous לֹא fused as 'no' or 'nothing' is aligned, headed on the negative, because both morphemes retain distinguishable contributions; close corpus precedent uses both aligned and noncompositional statuses.
- **EXO.12.43** — In EXO.12, בְּ plus a pronominal suffix rendered by transparent 'thereof' is aligned, headed on the preposition, because both contributions remain distinguishable; close corpus precedent uses both aligned and noncompositional statuses.
