# 1 Corinthians 13 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- An English preposition standing over a governed word belongs to that word only where it realizes the word's own case. Where the ASV builds a support-verb frame around the Greek verb and the frame's supplied noun is what licenses the preposition, the preposition stays inside the verb's unit and the governed word stands bare: 13.5 `οὐ λογίζεται τὸ κακόν` → *taketh not account of evil* gives *taketh … account of* to `λογίζεται` and a bare *evil* to `κακόν`, the accusative having no case for the *of* to answer to. The genitives of 13.1 (*of men*, *of angels*) and 13.13 (*of these*) are the contrast and are rule 21's ordinary case.
- Supplied English divides in this chapter by construction and not by the ASV's italics. A word or phrase completing the English expression of one source token is folded onto that token as a rule 7 helper — 13.2 *the gift of* on `προφητείαν`, 13.3 *the poor* on `ψωμίσω`, 13.8 *there be* on each of the three predicate nouns under rules 68 and 96 — while a juncture at an asyndetic seam between two like constituents is `added` under rule 98, as 13.4's *and* between `μακροθυμεῖ` and `χρηστεύεται` is. Both 13.3 and 13.4 name the competing status in their own notes. The cross-verse candidate table is keyed by source lemma and can never show an `added` unit, so this line is recorded here rather than left to be rediscovered from the dump.
