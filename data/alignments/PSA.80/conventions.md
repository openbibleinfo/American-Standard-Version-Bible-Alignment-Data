# Psalms 80 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Refrain (vv 3, 7, 19; variant v14): הֲשִׁיבֵנוּ and qal שׁוּב take 'Turn ... again' whole, discontinuous around 'us' (rules 44, 25), margin 'Restore' set aside; הָאֵר → 'cause/Cause ... to shine' headed on shine (rule 44), the v19 occurrence asyndetic with no connective to place; cohortative נִוָּשֵׁעָה → 'we shall be saved' with the pronoun and auxiliaries on the verb (rule 17).
- vv 15, 17: the asyndetic finite clause אִמַּצְתָּה after the written antecedent בֵּן takes a supplied relative ('that', 'whom') that is its own added unit (rules 46, 55); אָמֵץ → 'madest strong' heads on strong (rule 44); לָּךְ → 'for thyself' with the preposition rendered.
- Construct participles the ASV writes as English verbs (v1 יֹשֵׁב הַכְּרוּבִים → 'sittest above the cherubim', v12 עֹבְרֵי דָרֶךְ → 'pass by the way') keep the supplied preposition with the participle under rule 19's valency clause; the rectum takes only its own word and supplied 'the' (rule 15).
