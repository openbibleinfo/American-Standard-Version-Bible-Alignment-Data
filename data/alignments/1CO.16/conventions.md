# 1 Corinthians 16 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- A supplied English pro-verb *do*, written where the ASV expands a bare Greek pronoun or crasis into a finite clause, rides with that pronoun and is neither `added` nor folded onto the lexical verb it echoes. `ὡς κἀγώ` → *as I also do* keeps *also do* with the crasis at 16:10, and `πάντα ὑμῶν` → *all that ye do* keeps *that ye do* with the genitive at 16:14, each headed on the pronominal word. 16:10 is rule 56's elliptical comparative, ἐργάζεται being already spent on *worketh*; 16:14 is the same judgment where no comparative stands, the genitive being the only token the supplied clause can belong to. Marking *do* `added` under rule 103 remains defensible in both.
- προπέμπω is rendered by the whole idiom *set … forward on his journey* / *set … forward on my journey*, and the supplied possessive belongs inside the verb's own unit: no other source token licenses it, and the head is the verb word *set*, not *journey*. 16:6 and 16:11 read it alike, the preverb's *forward* staying inside the unit under rule 106 and the discontinuity permitted by rule 15.
- The article before a substantival τοιοῦτος is decided by whether the ASV writes it a separate antecedent pronoun, not by the Greek, which is identical in both instances. 16:16 `τοῖς τοιούτοις` → *unto such* writes none, so the article is `unrepresented` under rule 49 and the case preposition stays with the demonstrative under rule 21's determinerless clause. 16:18 `τοὺς τοιούτους` → *them that are such* writes *them*, so the article takes it under rules 20 and 92 and the demonstrative keeps *that are such*, headed on *such*. Reading 16:18 by rule 49 alone — article empty, the whole expression on the demonstrative — remains defensible; rule 49 has been amended to record which way this chapter took it.
