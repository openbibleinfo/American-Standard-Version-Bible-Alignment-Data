# Isaiah 42 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **ISA.42.1** — In Isaiah 42, English relative objects, arguments, and lexical predicates or modifiers with no source morpheme are separate added units; construction-supplied pronoun-relative frames, copulas, articles, and generic nominal completions remain with the source substantive.
