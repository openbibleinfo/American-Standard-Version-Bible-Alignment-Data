# Nehemiah 8 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **NEH.8.3** — A supplied predicate word the ASV writes beside the prepositional predicate of a verbless clause — 'were attentive unto' (8.3, italic 'attentive') and 'stood in' (8.7, italic 'stood') — is read as the construction's copular expansion and joins the preposition's unit under rules 20 and 49, not as an added lexical predicate: a bare 'were' in its place says what the Hebrew says. The competing reading (the supplied word its own added unit) is named in each note and was not taken.
- **NEH.8.15** — כַּכָּתוּב → 'as it is written': the assimilated article on the substantival passive participle takes the supplied 'it' under rule 41 (corpus [הַ] → 'it' at 2CH.30.5, 2CH.30.18 agrees), the participle keeps 'is written' with the construction copula (rule 20), and כַּ takes 'as'. The 'it' is neither added under rule 20 nor part of the participle's frame.
