# Proverbs 8 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **PRO.8.16** — שֹׁפְטֵי צֶדֶק -> *the judges of the earth*: the ASV renders a Hebrew reading the projection does not carry, and the packet records no ketiv/qere or other variant unit in that slot. Rule 9 forbids taking a textual decision inside the alignment file, so *of the earth* stays on צֶדֶק as the chain's rectum under rule 19, headed on *earth*, rather than the noun being marked `unrepresented` and the English `added` on the analogy of rule 53. The divergence is named in the unit's note instead. Any later verse of this chapter meeting an unmarked divergence of the same kind is aligned the same way.
