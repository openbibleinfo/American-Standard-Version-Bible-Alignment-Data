# Acts 26 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- A supplied English preposition standing over a Greek ADVERBIAL accusative rides with that noun as a rule 7 helper, not with the verb and not `added`. ACT.26.20's πᾶσάν τε τὴν χώραν τῆς Ἰουδαίας → *throughout all the country of Judaea* keeps *throughout* on χώραν, coordinated as that accusative is with dative complements whose own case prepositions the ASV likewise supplies in the same series (*at Jerusalem* on Ἱεροσολύμοις, *to the* on the article before ἔθνεσιν). Rule 21's sentence sending an English preposition standing over an accusative to the verb or the frame is scoped, like the rule 106 accusative arm it cites, to a GOVERNED OBJECT: here ἀπήγγελλον's English (*declared*) is already complete and no frame is built, and stretching *throughout* back over three intervening members would abuse the discontinuity rule 15 permits.
- The chapter's supplied *said* is placed two ways and the difference is the Greek clause, not convention drift. ACT.26.28 ὁ δὲ Ἀγρίππας πρὸς τὸν Παῦλον → *And Agrippa said unto Paul* folds *said* onto πρός under rule 62's main arm, a prepositional phrase standing as the verbless clause's predicate and Ἀγρίππας standing as the subject it is predicated of; ACT.26.29 ὁ δὲ Παῦλος → *And Paul said* folds it onto Παῦλος under rule 56, that clause expressing no predicate at all for it to attach to. The candidate table reports πρός as *unto* at 26.1 and *said unto* at 26.28 for this reason, and neither edge should be moved to make the two agree.
