# Jeremiah 3 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Interrogative he: all five occurrences in this chapter (3.1 twice, 3.4, 3.5, 3.6) are marked unrepresented. The ASV signals each question by subject-auxiliary inversion, and the fronted auxiliary (Will, Wilt, Hast) belongs to the imperfect or perfect verb under rule 17. The corpus is genuinely divided here — 140 units unrepresented against roughly 96 aligned to the fronted auxiliary, with most of the aligned ones conceding the unrepresented reading in their notes — so this is a chapter-wide choice rather than a settled ruling, and it is the reason for the Ti BSB disagreements.
- Rule 30 fusion is applied throughout this chapter wherever the ASV renders two source morphemes by a single English word, and the unit heads on the morpheme carrying the lexeme except where both are function words: מֵאִתּוֹ -> 'from' (3.1, headed on מִן as first token, both morphemes being prepositions), אֶל־תַּחַת -> 'under' (3.6, on תַּחַת), בְּשֶׁקֶר -> 'feignedly' (3.10, on שֶׁקֶר), נַפְשָׁהּ -> 'herself' (3.11, on נֶפֶשׁ), and אִשָּׁה זוֹנָה -> 'harlot's' (3.3, on זוֹנָה, corrected at reconciliation). The last of these establishes that a full lexical noun fuses on the same terms as a preposition or a suffix; do not mark one of the pair unrepresented to avoid a noncompositional unit.
- שְׁפִי is rendered 'bare heights' at 3.2 and 3.21 and both units are headed on 'bare', by rule 31's subtraction: 'heights' alone is any heights, while שְׁפִי names the barren height, exactly as בָּמָה -> 'high place' heads on 'high'. The competing reading, heading on 'heights' as the English phrase's noun, is recorded in both units' notes.
