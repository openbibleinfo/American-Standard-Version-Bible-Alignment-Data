# Psalms 15 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **PSA.15.3** — A fusion of function morphemes into one English word survives discontinuity. Rule 35's Nor (waw + a negative) and never (a negative + a temporal phrase) stay one unit headed on the negative even when a fronted object or the verb stands between the morphemes; rule 30's contiguity condition governs a fixed multi-word lexical expression, not this. Verses 3 and 5 had split them two different ways. The corpus is unanimous: all 20 waw-plus-negative Nor units are fused, including PSA.1:1 twice across a three-morpheme gap, and PSA.10:11 fuses a discontinuous negative with le-netzach as never.
