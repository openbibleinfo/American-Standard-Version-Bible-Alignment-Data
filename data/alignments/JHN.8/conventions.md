# John 8 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **JHN.8.8** — A supplied coordinating 'and' that the ASV inserts when it renders a Greek participle as a finite clause rides with the verb whose English it introduces, not with the participle it follows. JHN.8.2 (καθίσας ἐδίδασκεν → 'he sat down and taught'), 8:6 and 8:7 all put it on the finite verb; JHN.8.8 alone had put it on κύψας ('he stooped and') and was changed in reconciliation, which also removed a needless discontinuity around κάτω → 'down'. 8:6 and 8:8 are the same collocation (κύψας κάτω … ἔγραφεν) with the same English, so nothing licensed the difference. The corpus agrees 13 units to 1: JHN.1.36, 1.38, 1.42, JHN.2.15, JHN.6.17, EPH.2.17, EPH.6.9, REV.9.19, 11.1, 11.15, 14.7, 19.12, 19.17 all carry a leading supplied 'and' on the verb, while REV.1.1 (ἐσήμανεν ἀποστείλας → 'sent and signified') puts a trailing one on the fronted participle. Because that one finished chapter reads the other way, this stays a chapter convention and no contract rule was promoted.
- **JHN.8.38** — A neuter plural relative ἅ rendered 'the things which' is headed on 'things', the nominal word, not on the relativizer 'which'. JHN.8.26 headed on 'things'; JHN.8.38 had headed both of its instances on 'which' and was changed in reconciliation. This also keeps the chapter internally consistent with the substantival article at JHN.8.29 (τὰ → 'the things that', head 'things') and with rule 22. The corpus majority agrees 7 to 2 (PHP.4.9, REV.1.1, REV.1.19 ×3, REV.10.4 and one more head on 'things'; REV.2.10 and REV.4.1 head on 'which', and JHN.5.19 heads 'what things' on 'what'). Because finished chapters read the other way, this stays a chapter convention and no contract rule was promoted.
- **JHN.8.43** — διὰ τί rendered by the single word 'Why' is one noncompositional unit headed on the interrogative, not an unrepresented διά plus 'Why' on τί. JHN.8.46 already had it that way, following JHN.7.45; JHN.8.43 had split it and was changed in reconciliation. This generalized and was promoted to the contract as rule 45.
- **JHN.8.20** — οὔπω rendered 'not yet' is headed on 'not', not on 'yet'. JHN.8.57 headed on 'not'; JHN.8.20 had headed on 'yet' and was changed in reconciliation. This follows the convention JHN.7 recorded at JHN.7.8 and the finished John chapters (JHN.2.4, 3.24, 6.17, 7.6, 7.8, 7.30). REV.17.10 and 17.12 head on 'yet', so the corpus is split across books and this remains a chapter-and-book convention, not a promotable rule.
- **JHN.8.12** — A bare dative pronoun or noun whose case the ASV realizes with 'unto' keeps that preposition in its own unit, headed on the English pronoun or noun: αὐτῷ → 'unto him', ὑμῖν → 'unto you', αὐτοῖς → 'unto them' (JHN.8.12, 8.14, 8.23, 8.24, 8.25, 8.42, 8.51 and elsewhere). This is rule 21's case preposition riding with the word it belongs to, where no article intervenes; 'unto' is never left as its own unit and never marked added.
- **JHN.8.42** — The contrary-to-fact particle ἄν in the apodosis of an unreal condition is aligned to the English modal 'would', which is what realizes it; the verb keeps only its subject and lexical form. JHN.8.19 (ἂν ᾔδειτε → 'ye would know') had it that way and JHN.8.42 (ἠγαπᾶτε ἂν → 'ye would love') had left it unrepresented with 'would' as the verb's auxiliary helper; 8:42 was changed in reconciliation. The corpus contains no unrepresented ἄν at all, and JHN.4.10 ('wouldest', 'would') and JHN.5.46 ('would') align it to the modal in exactly this construction. Not promoted to the contract: eleven corpus units are too thin a base beside the many ἄν that form fixed relative clusters (ὃς ἄν → 'whosoever' and the like), which this convention does not touch.
