# Acts 19 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Complex frame carried by a proper preposition (rule 67): the frame rides undivided with the preposition and the governed noun stands bare, and this chapter heads the unit on the English word that realizes the preposition where the ASV writes one — 'for' in ἐπί → 'for the space of' at 19:8 and 19:10, 'touching' in περί → 'as touching' at 19:40 — and on the frame's lexical noun where the ASV writes none, ἐπί → 'the space of' headed on 'space' at 19:34. Rule 67 records the corpus as divided for a proper preposition; 19:34 is not a departure but the same arm with nothing prepositional in the frame to take the head.
- An English preposition standing on an accusative complement: where the accusative is the verb's object, the preposition is the verb's own phrasal government and stays inside the verb's unit under rule 106 — 'speaking evil of' 19:9, 'that dwelt in' 19:10, 'I adjure … by' 19:13, 'dwelt at' 19:17, 'beckoned with' 19:33, 'blasphemers of' 19:37 — and the governed word keeps a bare noun or name. An adverbial accusative of duration is not that case: its 'for' realizes the duration and rides with the time noun, χρόνον → 'for a while' at 19:22, which is the same word the chapter gives to ἐπί where a preposition is expressed at 19:8 and 19:10. An oblique object is unaffected and keeps its case preposition: ἐπιθέντος αὐτοῖς → 'upon them' at 19:6, Ἐφέσου → 'at Ephesus' at 19:26, Σκευᾶ → 'of … Sceva' at 19:14.
- Versification: the projection carries the Greek of ASV 19:41 — καὶ ταῦτα εἰπὼν ἀπέλυσεν τὴν ἐκκλησίαν — at the end of 19:40. Under rule 37 those six source tokens are their own unrepresented units in 19:40 and all ten target tokens of 19:41 are `added`; neither verse reaches across, and no nearby English is stretched over the orphaned tokens. A reference comparison reports all six source tokens as disagreements and that report is not a correction.
- A negator whose clause the ASV renders by a single positive English word joins the word it negates as one noncompositional unit, and an article standing between them is its own unrepresented unit under rule 93: οὐ τὰς τυχούσας → 'special' at 19:11, headed on the participle. Where the ASV instead writes an English negation word the pair splits in the ordinary way and the negator takes that word alone — οὐκ → 'no' with 'small' on ὀλίγος at 19:23, 'little' on ὀλίγην at 19:24, and 'gods' on θεοί at 19:26. The test is rule 45's, whether part of the English answers to a source token separately, and not whether the Greek is a litotes.
