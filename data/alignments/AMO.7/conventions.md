# Amos 7 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **AMO.7.2** — In the repeated entreaty formula, נָא licenses the complete ASV idiom I beseech thee; thee is part of the particle's rendering, not an added object of the imperative.
- **AMO.7.11** — In the repeated גָּלֹה יִגְלֶה exile formula, the infinitive absolute carries ASV's emphatic surely, while the finite verb carries shall be led away captive.
