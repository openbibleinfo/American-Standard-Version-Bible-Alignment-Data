# Ecclesiastes 10 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Qoheleth's anarthrous substantival adjectives (חָכָם, כְּסִיל, סָכָל, עָשִׁיר) take the whole English noun phrase the ASV supplies to make them substantival, headed on the word carrying the lexeme (rules 31, 46): *the rich* at 10.6 and 10.20, *a fool* at 10.12. Where the ASV writes the construct chain as an English possessive, the supplied generic *man's* and the possessor's own determiner both join that adjective's unit and the construct noun (לֵב, פֶּה) keeps only its own word — *A wise man's heart* and *a fool's heart* (10.2), *a wise man's mouth* (10.12), the determiner going to the possessor under rule 29's possessive paragraph.
- This chapter is heavily paratactic, and six of its seven added units are English connectives or subordinators standing over clauses with no conjunction morpheme in them: *so* and *and* (10.1), *that* (10.3), *which* (10.5), *and* (10.9), *yet* (10.14). Each was counted against its own clause's morphemes and left added rather than folded into a neighbouring waw, relative or verb. The seventh, *how* (10.15), is added because the לְ before לָלֶכֶת already holds the written *to*; do not read it as part of the infinitive's rendering, which rule 52's last sentence reaches only where no preposition stands.
- Where a preposition, an interrogative or a construct noun stands with a following morpheme and the ASV writes one English word for the pair, the chapter fuses under rule 30 and heads on the morpheme carrying the content: כְּשֶׁ -> *when* (10.3), מַה־שֶּׁ -> *what* (10.14), מֵאַחֲרֵי -> *after* (10.14), מִלִּפְנֵי -> *from* (10.5), בְּ+suffix -> *therewith*, *thereby* (10.9), בְּלוֹא -> *before* headed on the negative (10.11), and the lexical pairs בַּעַל הַלָּשׁוֹן -> *charmer* (10.11) and חַדְרֵי מִשְׁכָּב -> *bedchamber* (10.20). In 10.11 the article segmented between the two fused nouns still takes the phrase's written *the*, exactly as rule 30 states for דִּבְרֵי הַיָּמִים -> *chronicles*.
