# 2 Corinthians 9 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **2CO.9.3** — Discontinuous 'on ... behalf' for hyper + genitive pronoun is headed on the prepositional word 'on', not on the supplied noun 'behalf'. 2CO.9.2, 2CO.9.3 and 2CO.9.14 are the same wrap and now agree, following 2CO.7.4, 2CO.7.14 and 2CO.8.24. The contrast is the continuous 'on/in behalf of' idiom, which the corpus heads on 'behalf' (PHP.1.4, PHP.1.7, PHP.1.29, EPH.3.1).
- **2CO.9.6** — A preposition plus an abstract manner noun compressed into one English manner adverb is noncompositional, headed on the governed noun: 'ep eulogiais' -> 'bountifully' (9.6, twice) is analysed as 'ek lupes' -> 'grudgingly' (9.7). Rule 35's aligned contrast needs the English to spell the preposition in, as 'wherewith' and 'thereunto' do; an adverbial -ly does not, which is why 2CO.7.10 made 'kata theon' -> 'godly' noncompositional even though 'god-ly' carries the noun's own gloss.
- **2CO.9.6** — Where a Greek clause expresses no predicate at all and the ASV supplies a LEXICAL verb rather than a copula, the supplied verb rides with the sole token as rule 56 has it for a copula, headed on that token's own English and not on the supplied verb: 'Touto de' -> 'But this *I say*' heads on 'this' (9.6), and 'hekastos kathos proeretai' -> '*Let* each man *do* according as he hath purposed' heads on 'each' (9.7). 2CO.8.11 does the same with the supplied existential frames 'there was' / 'there may be'. Not promoted to the contract: EPH.4.4, EPH.5.9, EPH.5.24, PHP.2.1 and REV.21.23 mark such a supplied verb 'added' on the identical shape, so the corpus is divided and a rule would contradict finished chapters. Reach for 'added' only where no token stands in the clause at all, as at EPH.1.10 and 2CO.8.13, where the ASV supplies the object too.
