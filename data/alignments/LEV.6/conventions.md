# Leviticus 6 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The superlative construct קֹדֶשׁ קָדָשִׁים is split rather than fused: the rectum takes the ASV's 'most' under rule 19, the construct takes 'holy' together with the verbless clause's construction-supplied copula under rule 20, and an explicit הִוא standing in the clause takes only its own 'it'. Applied at LEV.6.17, 6.25 and 6.29. It is not read as one noncompositional unit, because rule 2 prefers the split wherever each member holds an English word of its own.
- אִשֶּׁה is rendered by the phrase 'offerings made by fire' and heads on 'offerings', the noun the phrase turns on by rule 31's subtraction; 'fire' was the competing head. The unit is discontinuous where the ASV splits 'made by fire' off across the divine name (LEV.6.18) and continuous at LEV.6.17.
- Where the ASV writes a noun's lexeme inside the following cognate verb's rendering, the noun takes only the pro-form the ASV writes for it and is `unrepresented` where none is written, while the lexeme stays with the verb: הָעֹשֶׁק אֲשֶׁר עָשָׁק -> 'the thing which he hath gotten by oppression' gives 'thing' to the noun, and הַגְּזֵלָה אֲשֶׁר גָּזָל -> 'that which he took by robbery' leaves the noun nothing, the article taking the demonstrative 'that' under rule 41. Both at LEV.6.4. BSB attaches the lexeme to the noun in the second case; that is the documented convention difference and not an error.
- For הַכֹּהֵן + an articled attributive, read where the ASV has put the attributive. Rendered postnominally as a relative clause, its article takes the 'that' under rule 41 and the first article takes the single written 'the' (LEV.6.26, הַמְחַטֵּא -> 'The priest that offereth it for sin'). Rendered prenominally, rule 47 governs the count: the first article takes the 'the' and the agreement article is `unrepresented` (LEV.6.22, הַמָּשִׁיחַ -> 'the anointed priest'), and the relative the ASV then supplies for the following prepositional predicate תַּחְתָּיו is `added` under rule 55. The competing reading gives that relative to תַּחְתָּיו's own unit, alongside the copula rule 49 already puts there, on the analogy of rule 41's articleless attributive; it is named here because the contract's articleless-attributive clause reaches an adjective or participle and not a prepositional phrase.
