# Exodus 37 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- מִן rendered by the fixed English phrase *of one piece with* (37.8, 37.17, 37.21 three times, 37.22, 37.25) is one unit on the preposition under rule 30, headed on *piece*. What follows keeps its own English: the article and noun of *with the mercy seat* in 37.8, and the suffix's own *it* in the suffixed clauses. No part of the four-word phrase is `added`, and none of it moves onto the noun or suffix beside it.
- In the dimension formulas (37.1, 37.6, 37.10, 37.25) the clause's copula — written or italic *was* — joins the measure predicate's head noun אַמָּה and not the trailing חֲצִי it happens to stand beside, so the unit runs discontinuously across *and a half* under rule 25. Every measurement clause in the chapter is aligned this way; the competing reading, which attaches *was* to *half* because that is the word it follows, is named in the verse notes and was not taken.
- זָהָב naming the material takes whatever function word the ASV spends on it and keeps it: *with* after צִפָּה (37.2, 37.4, 37.11, 37.15, 37.26, 37.28), *of* where it stands after the artifact noun (37.2, 37.6, 37.7, 37.13, 37.16, 37.17, 37.22, 37.23, 37.24, 37.26), and *golden* alone where the ASV renders it attributively (37.12, 37.27). MACULA's construct-versus-absolute tag on the artifact noun does not move that word — זֵר זָהָב with זֵר absolute (37.2, 37.26) and construct (37.12) place it identically — so cite rule 19 or rule 33 as the morphology warrants, but do not let the tag change the edge or the head, which is *gold* or *golden* throughout.
