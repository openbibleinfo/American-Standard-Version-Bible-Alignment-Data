# Genesis 24 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Interrogative he: where the ASV asks by inversion and the fronted auxiliary is licensed by another morpheme, the he is unrepresented and the auxiliary stays with that morpheme - 'must' with 'ashiv at 24.5, 'Is' with the existential yesh at 24.23, 'Wilt' with telekhi at 24.58. Where the ASV writes an indirect-question 'whether', the he takes it (24.21). The corpus reads 1KI.21.20 the other way, giving 'hast' to the he, so this is the chapter's judgment and not a settled reading.
- halakh plus 'acharei is rendered by the single English verb 'follow' (24.5, 24.8, 24.39, 24.61): the verb of going takes 'follow' and 'acharei is unrepresented under rule 40, since the ASV spends no word on what the preposition contributes. The suffix on 'acharei still takes its own pronoun.
- The directive he is never unrepresented in this chapter. It takes the written English direction word where the ASV writes one (24.16, 24.32, 24.45, 24.52, 24.67), and joins the fused unit where one English word carries both the place and the direction - shammah -> 'thither' (24.6, 24.8), hachutsah -> 'out' (24.29).
- ka'asher fused into a single 'as' or 'when' takes its head on the preposition rather than the relative (24.22, 24.51, 24.52). Both are defensible; the chapter chose the preposition and held to it in all three.
