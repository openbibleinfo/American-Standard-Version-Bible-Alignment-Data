# Leviticus 24 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Verbless clause with both a nominal predicate and a prepositional phrase: the construction-supplied copula and dummy subject go to the nominal, however the ASV orders the two. חֻקַּת עוֹלָם at v. 3 takes it shall be and בְּרִית עוֹלָם at v. 8 takes it is, the latter discontinuous around the fronted מֵאֵת בְּנֵי־יִשְׂרָאֵל, which keeps only on the behalf of. Rule 49 was not applied to a preposition that heads an adjunct rather than the predicate.
- מִחוּץ (לְ): the preposition and חוּץ are one fused unit under rule 30 whether the ASV writes without (vv. 3, 14) or out of (v. 23), headed on the token carrying the lexeme; the לְ that follows it and any אֶל standing before it are each their own unrepresented unit under rule 40, the one English rendering carrying the whole relation.
- Casuistic אִישׁ with כִּי: the doubled contiguous formula אִישׁ אִישׁ כִּי rendered by the single word Whosoever (v. 15) is one fused unit under rule 30, headed on the first אִישׁ, with כִּי inside it because Whosoever carries the subordination as well as the distributive. Where the Hebrew writes a single אִישׁ and the ASV spends a word on each morpheme — he / that at v. 17, a man / if at v. 19 — rule 30's condition is unmet and rule 2 splits, אִישׁ taking the nominal word and כִּי the subordinator.
- כַּגֵּר כָּאֶזְרָח rendered as well ... as (vv. 16, 22): the Hebrew writes a comparative preposition for each member, so this is not rule 14's correlative pair resting on one connective. The first כְּ takes the whole opening expression as well, headed on as, and the second takes the answering as. Where the ASV additionally writes a for before each noun (v. 22), that word is the noun's own under rule 33, the preposition having already spent its English.
