# 1 Chronicles 27 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The ASV and WLC divide 27:30-31 differently: the ASV closes verse 30 with *and over the flocks was Jaziz the Hagrite*, which WLC carries as `וְעַל־הַצֹּאן יָזִיז הַהַגְרִי` at the head of verse 31. Verse 30 puts that English into one `added` unit with a null target head; verse 31 marks its first seven morphemes `unrepresented`, each on its own. Nothing crosses the boundary in either direction, and the six BSB disagreements in verse 31 are this offset and not an analysis.
- In this chapter's officer formula the construction-supplied copula goes to the fronted `עַל` or `אַחֲרֵי` wherever the clause writes no nominal predicate, the name beside the copula being the subject (vv. 2, 4, 25-30, 34a), and to the written predicate noun wherever one stands (`נָגִיד` v. 16, `שָׂרֵי` vv. 22 and 31, `יוֹעֵץ` vv. 32-33, `רֵעַ` v. 33). The ASV writes *was* in the same slot either way, so read the Hebrew for which side predicates before placing it.
- *captain* in the monthly-course list is the written `שַׂר` in vv. 5 and 8 and a supplied generic on the substantival ordinal in vv. 7 and 9-15. Where an article morpheme stands at the phrase head it takes the written *The* (vv. 5, 7-13, 15, and in v. 5 the article on `הַשְּׁלִישִׁי` rather than a supplied determiner on the construct `שַׂר`); where none stands, the *The* joins the substantival numeral (v. 14). The chapter's two article morphemes per phrase are counted against its two written *the*s in every verse of the list.
- In 27:29 the attributive prepositional phrase `הַבָּקָר בָּעֲמָקִים` is recast as a relative clause, and the supplied *that were* joins the `בְּ` with the copula, headed on *in*: the relative realizes the attributive relation, as rule 41 has it for an articleless attributive, and no clause stands in the Hebrew for rule 55's supplied subordinator to reach. The parallel colon writes `הָרֹעִים` and gives its relative to the article, and 27:28 writes `אֲשֶׁר` and gives it there, so the chapter's three shapes differ by what the Hebrew writes. The competing reading marks the *that* `added` and is named in the verse's own note.
