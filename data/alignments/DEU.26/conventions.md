# Deuteronomy 26 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The presentative wehayah frame opened in DEU.26.1 has its apodosis in DEU.26.2. DEU.26.1 writes no complementizer, so its hayah takes only "it shall be" and rule 45 has nothing to place there; the "that" opening DEU.26.2 belongs to that verse's own waw, which is rule 45's second shape. Nothing else in this chapter crosses a verse boundary.
- Every BSB status disagreement in this chapter was reviewed and left standing. The twelve R disagreements are lamedhs the ASV's recasting leaves with no English word - the bare indirect object of "giveth thee" and "given us" (26.1, 26.2, 26.3, 26.9, 26.10, 26.15), the resultative before "became" and "to be" (26.5, 26.17, 26.18), and the three coordinated infinitival lamedhs of 26.17 for which only one "that" is written. The thirteen Td disagreements are articles the ASV renders by a demonstrative (26.3, 26.9, 26.16) or not at all (26.15 "heaven", 26.16 "ordinances", 26.19 "nations", the agreement article on the ordinal at 26.12). The Sh of 26.15 and the object marker of 26.16 go the same way. These are convention differences under rules 40, 52, 41, 47, 29, 32 and 16, not corrections, and the 94.8% figure is not to be improved by moving any of them.
