# Ezekiel 31 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- דָּמָה carries the ASV's like, which is the verb's own lexeme, in all four of the chapter's comparison clauses (31.2, 31.8 twice, 31.18). The אֶל beside it takes only a separately written English word — unto in 31.8, To in 31.18 — and is unrepresented under rule 40 where the ASV writes none (31.2, and the second clause of 31.8). The fourth clause of 31.8, which writes like unto, is the internal evidence for the split; the BSB disagreement at 31.8 is this documented convention and not an error.
- עֲבֹתִים → the thick boughs is headed on boughs throughout (31.3, 31.10, 31.14): thick is the ASV's specification of a word that already names the interlaced foliage, as sea monsters heads on monsters under rule 31. The competing head on thick is a genuine tie and is named in each unit's note under rule 8; head on boughs in any further Ezekiel occurrence rather than reopening it.
- אֶרֶץ תַּחְתִּית → the nether parts of the earth is split the same way in 31.14, 31.16 and 31.18: the adjective תַּחְתִּי takes the nether parts with the phrase's supplied the, headed on the lexeme-bearing nether (rule 31), and אֶרֶץ takes of the earth (rule 19). No word of the recasting is added.
- אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה → the Lord Jehovah (31.10, 31.15, 31.18): אֲדֹנָי takes the Lord with the supplied the under rule 15, and its 1cs suffix is unrepresented under rule 22, the ASV writing nothing separate for it. These are three of the chapter's four BSB disagreements and are a convention difference, not an error.
- Both homographs of אֵת occur here, and MACULA's class is what tells them apart. The object marker H853 is tagged To (31.4 s015, 31.14, 31.15, 31.16) and is unrepresented under rule 16 wherever the ASV writes its object bare. The preposition אֵת with (H854) is tagged R (31.4 s007, 31.16 s014, 31.17, 31.18 twice); it aligns to the written with, and is unrepresented under rule 40 only at 31.4, where the ASV recasts the clause with the rivers as its subject. Read the class before citing a rule: 31.4's two occurrences are one of each.
