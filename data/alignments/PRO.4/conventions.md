# Proverbs 4 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Preposition plus construct noun is split throughout this chapter wherever the ASV spends an English word on each morpheme - LIFNE -> in the sight (4.3), BE-DEREKH -> in the way (4.11), BE-TOKH -> in the midst (4.21) - and fused under rule 30 only where a single English rendering covers the pair, ME-ALAYW -> from (4.15) and LE-NOKHAH -> right on (4.25). Rule 30's corpus counts for LIFNE -> before and for a preposition with TOKH or QEREV -> among do not reach these places, because the ASV has not written those renderings here; read what the ASV wrote before reaching for the precedent. The fused units head on the noun carrying the lexeme, or on the governing morpheme standing first where neither noun carries content the other lacks (rule 23), and the split units give each morpheme the word written for it.
- Where a coordinating waw stands between two verbs and the ASV's only and is written inside one verb's own multi-word adverbial rendering, that and belongs to the verb's unit and the waw is unrepresented. HOLEKH WA-OR -> shineth more and more (4.18) gives more and more whole to HOLEKH, the progression verb rendered as an adverb under rule 56, whose one-rendering sentence leaves no part of such an expression to a neighbouring morpheme, and leaves the waw with no connective written for it under rule 14. Do not divide the expression to give the waw something to hold: the second more would then answer to no morpheme. This is the chapter's one BSB disagreement that is not a documented convention difference.
