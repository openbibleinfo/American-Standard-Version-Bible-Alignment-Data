# Leviticus 15 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The purity formula הַזָּב and its kin (vv. 4, 6, 7, 8, 9, 11, 12, 13, 32, 33): the substantival article takes the supplied he that or him that and heads on the pronoun (rule 41), while the participle takes the whole light-verb rendering hath the issue or hath an issue and heads on the complement issue (rule 44). Where the phrase stands as the nomen rectum of a construct chain, the relational of joins the participle's unit and not the article's, so the article holds only the supplied pronoun: בְשַׂר הַזָּב → the flesh of him that hath the issue (v.7), and likewise vv. 32 and 33, including v.33's הַדָּוָה → of her that is sick. Giving the of to the article's him that unit, which is where it stands, was the competing reading and is named in each unit's note.
- נגע בְּ throughout the chapter: the ASV promotes the object to a bare direct object, so the בְּ is unrepresented in every occurrence under rule 40 (vv. 5, 7, 10, 11, 12, 19, 21, 22, 23, 27), and a whosoever or whomsoever fusion standing over the clause does not absorb it — that word carries the quantifier and the relative, not the preposition's relation. The resumptive suffix on such a preposition is likewise unrepresented wherever the ASV writes a plain relative (whomsoever in v.11, which in v.12, whom in v.18) or a bare upon (vv. 9, 20, 22); it enters a unit only inside the whereon fusion of rule 22 (vv. 4, 6, 17, 23, 24, 26).
- כֹּל plus an article: before a noun the article is unrepresented, since the ASV writes no the and the every- or any- answers to כֹּל — Every bed (vv. 4, 24, 26), what saddle soever (v.9), everything and anything from כֹּל plus כְּלִי (vv. 4, 26 with the article, v.22 without). Before a substantival participle the article is inside the fusion, because whosoever carries the pronoun rule 41 gives it (vv. 10, 19, 21, 22, 27). Every fusion of this family heads on the quantifier or the noun and never on the relative or the article: כֹּל in whosoever, whatsoever and whomsoever, אִישׁ in v.5's whosoever, and כְּלִי in the article-plus-noun anything of vv. 6 and 23, where no כֹּל stands.
- שִׁכְבַת־זָרַע → seed of copulation (vv. 16, 17, 18, 32): the ASV inverts the chain, writing the rectum's English first, so the of stands before the construct noun's own word and belongs to it, headed on copulation, while the rectum זֶרַע keeps the bare seed. This reads which word the of stands before, as rule 29 reads its the and rule 42 its and, rather than applying rule 19's first sentence to a phrase the ASV has turned around. In v.18 the second supplied preposition, with seed, is the adverbial accusative's own under rule 33.
- שְׁתֵּי תֹרִים → two turtle doves (vv. 14, 29) heads on doves, not on turtle: the ASV writes doves as the class noun for the bird in the same verse (בְּנֵי יוֹנָה → young pigeons is its counterpart), so turtle is the specifier distinguishing one class word from another under rule 31's tie clause. Heading on turtle was the competing reading and is named in both units.
