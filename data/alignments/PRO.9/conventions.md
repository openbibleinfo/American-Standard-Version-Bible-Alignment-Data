# Proverbs 9 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- A preposition the ASV supplies to reach the bare direct object of a Hebrew verb joins the verb's unit under rule 44, not the noun's under rule 33, even where the ASV's recasting makes the English predicate intransitive and the object an English adjunct: וְיוֹסֶף לֶקַח -> *he will increase in learning* (9.9) and מְיַשְּׁרִים אֹרְחוֹתָם -> *go right on their ways* (9.15) both give the word to the verb, headed on the lexical verb. Rule 33's 'another would have served' test does not decide such a case, because a transitive Hebrew verb recast intransitively always leaves a relation to choose; read what governs the noun in the Hebrew instead. Rule 33 still governs a complement no verb takes as its object.
- מְרֹמֵי קָרֶת stands in both 9.3 and 9.14, and what מְרֹמֵי holds follows the outer construct noun. In 9.3 the three-level chain גַּפֵּי מְרֹמֵי קָרֶת is split so that each noun holds an English word of its own — גַּפֵּי takes *the highest* with the phrase's supplied *the* under rule 29, מְרֹמֵי takes *places* — rather than fusing the pair and marking one of them unrepresented, which rules 2 and 30 both refuse. In 9.14 no second construct noun stands before מְרֹמֵי, so it holds the whole *in the high places*, the *in* being the construct relation of כִּסֵּא recast under rule 19 and the head falling on *high* under rule 31. The head difference between the two verses is the consequence of that division, not a second judgment.
