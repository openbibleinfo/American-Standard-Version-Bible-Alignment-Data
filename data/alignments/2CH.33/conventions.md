# 2 Chronicles 33 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Regnal age formula. In `בֶּן` + numeral + `שָׁנָה` + name, the construct `בֵּן` is the clause's predicate: it takes the construction-supplied copula together with the English 'old' (rule 20), discontinuous around the numeral phrase under rule 25, while `שָׁנָה` takes 'years', the numerals take their own words and the king's name takes only its own. 2CH.33.1 `בֶּן־שְׁתֵּים עֶשְׂרֵה שָׁנָה מְנַשֶּׁה` and 2CH.33.21 `בֶּן־עֶשְׂרִים וּשְׁתַּיִם שָׁנָה אָמוֹן` are aligned identically. Do not attach 'was' to the subject name, and do not read 'old' as the counted noun's.
- Source-citation formula. The chapter writes it both ways and they divide differently. Where the Hebrew writes no verb (2CH.33.18 `הִנָּם עַל־דִּבְרֵי מַלְכֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל`), the prepositional phrase is the predicate and takes the construction-supplied copula on `עַל` under rule 49, headed on 'among', while the ASV's supplied lexical 'written' is its own `added` unit under rule 20. Where the Hebrew writes `כְּתוּבִים` (2CH.33.19), that participle takes 'are written' and `עַל` keeps only the 'in' written for its own relation. Read which shape the verse has before placing the copula.
- An adversative or restrictive particle standing in the clause is itself what a written 'but' says and takes it, so the word is not `added` under rule 14's closing sentences: `רַק` takes the whole 'but only' at 2CH.33.17 and `כִּי` takes 'but' at 2CH.33.23, as `אֲבָל` takes 'Nevertheless' at 2CH.33.17. This reaches a particle whose own sense is adversative, not a coordinator supplied for a clause no such morpheme stands in - the 'how' of 2CH.33.19 is `added`, because the only conjunction in that clause is a waw already holding the 'and' written for it.
- Line drawn on words the ASV writes inside a verb's rendering. Phrasal particles were kept whole with the verb throughout - `בָּנָה` -> 'built up' (33.16), `קוּם` hiphil -> 'reared up' (33.3), `סוּר` -> 'took away' (33.15), `עָמַד` hiphil -> 'set up' (33.19), `סָבַב` -> 'compassed about' (33.14), `שׁוּב` hiphil -> 'brought again' (33.13) - each discontinuous under rule 25 where the object intervenes and each headed on the lexical verb. Supplied objects and adjuncts were not: 'them' at 33.15 and 'with it' at 33.14 are `added` under rules 44 and 30, no object morpheme standing in either clause. The competing reading on 'built up', that rule 44's subtraction makes the particle a surplus because bare 'built' already writes banah, is recorded in that verse's note.
