# Nehemiah 11 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Superseded on 2026-09-13 after independent TS07 confirmations for NEH.11.5, NEH.11.25, NEH.11.26, NEH.11.27, NEH.11.29, NEH.11.30 and NEH.11.31: the earlier whole-name grouping does not apply to Col-hozeh (11.5), Kiriath-arba (11.25), Beth-pelet (11.26), Hazar-shual and Beer-sheba (11.27), En-rimmon (11.29), Beer-sheba (11.30), or Beth-el (11.31). Under current Hebrew rules 2 and 30, each source component independently owns its corresponding English name component and is a separate aligned unit, headed on that source component and its English lexical rendering. Shared name reference, token count, and the numeral tag on the second component of Beer-sheba do not decide the grouping. The external genealogical of in Col-hozeh (11.5) stays with Col under rule 19, headed on Col; the supplied at in Beth-el (11.31) stays with Beth under rule 33, headed on Beth. Kiriath-arba (11.25) keeps its article inside the second source token: no separate article morpheme is invented. These confirmed splits leave separately represented preceding prepositions in their own units and leave the unrepresented preposition before Beth-pelet (11.26) unrepresented. Where the ASV translates rather than transliterates (the valley of Hinnom 11.30, the valley of craftsmen 11.35), the construct and rectum are split as an ordinary chain.
- Census totals: a verbless clause whose predicate is a compound numeral (11.6, 11.18, 11.19) takes the construction-supplied were on the opening numeral of the Hebrew figure, not on the closing counted noun or the last numeral; the alternative was named in each note.
- Overseer clauses: where paqid is indefinite and a name stands in the clause (11.9 Joel ... was their overseer; 11.14 their overseer was Zabdiel) the indefinite noun is the predicate and takes the copula, and the al + suffix is unrepresented with its suffix taking their (rule 40); where paqid heads a definite construct (11.22 the overseer of the Levites ... was Uzzi) it is the subject and the copula goes to the predicate name. English word order was not the test; Hebrew definiteness was.
- The town-list formula uvenoteha is rendered and the towns thereof (11.25, 11.27, 11.28, 11.30, 11.31): the noun takes the towns with its supplied the under rule 15, the suffix takes thereof, and the margin's Hebrew daughters is read and set aside (rule 38). At 11.31, both independent deferred-investigation judgments place *dwelt from ... onward* on מִן, headed on *from*, under rules 20 and 49: *dwelt* expresses continuing settlement location, and *from ... onward* is the complete written range relation. This supersedes only the former classification of *dwelt* and *onward* as added in that scoped predicate. No verb is carried from 11.25, and the disjoint confirmed Beth/el units and the town-list allocations above remain untouched. The original convention is archived in the deferred-investigation audit.
