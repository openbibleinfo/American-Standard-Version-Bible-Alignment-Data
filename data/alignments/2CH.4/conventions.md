# 2 Chronicles 4 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Measure formula `N בָּאַמָּה` → *N cubits* (4.2 ×3, 4.3): the numeral takes whatever preposition the ASV supplies before the measure (*of ten*, *of thirty*, *for ten*) under rule 33, or the copula where the measure is a verbless predicate (*was five*, 4.2); the בְּ and its assimilated article are `unrepresented` under rules 40 and 12, because *by the cubit* is the relation the ASV leaves unstated; אַמָּה takes *cubits*. Do not give the supplied *of* to the בְּ. The margin's *ten in a cubit* (4.3) was read and set aside.
- Directive he in this chapter: it takes *toward* where no preposition stands on the noun (4.4 צָפוֹנָה, יָמָּה, נֶגְבָּה, מִזְרָחָה); in 4.10 `מִמּוּל נֶגְבָּה` → *toward the south* the *toward* is the compound preposition's (מִ + מּוּל fused under rule 30, headed on מִ) and the he is `unrepresented`, since the parallel 1KI.7.39 writes the same English for מִמּוּל נֶגֶב with no he at all; in 4.17 צְרֵדָתָה → *Zeredah* nothing is written for it. מִלְמַעְלָה → *above* (4.4) and בָּיְתָה → *inward*, קֵדְמָה → *eastward* fuse the he with its host under rules 30/32.
- Supplied subject *it* for a participial predicate of the sea (4.4 עוֹמֵד → *It stood*, 4.5 מַחֲזִיק → *it received*) is `added`: the sea is written in 4.2, not in these verses (rule 10), the 4.4 הַיָּם stands in a later clause of its own (rule 25), and the 4.5 suffixes already hold *it*/*thereof*. The list's bare material nouns (זָהָב, נְחֹשֶׁת in 4.9, 4.16, 4.20–22) take their supplied *of*/*with* under rule 33 and the copula where the ASV writes one (4.22 *were of gold*), discontinuous around סָגוּר → *pure* and מָרוּק → *bright*.
