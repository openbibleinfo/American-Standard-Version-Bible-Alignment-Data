# Matthew 11 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- δυνάμεις → "mighty works" is headed on "mighty" at 11:20, 11:21 and 11:23. The ASV's own marginal gloss on all three verses, "Greek powers", puts the lexeme in "mighty" and leaves "works" the generic completion under rule 66. The head on "works" is named as defensible in each unit's note. No corpus query on δύναμις was run for this chapter, so a later Synoptic chapter carrying that evidence should confirm or correct all three units together rather than one at a time.
- A supplied English "and" converting a Greek participle into a coordinate finite clause rides with the finite verb it introduces throughout this chapter under rule 88 (11:4 εἶπεν, 11:4 ἀπαγγείλατε, 11:17 λέγουσιν, 11:25 εἶπεν), and that holds where the ASV's verse division cuts the Greek clause. The "and" opening 11:3 is licensed by πέμψας in 11:2, rule 88 asking for a participle standing in the same clause and not in the same verse, so it is a helper on εἶπεν and not "added" under rule 86 or rule 98. A verse pass reading 11:3 alone sees no licensing token in its own verse and would reach for "added"; the licence is in the preceding verse.
