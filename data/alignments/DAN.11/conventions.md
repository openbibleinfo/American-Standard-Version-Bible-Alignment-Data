# Daniel 11 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **DAN.11.16** — In a construct phrase whose dependent bears the Hebrew article and whose ASV rendering has one fronted 'the', the article takes 'the' and the construct head remains bare.
- **DAN.11.14** — Where an explicit demonstrative supplies ASV 'these' or 'those', that pronoun takes the determiner and agreeing articles with no separate English determiner are unrepresented.
- **DAN.11.43** — For חֲמֻדֹות rendered as 'pleasant things' or 'precious things', the lexical adjective is the target head and the generic 'things' is not.
