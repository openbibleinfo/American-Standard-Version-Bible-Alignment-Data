# 1 Corinthians 2 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- A substantival article governing a bare adjective, where the ASV recasts the phrase as a finite relative clause, splits exactly as rule 32 splits a participle: the article takes the antecedent pronoun and the adjective keeps the relativizer, the supplied copula and its own word. τοῖς τελείοις → *them* / *that are fullgrown* at 2:6 and ὁ … πνευματικός → *he* / *that is spiritual* at 2:15. Rules 20 and 32 record the articular adjective only in apposition to an expressed pronoun, which empties the article; where no such pronoun stands the article is what supplies it.
- A bare conditional εἰ is its own `unrepresented` unit where the ASV marks the protasis by inversion and writes no *if*: 2:8 εἰ … ἔγνωσαν → *had they known it* leaves the particle empty, the fronted *had* being the verb's own pluperfect auxiliary under rule 9, as the same verse's ἔγνωκεν → *hath known* keeps its own. Rule 50's *had* belongs to ἄν only where ἄν stands, and here it stands in the apodosis and takes *would*. A reference comparison will report the particle as a disagreement, and that report is not a correction.
