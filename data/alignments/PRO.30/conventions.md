# Proverbs 30 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- A relative the ASV supplies over a clause with a written antecedent and no relative morpheme is `added` throughout this chapter, including where the clause is verbless and its predicate an articleless adjective: 30.12, 30.24 and 30.30 all leave the relative added and give the copula and the adjective's own English to the predicate under rule 20. Rule 41's articleless attributive was deliberately not applied to such a predication, because the same Hebrew shape is rendered as a main clause at 30.25 and 30.26 and because the English relative's scope runs on over a coordinated finite clause at 30.12 and 30.30. Where a morpheme stands for the word it takes it instead: the resumptive suffixes of 30.14 and 30.18 under rule 18, and the written כִּי of 30.23 under rule 55.
- The presentative *There is* / *There are* joins the anchor the ASV actually writes — דּוֹר at 30.11–30.14, or the substantival numeral with the supplied generic *things* at 30.15, 30.18, 30.24 and 30.29 — under rules 20 and 31, with the head staying on the numeral. The resuming הֵנָּה, הֵמָּה or הֵם is the unrendered occurrence and is `unrepresented` under rule 22; only where a line writes no other subject does the pronoun take its own word, as the second הֵמָּה of 30.24 takes *they*. BSB attaches those pronouns, which accounts for four of the eight BSB disagreements and is a convention difference rather than an error.
- A possessive determiner the ASV supplies where no morpheme licenses it is `added` under rule 18's closing sentence: *his father* and *his mother* at 30.17, *her hands* at 30.28, *their march* at 30.29, *thy hand* and *thy mouth* at 30.32. Where a determiner morpheme does stand in the phrase it takes the word instead, so the assimilated article of בַּשִּׂמְלָה at 30.4 takes the *his* of *in his garment* under rule 41. Read whether an article or suffix is present before marking such a word added.
- In 30.27 `מֶלֶךְ אֵין לָאַרְבֶּה` → *The locusts have no king*, the possessive לְ takes the *have* under rule 43 and the existential אַיִן takes only the *no* under rule 50. The אַיִן is holding the word written for it and is not a home for a second one, and the same possessive לְ carries the *have* at 30.2 and the *hath* at 30.15, so the chapter reads all three the same way.
