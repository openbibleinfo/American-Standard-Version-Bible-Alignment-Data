# Genesis 19 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **GEN.19.20** — Single-word host-plus-directive-he renderings in GEN.19 are ordinary aligned fusions, including 'abroad', 'unto', and 'thither'; noncompositional is reserved for genuinely indivisible phrases.
- **GEN.19.29** — The repeated presentative frame 'it came to pass' heads on lexical 'came', not on 'pass'.
