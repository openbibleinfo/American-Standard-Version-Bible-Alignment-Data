# Leviticus 12 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The purification formula appears in two shapes in this chapter and MACULA's morphology, not the English, decides the italic *her*: `דְּמֵי טָהֳרָה` at 12.4 and 12.5 has an absolute `טָהֳרָה` (Ncbsa) with no suffix on it, so the ASV's italic *her* before *purifying* answers to no morpheme and is its own `added` unit under rule 18's closing sentence, while `יְמֵי טָהֳרָהּ` at 12.4 and 12.6 carries a suffix that takes the written *her* under rule 18. The two phrases are rendered by the same English words, so read the tag before placing the possessive; the italic corroborates and decides nothing, by rule 7.
- Three construct chains here turn on whether the construct noun keeps an English word of its own, and rule 19 divides them two ways: `בֶּן־שְׁנָתוֹ` → *a year old* gives *old* to `בֶּן` and *a year* to the rectum (12.6), and `דֵּי שֶׂה` → *for a lamb* gives *for* to `דֵּי` and *a lamb* to the rectum (12.8), because in each the construct would otherwise be left holding nothing; but `תּוֹרַת הַיֹּלֶדֶת` → *the law for her that beareth* (12.7) leaves *law* on the construct, so the relational *for* is the rectum's and goes to the participle carrying the lexeme, discontinuous around the substantival article's *her that*, exactly as `of … purifying` runs around the suffix's *her* at 12.4 and 12.6. Count the ASV's words against the two nouns before placing a preposition in the parallel purification and offering laws.
