# Ezekiel 30 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **EZK.30.14** — Throughout EZK.30, ASV No renders the place-name Thebes (נֹא); align the Hebrew proper name to the written ASV form No.
- **EZK.30.23** — In the repeated dispersion formula of verses 23 and 26, ASV recasts the proper name Egypt as the people-name the Egyptians; the supplied article remains in that nominal unit.
