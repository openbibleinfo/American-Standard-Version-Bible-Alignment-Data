# Genesis 27 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **GEN.27.14** — Treat כַּאֲשֶׁר as one noncompositional expression in this chapter; when no internal morpheme uniquely heads it, use the first source token as the deterministic source anchor.
- **GEN.27.21** — In forms of נגשׁ with an Sh ending, English near belongs to the lexical verb and the Sh token is unrepresented.
- **GEN.27.33** — Treat בְּטֶרֶם rendered as the single English before as one noncompositional expression, headed on טֶרֶם and before.
