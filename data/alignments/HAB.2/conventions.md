# Habakkuk 2 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **HAB.2.8** — In the repeated refrain חמס ארץ קריה וכל ישבי בה, ASV done is a supplied lexical participle with no source verb and is a separate added unit; the construct noun and dependents retain for/the violence/to.
