# 1 John 3 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **1JN.3.6** — πᾶς ὁ + participle rendered by the single English word *whosoever*: πᾶς takes that word and the substantival article is its own `unrepresented` unit (rules 32, 23), not one joined unit under rule 39's test. Applied at 1JN.3.6 (twice), 1JN.3.9, 1JN.3.10 and 1JN.3.15, all `medium`. The corpus reads it this way at JHN.3.15, JHN.3.16, JHN.16.2 and 2JN.1.9 and joins only at JHN.11.26 and JHN.12.46, both `medium` and the latter conceding the split. Where the ASV instead writes *every one that*, as at 1JN.3.3 and 1JN.3.4, the article has a word of its own and takes *one*.
- **1JN.3.20** — A resumptive ὅτι re-opening the clause a first ὅτι already introduced is its own `unrepresented` unit. Rule 43's test is the presence of an English word, and the ASV writes *because* once, at the first; rule 43 forbids manufacturing one by stretching the conjunction onto the clause's subject or verb, and rule 41 is the parallel that forbids joining a non-adjacent redundant pair into one unit.
- **1JN.3.18** — Where the ASV italicizes a possessive before an anarthrous vocative — *My little children* at both 1JN.3.7 and 1JN.3.18 — it is a translator's supply and folds onto the noun under rules 7, 11 and 66, as 1JN.2.12 and 1JN.2.28 do. The variant μου at SBLGNT.1JN.3.18.v010 was therefore deselected in favour of r00-base; the italics are the evidence that the ASV's Greek lacked the pronoun.
