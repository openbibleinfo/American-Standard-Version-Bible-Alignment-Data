# Judges 20 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **JDG.20.39** — For לְהַכּוֹת חֲלָלִים rendered 'to smite and kill,' assign 'smite' to the infinitive, mark the unsupported coordinating 'and' added, and assign 'kill' to חֲלָלִים; JDG.20.31 and JDG.20.39 follow this split.
