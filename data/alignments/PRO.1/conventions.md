# Proverbs 1 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- lo abah rendered 'would none of' (1.25, 1.30): the negative takes the written 'none', and the 'of' goes to whatever governs the object - the written le- at 1.30, and the verb's own valency under rule 44 at 1.25, where the Hebrew writes the object bare and no preposition morpheme stands. 'none of' is not read as one partitive expression under rule 15, since a negative morpheme in the clause is what the 'none' renders, and that count runs before rule 15's supplied-quantifier sentence is reached.
- Adjective-plus-noun renderings of a single Hebrew substantive were all decided by rule 31's subtraction and divide two ways in this chapter: the head is the modifier where the English noun is a supplied generic - 'dark sayings' (1.6), 'broad places' (1.20), 'the chief place' (1.21), 'the young man' (1.4) - and the noun where the noun carries the lexeme and the adjective is the ASV's specifier - 'sound counsels' (1.5), 'the careless ease' (1.32). The divergence is the test's outcome and not an inconsistency. 'the young man' is the close one, and the competing head is named in its verse note.
- ad matay at 1.22 is split rather than fused, because the ASV writes no separate matrix duration frame there (rule 17's closing sentence): matay takes 'How', carrying the wh-lexeme, and ad takes 'long', carrying the duration. Rule 17's indivisible 'how long will it be' governs only where the ASV writes that frame over a following finite clause.
