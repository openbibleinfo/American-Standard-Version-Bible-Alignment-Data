# 1 Timothy 2 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Where one Greek word is rendered by an English phrase, this chapter heads on the word carrying the lexeme's own sense and treats the phrase's formal English noun as the generic completion: ὡσαύτως → *in like manner* on *like* (2.9), ὑπεροχῇ → *high place* on *high* (2.2), πλέγμασιν → *braided hair* on *braided* (2.9). Rule 71's frame-heads-its-lexical-noun does not reach these, being scoped in terms to an improper preposition governing a case, and none of these words governs anything. The discriminating contrast is a Greek compound whose elements are each glossed: τεκνογονίας → *child bearing* (2.15) is not a supplied frame, so the noun element *bearing* heads it. Corpus precedent supports the ὡσαύτως head (1TI.3.8, 1TI.3.11, 1TI.5.25 on *like*; LUK.22.20 and MRK.14.31 on *manner*, both conceding *like*); ὑπεροχή has no corpus precedent either way.
- This chapter marks nothing `added`. Both English words the ASV supplies with no source token of their own are folded onto the token that licenses them: *himself* at 2.5, a rule 7 pronoun helper on the appositional ἄνθρωπος, and *to be borne* at 2.6, a supplied verb on the expressed predicate noun μαρτύριον under rule 92 with the article keeping only its bare *the* under rule 96. Rule 105 does not decide either — it is scoped to a supplied appositional *even* — and rule 103's `added` case wants a source token already spent elsewhere, which neither of these has. Both readings are hedged in their unit notes.
