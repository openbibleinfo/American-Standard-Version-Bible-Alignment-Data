# Psalms 24 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **PSA.24.7** — The refrain יָבוֹא מֶלֶךְ הַכָּבוֹד → the King of glory will come in keeps in with בּוֹא as part of its lexeme (come in / enter, rule 44) in both v7 and v9; the competing reading marking in added was considered and not taken. The vocatives of the same refrain are allocated by position under rule 36: O ye gates and ye everlasting doors go to the nouns, the ye of be ye lifted up (v7) to the Niphal imperative.
