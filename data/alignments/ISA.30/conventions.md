# Isaiah 30 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **ISA.30.8** — Unsupported English function words remain added rather than being assigned to a neighboring source expression: the repeated 'of' in verse 1 and the waw-less 'and' in verse 8 follow the same boundary already used for the waw-less coordinators in verses 25 and 33.
