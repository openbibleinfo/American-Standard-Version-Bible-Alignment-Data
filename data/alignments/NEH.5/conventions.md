# Nehemiah 5 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The formula yesh asher omrim (vv. 2, 3, 4) is read yesh -> 'there were' (presentative dummy and copula, rules 43/50) and asher -> 'that'; a subject the ASV supplies for the existential ('Some' in v. 3) goes with the written asher as its antecedent under rule 46's first rider, not with yesh and not added. In v. 5, where the ASV writes only 'some' for yesh (ve-yesh mi-benotenu -> 'and some of our daughters'), yesh takes that word and the partitive min keeps only 'of'; competing reading (min -> 'some of', yesh unrepresented) is named in the unit note.
- **NEH.5.8** — vayyacharishu -> 'held they their peace' keeps the idiom's fixed possessive 'their' inside the verb's light-verb unit (headed on peace, rule 44), on the reasoning rule 30 gives for the pronouns of na -> 'I beseech thee'; it is not marked added under rule 18's closing sentence, since English writes no 'hold peace' without the possessive.
