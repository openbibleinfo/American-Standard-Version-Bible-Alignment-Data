# Leviticus 27 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The ASV writes 'whether ... or' six times in this chapter, and the pair always goes to whatever connective morpheme stands in the Hebrew, never to a bein: to the waw in bein tov u-vein ra (27.12, 27.14) and in me-adam u-vehemah (27.28), and to the lamed standing where the connective stands in bein tov la-ra (27.33), headed on 'or' in each. Every bein in the chapter is unrepresented, because the ASV writes no word for the interval relation and a preposition of interval is not what 'whether' says (rules 14, 26, 40). Where the Hebrew writes a connective of its own for each member - im shor im seh (27.26) - each im takes only the word written for it and there is no pair to place; where no connective morpheme stands at all - mi-zera ha-arets mi-peri ha-ets (27.30) - both words are a single added unit headed on 'or'. Five of the chapter's BSB disagreements are these bein tokens and follow from this reading.
- we-hayah hu u-temurato yihyeh qodesh (27.10, 27.33) writes hayah twice for one ASV 'shall be'. The chapter aligns that 'shall be' to the closing yihyeh, which stands where the English predicate stands, and marks the opening we-hayah unrepresented under rule 22. Rule 22's guidance about which occurrence is the rendered one is written for counted nouns and construct-headed phrases and does not reach a doubled verb, so the choice is recorded here. BSB attaches the word to the opening verb in both verses; that is the documented disagreement, not an error to fix.
- Where the ASV completes a Hebrew substantive with a generic English noun the Hebrew does not write, that noun joins the substantive's unit and the head stays on the word carrying the lexeme: chamishit -> 'the fifth part' headed on 'fifth' (27.13, 27.15, 27.19, 27.27, 27.31), cherem -> 'devoted thing' and 'one devoted' headed on 'devoted' (27.28 twice, 27.29), qodesh -> 'a holy thing' headed on 'holy' (27.23). Rule 31's tie-breaker comes out the same way each time, because the ASV never writes 'part', 'thing' or 'one' alone for any of these words.
- Two construct chains of identical shape are counted differently, and the ASV's number of 'the's is what separates them. shenat ha-yovel -> 'the year of jubilee' (27.17, 27.18, 27.23, 27.24) writes one 'the', which rule 29 fronts onto the rectum's article, leaving the construct noun bare. sheqel ha-qodesh -> 'the shekel of the sanctuary' (27.3, 27.25), achuzzat ha-arets -> 'the possession of the land' (27.24) and maasar ha-arets -> 'the tithe of the land' (27.30) write two, so nothing is fronted: the article takes the 'the' standing before its own word and the construct takes a supplied one. Do not level these to each other; the difference is in the English, not in the Hebrew.
