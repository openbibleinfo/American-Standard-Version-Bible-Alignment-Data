# Proverbs 25 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Emblematic similes. Where the chapter juxtaposes two nominal cola with no verb, the Hebrew predicate is read as the first colon, and where that colon lists several items it is the first of them; the construction-supplied copula joins that unit under rule 20, discontinuous under rule 25, whichever colon the ASV puts first in English. Applied at 25.11, 25.12, 25.14, 25.18, 25.19, 25.20, 25.25, 25.26 and 25.28, five of which the ASV reorders. Where the first colon is instead headed by a written comparative kaph, rule 49 puts the same copula on the preposition, as at 25.13. The competing reading, which attaches the copula to the second colon's noun, is named in the units it touches.
- Supplied comparative frame. No morpheme licenses the opening As or like of these similes, so it is added (25.3, 25.11, 25.12, 25.14, 25.19, 25.20, 25.25, 25.26, 25.28), while the answering So belongs to the waw opening the second colon where one stands (25.3, 25.20, 25.23, 25.25, 25.27) and is added where none does (25.12, 25.13, 25.14, 25.26). The ASV's italics track the Hebrew exactly here: every italic So in this chapter stands in a waw-less verse and every So aligned to a waw is set in roman, which corroborates the count without deciding it (rule 7).
- Existential ayin with its noun. At 25.3 the ASV writes one word, unsearchable, for ein cheqer, so the pair shares one unit under rule 30, headed on the noun carrying the lexeme, with the rule 50 reading that heads on the negation named in the unit's note; the fused unit is indexed under cheqer rather than ayin, so it does not appear beside the chapter's other ayin units. Where the ASV instead spends a word on each morpheme - without rain at 25.14, without walls and is without restraint at 25.28 - rule 2 splits them and each ayin is its own unit headed on without.
