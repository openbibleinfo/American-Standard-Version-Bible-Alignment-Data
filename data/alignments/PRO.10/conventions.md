# Proverbs 10 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Verbless couplet copula. In this chapter's `X le-Y` proverbs the copula was placed by rule 49's closing count -- the clause's nominals weighed against the subjects the ASV writes. Where the only nominal is itself the ASV's subject, the prepositional phrase predicates and the lamed takes the copula or frame: 10.6 *are upon*, 10.13 *is for*, 10.16 *tendeth to*, 10.23 *is to*. Where the ASV writes a separate subject besides the nominal standing by the preposition, that nominal predicates and takes the copula while the preposition keeps only its own word: 10.29 *is a stronghold to the upright* and *is a destruction to the workers of iniquity*, both with derekh YHWH as the written subject. The one-in-five difference at 10.29 is that count and not a departure.
- Supplied English in a gapped or verbless second colon was sorted three ways, each by subtraction: a bare copula joins the predicate noun (10.28 italic *shall be*, 10.29 *is*); a result frame written over a present lamed joins that preposition, since dropping it removes the preposition's own relation and the parallel colon leaves the same lamed carrying it alone with no verb at all (10.16 italic *tendeth*); a whole lexical verb the Hebrew never wrote is `added` (10.32 italic *speaketh*, which introduces a speaking the clause does not predicate). The ASV italicizes all three alike, so the italic corroborates and settles none of them.
- A preposition the ASV supplies before a bare noun was placed by the Hebrew noun's own function, not by the shape of the English verb phrase. An instrumental or locative accusative takes the word itself under rule 33: 10.4 *with a slack hand*, 10.10 *with the eye* after qorets, 10.30 *in the land* after intransitive shakhan, and the bare predicate nominals of 10.17 *is in the way* and 10.18 *is of lying lips*. A preposition written to reach the verb's own object joins the verb under rule 44: 10.24 *shall come upon* with the object suffix on the verb. 10.30's *in* was moved from the verb onto erets on that basis during the verse pass, and the two families are distinguished by whether a bare direct object stands there.
