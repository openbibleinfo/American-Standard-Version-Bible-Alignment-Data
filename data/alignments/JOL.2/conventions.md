# Joel 2 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **JOL.2.28** — The ASV's presentative frame 'And it shall come to pass ... that' realizes וְהָיָה, and the complementizer 'that' belongs to the הָיָה unit rather than being 'added'. JOL.2.28 and JOL.2.32 both take this treatment; in 2.28 the unit is discontinuous across 'afterward' under rule 25. Corpus: 11 units fold it in (it came to pass that x7, it shall come to pass that x3, it will come to pass that x1) against one added at 1KI.21.1; where a waw introduces the apodosis, that waw carries the 'that' instead (1KI.1.21, 1KI.18.12).
