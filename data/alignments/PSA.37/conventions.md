# Psalms 37 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **PSA.37.12** — Where a Hebrew verb's bare direct object is recast by the ASV as an instrument or an oblique (upholdeth him with his hand 37.24, gnasheth upon him with his teeth 37.12, feed on 37.3, prospereth in 37.7, delighteth in 37.23, talketh of 37.30), the supplied preposition goes to the verb's unit and the noun keeps only its own word; the chapter reads none of these as rule 33's instrumental or adverbial accusative, since in each the noun is what the Hebrew verb governs.
- **PSA.37.14** — Phrasal particles in this chapter are read as completing the verb's lexeme and stay with the verb (rule 44): cut down for מלל 37.2, drawn out for פתח 37.14, cast down for הִפִּיל 37.14 and the hophal of טול 37.24, consume away for כלה 37.20, payeth again for שלם 37.21, passed by for עבר 37.36. Marking the particle added was the competing reading only for 'drawn out', and the note there records it.
