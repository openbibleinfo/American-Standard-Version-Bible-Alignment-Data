# Galatians 2 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **GAL.2.5** — A supplied English noun inside a case frame is not the Greek noun's determiner. In 'in the way of subjection' the ASV's 'the' determines the supplied 'way', not the noun, so rule 21's test finds the noun determinerless: the article is unrepresented under rule 23 and the whole frame rides with the noun. GAL.2.5 tei hupotagei and GAL.2.13 tei hupokrisei are analysed alike on this ground.
