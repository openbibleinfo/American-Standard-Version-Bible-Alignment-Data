# Isaiah 41 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **ISA.41.12** — Where the ASV supplies emphatic ‘even’ without a waw or other licensing morpheme, it is added; the construct substantive takes only ‘them that’.
- **ISA.41.28** — The waw before ‘I ask’ takes the ASV temporal connective ‘when’; the opening ‘when’ remains added because that clause’s waw already renders ‘And’.
