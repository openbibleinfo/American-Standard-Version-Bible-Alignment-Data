# Numbers 18 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Appositional 'even': read which morpheme stands on the phrase the word introduces. The le of specification takes it in 18.8 (le-khol qodshey), 18.9 (le-khol minchatam) and 18.11 (le-khol tenufot); the object marker takes it in 18.21 and 18.29, where the marker itself stands on the appositive. Neither is 'added', and the two are not interchangeable: a marker standing elsewhere in the clause does not carry an 'even' written before a phrase a le introduces, and vice versa.
- le plus a pronominal suffix rendered by a bare English possessive with no preposition written is one fused unit headed on the suffix, under rule 49's closing paragraph with rule 30: zeh yihyeh lekha -> 'thine' (18.9, 18.13, 18.14, 18.15, 18.18 twice), ve-zeh lekha -> 'is thine' (18.11, the construction's copula joining that one unit because the prepositional phrase is the predicate), lakhem -> 'your' (18.31, where sakhar is the written predicate and keeps the copula). Do not reach for rule 40 here: that rule governs a dative whose own pronoun the ASV writes separately. Where the ASV does write a preposition the pair splits (18.10 'unto thee'), and where hayah recasts the clause with 'have' the preposition is unrepresented and the suffix takes the subject pronoun (18.20).
- Superlative qodesh haqqodashim: the construct takes 'most holy', the article takes the written 'the', and the plural rectum takes 'things' (18.9, 18.10). Where the ASV writes only 'most holy', the rectum is unrepresented under rule 22 rather than taking 'most' (18.9, qodesh qodashim hu lekha). The competing division, 'most' to the rectum and 'holy' to the construct, is defensible and is named in the verse notes; it is also where BSB disagrees, and that disagreement is convention and not error.
- The waw of a negative purpose or result clause takes the ASV's bare 'that': ve-lo yamutu -> 'that they die not' (18.3), ve-lo yihyeh od qetsef -> 'that there be wrath no more' (18.5), ve-lo tamutu -> 'that ye die not' (18.32), and ve-yilavu -> 'that they may be joined' (18.2). It is neither 'added' under rule 55 nor fused with the following lo as 'neither' under rule 35, because a conjunction stands in the clause and the negative has its own written word.
- be-tokh fuses as the single word 'among' (18.20 twice, 18.23, 18.24), while mi-tokh splits into 'from' plus 'among' (18.6): rule 30's subtraction test is what separates them, since dropping 'among' in 18.6 leaves tavekh with no rendering of its own while dropping it in the fused cases leaves only the expression as a whole diminished.
