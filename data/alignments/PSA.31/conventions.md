# Psalms 31 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **PSA.31.22** — A topicalizing 'As for' the ASV writes for a clause-opening waw plus explicit pronoun (va'ani -> 'As for me') is the waw's connective under rule 14's open list, headed on 'as'; it is marked added under rule 16 only where the waw already holds a separately written 'And'. Corpus: 8 units in 6 chapters (2SA.9.11, EZK.1.10/13/18 ...).
