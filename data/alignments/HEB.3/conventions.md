# Hebrews 3 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Versification: the ASV renders the projection's HEB.3.10 opening `τεσσεράκοντα ἔτη` at the end of HEB.3.9. Under rule 37 the English *forty years* is `added` at 3:9 and both source tokens are `unrepresented` at 3:10, each side carrying a note naming the other verse. 3:17's own `τεσσεράκοντα ἔτη` is aligned in the ordinary way, so the two treatments of that phrase are a boundary artifact and not a split. A reference comparison reports both 3:10 tokens as disagreements and that report is not a correction.
- Supplied possessives divide the chapter's articles two ways, and the test is whether a source word is free to take the possessive. Where a genitive pronoun or relative stands — `αὐτοῦ` (3:2, 3:5, 3:6, 3:7, 3:15, 3:18), `ὑμῶν` (3:8, 3:9, 3:15), `μου` (3:9, 3:10, 3:11), `ὧν` (3:17) — rule 20 proper empties the article. Where none does, the article carries the supplied possessive under rule 20's second arm: `τὴν παρρησίαν` → *our boldness* and `τῆς ἐλπίδος` → *of our hope* at 3:6, `τῆς ὑποστάσεως` → *of our confidence* at 3:14, and `τῇ καρδίᾳ` → *their heart* at 3:10, where rule 76 also applies because the only `αὐτοί` in the verse is a nominative already spent on the subject *they* of the following clause.
- The Hebraic oath particle `εἰ` at 3:11 (`ὤμοσα … Εἰ εἰσελεύσονται`, the ASV's own gloss reading *Greek If they shall enter*) carries the oath's negative force and is aligned to the English *not*, not left `unrepresented`: no other source token stands for that negation. This matches 3:18's ordinary `μή` → *not* in the same oath report, so the chapter gives the negation to the negating token in both. The formula recurs in Hebrews 4.
- The supplied appositional *even* of `Ἰησοῦν` → *even Jesus* at 3:1 is folded onto the name as a rule 7 helper, headed on *Jesus*. Rule 105's `added` branch is scoped to a substantival article carrier, whose limit is rule 32's *and nothing else*; a bare anarthrous name carries no such limit, and the corpus's one unit for that carrier folds (JHN.5.45 `Μωϋσῆς` → *even Moses*). Corrected in reconciliation from an earlier `added` reading.
- `ἄχρις οὗ` at 3:13 → *so long as* is split rather than joined: the whole three-word clause-introducer goes to `ἄχρις` headed on the meaning-bearing *long*, and the relative `οὗ` is its own `unrepresented` unit. Rule 94's precondition bars a compound unit here, the ASV having written separate words rather than one.
