# Zephaniah 1 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **ZEP.1.5** — In a construct chain whose nomen rectum bears the only article, a single ASV ‘the’ fronted before the construct belongs to that article when the rectum is rendered anarthrously; the construct noun remains bare.
- **ZEP.1.18** — The discontinuous גַּם … גַּם … לֹא correlative is one noncompositional unit aligned to ASV ‘Neither … nor,’ source-headed on the negative and target-headed on ‘Neither’; the rejected smaller split is retained in the unit note.
