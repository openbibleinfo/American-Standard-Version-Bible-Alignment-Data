# 2 Chronicles 23 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- A circumstantial waw standing before a nominal that carries its own prepositional phrase takes the ASV's *with*: `אִישׁ וְכֵלָיו בְּיָדוֹ` → *every man with his weapons in his hand* (23.7) and `וְאִישׁ שִׁלְחוֹ בְיָדוֹ` → *every man with his weapon in his hand* (23.10) both give *with* to the waw under rules 11 and 14, rather than leaving the waw unrepresented and giving the word to the noun under rule 33. A present function morpheme whose sense the word carries takes it before a supplied-word analysis does; the competing reading is named in each verse's note.
- A bare `בֵּית` of direction or place takes the preposition the ASV supplies before it — *into* (23.6, 23.12), *to* (23.15, 23.17), *in* (23.9, 23.14), *unto* (23.20) — as an adverbial accusative under rule 33, and not as a preposition supplied for the governing verb's valency under rule 44. Rule 33's own *to your mountain* is the terminative accusative this answers to, so the direct-object question that rule asks is answered in the negative throughout the chapter; in 23.9 the construction's copula joins the same unit under rule 20.
- Where the ASV renders a substantival article by a single word combining the pronoun and the relative — *whosoever* (23.7), *whoso* (23.14) — that whole word is the article's under rule 41 and the participle keeps only its verb. The article is not left unrepresented and the word is not split between the two morphemes.
