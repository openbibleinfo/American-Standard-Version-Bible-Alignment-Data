# Exodus 6 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- In the repeated identifying formula הוּא plus coordinated proper names rendered 'These/these are that X and Y,' align 'These/these ... that' discontinuously to הוּא and place the construction-supplied copula 'are' on the first predicate name, following rule 20 and the exact NUM.26.9 corpus precedent.
