# Genesis 38 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **GEN.38.27** — In the presentative frame it came to pass ... that, when the apodosis opens with its own waw, the waw carries that and hayah carries only it came to pass, including before behold (GEN.38.24, GEN.38.27-29).
