# Psalms 135 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The chapter's ten English relatives (*Who*, *that*, *Ye that*, *They that*) answer to three different things and are allocated by asking what stands in the colon, not by matching the English. A written relative takes the word together with any supplied antecedent pronoun under rule 46's first rider: `שֶׁ` → *Ye that* (v2), *Who* (vv. 8, 10), and `אֲשֶׁר` → *that* after the substantival `כֹּל` (v18). An anarthrous participle or adjective supplies its own noun phrase under rule 46 and heads on its lexeme: *Who causeth* and *Who bringeth forth* (v7), *They that make* (v18), *Ye that fear* (v20), *Who dwelleth* (v21). Where the colon writes a finite verb with no relative and no conjunction in it, the word answers to no morpheme and is `added` under rule 55: *Who maketh* (v7, over `עָשָׂה`) and *Who sent* (v9, over `שָׁלַח`). The written `שֶׁ` of v8 is not distributed forward into v9 to rescue that `added`: rule 55's distributed subordinator reaches clauses coordinated under one written subordinator, and rule 10 makes the verse the whole unit of work.
