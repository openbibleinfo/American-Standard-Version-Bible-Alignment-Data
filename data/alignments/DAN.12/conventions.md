# Daniel 12 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **DAN.12.6** — The repeated compound מִמַּעַל, rendered ‘was above,’ is noncompositional in DAN.12.6–7: the first source token is the deterministic source head, ‘above’ is the target head, and the supplied copula remains in the compound predicate’s unit.
