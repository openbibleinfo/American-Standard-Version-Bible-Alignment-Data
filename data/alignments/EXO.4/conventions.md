# Exodus 4 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **EXO.4.8** — In the presentative formula “come/came to pass,” היה owns the full frame and heads on “pass,” the complement carrying the occurrence lexeme; “come/came” is light support.
- **EXO.4.9** — A waw plus negative fused as the single English connective “nor” or “neither” is noncompositional and heads on the negative.
