# Colossians 4 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Where the ASV completes a partitive ἐκ phrase with a supplied *one* — `ἐξ ὑμῶν` → *one of you* at COL.4.9 and *is one of you* at COL.4.12 — this chapter gives the supplied *one* to the preposition rather than to the genitive pronoun, and heads the unit on the prepositional word *of*, the pronoun standing bare. That follows rule 67's clause for a prepositional predicate the ASV completes with a supplied nominal; but rule 67 leaves the head open where the frame's carrier is a proper preposition, so the head is this chapter's choice and not the contract's. Attaching *one* to `ὑμῶν`, or heading the unit on *one*, remains defensible, and a chapter departing from this reading should say so in the note.
- A verbless clause whose predicate is a bare adverb carries the ASV's supplied verb on the adverb, whether that verb is a copula or a lexical one: `τοὺς ἔξω` → *them that are without* puts *are* on `ἔξω` (COL.4.5) and `τὰ ὧδε` → *things that are done here* puts the supplied *are done* on `ὧδε` (COL.4.9), the substantival article keeping only its nominal word and the supplied relativizer. Rule 53 names an adverbial predicate for a supplied copula and rule 62 names a prepositional one for a supplied lexical verb; this chapter read rule 62's carrier test as turning on what is left in the clause to carry the verb and not on the predicate's own class. The contract was left unamended for want of corpus evidence beyond this chapter's single lexical-verb instance, so a later chapter meeting the same shape should cite this entry.
