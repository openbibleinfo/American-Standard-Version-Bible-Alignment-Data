# Romans 5 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The ASV's marginal alternative renderings and Greek glosses are not the target text, and nothing in this chapter was aligned to one. 5.1's 'Many ancient authorities read let us have', 5.2 and 5.3's 'Or, let us rejoice', and 5.20's 'Or, law' were set aside for the printed indicative and the printed 'the law'; 5.9 and 5.10's 'Greek in' gloss was set aside and ἐν takes the written 'by' at both places, as ἐν takes the written 'in' at 5.3, 5.5, 5.11, 5.13, 5.17 and 5.21. Rule 12 forbids remaking the source-side decision a margin bears on, and rule 97 makes a printed textual note corroboration only; neither licenses reading a margin as English to align.
- In οὐ μόνον ... ἀλλὰ καί at 5.3 and 5.11, the ASV's supplied 'so' of 'not only so' rides inside μόνον's own unit as the completion of that adverb's English idiom, headed on 'only', with οὐ keeping 'not' and καί keeping 'also' as separate units. No corpus query on μόνος was made, and marking the 'so' `added` under rule 5 remains defensible, so a chapter meeting this idiom should query the lemma before following the reading rather than treating these two verses as settled precedent.
