# Psalms 138 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **PSA.138.8** — The italicized 'endureth' of חַסְדְּךָ לְעוֹלָם → *Thy lovingkindness ... endureth for ever* is read as the construction-supplied copula on the prepositional predicate: it joins the לְ unit, headed on *for* (rules 20 and 49), and is not a supplied lexical verb marked `added` under rule 20's supplied-lexical-verb clause. Rule 20's subtraction decides it — dropping the word leaves the subject and its prepositional predicate standing, so what has gone is the link English writes and not the predication — and 'endureth' links without introducing any participant the Hebrew does not write, as *remain* does in that rule's list. The ASV italic corroborates the supply and decides nothing (rule 7).
- **PSA.138.3** — בְּיוֹם קָרָאתִי → *In the day that I called*: MACULA writes יוֹם absolute and a finite verb follows it, so no construct relation stands and rule 52's בְּיוֹם הֵעָשׂוֹתוֹ division does not reach the phrase. בְּ takes *In*, יוֹם takes *the day* with the determiner rule 15 supplies, the finite verb takes *I called*, and the supplied *that* is its own `added` unit under rule 55, יוֹם being a written antecedent that puts rule 46 out of reach. The apodosis waw of וַתַּעֲנֵנִי, for which the ASV writes no connective, is `unrepresented` under rule 14; it is not a home for the *that*, which stands ahead of the temporal clause rather than at the apodosis's head. This is the chapter's only BSB disagreement.
