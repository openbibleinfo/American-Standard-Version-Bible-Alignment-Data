# Leviticus 21 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Disqualification items: where a single participle or adjective is the whole of the item, the entire English clause the ASV builds around it joins that one morpheme's unit — pronoun, relative and supplied verb alike — under rules 46 and 31, as `charum` -> *he that hath a flat nose* and `sarua'` -> *anything superfluous* do at LEV.21.18, and `gibben` -> *crook backed*, `daq` -> *a dwarf* at LEV.21.20. Where the item instead writes further morphemes that each hold their own English, a verb the ASV supplies beside them is `added`: LEV.21.20's *hath* is added twice, before *a blemish in his eye* and before *his stones broken*, because `be-` holds *in*, the suffix *his*, `'ayin` *eye*, `tevallul` *a blemish*, `meroach` *broken* and `'ashekh` *stones*.
- `'ish` heading a disqualification or eligibility clause takes the whole universalizing frame the ASV writes for it: *Whosoever he be* at LEV.21.17, where no `kol` stands, headed on *Whosoever*; *man he be* at LEV.21.18, where `kol` takes *whatsoever*, headed on *man*. The supplied *he be* is not given to a following preposition as a prepositional predicate's copula under rule 49, and is not `added`.
- `'ishshey YHWH` -> *the offerings of Jehovah made by fire* is divided the same way at LEV.21.6 and LEV.21.21: `'ishsheh` takes *the offerings ... made by fire* as one discontinuous unit headed on *offerings* by rule 31's tie-breaker, the supplied *the* joining the construct under rule 29, while `YHWH` takes *of Jehovah*. *made by fire* is not split off and no morpheme of the phrase is left unrepresented.
- `ki 'im` is one fused unit under rules 30 and 35 wherever it stands in this chapter — *except* at LEV.21.2, *but* at LEV.21.14 — and, neither morpheme carrying a lexeme, the source head is `ki`, the first token, as the deterministic display anchor.
