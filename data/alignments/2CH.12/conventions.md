# 2 Chronicles 12 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **2CH.12.11** — A bare בַּיִת (or other place noun) standing after בּוֹא with no preposition or object marker is a directional accusative: the ASV's supplied *into* belongs to the noun under rule 33, headed on the lexeme, and the verb keeps only *entered*/*came*. The corpus places the word this way in 50 units against 10 on the verb under rule 44; the 12.11 verse pass first took the minority reading and was corrected at reconciliation.
- **2CH.12.9** — *away* after לָקַח → *took away* is a surplus particle under rule 44 in all three occurrences of the verse: *took* alone writes the whole lexeme and nothing in the clause writes the removal, so each *away* is its own `added` unit (as with the *back* of בּוֹא hiphil → *bring him back*), unlike סוּר → *take away*, where the particle completes the lexeme. The competing reading keeps the particle with the verb.
