# 2 Samuel 10 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- בְּעֵינֵי with a pronominal suffix, where the ASV writes an English verb of judgment in its place (`Thinkest thou` v. 3, `seemeth him` v. 12), is read as one fused unit of the preposition and the construct noun headed on the noun (rules 30, 40, as `רַע בְּעֵינֵי` → `it displease`), the suffix taking the English pronoun written for it; the competing reading that marks the verb `added` and the noun unrepresented was set aside in both verses.
- בּוֹא with a bare place noun and no preposition or directive he (`into the land` v. 2, `into the city` and `to Jerusalem` v. 14, `to Helam` v. 16) gives the supplied preposition to the accusative of direction under rule 33, not to the verb under rule 44; where the noun carries a directive he (v. 17 חֵלָאמָה → `to Helam`) the he takes the word under rule 32 and the noun keeps only the name.
