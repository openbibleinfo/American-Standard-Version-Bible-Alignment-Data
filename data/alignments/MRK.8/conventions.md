# Mark 8 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Rule 58 governs a quantity adjective as it governs a quality one: where an anarthrous adjective and an expressed anarthrous noun share one supplied English determiner, the determiner is the noun's, discontinuously under rule 15. πολλοῦ ὄχλου -> *a ... multitude* plus bare *great* at 8:1 and ἰχθύδια ὀλίγα -> *a ... small fishes* plus bare *few* at 8:7. Do not read *a few* as a fused quantifier; rule 29's *a hundred* is scoped to a compound numeral idiom, not to an adjective inside a noun phrase.
- The chapter divides rules 88 and 98 by whether the ASV converted the participle into a coordinate finite clause. Where it did, the supplied *and* is a helper on the finite verb it introduces, even across an intervening participial phrase (8:1, 8:6 *and he brake* over εὐχαριστήσας, 8:10, 8:12, 8:13, 8:23 *and brought*, 8:24, 8:29, 8:32, 8:34). Where two participles stand asyndetically and both are rendered alike as subordinate clauses, the seam is `added` under rule 98 (8:23 t027, between πτύσας and ἐπιθείς). Proximity does not decide either case.
- A single Greek numeral compound rendered by multiplier plus base heads on the base: τετρακισχίλιοι -> *four thousand* on *thousand* at 8:9 and 8:20, πεντακισχιλίους -> *five thousand* on *thousand* at 8:19, on the model of rule 29's χιλιάδες -> *thousand*. The article and the numeral still split separately under rule 13 (τοὺς πεντακισχιλίους -> *the* plus *five thousand*).
