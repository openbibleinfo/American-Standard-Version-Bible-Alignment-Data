# 1 Samuel 28 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **1SA.28.3** — 'ob with the article and no ba'alat (28.3 ha'obot → 'those that had familiar spirits', 28.9 → 'those that have familiar spirits'): the article takes the 'those' written in its place (r41) and the noun takes the whole relative clause of having as its own recasting (r46 closing), headed on 'familiar' since the ASV never writes 'spirit' alone for 'ob (r31). Where ba'alat is written (28.7 'eshet ba'alat-'ob → 'a woman that hath a familiar spirit') the 'that hath' is ba'alat's under r43's noun-of-possession clause and 'ob keeps 'a familiar spirit'. The competing allocation ('those that' to the article) is named in the notes.
- **1SA.28.20** — gam standing at a clause head with no waw (gam-koach lo-hayah bo → 'and there was no strength in him') takes the ASV's connective 'and' rather than leaving it added: a present additive particle is the morpheme the connective answers to (r11). Beside a waw rendered 'with' (28.23 wegam-ha'ishah → 'together with the woman') gam takes 'together' and the waw 'with'; the competing reading gives 'together with' whole to the waw.
- **1SA.28.25** — halak → 'went away' with no min or other directional morpheme in the clause: 'away' is marked added under r44's surplus-particle sentence, since 'went' writes the whole lexeme and the departure is stated nowhere in the Hebrew; qum → 'rose up' keeps its particle because rising is what the verb writes. The alternative (keeping 'away' with halak) is named in the note.
