# Ezekiel 26 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- מִשְׁטַח חֲרָמִים (vv. 5, 14) is divided with מִשְׁטָח taking the whole multi-word rendering 'a place for the spreading', headed on spreading (rule 31), and חֵרֶם taking 'of nets' (rule 19). The supplied 'place', 'for' and 'the' complete that one substantive's phrase and are not read as the construct relation, which the rectum's own 'of' already carries.
- In נָתַן + לִצְחִיחַ סָלַע (vv. 4, 14) the resultative לְ is its own unrepresented unit (rules 21, 40): נָתַן is present and rendered 'make', and the ASV writes the complement bare. The chain is rendered attributively, so צְחִיחַ takes 'bare' (rule 28) and סֶלַע takes 'a rock' with its indefinite article (rule 15).
- In the formula כִּי אֲנִי (יְהוָה) דִּבַּרְתִּי → 'for I [Jehovah] have spoken it' (vv. 5, 14) the object pronoun 'it' is added: no object morpheme stands in the clause, so under rule 44 it does not join the verb merely by standing inside its rendering.
- In the divine title אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה (vv. 3, 5, 7, 14, 15, 19, 21) the ASV's 'the' before 'Lord' is a supplied article belonging to the nominal expression (rule 15), and the frozen 1cs suffix of אֲדֹנָי is its own unrepresented unit (rule 22). BSB represents that suffix; the divergence is conventional and is the largest single block of BSB disagreements in this chapter.
