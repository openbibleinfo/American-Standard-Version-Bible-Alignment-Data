# Ezra 9 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **EZR.9.7** — The formula כְּ + הַיּוֹם הַזֶּה → 'as it is this day' (9.7, 9.15): כְּ takes 'as', 'it is' is one added unit headed on 'is' (rule 20's impersonal it), both articles are unrepresented and זֶה takes 'this'. Competing reading folds 'as it is' into the כְּ as one rendering; not taken, because the ASV writes the copula frame beside the preposition rather than inside it.
- **EZR.9.8** — פְּלֵיטָה heads on the word carrying the escape lexeme wherever the ASV writes one — 'a remnant to escape' (9.8) and 'any to escape' (9.14) on escape, 'a remnant that is escaped' (9.15) on escaped — and on remnant only in 9.13, where 'a remnant' is all that is written. The whole supplied phrase joins the noun's unit under rule 31; 'remnant' is the named competing head in 9.8 and 9.15 since 9.13 shows the ASV writing it alone for this word.
- **EZR.9.6** — לְ before a directional adverb rendered by a single English word fuses with it under rules 30 and 32 and heads on the adverb: לְמַעְלָה (with its directive he) → 'over' (9.6), לְמַטָּה → 'less' (9.13). The three-morpheme unit in 9.6 is one rendering and was reviewed for atomicity.
- **EZR.9.7** — בְּאַשְׁמָה → 'have been guilty' (9.7) fuses the preposition into the predicate noun's unit with the copula, because the ASV writes no preposition and the adjective carries the relation (rules 40/49 closing paragraphs); גְדֹלָה takes 'exceeding' alone. The same noun with a written 'in' — בְּאַשְׁמָתֵינוּ → 'in our guiltiness' (9.15) — splits as usual. Different English, not a split.
- **EZR.9.8** — Existential הָיְתָה with a supplied passive participle — 'grace hath been showed from Jehovah' — is taken whole as the verb's rendering under rule 21, headed on showed; the competing reading marks 'showed' added as a supplied lexical verb. Taken whole because a written הָיָה stands to carry the predicate, unlike rule 20's 'were given' where no verb is written at all.
