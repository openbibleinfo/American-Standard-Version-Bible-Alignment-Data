# 2 Chronicles 1 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The a of a thousand / a hundred belongs to the numeral, not to the counted noun. In 1.6 (a thousand burnt offerings), 1.14 (a thousand and four hundred chariots) and 1.17 (a hundred and fifty) the indefinite article is the English cardinal's own word and joins אֶלֶף / מֵאָה; rule 15's direction to put a supplied determiner on the noun the numeral counts governs a determiner of the noun (the fifteenth day) and does not reach an article that is part of the numeral's own English, as 1.14 shows where a ... chariots could not be read at all.
- הוֹצִיא מִן → brought out of gives out to the hiphil of יָצָא as part of its lexeme (the causing to go out) and of alone to מִן (1.17 twice, and 1.16 where the construct noun מוֹצָא is recast as were brought out). Do not read the corpus's מִן → out of precedent onto this pair: that precedent is the bare-מִן case with no verb of going out beside it.
