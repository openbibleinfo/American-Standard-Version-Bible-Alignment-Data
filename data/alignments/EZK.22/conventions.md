# Ezekiel 22 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Where the Hebrew writes a bare direct object, with no object marker and no preposition on it, and the ASV supplies an English preposition for the verb to reach that object, the preposition joins the verb's unit rather than the object noun's: הֵקַלּוּ -> *set light by* (22.7), תְּבַצְּעִי -> *greedily gained of* (22.12), חָמְסוּ -> *have done violence to* (22.26), טָחוּ -> *daubed ... with* (22.28), אֲבַקֵּשׁ -> *sought for* (22.30). This is rule 16's reasoning applied where no marker stands to take the word, and the head is unaffected. It does not touch rule 33: a function word supplied for an adverbial accusative stays with the noun whose case-function licenses it.
- The lexicalized 1cs suffix of אֲדֹנָי receives no separate English pronoun and is its own unrepresented unit throughout the chapter (22.3, 22.12, 22.19, 22.28, 22.31), the construct noun taking the supplied *the* under rule 29. BSB represents this suffix and so disagrees at all five, which is a convention difference and not an error.
- לָכֵן (22.19 twice) and עַל־כֵּן (22.4) are fused units under rule 30, each headed on the preposition as the morpheme carrying the causal relation. Heading them on כֵּן is defensible and is named in the unit notes; the chapter takes the preposition uniformly, and a later reader should not split the difference between the three occurrences.
