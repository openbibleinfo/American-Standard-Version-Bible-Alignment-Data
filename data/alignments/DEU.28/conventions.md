# Deuteronomy 28 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Fused multi-morpheme expressions in this chapter, each one unit under rule 30: ka-asher -> 'as' headed on the preposition (28.9, 28.29, 28.49, 28.63); tachat asher -> 'Because' / 'whereas' (28.47, 28.62); asher ... shammah -> 'whither' across intervening material (28.21, 28.37, 28.63); mi yitten -> 'Would it were' (28.67 twice); kol ha-yamim -> 'alway' (28.29, 28.33); ba-setter -> 'secretly' (28.57); le-maalah / le-mattah -> 'above' / 'beneath' (28.13); mippeney -> 'because of' (28.20) and 'of' (28.60); minneged -> 'before' (28.66); lifney -> 'before' (28.7 twice, 28.25 twice). The counter-case is the one to carry forward: milfaneykha at 28.31 is split three ways, because the ASV writes 'from before thy face' and so spends 'face' on paneh, and kol ha-yom at 28.32 is split three ways for the same reason, because the ASV writes 'all the day'. Read the subtraction test on each occurrence rather than the lemma.
- The 'again' of the hiphil of shuv is what that stem contributes and joins the verb's unit, discontinuous under rule 25, rather than being added: heshiv -> 'will bring ... again' at 28.60 and 28.68. Do not read it as a supplied English adverb.
- This chapter writes three English 'have's and they go three different ways. 28.40 has hayah present, so hayah takes the 'have' and its le is unrepresented (rule 43, first branch). 28.31 is verbless, so the possessive le takes the 'have' and its suffix takes the subject 'thou' (rule 43, second branch). 28.66's 'shalt have assurance' is a light verb plus complement rendering the hiphil of aman, headed on 'assurance' (rule 44), and is not possessive at all. Test each 'have' by subtraction before reaching for rule 43.
