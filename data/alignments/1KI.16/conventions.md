# 1 Kings 16 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **1KI.16.8** — The regnal-date formula repeats shanah — construct shenat plus the counted absolute — where the ASV writes 'year' once. The construct takes it, since it heads the phrase and bears the 'of PN' that follows, and the absolute is unrepresented (rule 22). English word order, which puts 'year' next to the absolute, is a real competing analysis, so every instance records this at medium confidence on the unrepresented unit: 16:8, 16:15, 16:23, 16:29.
