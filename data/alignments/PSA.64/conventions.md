# Psalms 64 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Where the ASV recasts an active clause as an English passive, the suffix whose referent becomes the English subject takes that subject pronoun (they), whether it is a verbal object suffix (64.8 יַּכְשִׁילוּהוּ → they shall be made to stumble) or a possessive suffix on the Hebrew subject noun (64.7 הָיוּ מַכּוֹתָם → shall they be wounded); the finite verb keeps only its auxiliaries and lexical verb, and the verb's own 3mp agent goes unexpressed rather than taking the pronoun under rule 17. Rules 18 and 40 read together.
