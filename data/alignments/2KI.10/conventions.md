# 2 Kings 10 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **2KI.10.23 — retrospective supersession, 2026-09-13** — For this verse's “only,” לְ + בַד remain one aligned unit headed on בַּד; the pronominal suffix is separately unrepresented because English writes no pronoun (current rules 30 and 40). The explicit construct-suffix qualification supersedes this convention's earlier mandatory three-morpheme fusion. The original convention and reconciliation remain historical evidence in `data/audits/retrospective-new-chapters-2026-09-12/parity-single-batch-2026-09-13-baselines/conventions.md` and the preserved verse/review baselines. The fresh authority adjudication is recorded in `parity-single-batch-2026-09-13-adjudication.json` in that audit directory. This scoped supersession does not reopen other verses or extend to other renderings.
- **2KI.10.12** — The place name Beth-eked (בֵּית עֵקֶד, two or three Np tokens) is read as a construct chain: the בֵּית token takes the supplied the together with any preposition or copula the clause supplies (was at the house 10.12; of the house 10.14), the עֵקֶד token takes shearing, and in 10.12 the הָרֹעִים token (article unsegmented) takes of the shepherds. The margin house of gathering is read and set aside (rule 38).
