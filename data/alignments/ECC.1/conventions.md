# Ecclesiastes 1 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Positive existential יֵשׁ is read as rule 50 reads the Aramaic אִיתַי: it takes whatever overt finite frame the ASV writes for it, a dummy *there* joins that unit as it does an אַיִן's, and the unit heads on the finite word rather than on the dummy. ECC.1.10 יֵשׁ דָּבָר → *Is there a thing* gives *Is there* to יֵשׁ, headed on *Is*. Rule 50 names אַיִן, אֶפֶס and אִיתַי but not the Hebrew positive particle, so this is recorded rather than inferred; the question is marked by inversion alone and no הֲ stands in the clause to claim the fronted word under rule 17.
