# Leviticus 14 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **LEV.14.13** — In technical offering expansions, the lexeme-bearing discriminator is the target head: trespass in trespass offering, sin in sin offering, burnt in burnt offering, and meal in meal offering; generic offering is not the display head.
- **LEV.14.22** — In the recurring hand-reaching ability idiom, the verb takes to get, hand takes the ability predicate, and the possessive suffix takes the supplied English subject when that subject is independently written; a continued subject already realized elsewhere leaves the suffix unrepresented.
- **LEV.14.3** — The contiguous expression min plus hutz plus lamed is fused when the ASV renders it by the single compound preposition out of; a preceding el left silent by the directional recast remains unrepresented.
