# Nehemiah 13 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- יוֹם הַשַּׁבָּת → 'the sabbath day' throughout the chapter (13.15, 17, 19, 22): the rectum's article takes the phrase's single fronted 'the', שַׁבָּת takes the attributive 'sabbath', יוֹם takes 'day'. The same fronting reads בֵּית הָאֱלֹהִים → 'the house of God' (13.7, 9, 11) and קֻרְבַּן הָעֵצִים → 'the wood offering' (13.31).
- Head choices where rule 31 ties on a two-word rendering of one substantive: מִנְחָה → 'meal offerings' heads on 'meal' (13.5, 9), תְּרוּמָה → 'heave offerings' on 'heave' (13.5), בִּכּוּרִים → 'first fruits' on 'first' (13.31), while תִּירוֹשׁ → 'new wine' heads on 'wine' (13.5, 12) because the ASV writes a bare 'wine' for יַיִן in 13.15 and 'new' is its specifier. The other reading is named in each unit's note.
- Hiphil בּוֹא rendered 'bring in' / 'brought in' / 'there should be brought in' keeps the particle 'in' inside the verb's unit (13.15, 16, 19); it is not added, since the hiphil's bringing-in is what the particle restates.
- The refrain זָכְרָה־לִּי אֱלֹהַי is read off the ASV each time: לִּ unrepresented where the ASV writes a bare 'Remember me' (13.14, 31), aligned to 'unto' where it writes 'Remember unto me' (13.22); the suffix takes 'me', אֱלֹהַ takes 'O God' with the vocative 'O' (rule 36), and the paragogic he is unrepresented.
