# Job 30 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Where the ASV recasts a pronominal suffix's clause as a relative clause and supplies a lexical antecedent noun for it, the noun and the relative both belong to the suffix's unit and neither is `added`; head on the noun carrying the referent. JOB.30.2 gives *Men ... whom* to the מוֹ of עָלֵימוֹ, discontinuous around the *in* that stays with עַל, and JOB.30.13 gives *men that* to the מוֹ of לָמוֹ while the possessive לְ keeps its *have*. Heading such a unit on the relative was the competing reading in both.
- This chapter's verb renderings head on the lexical verb wherever the second English word is a directional particle — *cast off*, *thrust aside*, *set forward*, *poured out*, *passed away*, *stand up*, *bindeth about*, *gathered together*, *lift up* — and on the second word only where it is a predicative complement carrying the Hebrew verb's own lexeme: JOB.30.10 heads רָחֲקוּ → *they stand aloof* on *aloof*, because *stand* alone names posture and *aloof* alone names the distance רָחַק means. The two shapes look alike in the dump and the line between them is rule 44's, not a difference of policy.
