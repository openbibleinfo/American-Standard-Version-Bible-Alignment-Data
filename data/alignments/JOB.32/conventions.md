# Job 32 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- A supplied English relative is `added` where it subordinates a finite clause to a written antecedent, and joins its neighbour's unit where it attaches an anarthrous participle to an antecedent written elsewhere. JOB.32.9 marks both cleft *that*s `added` over the finite יֶחְכָּמוּ and יָבִינוּ, and JOB.32.19 marks *which* `added` over the finite יִפָּתֵחַ after the written יַיִן, while JOB.32.12 keeps *that convinced* and *that answered* whole with their participles, the antecedent *none* being אַיִן's under rule 46's coordinated-substantive rider. What decides it is whether a participle or a finite verb follows, never that the ASV wrote a relative; JOB.32.9's units are a recorded correction from a reading that folded them into the substantival adjectives.
- לְיָמִים standing after a predicate adjective leaves both the preposition and יוֹם `unrepresented`: צָעִיר אֲנִי לְיָמִים → *I am young* (JOB.32.6) and זְקֵנִים־הֵמָּה מִמֶּנּוּ לְיָמִים → *they were elder than he* (JOB.32.4). Rule 40's fusion sentence does not reach either, because the explicit pronoun — and at JOB.32.4 the comparative phrase besides — stands between the adjective and the prepositional phrase, so the contiguity that sentence requires fails and rule 2 splits them, as it splits גָּדַל ... מִן → *exceeded*. BSB represents יָמִים at JOB.32.6; that is the convention difference rule 21 describes, not an error.
