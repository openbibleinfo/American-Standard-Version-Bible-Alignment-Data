# Judges 15 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- A supplied indefinite article in a numeral phrase goes to the word it stands before, and a numeral the ASV leaves with no English of its own is `unrepresented`. `אֶלֶף אִישׁ` → *a thousand men* (15.15, 15.16) gives *a thousand* to the numeral, which is the word the *a* determines, and *men* to `אִישׁ`; `לַפִּיד אֶחָד` → *a firebrand* (15.4) gives *a firebrand* to the noun under rule 15 and leaves the postposed `אֶחָד` nothing, no distributive *each* or *every* being written for it there. Rule 15's direction to read the noun of the expression rather than its leftmost word governs a supplied *the* before a numeral and its counted noun and does not reach these, where the ASV's one determiner stands before the numeral's own word in the first shape and before the noun's own word in the second. BSB represents the 15.4 numeral; the competing reading is named in that unit's note.
- At 15.19 the second element of `עֵין הַקּוֹרֵא` arrives as a single source token whose lemma field reads `הַ` while its surface is `הַקּוֹרֵא֙` and its morphology `Np`. It is aligned to the transliterated *hakkore* as an ordinary proper-name unit: the surface and morphology govern over the lemma field, as rule 39 directs for a gloss that contradicts them. Rule 29's spelled-into-the-name sentence is not what decides this — no separate article token stands here to be counted — so a later reader auditing lemma `הַ` occurrences should not read this one as an article left holding a name.
