# Genesis 37 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- In the repeated ויוספו עוד שנא construction (vv. 5, 8), יָסַף takes the finite subject and the comparative expression 'the more', while עוֹד takes 'yet'.
- In הֲ + infinitive absolute + finite-verb questions rendered with postpositive 'indeed' (vv. 8, 10), English question syntax realizes הֲ without a separate token, and the infinitive absolute takes 'indeed'.
