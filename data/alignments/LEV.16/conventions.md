# Leviticus 16 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- כָּל on a noun and a negative on its verb, rendered by a single *no*, share one discontinuous unit headed on the negative: כָל־אָדָם לֹא־יִהְיֶה → *there shall be no man* (16.17) and וְכָל־מְלָאכָה לֹא תַעֲשׂוּ → *shall do no manner of work* (16.29). The noun keeps its own English, including the supplied *manner of* that expands it under rule 31. Neither morpheme is marked unrepresented beside the other.
- The appositional לְ of לְכָל־חַטֹּאתָם takes the ASV's *even* (16.16, 16.21), since it is the morpheme standing on the phrase that word introduces. Where the ASV writes *even* before an appositive on which no marker and no preposition stands, the word is `added` (16.32, בִּגְדֵי הַקֹּדֶשׁ, the clause's one marker standing on אֶת־בִּגְדֵי הַבָּד). Count the ASV's *even*s against the morphemes standing on the appositive rather than against the markers elsewhere in the clause.
- The English *make atonement* reaches its object through *for*, and that word goes to whichever morpheme stands on the object: an אֵת marker takes it (16.20 once, 16.33 three times) and the markers for which the ASV writes no second *for* stay unrepresented (16.20 twice); an עַל or בַּעַד standing there takes it instead (16.6, 16.11, 16.16, 16.17, 16.24, 16.30, 16.33, 16.34). Count the ASV's *for*s against the marked objects, one per marker.
- In אֶל־מִבֵּית לַפָּרֹכֶת (16.2, 16.12, 16.15) and אֶל־מִחוּץ לַמַּחֲנֶה (16.27) the מִן and its construct noun fuse to the single word *within* / *without* under rule 30, and both the governing אֶל and the following לְ are unrepresented under rule 40, because the one English word states the whole relation. This accounts for eight of the chapter's BSB status disagreements.
- Where the nomen rectum is rendered as a prenominal attributive under rule 28 — הַבָּד → *linen*, הַקֹּדֶשׁ → *holy* — the article on that rectum takes the phrase's single written *the* and the construct noun takes none: בִּגְדֵי הַבָּד → *the linen garments* (16.23, 16.32), בִּגְדֵי הַקֹּדֶשׁ → *the holy garments* (16.32), מִקְדַּשׁ הַקֹּדֶשׁ → *the holy sanctuary* (16.33). This is rule 29's anarthrous-rectum fronting, and it is what distinguishes those phrases from כְּתֹנֶת בַּד קֹדֶשׁ → *the holy linen coat* (16.4), where no article morpheme stands and the construct takes a supplied *the*.
