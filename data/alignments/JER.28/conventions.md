# Jeremiah 28 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **JER.28.3** — In the repeated בְּעוֹד שְׁנָתַיִם יָמִים construction rendered 'within two full years,' בְּ takes 'within,' עוֹד is unrepresented, the dual noun takes 'two years' headed on 'years,' and יָמִים takes 'full.'
