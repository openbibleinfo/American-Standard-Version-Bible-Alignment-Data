# Psalms 56 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- כָּל־הַיּוֹם → 'all the day long' (vv. 1, 2, 5): 'long' joins יוֹם as 'day long', headed on day (rule 31; corpus 'day long' head=day ×8); כֹּל takes only 'all' and the article its 'the'. Do not give 'long' to כֹּל or mark it added.
