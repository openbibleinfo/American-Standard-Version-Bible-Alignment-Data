# 2 Samuel 1 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- naar → 'young man' / 'young men' heads on the class noun (man/men) throughout the chapter (1.5, 1.6, 1.13, 1.15), reading rule 31's tie by the ASV's own use of 'man' alone for words of the same class; 'young' is the alternative head and is named in each note.
- ey mi-zeh ('from where') is read as rule 22's ey-zeh compound with the min between them: where the ASV writes 'From whence' (1.3) the min takes 'From' as its own unit and ey+zeh share the discontinuous 'whence', headed on ey; where the ASV writes only 'Whence' (1.13) all three morphemes share that one word, still headed on ey, and as the verbless clause's predicate the unit also takes the copula 'art' under rule 20.
