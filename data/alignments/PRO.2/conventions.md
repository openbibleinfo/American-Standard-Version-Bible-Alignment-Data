# Proverbs 2 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **PRO.2.4** — An English function word another rule has already assigned to a morpheme's unit — here the *for* rule 44 gives `תַּחְפְּשֶׂנָּה` to reach its bare object suffix — is given to that same morpheme again where the ASV repeats it over an elliptical parallel member: *search* ***for*** *her as* ***for*** *hid treasures* is one discontinuous verb unit, and each comparative `כְּ` keeps only the *as* written for it. Do not read the repeated word as a second word for the particle standing beside it.
- **PRO.2.12** — `דֶּרֶךְ רָע` is tagged Ncbsa + Aamsa by MACULA — an absolute noun with an absolute modifier — so its supplied *the* is rule 15's determiner on the noun and its supplied *of* is rule 33's word for what the bare nominal contributes, not rules 29 and 19. The visually parallel `דַרְכֵי־חֹשֶׁךְ` (2.13) and `דֶרֶךְ טוֹבִים` (2.20) are construct chains and take rules 29 and 19. The morphology, not the English, decides which of the two a phrase is.
- **PRO.2.7** — The supplied *He* opening the second colon is `added`. The only morpheme in the verse denoting that referent is `יִצְפֹּן`'s own person and number, which rule 17 has already realized as the first *He*; rule 25's distributed reading of a resumed subject rests on a written pronoun, noun or name, and a finite verb's inflection standing under a pronoun of its own is not one. The supplied *is* beside it is different — that is rule 20's construction-supplied copula and joins the nominal predicate `מָגֵן`.
- **PRO.2.7** — `תּוּשִׁיָּה` → *sound wisdom* heads on *wisdom*. Rule 31's subtraction test ties here, and the tie is broken by the chapter's own usage: the ASV writes a bare *wisdom* for `חׇכְמָה` at 2.2, 2.6 and 2.10, which makes *wisdom* the class noun and *sound* the ASV's specifier. `תַּהְפֻּכוֹת` → *perverse things* (2.12) is the other case and heads on *perverse*, because *things* is a generic the ASV never writes alone for that word.
