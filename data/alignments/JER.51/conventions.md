# Jeremiah 51 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The WLC and the ASV divide 51:21/22 differently: the chariot clause of WLC 51.21b (wə-nippaṣtî bəkā rekeb wə-rōkəbô) is printed by the ASV at the head of 51:22. Rule 10 forbids reaching into a neighbouring verse, so the eight source tokens s009-s016 are each unrepresented in 51.21 and the fifteen English tokens t001-t015 are one added unit with a null target head in 51.22. Nothing is missing from either verse; do not re-align one against the other, and read the eleven BSB disagreements clustered in 51.21 as this offset rather than as candidates.
- Where an article morpheme stands in a phrase for which the ASV writes a possessive or another determiner instead of *the* — ba-mməṣādôt → *in their strongholds* (51.30), ba-ššānāh → *one year* and *another year* (51.46), ka-ttannîn → *like a monster* (51.34) — the article is unrepresented under rule 29, since rule 15 makes the article's English *the* and nothing else, and the supplied *their*, *one*, *another* or *a* joins the noun's own unit and is never added.
- hāyāh + lə is not uniform in this chapter, and the difference is the ASV's rather than the Hebrew's: the preposition is unrepresented under rule 21 at 51.37 (*shall become heaps*), 51.41 (*is become a desolation*) and 51.43 (*are become a desolation*), but at 51.30 the ASV writes *are become as women* and the lə takes that *as*. Read what the ASV spends on the preposition before reaching for rule 21.
- mē-ʾên → *without* is one fused unit headed on the negation, at 51.29 and 51.37 alike (rules 30, 50).
