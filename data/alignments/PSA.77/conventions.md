# Psalms 77 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **PSA.77.8** — Second-colon question auxiliary: where the second colon of a double question carries its own particle (אִם in 77.9) that particle takes the rewritten auxiliary (Hath); where the second colon is asyndetic (77.8 גָּמַר אֹמֶר) the rewritten auxiliary (Doth) is the first הֲ's distributed occurrence under rule 25, headed on the first, and is not given to the colon's verb.
