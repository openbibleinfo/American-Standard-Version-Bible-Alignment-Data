# 1 Samuel 23 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **1SA.23.27** — The lengthened-imperative ending (מַהֲרָה, לֵכָה, הַגִּישָׁה) is unrepresented whether MACULA tags it Sh (23.9) or Sd (23.27): it carries no motion or place, so rule 32's Sd sentence has nothing for it to take. English the ASV writes beside such an imperative — 'hither' in 'Bring hither', 'thee' in 'Haste thee' — belongs to the verb's own rendering under rule 44.
- **1SA.23.15** — The toponyms חֹרְשָׁה (15, 16, 18, 19) and יְשִׁימוֹן (19, 24) are rendered by the ASV as common nouns ('the wood', 'the desert') with the margin's 'Horesh'/'Jeshimon' set aside (rule 38). The name takes the common noun; an article morpheme, assimilated or written, takes the ASV's 'the' (rules 12, 29), and the bare form in 23.16 takes the supplied 'into the' as an accusative of direction (rules 33, 15).
