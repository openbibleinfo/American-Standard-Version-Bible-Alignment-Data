# 1 Corinthians 14 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- An ASV repetition whose source tokens are all spent is `added` under rule 103 — *is* *for* *a* *sign* at 14:22, where εἰσιν, εἰς and σημεῖον are spent on the first clause, and *a* *God* at 14:33, where θεός is spent on the subject. A word repeated inside a fixed English idiom rendering a single source token is not: `καθ᾽ ἕνα` → *one by one* at 14:31 keeps both occurrences of *one* on ἕνα as one discontinuous unit under rule 15. The test is rule 103's own — whether the licensing token's English expression is still incomplete — and never the bare recurrence of the word.
- A conditional particle and `μή` are joined into one `noncompositional` unit only where the ASV writes a single English word for them. `ἐὰν μή` → *unless* is one unit at 14:6 and 14:9, and `ἐκτὸς εἰ μή` → *except* at 14:5 takes the governing adverb into the same unit, no separate English word standing for it. Where the ASV writes two words the pair splits and each particle takes its own: `ἐὰν … μή` → *if* … *no* at 14:28.
- Where the ASV writes a complex prepositional frame once over a coordinated series of expressed prepositions, the frame rides undivided with the first under rule 67 and each later preposition takes only the bare word the ASV wrote for it. 14:6 gives *by way of* to the first `ἐν`, headed on *by* with the corpus units that head *by reason of* on the prepositional word, and a bare *of* to each of the three following `ἐν`. Heading the frame on its supplied noun remains defensible under rule 67's divided case for a proper preposition.
