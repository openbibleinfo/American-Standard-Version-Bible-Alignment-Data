# Ezekiel 11 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- An independent הִיא with no noun written for it takes the ASV's whole supplied expression *this city* (11.3, 11.7, 11.11), headed on *city*: the pronoun names a referent and the italic *city* is what the ASV writes for that referent, so it is not `added` under rule 30's *no morpheme licenses it at all*. Where the referent's noun is written (11.15 הָאָרֶץ), the pronoun takes only the determiner *this* and the article is `unrepresented` under rule 41's limit. The competing reading, which leaves the pronoun a bare *this* and marks *city* `added`, is named in each verse's notes.
- The Sp1cs suffix of אֲדֹנָי is `unrepresented` in all six occurrences of the divine title (11.7, 11.8, 11.13, 11.16, 11.17, 11.21): the ASV writes *the Lord*, never *my Lord*, and where a *the* is written it is the construct noun's supplied one under rule 29 — 11.13's vocative *Ah Lord Jehovah* writes none, so אֲדֹנָי there is bare. BSB represents this suffix, and the six-token disagreement is convention rather than error.
- A resumptive element inside an אֲשֶׁר clause that the ASV renders with a single *where* is `unrepresented` rather than fused into the relative: שָׁם at 11.16 and בָּהֶם with its suffix at 11.17, on rule 22's first sentence, since *where* is the ordinary rendering of אֲשֶׁר by itself. Where the ASV does write a word for the resumptive preposition it takes it — 11.15's לָהֶם gives *unto* to the preposition beside *whom* on the relative, and only the suffix goes unrepresented. The corpus contains a contrary precedent, 1KI.17.19 fusing a non-contiguous אֲשֶׁר … שָׁם into one *where* unit under rule 35, which is why no rule was promoted; each verse's notes name it.
- In the covenant and predicate formulas built on הָיָה לְ (11.11, 11.16, 11.20), the written הָיָה carries the copula under rules 17 and 21, so the predicative לְ before the predicate noun is `unrepresented` and rule 49's resultative frame has nothing left to attach; a dative or possessive לְ in the same clause is likewise `unrepresented` where the ASV recasts it as a bare possessive (*your caldron*, *my people*, *their God*), its suffix taking the possessive under rule 40, while the one the ASV spells out (*to them*, 11.16) goes to the preposition under rule 11.
- Directive he is read off the ASV and not off the token: fused with its host into one English word at 11.1 קָדִימָה → *eastward*, 11.18 שָׁמָּה → *thither* and 11.22 מִלְמָעְלָה → *above* (rules 30, 32), but its own aligned unit at 11.24 כַשְׂדִּימָה, where the ASV writes *into* for it and *to* for the following אֶל. None of the four is treated as a paragogic he.
