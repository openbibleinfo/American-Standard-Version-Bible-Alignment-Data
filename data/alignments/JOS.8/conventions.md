# Joshua 8 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Where kol carries a pronominal suffix (kullekem, kullam) and the ASV writes one subject pronoun for the finite verb of that clause and nothing but all for the quantifier, the pronoun is the finite verb's under rule 17 and the suffix is the unrendered occurrence under rule 22: JOS.8.4 be ye all ready and JOS.8.24 they were all fallen both put the pronoun on the verb. Rule 19's of-plus-pronoun unit needs a written of, and its bare-pronoun sentence governs a pronoun written in the quantifier phrase's own place; rule 18's subject-pronoun case reaches a suffix on an infinitive construct, which is why JOS.8.24's until they were consumed keeps its they on tummam. BSB represents both suffixes, and that is the documented convention difference rather than evidence against this reading.
- Which morpheme bears a presentative there was is decided by which side of the Hebrew clause is the predicate, not by the English frame: a prepositional predicate takes it (JOS.8.11 benayw, there was ... between, rule 49), a predicative participle takes it while the preposition beside it keeps its own word (JOS.8.14 orev lo, there was an ambush against him, rule 20), and a finite verb takes it wherever one stands (JOS.8.17 nishar, there was not a man left, rule 17). Read the clause before placing the dummy; the three shapes recur across the chapter and are not a split.
