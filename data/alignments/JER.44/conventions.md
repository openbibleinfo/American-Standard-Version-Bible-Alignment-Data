# Jeremiah 44 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- In the repeated resultative predicates, when ASV writes no preposition and supplies a/an before the predicate noun, leave לְ unrepresented and attach the indefinite article to the noun.
- In מְלֶכֶת הַשָּׁמַיִם rendered 'the queen of heaven,' the rectum article takes the fronted 'the,' the construct noun takes 'queen,' and the rectum noun takes 'of heaven.'
