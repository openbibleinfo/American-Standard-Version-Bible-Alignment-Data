# Luke 16 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Substantival article whose complement is a predicate adjective: the article takes the English antecedent pronoun and the relativizer together, and the adjective takes the supplied copula and its own word. Ὁ πιστός -> 'He that' + 'is faithful' and ὁ ... ἄδικος -> 'he that' + 'is unrighteous' at 16:10, τῷ ἀλλοτρίῳ -> 'that which' + 'is another's' at 16:12, and τὸ ἐν ἀνθρώποις ὑψηλόν -> 'that which' + 'is exalted' at 16:15 are one analysis. An adjective has no verb of its own to carry the relativizer, so rule 32's pronoun-only split, which is scoped to a participle, does not reach it; rule 31's verbless-modifier branch and rule 22's τῷ -> 'that which' do. Every participial article in the chapter still splits under rule 32.
- At 16:16 the ASV's 'the gospel of the kingdom of God is preached' renders εὐαγγελίζεται with a supplied nominal. The whole discontinuous frame 'the gospel of ... is preached' is folded onto the verb under rule 103's licensing test — no source token has spent that English, and the ASV's own margin 'Or, good tidings' shows it renders the εὐαγγελ- element — headed on the verbal core under rule 66. The 'of' inside that frame is licensed by the supplied 'gospel' and stays in the verb's unit under rule 21's frame clause; it is not a case preposition for ἡ βασιλεία, which is nominative and has no case for it to realize, so the article keeps a bare 'the' and βασιλεία a bare 'kingdom'.
