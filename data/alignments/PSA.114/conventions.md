# Psalms 114 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **PSA.114.5** — The ASV's second *that* (before *thou turnest back*) is the one written כִּי's distributed realization over the asyndetic second colon, in which מַה־לְּךָ and כִּי are both gapped (rules 25, 55); it is not `added`. Verse 6, which repeats the same frame with no כִּי in its own Hebrew, marks its *that* `added` under rules 10 and 55. The two verses are one judgment, not a split.
- **PSA.114.4** — גְּבָעוֹת → *The little hills* / *Ye little hills* (vv.4, 6): *little* is the ASV's specifier distinguishing hills from the mountains of the first colon, on a word the ASV writes bare elsewhere; it joins the noun's unit and is not `added`, and the head stays on *hills* (rule 31). The vocative *Ye* of v.6 and the supplied *The* of v.4 are likewise the noun's (rules 15, 36), with the article of הֶהָרִים unrepresented in the vocative verses.
