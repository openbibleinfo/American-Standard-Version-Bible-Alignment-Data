# Ecclesiastes 8 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- A לְ plus pronominal suffix whose relation the ASV's recasting leaves unstated is read under rule 40 whatever the case of the English pronoun: the preposition is its own unrepresented unit and the suffix takes the word written for it, objective at 8.7 (tell him) and 8.15 (given him) and possessive at 8.9 (to his hurt) and 8.12 (prolong his days). Rule 49's fused genitive was read and set aside, because it governs a לְ that is itself a verbless clause's predicate, and because his is the word a bare suffix takes in any possessive context, so no English word is spent on the preposition's own relation.
- A presentative there is the ASV supplies joins the nominal that bears it where the Hebrew clause is verbless (8.9, עֵת → there is a time, rule 20), and is added where the clause writes its own predicate and the only existential morpheme in it is an אַיִן the ASV has already rendered by a negation word (8.16, where אֵין takes the neither of neither ... nor under rule 14 and the suffix takes the that under rule 18). What decides it is whether the clause has a unit to bear the frame, not that the English words are the same.
