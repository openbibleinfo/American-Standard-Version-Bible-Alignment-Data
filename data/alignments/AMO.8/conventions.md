# Amos 8 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **AMO.8.1** — In the repeated title אֲדֹנָי יהוה rendered 'the Lord Jehovah,' the supplied definite article belongs to the construct noun, and the first-person suffix is unrepresented because the ASV writes no possessive pronoun.
