# Judges 10 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Where the ASV renders a Hebrew proper name or a bare gentilic by an English plural gentilic carrying a determiner no article morpheme licenses — `מִצְרַיִם` → *the Egyptians*, `עֲמָלֵק` → *the Amalekites*, `מָעוֹן` → *the Maonites*, `צִידֹנִים` → *The Sidonians*, `פְּלִשְׁתִּים` → *the Philistines* — the supplied determiner joins that noun's own unit under rule 15 and is never `added`, exactly as a supplied *a* or *the* on a common noun is. Where an article morpheme does stand on the gentilic it takes the written *the* first, as `הָאֱמֹרִי` → *the Amorites* does in 10.8 and 10.11. JDG.10.6, 10.7, 10.11 and 10.12 all read that way, and the chapter records no gentilic determiner marked `added`.
