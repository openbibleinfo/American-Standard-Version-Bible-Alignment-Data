# 1 Chronicles 22 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- An article on הָאֱלֹהִים standing in apposition to the rectum יְהוָה of a construct chain (בֵּית יְהוָה הָאֱלֹהִים 22.1, מִקְדַּשׁ יְהוָה הָאֱלֹהִים 22.19) is not the rectum's article and does not take the phrase's fronted the under rule 29: the ASV writes God bare, so the article is unrepresented and the construct noun takes a supplied the. Where the article stands on the rectum itself (בֵּית הָאֱלֹהִים 22.2, כְלֵי קֹדֶשׁ הָאֱלֹהִים 22.19) rule 29's fronting case governs and the article takes the the while the construct is bare.
- מִסָּבִיב at 22.9 remains one fused unit with מִן for round about, headed on round, as previously recorded. At 22.18, the scoped retrospective judgment supersedes the former fusion of on every side: on is separate from every side under rules 2 and 11, because the ASV separately expresses the relation and domain. This amendment is limited to 22.18.
