# Psalms 49 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The refrain נִמְשַׁל כַּבְּהֵמוֹת נִדְמוּ (vv. 12, 20): the Niphal of מָשַׁל and the contiguous comparative כַּ it governs share one unit on the ASV's 'He is like' / 'Is like' under rule 30, since the one word 'like' carries both the verb's lexeme and the kaph's comparison; source head on the verb, target head on 'like'. The assimilated article takes 'the', בְּהֵמוֹת takes 'beasts', the supplied relative 'that' is added (rules 46, 55) because the antecedent is written and נִדְמוּ is a juxtaposed finite clause, and the verb keeps 'perish'.
- Impersonal 3mp verbs the ASV supplies a subject for — יִרְצוּ 'men approve' (v. 13), יוֹדֻךָ 'men praise thee' (v. 18) — leave 'men' as its own added unit under rule 17; the verb takes only its lexical verb and any object suffix takes its own pronoun.
- A temporal בְּ on a noun with a possessive suffix that the ASV recasts as a finite clause — בְמוֹתוֹ 'when he dieth' (v. 17), בְּחַיָּיו 'while he lived' (v. 18) — gives the subordinator to the preposition (rule 52), the English verb to the noun, and the subject pronoun to the suffix (rule 40).
