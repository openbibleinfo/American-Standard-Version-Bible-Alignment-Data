# Luke 3 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Genealogy, 3.23-3.38: each `τοῦ` + name link is split under rule 102 - the article takes the supplied *son* together with its determiner and the case *of*, headed on *son*, and the name stands bare. Rule 52's whole-expression-on-the-name reading was declined because an article stands here to take the supplied nominal, and rule 103 does not make the repeated *son* `added`, the article's own phrase being incomplete without it. The chain's last link, `τοῦ θεοῦ` at 3.38, is read identically though its member is a noun and not a name.
- An English preposition standing over an accusative object stays inside the verb's unit throughout the chapter, under rule 106's accusative arm: `μηδένα διασείσητε` -> *Extort from no man by violence* at 3.14 keeps *from*, and `εὐηγγελίζετο τὸν λαόν` -> *preached he good tidings unto the people* at 3.18 keeps *unto*, the article taking only *the* and the noun standing bare. Giving *unto the* to the article under rule 21 is the competing reading and was declined because an accusative has no case for an English preposition to realize. A dative or genitive object is the contrast and keeps its own case preposition, as `τῷ Ἀβραάμ` -> *unto Abraham* does at 3.8.
- Two form-ambiguous articles are read under rule 57 as governing their head noun across an interposed genitive rather than as standing before the name that follows them: `τῆς Ἰτουραίας καὶ Τραχωνίτιδος χώρας` at 3.1 gives *of the* to `τῆς` and leaves `χώρας` bare, and `τὸν Ζαχαρίου υἱόν` at 3.2 gives *the* to `τόν` and leaves `υἱόν` bare. The three province articles of 3.1 (`τῆς Ἰουδαίας`, `τῆς Γαλιλαίας`, `τῆς Ἀβιληνῆς`) are the contrast: no determiner is written for their nouns, so they are `unrepresented` under rule 21's determinerless clause and each *of* stays with its noun.
