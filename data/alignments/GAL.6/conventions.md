# Galatians 6 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **GAL.6.10** — A substantival article whose complement is an adjective (no participle, no prepositional phrase) is settled, not contested: the article takes the pronoun where none is expressed, plus the relativizer, and the adjective takes the supplied copula with its own English. Record it high. GAL.6.1 hoi/pneumatikoi, GAL.6.10 to/agathon and tous/oikeious were aligned this way by three sessions that could not see each other but recorded three different confidence patterns; rule 53 forecloses giving the copula to the article, and EPH.3.8, 2CO.13.7, 3JN.1.11, JHN.2.10, JHN.8.7 and REV.3.7 are unanimous at high.
