# Exodus 39 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **EXO.39.7** — In arthrous construct phrases, the segmented article takes only the, while the relational of belongs to the nomen rectum; a fronted the stays with a present article.
- **EXO.39.4** — Where כָּתֵף is rendered shoulder pieces, shoulder is the lexical target head and pieces is the generic completion.
- **EXO.39.20** — In מִמּוּל פָּנִים rendered in the forepart, the initial preposition takes in, מוּל takes the forepart, and פָּנִים is unrepresented.
