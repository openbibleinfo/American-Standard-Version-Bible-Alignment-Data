# 2 Samuel 17 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **2SA.17.28** — Verse boundary: the ASV opens 17.28 with 'brought', which renders הִגִּישׁוּ standing in the middle of 17.29. Handled under rule 54: 'brought' is one added unit with a null target head in 17.28, and הִגִּישׁוּ is its own unrepresented unit in 17.29. Nothing is to be hunted for across the boundary.
- **2SA.17.11** — Superseded on 2026-09-12 after independent TS07 confirmations for 2SA.17.11, 2SA.17.17 and 2SA.17.27: the earlier whole-name grouping of Beer sheba, En rogel and Lo debar does not apply. Under current Hebrew rules 2 and 30, these names have independently recoverable component renderings: 2SA.17.11 gives s013 → t016 (Beer) and s014 → t017 (sheba); 2SA.17.17 gives s007 → t008 (En) and s008 → t009 (rogel); 2SA.17.27 gives s021 → t032 (Lo) and s022 → t033 (debar). Each correspondence is its own aligned singleton unit, headed on its sole source and target tokens. The shared place-name reference does not defeat component ownership; token count alone is not the reason for splitting.
- **2SA.17.18** — A waw opening a verbless possessive or naming clause that the ASV recasts as a relative clause (וְלוֹ בְאֵר → 'who had a well', וּשְׁמוֹ יִתְרָא → 'whose name was Ithra') is unrepresented: the relative pronoun realizes the suffix under rule 18, and rule 17's waw-takes-which reading governs only a clause with a finite verb. Both verses name the competing reading in their notes.
