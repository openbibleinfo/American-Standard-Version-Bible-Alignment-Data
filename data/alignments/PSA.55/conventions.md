# Psalms 55 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The cleft frame running across vv12–13 (it was not an enemy that ... it was thou ...) is read clause by clause: where the clefted clause carries a finite verb (v12, יְחָרְפֵנִי / הִגְדִּיל) the dummy-plus-copula and the cleft relative are added; where it is verbless (v13, אַתָּה אֱנוֹשׁ) the copula joins the predicate noun and only the dummy it is added (rule 20).
- A bare prepositional or nominal attributive the ASV recasts as a relative clause keeps the whole recasting on the source morpheme it renders: v18 לִי on battle → that was against (relative, copula and preposition on the לְ, rules 46/49), v20 שְׁלֹמָיו → such as were at peace with (on the noun, rule 46 closing paragraph). The relative is not marked added; rule 55's added subordinator is reserved for a relative over a finite clause the Hebrew writes (v18 that strove, which is added together with its supplied verb).
