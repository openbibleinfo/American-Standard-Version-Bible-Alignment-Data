# 2 Chronicles 16 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- עַד־לְ before a noun or adverb of degree rendered by a single English degree expression fuses as one unit under rules 30 and 40, headed on the final lexeme-bearing morpheme, with a construction copula joining the unit where the phrase is the clause's predicate: 16.12 עַד־לְמַעְלָה חָלְיוֹ → his disease was exceeding great (target head exceeding; great is the competing head), 16.14 עַד־לִמְאֹד → very. Neither עַד nor לְ is marked unrepresented beside the fused word.
