# Psalms 123 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **PSA.123.3** — שָׂבַע with a bare accusative complement (בוּז, v3 and v4): the ASV's 'with' is the verb's valency preposition and joins the verb's unit headed on 'filled' (rules 33/44); the noun keeps only its own word. In v4 the second 'with' over the juxtaposed second complement is the same verb's distributed rendering under rule 25, and the ASV's 'And' between the two complements is added under rule 14 since no waw stands there.
