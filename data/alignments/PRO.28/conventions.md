# Proverbs 28 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Supplied English *have*: place it by what the clause writes, running rule 43's subtraction. Where a finite verb stands and the ASV renders it as *have* plus a complement carrying its lexeme, the whole phrase is that verb's one light-verb unit under rule 44 and heads on the complement — 28.19 יִשְׂבַּע → *shall have plenty of* and *shall have ... enough*. Where a written noun is recast as a head noun and a relative clause of having, the *have* is that noun's under rule 43's nominal-recasting paragraph — 28.22 אִישׁ רַע עָיִן → *He that hath an evil eye*, headed on *hath*. Where the clause writes neither, and its predicate adjective already holds its own English, the word answers to no morpheme and is `added` — 28.27 רַב מְאֵרוֹת → *shall have many a curse*, with *shall ... many* on רַב and *have* `added`.
- The relational *of* or *with* the ASV writes between a predicate and the nominal completing it is placed by MACULA's morphology, not by the English shape. Where the head is tagged construct and the nominal is its nomen rectum, the word is the chain's own relation and belongs to the rectum under rule 19: 28.17 דַם־נָפֶשׁ → *the blood of any person*, 28.20 רַב־בְּרָכוֹת → *shall abound with blessings*, 28.21 פַּת־לֶחֶם → *a piece of bread*, 28.25 רְחַב־נֶפֶשׁ → *of a greedy spirit*. Where the nominal is absolute and the head a verb or participle reaching it, the word answers to the English predicate's valency and joins the predicate's unit under rule 44: 28.7 רֹעֶה זוֹלְלִים → *a companion of gluttons*, 28.19 יִשְׂבַּע־לָחֶם → *plenty of bread*, 28.21 הַכֵּר־פָּנִים → *respect of persons*, 28.23 מַחֲלִיק לָשׁוֹן → *flattereth with the tongue*.
- A possessive the ASV supplies with no suffix in the phrase is `added` under rule 18's closing sentence wherever it determines a noun the Hebrew writes — 28.2 *the state thereof*, 28.6 and 28.18 *in his ways*, 28.16 *shall prolong his days*. It is not `added` where it stands inside a single morpheme's own fixed English rendering on a noun the Hebrew does not write: 28.25 בוֹטֵחַ → *he that putteth his trust*, where *trust* is the light-verb complement carrying the participle's lexeme under rule 44 and the *his* is what the English formula requires, exactly as rule 30 takes the pronouns of נָא's *I beseech thee* from the particle. Read whether a Hebrew noun stands under the possessive before marking it.
