# Numbers 22 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- yasap hiphil with a complementary infinitive (22.15, 22.19, 22.25, 22.26): the finite yasap takes only the repetition word the ASV writes for it - yet, again, further, more - and the infinitive keeps the lexical verb, each morpheme taking the word written for it under rule 2. A clause auxiliary goes with the finite form (22.19, will ... more); a subject pronoun supplied for the recast English clause goes with the infinitive that carries the English verb (22.25, he smote ... again), as rules 18 and 48 keep a supplied infinitival subject with its infinitive.
- A fused preposition plus a nominal preposition is headed on the second morpheme in this chapter - mimmul -> over against (22.5), le-neged -> before (22.32) - read as the element carrying content under rule 23's first sentence rather than as the pure function pair its me-'al example governs. This matches the chapter's heading of mippene, li-pne, liqra't and me-'eber on their construct nouns. Both units name the competing head in their notes, and no target edge depends on the choice.
- A compound proper name arriving as two source tokens is aligned token by token rather than fused: qiryat huzot -> Kiriath | huzoth (22.39), with the supplied unto of the directional accusative going to the first token under rule 33. Where the ASV translates such a name instead of transliterating it, the pair is read as construct plus rectum: bamot ba'al -> the high places of Baal (22.41), bamot taking into the high places (rules 31, 33) and ba'al the of Baal (rule 19). The ASV's margin, Or, Bamoth-baal, corroborates the translated reading without deciding it, by rule 7.
- zeh in the counting formula zeh shalosh regalim takes the plural these the ASV writes for it (22.28, 22.32, 22.33). The singular demonstrative is not left unrepresented and the plural determiner is not added, since no other morpheme in the phrase answers to it; the numeral and regalim keep three and times.
