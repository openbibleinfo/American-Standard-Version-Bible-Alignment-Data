# Jeremiah 31 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **JER.31.10** — Split the chapter's two-word remoteness renderings morpheme by morpheme: מן takes 'of' or postposed 'off', while רחוק/מרחק takes 'old' or 'afar'; do not fuse the pair when both English pieces remain independently meaningful.
