# Proverbs 17 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The chapter places the English 'hath' four different ways and each is licensed by what stands in its own colon, so a later reader should not read the set as a split: PRO.17.20a leaves it added, because the colon writes only 'iqqesh-lev, a construct chain of quality, with no hayah, no possessive preposition, no suffix and no verb the word renders; PRO.17.20b gives it to the bet that makes its clause possessive; PRO.17.8 gives it to the noun of possession ba'al, whose own lexeme it names; and PRO.17.21 and PRO.17.27 give it to the verb the ASV has itself rendered by it. Run rule 43's subtraction colon by colon rather than reading the chapter's other placements across.
- Where a Proverbs colon is recast as a comparison, the ASV's italic 'as' joins the predicate or nominal it construes (rule 33) and any supplied 'when' beside it is its own added unit (rule 55): PRO.17.14 gives 'as' to the participial predicate poter and leaves 'when' added, PRO.17.8 gives 'as' to the predicate nominal 'even, and PRO.17.28 gives it to the predicate participle navon. The two words are placed separately because the Hebrew writes no conjunction for the second; the whole-expression reading of 'as when' is named in PRO.17.14's own note.
- A preposition on a construct noun is fused in one verse of this chapter and split in two others, and rule 30's condition is what decides it rather than the lemma: PRO.17.14's lifne is one word 'before' and fuses, while PRO.17.18 writes 'in' for the preposition and 'the presence' for panim and PRO.17.24 writes 'before' for 'et and 'the face' for pene, so each morpheme has a rendering of its own and rule 2 splits them. Count the ASV's words against the morphemes before reading the three as inconsistent.
