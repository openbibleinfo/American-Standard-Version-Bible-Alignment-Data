# Acts 18 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- τε is decided by rule 70's count of the English conjunctions the ASV wrote in the stretch, and the clause-seam 'and' counts. ACT.18.4 ἔπειθέν τε Ἰουδαίους καὶ Ἕλληνας → 'and persuaded Jews and Greeks' has two, so τε takes the seam 'and' introducing its clause and καί takes the one between the nouns, as rule 70's ἐλάλει τε καὶ συνεζήτει gives an 'and' to each; ACT.18.5 ὅ τε Σιλᾶς καὶ ὁ Τιμόθεος → 'Silas and Timothy' has one, so that word is the καί's and τε is unrepresented; ACT.18.26 οὗτός τε has no answering καί and takes the 'and' written for it. Do not read the internal 'and' alone as 'the one English conjunction for the pair' and then mark the seam 'and' added under rule 98: a τε standing in that seam is the token it answers to.
- ἀρχισυνάγωγος → 'ruler of the synagogue' is one unit headed on 'ruler' at ACT.18.8 and ACT.18.17: the compound denotes a person, 'ruler' is the word naming that referent, and 'synagogue' stands in a supplied prepositional complement rather than as the phrase's referring noun. Heading on 'synagogue' by analogy with rule 66's ἀρχιερεύς → 'chief priest' on 'priest' remains defensible and is the competing reading; the analogy is imperfect because there the second element names the person and here it does not.
- A periphrastic εἰμί + participle is split by the English the ASV actually wrote, not joined into one unit. ACT.18.25 ἦν κατηχημένος → 'had been instructed' gives 'had been' to the copula and 'instructed' to the participle; ACT.18.7 ἦν συνομοροῦσα → 'joined hard' gives the whole English to the participle and leaves ἦν its own unrepresented unit, no English word answering to it separately. Rule 2 is what prefers the split and rule 94's count-of-English-words test is the shape of it. Joining the pair into one unit remains defensible, and a reference comparison will report the unrepresented copula as a disagreement.
