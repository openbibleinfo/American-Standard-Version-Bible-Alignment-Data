# Psalms 71 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The supplied 'Thou' before 'who' is placed by whether an antecedent nominal stands earlier in the same verse. PSA.71.19 gives it to the vocative אֱלֹהִים that precedes אֲשֶׁר as a rule-25 resumption; PSA.71.20 opens with אֲשֶׁר and no antecedent in the verse, so 'Thou' joins the relative's unit under rule 46's first rider. The two verses are one analysis read across the verse boundary of rule 10, not a split.
