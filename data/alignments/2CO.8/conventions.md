# 2 Corinthians 8 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **2CO.8.19** — A complex supplied English prepositional frame ('in the matter of', 'in regard of', 'by reason of', 'being a supply for') rides undivided with the token that would carry the plain case preposition: the Greek preposition where one is expressed (8.14 eis, 8.19 syn, 8.20 en, 8.23 hyper), the article where the article takes the English determiner (8.4 ten -> 'in regard of this'), and the noun where neither does (8.22 pepoithesei -> 'by reason of the confidence'). Head stays on the token's own primary English realization, so 'in' for en/syn and 'for' for eis, not on the supplied frame noun. The corpus is unanimous on the locus and split on the head: 'in regard of'/'in furtherance of'/'in behalf of' head the frame noun at EPH.5.32, PHP.1.5, PHP.2.22 and EPH.3.1, while 2CO.7.4, 2CO.7.14 and 2CO.8.24 head 'on behalf' on 'on'. This chapter follows rule 17's lexical-anchor test and 2 Corinthians' own practice; 8.19 and 8.20 stay medium because the majority corpus reading differs. 2CO.8.21 is not an exception: enopion's own lexeme is 'sight', so 'in the sight' heads there on 'sight'.
