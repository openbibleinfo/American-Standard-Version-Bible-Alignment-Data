# 1 Kings 15 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **1KI.15.15** — In v15 the ASV follows the ketiv וקדשו ("and his holy things"), while the projected tokens are the qere וְקָדְשֵׁי, a construct plural with no suffix. So "the things that himself had dedicated" faces s007+s008 with no morpheme for the reflexive possessor: keep "himself" inside that unit as a licensed helper rather than marking it added. The ASV also attaches בֵּית יְהוָה to the verb ("brought into the house of Jehovah") instead of to קָדְשֵׁי.
