# Deuteronomy 21 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- וְהָיָה frame: the frame's complementizer is the 'that' the ASV writes at the head of the APODOSIS, not the first 'that' in the verse. DEU.21.3 writes it contiguously ('it shall be that the city ... shall take'); DEU.21.16 writes it after a full temporal clause whose own 'that' belongs to the infinitive construct of בְּיוֹם הַנְחִילוֹ under rule 52, so the frame's 'that' is the one before 'he may not make'; DEU.21.14 writes none and there is nothing to place. Count the ASV's 'that's against the clauses that license them before assigning any of them, and do not mark the apodosis one added.
- אֵינֶנּוּ שֹׁמֵעַ בְּקוֹל (DEU.21.18, DEU.21.20): אַיִן keeps the negation and heads on it (rule 50); the participle keeps 'will ... obey', the auxiliary staying with the predicate because the ASV writes the negation and the auxiliary as two words (rule 35); the בְּ is unrepresented, its object promoted to a bare direct object of 'obey' (rule 40); and the suffix takes whichever pronoun the ASV's recasting writes for it — the relative 'that' where the clause is recast as a relative modifying בֵּן (rule 18), the referential 'he' where the clause stands on its own. The two verses differ only in what the ASV wrote, and a present pronominal morpheme takes that word before any supplied-relative analysis (rule 11).
