# Job 34 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Status of rule-30 fusions in this chapter: a multi-morpheme fusion is recorded `aligned` wherever a rule fixes its source head — the morpheme carrying the rendering's lexeme (לָכֵן → *Therefore* on כֵּן, 34.10 and 34.25), the negation (rules 35 and 48: וְלֹא → *Neither* and *Nor*, 34.12 and 34.19; וְאֵין → *nor*, 34.22; לֹא בְ → *without*, 34.20 and 34.35 twice), the construct noun of a preposition-plus-construct compound (rule 23: לִפְנֵי → *more than*, 34.19), or the governing first preposition of a compound preposition (rule 23: מֵעִם → *as … wilt*, 34.33). It is recorded `noncompositional` only where no morpheme's own lexeme is uniquely written in the single English rendering and rule 6's deterministic first-token anchor is the sole basis for the source head: חֲצוֹת לָיְלָה → *midnight* (34.20) and אֲשֶׁר עַל־כֵּן → *Because* (34.27). The corpus records both statuses for one and the same fusion, so this is a recording convention for readers of this chapter and not a claim that the aligned fusions are divisible.
- Head of a preposition unit that holds a recast clause's complementizer: this chapter heads it on the subordinating word, whatever else the unit also carries. The privative מִן takes *That … not* (34.30 s001) and *That there be none* (34.30 s005) and heads on *That* in both, as it heads on *that* at 34.10 where it holds the complementizer alone; the purpose לְ takes *So that* (34.28) and heads on *that*. A waw rendered by a coordinator and a subordinator written together still heads on the coordinator under rule 55 (*so that* at 34.25, *And when* at 34.29), so the two shapes are headed on opposite words and the difference is the morpheme, not the English.
- Presentative negative frame in a privative clause: where a privative מִן is recast as a negative existential clause and no אַיִן or other negative morpheme stands in it, the whole supplied frame — *there be none* at 34.30 — joins the מִן unit, on the reasoning by which rule 50 gives the dummy and copula to the morpheme carrying the negation and rule 48 gives that negation to the מִן. Compare 34.22, where a written אַיִן takes *There is no* itself. Do not mark *there be* `added`: the negation is the preposition's own contribution and the frame is what English writes around it.
