# Matthew 14 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- `κατ᾽ ἰδίαν` is one `noncompositional` unit under rule 45, taking the whole English *apart* and headed on `ἰδίαν`. Neither the preposition nor the possessive adjective is separately recoverable in that single word, and rule 47 does not reach it because no head noun is governed. Read 14:13 and 14:23 alike.
- An English phrasal particle that answers to no Greek preverb is a rule 7 helper riding inside its verb's own unit, and never heads it - exactly as rule 106's preverb particle does. So *up* of `ἦραν` → *took up* at 14:12 and 14:20, `αἴρω` being uncompounded; *out* of `ἔκραξαν`/`ἔκραξεν` → *cried out* at 14:26 and 14:30; *over* of `περισσεῦον` → *remained over* at 14:20; and *down* of `ἀνακλιθῆναι` → *to sit down* at 14:19, where the preverb `ἀνα-` is not what *down* glosses. Do not mark such a particle `added`, and say in the note either that the verb is uncompounded or that the particle does not gloss its preverb.
- Where the Greek copula is **expressed**, the ASV's supplied expletive *it* is that copula's own rule 7 helper and rule 68's supplied-subject clause does not arise, rule 68 speaking of a verbless clause: `ἐστιν` → *It is* at 14:26, `εἰμι` → *it is* at 14:27, `εἶ` → *it be* at 14:28, and `ἔξεστίν` → *It is … lawful* at 14:4. An expressed copula likewise keeps its subject pronoun (`ἦν` → *he was* at 14:23, `εἶ` → *thou art* at 14:33) and leaves an expressed predicate adjective bare (14:15, 14:23, 14:24). Do not hand the supplied *it* to the predicate noun.
- At 14:17 the ASV renders `Οὐκ ἔχομεν … εἰ μή` as *We have here but*, writing no English negative word at all. The whole *but* answers to the `εἰ μή` pair as one `noncompositional` unit headed on `εἰ` under rule 39, and `οὐκ` is left its own `unrepresented` unit under rule 23. Rules 41 and 59 are not the licence, `εἰ μή` being a particle cluster rather than a negative indefinite. A reference comparison will report the empty `οὐκ` as a disagreement, and that report is not a correction.
