# 2 Chronicles 25 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Appositional *even* / *to wit* before a phrase the Hebrew sets in apposition: where a לְ stands on the appositive phrase (25.5 לְכָל־יְהוּדָה → *even all Judah*; 25.10 לַגְּדוּד → *to wit the army*) that לְ takes the word under rule 11's priority of a present function morpheme, and the parallel לְ the ASV leaves without a second *under* is unrepresented. Where nothing stands on the appositive (25.7 *to wit* with the second *with* going to the single עִם under rule 25; 25.19 *even thou* where אַתָּה already holds *thou*) the word is added under rule 16. The competing reading in 25.5 and 25.10 — לְ unrepresented and the word added — is named in each unit's note.
