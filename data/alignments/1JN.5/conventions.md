# 1 John 5 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **1JN.5.4** — The ASV's supplied appositional *even* is a rule 7 helper on the source token that opens the appositive, never mechanically `added` (rule 11), and is headed on that token's own English word and never on *even* (rule 17). The chapter folds all four uniformly: 5.4 ἡμῶν -> *even our* with the article displaced under rule 20, exactly as 2CO.13.9's τὴν ὑμῶν κατάρτισιν -> *even your* + *perfecting* reads unhedged; 5.6 Ἰησοῦς -> *even Jesus* (JHN.5.45, EPH.4.15); 5.13 τοῖς -> *even unto you* (JHN.11.27, JUD.1.4, REV.8.9); 5.20 ἐν -> *even in* (GAL.1.15, GAL.5.14). The host is whichever token realizes the appositive's first English word, and a displaced article is never the host. Recorded medium, not high: the NT corpus is genuinely divided, roughly 21 folded against 19 `added`, so this is not promoted to the contract. 1JN.2's convention makes such an *even* `added` wherever it stands; its reasoning is article-specific and does not reach a name, a case-marked article or a preposition.
- **1JN.5.7** — A rule 37 `added` unit carrying displaced English takes a non-null target head, on the displaced clause's lexical predicate: 5.7 heads on *witness* and 5.8 on *witness*. Rule 5 permits a null head and rule 37 does not choose, but rule 37's own three named precedents all head non-null (PHP.2.8 *found*, EPH.2.15 *flesh*/*enmity*, REV.2.27 *received*), and rule 17's *bear witness* clause fixes the word. The moved English stays one unit per verse, as 1JN.2.13 keeps its displaced sentence whole; rule 2's smallest-unit preference has no source side to split against here. Corpus-wide this is 11 episodes to 3 in favour of a non-null head, the three null episodes giving no reasoning, but 30 finished units still read the other way, so it is not promoted to the contract.
