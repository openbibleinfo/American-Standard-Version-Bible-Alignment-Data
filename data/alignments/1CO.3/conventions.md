# 1 Corinthians 3 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Supplied copula in a verbless clause whose predicate is a genitive the ASV renders by an English possessive: the subject's class decides, and only a lexical noun sends the copula to the predicate. 3:22 `πάντα ὑμῶν` → *all are* on the quantifier and a bare *yours* on `ὑμῶν`; 3:23 `ὑμεῖς … Χριστοῦ` → *ye are* on the pronoun and a bare *Christ's*; 3:23's second clause `Χριστὸς … θεοῦ` has the lexical-noun subject, so rule 63 holds and *is God's* goes to the predicate.
- Rule 67 leaves the head of a complex frame open where the carrier is a proper preposition; this chapter takes it on the prepositional word. `κατὰ ἄνθρωπον` → *after the manner of* heads on *after* at 3:3, matching the bare `κατά` → *according to* on *according* at 3:8 and 3:10. A head on *manner* remains defensible in the corpus.
