# Ezekiel 38 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The divine title 'adoNAI YHWH -> *the Lord Jehovah*: no article morpheme stands in the title, so the ASV's supplied *the* belongs to 'adoNAI as the nominal expression it determines (rule 15); the title's Sp1cs suffix is its own `unrepresented` unit, the ASV spending no separate word on it; and YHWH takes *Jehovah*. So read in EZK.38.3, 10, 14, 17, 18, 21. BSB attaches the suffix, which is the documented convention difference behind the Sp1cs x 6 line in the validation report and not a correction.
- In ba-YOM ha-HU' -> *in that day* and ba-yaMIM ha-HEM -> *in those days*, the written demonstrative pronoun hu'/hem is what the ASV's single determiner realizes, and BOTH article morphemes are `unrepresented`: the article assimilated into the pointed preposition and the agreement article standing on the demonstrative. Rule 41's limit - a determiner goes to the article only where no other morpheme licenses it - is met here by the written pronoun, exactly as a pronominal suffix meets it there, and rule 29's closing sentence then disposes of the articles as it does for he-'aRIM ha-'ELleh -> *these cities*. So read in EZK.38.10, 14, 17, 18, 19. This is the chapter's largest single source of Td disagreement with BSB, which represents the articles.
- la-BEtah -> *securely* meets rule 30's one-English-word condition: the preposition and the noun share one unit, headed on the lexeme-bearing noun, rather than the le- being marked `unrepresented` beside it. Dropping the English word diminishes only the expression as a whole and no morpheme owns a part of it. So read in EZK.38.8, 11, 14.
- EZK.38.9's 'oTAKH, which MACULA tags To, is read as the comitative 'et (H854) and takes the ASV's *with*. The chapter writes the same formula three times - we-'ammim rabbim 'itTAKH / 'oTAKH, *and many peoples with thee* - and vv. 6 and 15 carry the R-tagged preposition, so the tag is what varies and not the construction. Rule 16's subtraction test reaches the same result independently, since dropping *with* leaves the suffix holding *thee* and nothing else in the clause diminished, and the corpus records To-tagged 'et aligned to *with* (EZK.2.6). Do not read the To tag alone as forcing `unrepresented` where the ASV has written a comitative word.
