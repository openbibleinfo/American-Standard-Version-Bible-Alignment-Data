# Psalms 44 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- כָל־הַיּוֹם → all the day long (v8, v15, v22): כל takes all, the article takes the, and the supplied long joins יוֹם as the completion of its phrase under rule 31, headed on day; it is not added and not given to כל. Corpus agrees (11 'day long' units on יום).
