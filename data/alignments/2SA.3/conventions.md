# 2 Samuel 3 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Oath formula koh ya'aseh ... wekhoh yosif -> 'God do so ... and more also' (3.9, 3.35): the second koh takes also, yasaf takes more, and a dative lo the ASV writes nothing for is unrepresented with its suffix. The alternative (second koh unrepresented, also added) is named in each unit note.
- Fused ki im is headed on ki, the first of a pair of particles (rule 23), whatever the ASV wrote for it: 3.13 except and 3.35 if both head on ki, with im named as the competing head. A bare ki in the same oath formula takes if by itself (3.9).
- MACULA splits Ish-bosheth (3.8, 3.14, 3.15) and Asahel (3.27) into two Np tokens. Where the ASV writes the name in two tokens (Ish bosheth) each source token is its own unit and a chain's of goes with the first; where it writes one word (Asahel) the two tokens fuse under rule 30. The single-token Asahel of 3.30 is ordinary.
