# Matthew 25 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- εὐθέως at the end of MAT.25.15 is a rule 37 versification split between the projection and the ASV: the token stands in 25:15 and its English is the opening 'Straightway' of 25:16. The token is its own `unrepresented` unit in 25:15 and 'Straightway' is `added` in 25:16, each naming the other verse. Do not stretch a nearby English phrase over the token to avoid an empty target. A reference comparison reports it as a disagreement and that report is not a correction.
- ἐφ᾽ ὅσον → 'Inasmuch as' (25:40, 25:45) is one `aligned` unit, source head on ὅσον and target head on 'Inasmuch'. Rule 94's precondition is met by its spelt-apart-lexeme clause: 'inasmuch as' is one fixed English clause-introducer whose parts answer to both tokens, 'in-' to the preposition and 'as much as' to the correlative. Do not split it across the two and do not reach for `noncompositional`.
- In the verbless relative ὁ τὰ δύο (τάλαντα) at 25:17 and 25:22 the ASV supplies the lexical verb 'received'. Under rules 62 and 96 it rides with the predicate nominal — δύο where no noun is expressed, τάλαντα where one is — discontinuously under rule 15, and never with the determiner-carrying article; it is not `added`. Where the participle λαβών or εἰληφώς is expressed (25:16, 25:18, 25:20, 25:24) nothing is supplied and rule 32's ordinary split applies.
- ποτίζω rendered 'gave … drink' (25:35, 25:37, 25:42) is one `aligned` unit headed on 'drink' under rule 91: 'drink' glosses the lexeme and 'gave' is the empty support verb. Any supplied object rides inside the unit under rule 74's contrast, discontinuously under rule 15.
- τῶν ἐλαχίστων is analysed differently at 25:40 and 25:45 because the ASV differs, not because the chapter split. 25:40 writes a second 'these' for the article, which takes it under rule 60; 25:45 writes only one, already spent on τούτων, so its article is `unrepresented` under rules 38 and 23. The appositional 'even*' at 25:40 is its own `added` unit under rule 105's substantival-article arm, the article keeping only its own determiner and nothing else.
