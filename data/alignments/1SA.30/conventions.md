# 1 Samuel 30 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **1SA.30.19** — Negative ranges 'neither X nor Y': where the range opens with מִן (מִן־הַקָּטֹן וְעַד־הַגָּדוֹל, מִשָּׁלָל וְעַד), the מִן takes the opening 'neither' as rule 14's מִן takes 'both', the waw takes 'nor', and the עַד is unrepresented; a waw standing before such a מִן is unrepresented. Where no מִן opens the member (וְעַד־בָּנִים וּבָנוֹת), the waw takes the 'neither' itself and the עַד is unrepresented. 30.2 'both small and great' follows the same division with 'both'/'and'.
- **1SA.30.27** — The recipient list of 30.27–31 (לַאֲשֶׁר בְּ + place): לְ → 'to', אֲשֶׁר → 'them that' headed on 'that' (rule 46 rider), בְּ → 'were in' headed on 'in' (rule 49), the place name its own unit. Two-token toponyms (בֵּית אֵל, בּוֹר עָשָׁן) are aligned token by token to 'Beth'/'el', 'Bor'/'ashan'; the second token of רָמוֹת נֶגֶב takes 'of the South' as a construct rectum.
- **1SA.30.10** — פִּגְּרוּ מֵעֲבֹר / מִלֶּכֶת recast as 'were so faint that they could not go over / follow': the verb takes 'were faint', the privative מִן takes 'so … that … not' headed on 'that' (rule 48), and the infinitive takes 'they could' with the lexical verb; in 30.21 the infinitive and its אַחֲרֵי fuse as 'follow' (rule 30).
- **1SA.30.3** — A narrative clause the ASV opens 'And when …' (30.3, 30.12, 30.16, 30.21, 30.26) gives both words to the waw of that clause, headed on the coordinator (rule 55; 'when' is the corpus's competing head), and the apodosis waw that follows is unrepresented where the ASV writes no connective for it. The same 'that' at an apodosis head after וַיְהִי goes to the apodosis waw (30.1, 30.25, rule 45).
- **1SA.30.7** — The lengthened imperatives הַגִּישָׁה and הִשָּׁבְעָה carry a he MACULA tags Sh (paragogic), not Sd: it is unrepresented under rule 32, and the 'hither'/'thither' of 'bring … hither', 'brought thither' joins the Hiphil of נָגַשׁ as the particle completing 'bring near' (rule 44). BSB represents the he; this is a tagging-driven convention difference.
- **1SA.30.24** — In both scoped *his share ... that* constructions, the substantival article owns the discontinuous *his ... that* frame, headed on *his*, under current rule 41. Both independent deferred-investigation judgments retain each participle's existing predicate rendering and copular allocation, transferring only the nominalizing possessive from the participle to the article. This supersedes the former application of rules 19 and 25 to possessive ownership at these two occurrences only; the possessives remain represented, all out-of-scope units are retained, and the original convention is archived in the deferred-investigation audit.
