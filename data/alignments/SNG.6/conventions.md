# Song of Solomon 6 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **SNG.6.9** — Read the two occurrences of אֶחָד as uniqueness renderings: 'but one' belongs whole to the first numeral, headed on 'one' (GEN.27.38; ISA.51.2), rather than treating italic 'but' as added. In 'the only one' of her mother, retain 'only' as the contextual uniqueness anchor and record 'one' as the competing numerical head; the differing English realizations do not require identical heads.
