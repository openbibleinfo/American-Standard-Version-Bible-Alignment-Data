# John 16 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Where the ASV supplies an English preposition before an articular accusative and no Greek preposition is present, which side takes it is decided by whose government supplies it, not by adjacency. *ask … of the Father* (JHN.16.23) gives *of the* to the article, following JHN.15.16 on the identical clause, and JHN.8.27 and EPH.2.15 do the same for an *of*-phrase that stands on its own. *she is delivered of the child* (JHN.16.21) gives *of* to the verb, because the ASV has recast an active `γεννάω` + direct object into a passive idiom whose *of* is the English verb's own government — as EPH.1.15 *having heard of*, EPH.3.2 *ye have heard of*, JHN.4.35 *look on*, TIT.2.13 *looking for* and REV.18.9 *they look upon* all do. The two verses were examined together and left apart deliberately; the corpus supports both sides and neither reading generalizes, so nothing is promoted.
- The ASV renders bare dative *παρρησίᾳ* and *ἐν παρρησίᾳ* alike as *plainly* (JHN.16.25, JHN.16.29), so the preposition is realized nowhere separately and is its own unrepresented unit under rules 34/36 rather than paired with the noun. JHN.7.4 pairs the two tokens as one aligned unit for *openly*; the corpus is divided, so this is recorded for the chapter and not promoted.
