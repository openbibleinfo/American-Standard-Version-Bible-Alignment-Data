# Mark 4 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- κατά with an adverbial complement is decided by rule 93's test — what the corpus spends on the governed word — and the chapter divides on it rather than treating the two idioms alike. `κατὰ μόνας` → *alone* at 4:10 is split, `μόνος` taking *alone* in sixteen corpus units, so the preposition is pleonastic and `unrepresented` under rule 34; LUK.9.18's `κατὰ μόνας` → *apart* joins instead because *apart* is a sense the adverb alone never carries. `κατ᾽ ἰδίαν` → *privately* at 4:34 is one `noncompositional` unit under rule 45: the corpus spends an *own* word on `ἴδιος` in 54 of its 69 units and never *privately* for the bare adjective, and carries the phrase undivided in all nine of its units. Do not read the two verses as a chapter split.
- The distributive `ἕν` before each member of the thirtyfold/sixtyfold/hundredfold series is its own `unrepresented` unit at 4:8 and 4:20 alike: the ASV writes only the numeral, and the supplied *a* of *a hundredfold* is the numeral's own helper under rules 7 and 29 rather than the particle's English. The tokens are variously tagged PREP and A-NSN and their variant units are underdetermined between the ἕν and ἐν readings; the analysis does not turn on either, and no textual decision is made inside the alignment (rule 12). A reference comparison reports every such token as a disagreement, and that report is not a correction.
