# Malachi 3 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Recorded confidence: a construction the contract settles is recorded high, even where the aligner can name a road not taken. The piel of אשר rendered 'call happy' heads on 'happy' by rule 44's subtraction (MAL.3.12, MAL.3.15), and an anarthrous substantival adjective takes the ASV's supplied 'the' into its own unit by rule 29 (MAL.3.15 'the proud', MAL.3.18 'the righteous', 'the wicked'). Both were high in one verse and medium in another; both are now high.
- Waw rendering: the chapter aligns each waw to the English word the ASV actually writes for it — And, But, Yet, Then, therefore, for, that, neither — rather than defaulting to 'and' (rule 14).
