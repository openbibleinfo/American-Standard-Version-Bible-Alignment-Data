# Joshua 13 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Construct chain whose nomen rectum is a toponym carrying an article: count the ASV's *the*s for the whole phrase before placing them, and do not read the toponym's article as lexicalized in the name. Where a single *the* is written, it is that article's and the construct noun stands bare — אַשְׁדּוֹת הַפִּסְגָּה → *the slopes of Pisgah* (13.20), שֵׁבֶט הַלֵּוִי → *the tribe of Levi* (13.14, 13.33), חֲצִי הַשֵּׁבֶט → *the half tribe* (13.7), עָרֵי הַגִּלְעָד → *the cities of Gilead* (13.25). Where the ASV writes no *the* for the phrase at all, the article is `unrepresented` and nothing is supplied on the construct — *all Bashan* (13.11, 13.30), *half Gilead* (13.31), *king of Bashan* (13.30), *Ramath mizpeh* (13.26), *from Lebanon* (13.6). Where two *the*s are written, each morpheme takes the one standing before its own word — *the border of the Geshurites* (13.11), *the cities of the plain* (13.21), *the remnant of the Rephaim* (13.12). What decides which case a phrase is, is whether a *the* is written for the phrase, not whether one stands before the toponym's own word.
- The allotment formula וַיְהִי לָ+suffix הַגְּבוּל → *And their border was* divides four ways, and the same division holds wherever it recurs: הָיָה takes the copula (rule 43), the suffix takes the possessive *their* (rules 18, 40), the לְ is `unrepresented` because the ASV spends no word on its relation (rule 40), and the noun's own article is `unrepresented` because the possessive is the phrase's one written determiner and the suffix, not the article, is what licenses it (rule 41). JOS.13.16 and JOS.13.25 are the formula with the preposition; JOS.13.30 writes גְבוּלָם with the suffix on the noun itself and divides identically apart from having no לְ to silence.
