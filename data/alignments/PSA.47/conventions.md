# Psalms 47 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **PSA.47.9** — A bare appositional nominal that the ASV construes as a result complement and introduces with a supplied infinitival copular frame — עַם אֱלֹהֵי אַבְרָהָם → 'To be the people of the God of Abraham' (To be italic) — takes that frame into the noun's own unit under rule 33, on the terms that give a bare nominal its supplied 'for' or 'as', with the copula joining the predicate nominal under rule 20 and the head staying on the noun. No לְ stands in the phrase for rule 49 to place the frame on. The competing reading marks 'To be' one added unit; it was named in the note and not taken.
