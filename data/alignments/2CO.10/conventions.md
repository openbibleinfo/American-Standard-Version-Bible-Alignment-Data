# 2 Corinthians 10 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **2CO.10.13** — eis ta ametra (10.13, 10.15) is analysed the same way in both verses: eis takes 'beyond' (rule 36, which empties a preposition only where English realizes it nowhere), the substantival article takes the supplied possessive 'our' (rules 20, 49 -- no other source word competes for the determiner slot, and a realized nominative subject such as hemeis is not one), and ametra takes 'measure'. All three high: the ASV writes the identical string in both verses and no token competes for any of the three words.
- **2CO.10.2** — A supplied English comparative helper after hos -- 'if' at 10.2 and 10.9, 'though' at 10.14 -- is the particle's own helper under rules 7 and 11, never 'added'; the corpus settles this at high (hos -> 'as though' REV.5.6, REV.13.3; 'as it were' x14). Record high unless a rival SOURCE token could take the supplied word instead: at 10.9 the following an could, since JHN.16.23 aligns a bare an to a supplied 'If', so that verse alone keeps medium -- and the doubt sits on hos, which holds the contested word, while an -> 'would' is high (rule 50).
- **2CO.10.16** — The supplied clause juncture 'and' on ouk (10.16) is a helper under rules 7, 11 and 29, not 'added'. The NT corpus runs about 86 to 3 for the helper reading of a supplied connective with no Greek counterpart (REV.19.17, JHN.21.12, JHN.12.24, REV.19.8, REV.19.14). Kept medium because no corpus unit puts a supplied connective on a negative particle -- 0 of 422 ou units -- so the host is novel here even though the practice is not. Considered for promotion to the contract and declined: the corpus contradicts itself inside one book (REV.15.6 marks the same 'pure *and* bright' juncture 'added' where REV.19.8 and REV.19.14 make it a helper), and a rule settling that would strand finished units this pass cannot reopen.
