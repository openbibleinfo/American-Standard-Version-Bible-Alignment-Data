# Psalms 124 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **PSA.124.1** — The verbless לוּלֵי clause 'If it had not been Jehovah who was on our side' (vv.1–2) divides as: לוּלֵי → discontinuous 'If … not' (rule 25); the cleft 'it' is its own added unit (rule 20's verbless-cleft sentence); the construction-supplied copula 'had been' joins the name as the clause's anchor (rule 20); the written שֶׁ takes 'who'; הָיָה takes 'was'; the dative לְ takes 'on … side' headed on 'side' (rule 24) with 'our' on the suffix (rule 18). The competing reading — the whole 'If it had not been' as the particle's fixed formula under rule 30 — was named in the notes and not taken.
