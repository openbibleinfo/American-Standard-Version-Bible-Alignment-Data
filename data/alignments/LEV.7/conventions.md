# Leviticus 7 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- kol on the noun and lo on the verb, with one English negative word answering to the pair, are one discontinuous unit headed on the negative wherever the ASV spends a single word on them, whether that word stands alone (7.23, no fat) or before a generic the ASV supplies to expand the noun (7.26, no manner of blood). The generic and the chain's of belong to the noun, not to kol. This is rule 35's wekol-melakhah lo taasu case, and the chapter reads both of its occurrences that way after reconciliation.
- isheh is rendered by the whole phrase (an) offering made by fire, which is one rendering of the lexeme headed on fire (rule 31), never split across its words: 7.5 and 7.25 take it bare, with the supplied for at 7.5 licensed by the adverbial accusative (rule 33), and at 7.30 and 7.35 the plural construct ishshey YHWH takes the offerings ... made by fire discontinuously around the rectum, which keeps of Jehovah.
- In the entitlement formula lo yihyeh (7.7, 7.8, 7.9, 7.14, 7.33), hayah always takes the English shall be or shall have (rule 43) and the lamed is unrepresented unless the ASV writes a word for it, as the to of to himself at 7.8. The suffix takes an English subject pronoun only where the ASV writes one for it (he at 7.7); where the subject pronoun the ASV writes belongs to another morpheme - the substantival article's He that at 7.33 - or where the clause is recast as a genitive (the priest's at 7.9 and 7.14, which fuses the lamed with its noun under rule 49), the suffix is the unrendered resumptive under rule 22.
