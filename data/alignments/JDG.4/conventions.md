# Judges 4 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The compound toponym Harosheth-haggoyim arrives as two Np tokens. The first takes Harosheth and the second takes of the Gentiles as an ordinary nomen rectum under rule 19; the ASV margin Or, nations was read and set aside under rule 38. Applied at 4.2, 4.13 and 4.16.
- nus is the fleeing and the ASV's bare fled writes that lexeme already, so the away of fled away adds a direction the Hebrew does not write and is its own added unit under rule 44's subtraction test, not a phrasal particle folded into the verb. Applied at 4.15 and 4.17.
- An appositional even the ASV writes before a phrase no object marker stands on is added under rule 16's limit, whatever markers stand elsewhere in the clause: at 4.11 no marker stands in the phrase at all, and at 4.13 the clause's marker stands on kol rikhbo, which the ASV renders bare. It is not folded into the neighbouring min or into the appositive.
