# Song of Solomon 8 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **SNG.8.8** — Negative possession: in אֵין לָהּ, assign no to אֵין, hath to possessive לְ, and she to the suffix. This agrees with the opening positive possessive clause and the accepted GEN.11.30 split (no / had / she); the competing hath no existential unit obscures the preposition’s possession relation. Keep SNG.8.11 distinct because its written היה carries had. No contract promotion: corpus-wide uniformity was not established.
- **SNG.8.14** — Retain thou with the imperative דְמֵה and leave ethical לְ plus its suffix unrepresented. The competing suffix-subject analysis is defensible: GEN.27.43 assigns thou to the ethical suffix, whereas EZK.12.5 assigns it to the imperative. Record the local choice without promoting a rule over the divided corpus.
