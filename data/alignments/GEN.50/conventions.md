# Genesis 50 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **GEN.50.21** — When וְעַתָּה is rendered ‘Now therefore,’ the waw takes the inferential connective ‘therefore’ and the temporal adverb עַתָּה takes ‘Now’; the reverse allocation remains materially defensible but obscures the adverb's lexeme.
- **GEN.50.24** — A cognate infinitive absolute given its own ASV intensifier is aligned independently to that intensifier, while the finite cognate verb retains the lexical verb phrase; thus פָּקֹד takes ‘surely’ and הָשֵׁב takes ‘fully.’
