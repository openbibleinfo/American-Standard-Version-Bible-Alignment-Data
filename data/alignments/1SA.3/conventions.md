# 1 Samuel 3 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The presentative frame opened at 1SA.3.2 spans the verse boundary. וַיְהִי takes And it came to pass with no complementizer of its own; the circumstantial clauses of 3.2 and 3.3 stand inside the frame; and the waw opening 3.4 carries the frame's that under rule 45. Read 3.4's opening that against 3.2 rather than as a subordinator supplied for its own clause. The second frame, at 3.9, writes its apodosis waw that within the verse.
- הִנְנִי → Here am I (3.4, 3.5, 3.6, 3.8, 3.16) is read as a verbless clause with a written predicate: הִנֵּה is that predicate and takes Here together with the construction-supplied am (rule 20), while the suffix is the clause's subject and takes I (rules 18, 40). Do not give the copula to the suffix under rule 20's wholly-elided-predicate clause, and do not mark the am added.
- The Hiphil יָסַף before an infinitive is rendered adverbially throughout — yet (3.6), again (3.8, 3.21), more (3.17) — while the infinitive keeps the lexical verb (rule 56); שׁוּב before שְׁכָב is read the same way at 3.5 and 3.6. Where עוֹד also stands and the ASV writes two adverbs, as at 3.6 (called yet again), each morpheme takes one word, neither is fused or left unrepresented, and this chapter allocated them in the source order of the morphemes; the competing allocation is named in that unit's note under rule 8.
- A waw standing before a limit expression takes the ASV's even whether the relational word is written or supplied. 3.20's וְעַד־בְּאֵר שָׁבַע → even to Beer-sheba gives even to the waw and to to the עַד (rules 14, 16, 26), and 3.12's הָחֵל וְכַלֵּה → from the beginning even unto the end gives even to the waw while the supplied unto joins the infinitive absolute it stands before under rule 33.
- Where a partitive מִן stands before a כֹּל chain and the ASV writes a single of, that of is the preposition's and the construct relation takes none: 3.17's מִכָּל־הַדָּבָר → of all the things and 3.19's מִכָּל־דְּבָרָיו → none of his words both put the of on מִן, leaving the rectum only its own word. Rule 19's chain of is not written a second time, and at 3.19 the כֹּל has no separate word at all, being fused with לֹא into none under rule 35.
