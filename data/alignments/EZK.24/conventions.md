# Ezekiel 24 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Demonstrative date phrases (בְּעֶצֶם הַיּוֹם הַזֶּה at 24.2 twice, בַּיּוֹם הַהוּא at 24.26 and 24.27): the ASV writes one determiner for the phrase and it is the written demonstrative pronoun's — זֶה takes *this*, הוּא takes *that* — while both articles, the one on יוֹם and the agreement article on the demonstrative, are unrepresented (rule 41's limit, rule 22). This reads rule 41's 'only where no other morpheme licenses that determiner' of a written demonstrative pronoun and not only of a suffix. It is recorded here rather than promoted because the corpus is divided: four Td+Pp3ms units fuse the demonstrative's article with the pronoun as one noncompositional *that*, while a bare Pp3ms is aligned to *that* elsewhere. Six of the chapter's Td disagreements with BSB are this judgment.
- הָיָה לְ with a predicate noun (24.24 *shall Ezekiel be unto you a sign*, 24.27 *shalt thou be a sign unto them*): the written הָיָה takes the copula and the לְ before מוֹפֵת is unrepresented, because the ASV leaves *a sign* bare (rules 21, 40). Each clause holds two לְs; the single written *unto* belongs to the one on the person, not to the predicate complement. Both predicate לְs are BSB R disagreements.
