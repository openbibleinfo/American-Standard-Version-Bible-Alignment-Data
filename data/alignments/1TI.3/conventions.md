# 1 Timothy 3 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- ὡσαύτως rendered by the frame *in like manner* is headed on *like*, not on *manner* (3.8, 3.11). *Manner of* is the supplied frame under rule 99's shape and *like* carries the lexeme's similarity sense; the corpus heads this English string on *like* in ten of thirteen units, including 1TI.5.25 for this same lemma, and on *manner* in three (JUD.1.8, LUK.22.20, MRK.14.31), both ὡσαύτως units among those three conceding *like*.
- Impersonal δεῖ governing an infinitive takes the bare English modal alone, and the supplied subject the Greek leaves unexpressed rides with the infinitive under rule 74: 3.7 gives *must* to δεῖ and *he have* to ἔχειν, as 3.15 gives *ought* to δεῖ and *men to behave themselves* to ἀναστρέφεσθαι. The ASV's margin at 3.15 (*how thou oughtest to behave thyself*) shows the supplied word is the infinitive's subject.
- A compound adjective rendered by an English frame plus noun is headed on the word glossing the compound's own head element where the ASV glosses it, and on the glossed element where the frame swallows the head element: 3.3 ἀφιλάργυρον → *no lover of money* heads on *lover* (φιλ- glossed), 3.8 αἰσχροκερδεῖς → *greedy of filthy lucre* on *lucre* (κερδ- being the compound's head, *greedy of* the supplied frame), 3.2 φιλόξενον → *given to hospitality* on *hospitality* (φιλ- realized only by the frame, as TIT.1.8 and 1PE.4.9 have it), 3.2 διδακτικόν → *apt to teach* on *teach* (2TI.2.24), 3.8 διλόγους → *double tongued* on *tongued*. The corpus's one unit of αἰσχροκερδής, TIT.1.7, heads that identical English on *greedy* with no reasoning recorded; this chapter departs from it to stay consistent with its own 3.2.
