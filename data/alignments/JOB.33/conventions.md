# Job 33 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- A preposition standing on a construct noun that the ASV renders by one English relational expression is fused into a single noncompositional unit headed on the construct noun, with any pronominal suffix keeping its own English pronoun as a separate unit. The chapter reads lə + pānay -> 'before' (33.5) and kə + pî -> 'even as ... art' (33.6) alike; the construct noun is never marked unrepresented beside a preposition holding the whole rendering.
- Where a waw whose clause the ASV recasts as subordinate is rendered by the pair 'So that', the unit is headed on the coordinator 'So' (33.20, 33.26). Rule 55 leaves this head a genuine choice and the corpus heads such pairs both ways; the chapter took the coordinator throughout.
