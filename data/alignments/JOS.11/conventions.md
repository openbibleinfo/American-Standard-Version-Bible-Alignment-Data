# Joshua 11 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- הַר: rendered 'hill country' at 11.2, 11.3, 11.16 and 11.21, each unit headed on 'hill' because 'country' is the supplied generic and 'hill' is what loses the lexeme when dropped (rule 31); rendered 'mount' before a name at 11.17 (mount Halak, mount Hermon), where the article standing on it is unrepresented because the ASV writes no 'the', and הַר חֶרְמוֹן is rendered attributively under rule 28 rather than with an 'of'. Read what the ASV wrote before applying either.
- לְפִי־חֶרֶב → 'with the edge of the sword' is split three ways at 11.11, 11.12 and 11.14: לְ takes 'with', פֶּה takes 'the edge' with its supplied determiner, and חֶרֶב takes 'of the sword' with its own. Rule 30's fusion of לְפִי does not reach it, because the ASV spends a word on each morpheme and rule 2 splits wherever ownership is independently recoverable.
- 'even' beside a limit preposition: where a conjunction morpheme stands, the waw takes the 'even' and the preposition takes the relational word alone, so וְעַד־בַּעַל גָּד → 'even unto Baal gad' at 11.17 divides exactly as וְעַד מִשְׂרְפוֹת מַיִם → 'and unto Misrephoth maim' does twice at 11.8, the waw taking whichever connective the ASV wrote. Where no conjunction stands, the whole expansion is the preposition's: כַּחוֹל → 'even as the sand' at 11.4 keeps both words on the כְּ. See the amendment to rule 16.
- בֵּלֶת is the morpheme the ASV's negative realizes wherever it writes one, whatever preposition it stands on: 'none' at 11.8 after עַד, which keeps only 'until', and 'no' at 11.20 after the privative לְ, which keeps only the complementizer 'that' (rules 15, 48). Where the ASV instead writes the exceptive 'save', as at 11.19, the particle takes that word and there is no negation to place.
