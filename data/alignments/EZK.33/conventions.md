# Ezekiel 33 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- In the title `אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה` the ASV's *the Lord* is the noun `אֲדֹנָי` together with the supplied *the* that determines it (rule 15), the `Sp1cs` suffix is `unrepresented` because the ASV writes no possessive for it, and `יְהוִה` takes *Jehovah*; in a construct the rectum's *of* joins the same unit (*of the Lord*). Applied at 33.11, 33.17, 33.20, 33.25, 33.27. BSB represents the suffix, which is a convention difference and not an error.
- Where two particles are fused into one English word under rule 30, this chapter heads the unit on the morpheme whose sense that word most directly carries, and on the opening particle where neither morpheme does: `כָּל … לֹא` → *none* (33.13, 33.16) and `וְ … לֹא` → *neither* (33.12) head on the negative, as rule 35 heads *Nor*, while `אִם לֹא` → *surely* (33.27) heads on the oath particle and `כִּי אִם` → *but* (33.11) on `כִּי`. Every one of these units names the competing head in its note.
- Every rule 30 fusion in this chapter is filed `aligned`; the chapter records no `noncompositional` unit (33.12 *thereby*, 33.13 and 33.18 *therein*, 33.21 *twelfth*, 33.22 and 33.31 `לִפְנֵי` → *before*, 33.25 `לָכֵן` → *Wherefore*, 33.29 `עַל` → *because of*, 33.30 `מֵאֵת` → *from*, 33.33 `בְּתוֹךְ` → *among*). The corpus files `לִפְנֵי` → *before* both ways (143 aligned, 220 noncompositional), so this is a chapter-uniform choice rather than a correction of either.
- For rule 55's count this chapter reads *whereas* and *if* as two subordinating words rather than as the coordinator-plus-subordinator pair that rule exempts: `וְהוּא נִזְהָר` → *whereas if he had taken warning* (33.5) gives *whereas* to the waw and leaves the *if* `added`, and `וְרָאָה` → *if when he seeth* (33.3) gives *if* to the waw and leaves the *when* `added`. The pair reading, which would give both words to the waw and mark nothing `added`, is named in each unit's note and is the likelier place a later reader will disagree.
