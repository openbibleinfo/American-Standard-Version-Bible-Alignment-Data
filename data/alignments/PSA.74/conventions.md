# Psalms 74 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **PSA.74.9** — A simple 'how long' for עַד־מָה / עַד־מָתַי with no matrix frame is split morpheme by morpheme (rule 17): 'long' to עַד as the extent word, 'how' to the interrogative. Applied at 9 and 10; the competing noncompositional fusion is named in each note.
- **PSA.74.2** — Where a bare noun stands as predicate complement of a verb and the ASV writes a supplied 'to be' before it, the frame is placed on that noun (rules 20/33), never added: v. 2 'to be the tribe' on שֵׁבֶט, v. 14 'to be food' on מַאֲכָל.
- **PSA.74.3** — A supplied relative or subordinator is added under rule 55 where the Hebrew writes a finite clause asyndetically after a written antecedent (v. 2 'which' twice, v. 18 'that'), but stays with the morpheme where the ASV has recast a single morpheme into a clause: v. 3 הֵרַע → 'the evil that ... hath done' (headless clause after כָּל, rule 46) and v. 22 חֶרְפָּה → 'how ... reproacheth' (rule 46 closing).
- **PSA.74.1** — Every phrasal particle in the chapter (cast off, Lift up, set up, break down, burned up, drawest back, brakest in pieces, driedst up, rise up against) is read as part of the verb's rendering under rule 44 rather than a surplus, the head staying on the lexical verb; the notes at 6, 8 and 14 name the competing added/complement readings.
- **PSA.74.16** — Possessive לְ + suffix rendered by the single genitive 'thine' fuses with the copula ('is thine') under rule 49 closing, headed on the suffix; both cola of v. 16 are read alike.
