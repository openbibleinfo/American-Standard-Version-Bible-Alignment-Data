# 2 Kings 24 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **2KI.24.16** — Casus pendens with a marked fronted object resumed by waw + object suffix (ואת X ... ויביאם): where the ASV renders the fronted list bare and writes 'even them' at the resumption, the 'even' is the apodosis waw's under rule 14 and the object marker on the list is unrepresented; 'them' is the suffix's. The marker takes an 'even' only where the word introduces the marked phrase itself (rule 16).
- **2KI.24.3** — Impersonal 3fs היתה in the 'through/at the mouth of Jehovah it came upon Judah' clauses (vv3, 20): the English subject the ASV supplies — 'this' in v3, the dummy 'it' of the 'did it come to pass' frame in v20 — realizes the verb's own subject and joins the verb's unit under rules 17/21; it is not an added lexical subject.
