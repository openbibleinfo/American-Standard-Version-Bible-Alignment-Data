# Mark 16 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **MRK.16.6** — In the co-referential chain Ἰησοῦν … τὸν Ναζαρηνὸν τὸν ἐσταυρωμένον, the second attributive article has no English realization because the name is the expressed head; the participle carries ‘who hath been crucified.’
