# Daniel 3 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **DAN.3.13** — When ASV tokenizes Abed-nego as Abed and nego, align the two Aramaic source tokens separately; the proper name is not noncompositional because each component has its own English token.
- **DAN.3.5** — In the repeated temporal relative rendered what time, assign what to the written relative di and leave the determined-state ending unrepresented because ASV writes no separate article.
