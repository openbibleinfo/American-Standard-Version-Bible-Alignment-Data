# Luke 11 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- A second article standing between an expressed head noun and a participle is `unrepresented`, and the participle carries the supplied relativizer, under rule 31. This holds whether the head noun is arthrous — ἡ κοιλία ἡ βαστάσασα → *the womb that bare* at 11:27, οἱ ἄνθρωποι οἱ περιπατοῦντες → *the men that walk* at 11:44, τὸ αἷμα … τὸ ἐκκεχυμένον → *the blood which was shed* at 11:50 — or an anarthrous proper name, Ζαχαρίου τοῦ ἀπολομένου → *Zachariah who perished* at 11:51. Rule 90's single-relativizer allowance is scoped to a substantival article and does not reach this configuration; rule 31's anarthrous-head clause is scoped to a following prepositional phrase, where no verb exists to carry the relativizer.
- Where the ASV collapses a negator and a following non-negative pronominal into one English negative word, the two tokens are one `aligned` unit on that word under rule 39's test, even where the second token is a relative rather than the τις/τι rule 59 names: οὐκ … ὃ παραθήσω → *nothing* at 11:6, μὴ … τι → *no* at 11:36. Where no such second token stands, the negator keeps the whole English negative alone and the verb keeps its own word: μὴ εὑρίσκον → *finding none* at 11:24 gives *none* to μή, and σημεῖον οὐ δοθήσεται → *there shall no sign be given* at 11:29 gives *no* to οὐ, σημεῖον being a plain noun that rule 41 cannot empty a negator for.
