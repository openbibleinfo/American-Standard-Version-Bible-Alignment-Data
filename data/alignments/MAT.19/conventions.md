# Matthew 19 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Where the ASV recasts an oblique Greek pronoun as the subject of its English clause, the pronoun is aligned to that subject word rather than left unrepresented with the English word marked added: σου → *thou* in *sell that which thou hast* at 19:21, and ἡμῖν → *we* in *what then shall we have* at 19:27. Rule 15 permits the reordering; what this settles is that a change of grammatical role is not a change of realization, so no token is emptied and nothing is added.
- νεανίσκος → *young man* heads on *young* at 19:20 and 19:22. The *man* is the generic completion rule 66 names and the lexeme's own sense sits in the νεαν- element, as πλεονέκτης → *covetous man* heads on *covetous* and πτωχός → *a poor man* on *poor*. Heading on *man* is conceded in both units.
- Rule 32's apposition clause — another source token having already written the antecedent — reaches a nominal standing inside the article's own phrase, not one standing across an expressed copula. ὑμεῖς οἱ ἀκολουθήσαντές → *ye who have followed* at 19:28 empties the article, ὑμεῖς having written the *ye*; but εἷς ἐστιν ὁ ἀγαθός → *One there is who is good* at 19:17 does not, εἷς and ὁ ἀγαθός being the two terms of an expressed copula rather than one phrase, so the article keeps the single relativizer under rule 90 and the adjective takes the supplied copula with its own word under rule 53. The expressed ἐστιν is spent on *there is*, so the *is* of the relative clause is a second, supplied copula.
