# Proverbs 29 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- אִישׁ in construct on a following noun is decided by what the ASV writes for the pair, and this chapter writes all three shapes. One English word fuses the two morphemes under rule 30, headed on the morpheme carrying the lexeme: אַנְשֵׁי לָצוֹן → *Scoffers* (29.8), אַנְשֵׁי דָמִים → *The bloodthirsty* (29.10), אִישׁ תְּכָכִים → *the oppressor* (29.13). Two content words split under rule 28's attributive rendering, אִישׁ taking the generic noun with its determiner and the rectum the attributive: אִישׁ אַף → *An angry man* (29.22), אִישׁ עָוֶל → *An unjust man* (29.27). A relative clause puts the whole recasting on the rectum under rule 19, אִישׁ keeping only the substantival pronoun: אִישׁ תְּרוּמוֹת → *he* + *that exacteth gifts* (29.4), אִישׁ תּוֹכָחוֹת → *He* + *being often reproved* (29.1). Where אִישׁ is instead absolute before an attributive participle it takes its own word and the participle keeps the verb: אִישׁ אֹהֵב → *Whoso* + *loveth* (29.3).
- An articleless attributive participle that the ASV recasts as a relative clause keeps the supplied *that* under rule 41 even where the ASV interposes another morpheme's rendering between the relative and the verb, and the unit is discontinuous under rule 25 rather than broken: מַחֲלִיק → *that flattereth* (29.5), אָץ → *that is hasty* (29.20), שׁוֹפֵט → *that ... judgeth* around *faithfully* (29.14), מַקְשֶׁה → *that ... hardeneth* around *being often reproved* (29.1). Do not hand such a relative to the construct noun's rectum standing between them: what licenses the word is the clause whose finite verb the participle renders. The rectum takes the relative only where no attributive participle stands in the phrase for it, which is what 29.4 is.
