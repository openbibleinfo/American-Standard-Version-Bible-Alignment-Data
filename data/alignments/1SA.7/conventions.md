# 1 Samuel 7 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- levaddo -> *only* (1SA.7.3, 1SA.7.4): the preposition and baddo fuse under rule 30, since one English word carries the pair, and the unit heads on the construct noun bad. The pronominal suffix is no part of that fusion and is its own `unrepresented` unit, the ASV writing no pronoun for it — the *him* of *serve him* in 7.3 is the verbal object suffix, and the object in 7.4 is the written divine name.
- A compound proper name that MACULA segments into two source tokens (Kiriath-jearim in 7.1 and 7.2, Beth-car in 7.11, Eben-ezer in 7.12, Beth-el in 7.16) takes one English word of the name per token, and whatever relational or determining English the phrase carries joins the first token's unit: the chain's *of* in 7.1 goes with *Kiriath*, and the second token takes only its own word.
- A cardinal echad or achat standing on a written noun where the ASV writes only an indefinite article (7.9 tleh chalav echad -> *a sucking lamb*, 7.12 even achat -> *a stone*): the *a* belongs to the noun under rule 15 and the numeral is its own `unrepresented` unit. Rule 15's carve-outs give a numeral standing in the phrase a distributive *each* or *every*, and rule 31 gives a substantival numeral its *one*, but neither reaches an indefinite article written before a noun the Hebrew writes. BSB represents the numeral in both places; that is the convention difference and not an error.
