# Numbers 35 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The hiphil/hophal death formula is a light verb plus complement under rule 44 and heads on 'death' throughout this chapter: yumat -> 'shall be put to death' (35.16, 17, 18, 21, 31) and yamit -> 'shall put to death' (35.19 twice, 35.21). The corpus heads the same formula on 'put' in a minority of units, so the choice is recorded rather than left to be rediscovered verse by verse.
- The object marker takes an English word only where the ASV spends one on the object relation itself, and the chapter met all three outcomes: the marker takes the supplied 'for' before each measured side phrase (35.5, four units) and the 'beyond' through which the verb reaches its object (35.26); it is unrepresented wherever the ASV writes the marked object bare (35.6, 35.7, 35.14, 35.19, 35.21, 35.25, 35.27, 35.30, 35.33, 35.34); and a topicalizing word the ASV writes before a fronted phrase carrying no marker at all is added, which is the 'concerning' of 35.8.
- Where a preposition fuses with its construct noun into one English word and a lamed follows, that lamed carries no English of its own and is unrepresented under rule 40: min-chuts le -> 'without' (35.5, 35.27) and me-ever la- -> 'beyond' (35.14). Contrast 35.4, where the construct noun qir holds its own word 'wall', so rule 30's one-rendering condition is unmet and min takes its own preposition.
- NUM.35.6 fronts ha-arim with an object marker and the ASV resumes it with a bare 'they' inside the verbless clause. Both realizations were given to arim as one discontinuous unit, on the reasoning rule 18 uses for a twice-realized suffix and rule 25 for a twice-realized pronoun; no resumptive morpheme stands in the Hebrew and rule 16's 'as for' family gives the marker nothing here, so the alternative was to mark the pronoun added under rule 30. This is the chapter's one genuinely open call and it is recorded rather than promoted, since it occurs once and no corpus precedent was available to settle it.
