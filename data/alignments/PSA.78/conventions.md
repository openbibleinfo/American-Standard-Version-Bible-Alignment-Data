# Psalms 78 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Supplied English in this chapter divides on one line: an object pronoun the ASV writes for a verb that carries no object morpheme is its own added unit (78.4 them, 78.6 them twice, 78.15 them, 78.28 it, 78.38 their and them, 78.51 their, 78.59 this), including where it stands inside a causative or light-verb rendering; everything else the ASV supplies joins the morpheme that licenses it, namely the copula (78.9, 78.30, 78.35, 78.37, 78.38, 78.39, 78.57, 78.71), the subject pronoun and modal, the article and the indefinite article, the relative of an asyndetic relative clause (78.6, 78.8, 78.39, 78.54, 78.60, 78.65, 78.69) and the emphatic own written for a possessive suffix (78.29, 78.52).
- A clause connective the ASV supplies is aligned to a waw only where that waw stands at the head of the clause the connective introduces: 78.20 and 78.53 so that, 78.31 When, 78.60 So that. Where the clause is asyndetic the connective is added, since no morpheme is there to carry it: 78.9 and between the coordinated participles, 78.40 And, 78.42 Nor, 78.44 so that. PSA.78.59 is the case that decides the rule rather than an exception to it: a waw is present, but at the head of the apodosis, while the ASV's When subordinates the preceding asyndetic clause, so the waw is unrepresented and the When is added.
- An English preposition the ASV supplies only to attach a verb to its object stays in the verb's unit, discontinuously under rule 25, wherever the Hebrew verb governs its object directly: 78.1 Give ear ... to, 78.17 and 78.40 rebel against, 78.34 inquired after, 78.66 put ... to. Where the object marker is present it takes that word instead under rule 16, as at 78.56 rebelled against. Read whether a morpheme is there to carry the preposition before deciding which of the two it is.
