# 2 Chronicles 11 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Two-token city names in the fortified-cities list (בֵּית לֶחֶם 11.6, בֵּית צוּר 11.7) retain separate aligned units for their independently rendered source and English components, each headed on its own source and English token (the earlier mandatory-fusion clause is superseded by the independently confirmed TS07 retrospective parity corrections); the object marker before each name is its own unrepresented unit and each verse-initial waw takes only the connective the ASV writes for it (11.6 opens 'He built' and its waw is unrepresented).
