# Job 11 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Existential אֵין before an anarthrous hiphil participle (11.3 וְאֵין מַכְלִם, 11.19 וְאֵין מַחֲרִיד): the negation word alone is אֵין's ('no', 'none'; rule 50), and the participle takes its light-verb rendering headed on the complement ('make ... ashamed', 'make ... afraid'; rule 44) together with any generic noun the ASV supplies to make it a noun phrase ('man' in 11.3; rule 46). 'no man' is not read as an equivalent of 'none' resting whole on אֵין; the supplied object 'thee' is added in both verses.
