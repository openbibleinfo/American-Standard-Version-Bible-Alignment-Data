# Amos 6 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **AMO.6.3** — For a vocative relative rendered ‘ye that …’, the article takes ‘that’, while vocative ‘ye’ stays with the participle or adjective.
- **AMO.6.14** — When a construct chain has an article on its nomen rectum and ASV writes a single fronted ‘the’, that article takes ‘the’; the construct noun takes only its lexical noun.
