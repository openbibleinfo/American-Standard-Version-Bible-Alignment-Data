# Leviticus 23 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **LEV.23.7** — Where כֹּל and a distant לֹא are rendered by the single word ‘no,’ they share one discontinuous noncompositional unit headed on the negative; where ASV writes ‘no manner of,’ ‘no’ belongs to לֹא and ‘manner of’ to כֹּל.
- **LEV.23.6** — In an explicit יוֹם phrase rendered ‘the fifteenth day’ without a source article morpheme, supplied ‘the’ belongs to the nominal expression headed by ‘day,’ discontinuous around the ordinal; the compound numeral takes only ‘fifteenth.’
- **LEV.23.34** — In the chapter's calendar clauses, an opening ‘On ... day’ phrase is a temporal adjunct, not a prepositional predicate; a construction-supplied copula belongs to the nominal predicate.
