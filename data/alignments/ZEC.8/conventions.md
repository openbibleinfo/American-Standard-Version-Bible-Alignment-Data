# Zechariah 8 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **ZEC.8.1** — In ZEC 8, the lamed of le'mor is unrepresented. In verse 1 the ASV's italicized 'to' and 'me' are unsupported additions, not a rendering of that lamed.
- **ZEC.8.6** — In the repeated remnant-of-this-people construct chain (vv. 6, 11, 12), the article on people takes the ASV's fronted 'the'; remnant takes only its own noun plus 'of' where required.
- **ZEC.8.23** — Where verses 20 and 23 supply 'it shall come to pass' without a Hebrew verb, the phrase is added and its target head is the lexical predicate 'come'.
