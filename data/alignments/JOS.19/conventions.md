# Joshua 19 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **JOS.19.11** — For לַיָּמָּה rendered ‘westward,’ the directional preposition, west noun, and locative he share the one-word rendering, while the assimilated article is unrepresented because the ASV writes no ‘the.’
