# Ecclesiastes 7 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Comparative and equative clauses: the English degree word belongs to the word whose degree it expresses, and the preposition takes only the connective written beside it. Where a gradable predicate stands in the clause, that word carries the whole comparative form and מִן takes a bare 'than' — טוֹב as 'is better' (ECC.7.1, 7.2, 7.3, 7.5, 7.8, 7.10) and מַר as 'more bitter' (ECC.7.26). Where none stands, the preposition takes the whole expression, headed on the relational word: מֵעֲשָׂרָה שַׁלִּיטִים as 'more than ten rulers' (ECC.7.19), which is how the corpus heads its 'more than' units. The equative goes the same way: טוֹבָה חָכְמָה עִם־נַחֲלָה as 'Wisdom is as good as an inheritance' (ECC.7.11) gives 'is as good' to טוֹב and the closing 'as' to עִם, the morpheme standing where the second correlative stands.
- The positive existential יֵשׁ is read on the terms rule 50 gives the Aramaic אִיתַי: it takes whatever frame the ASV writes for it, the supplied dummy included — יֵשׁ צַדִּיק אֹבֵד as 'there is a righteous man that perisheth' (ECC.7.15, twice) — and heads on the copula rather than on 'there', which English syntax supplies, exactly as rule 50 heads אַיִן on its negation and not on the dummy. Where the ASV writes no frame, it is unrepresented.
