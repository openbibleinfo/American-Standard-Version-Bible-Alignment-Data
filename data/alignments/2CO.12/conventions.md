# 2 Corinthians 12 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **2CO.12.5** — A rule 67 frame carried by an expressed **proper** Greek preposition heads on the prepositional word, not on the frame's supplied noun: ὑπέρ → *On behalf of* heads on *On*, *on … behalf* on *on*, *Concerning … thing* on *Concerning*, *for … sake* on *for*. Rule 67 leaves this open and says the corpus is divided; it is, but 2 Corinthians is not — 2CO.7.4, 7.14, 8.19, 8.20, 8.24, 9.2, 9.3, 9.14 and 10.16 are unanimous for the prepositional word, and contiguity makes no difference among them. Recorded medium where the frame is *behalf*, because EPH.3.1, EPH.5.32, PHP.1.4, PHP.1.5, PHP.1.7 and PHP.1.29 head the noun.
- **2CO.12.19** — An **improper** preposition — an adverb governing a case — is the contrast to the convention recorded at 12.5, and keeps its head on the frame's lexical noun: κατέναντι → *In the sight of* heads on *sight*. The corpus is 19-0 for the noun on improper prepositions (ἐνώπιον, πέραν, ἐγγύς, χωρίς), and 2CO.7.12 is this same string with this same head. So the two frame conventions in this chapter differ by the preposition's class, not by inconsistency.
- **2CO.12.6** — A relative pronoun rendered by a supplied antecedent plus a relativizer heads on the **relativizer**: ὃ → *that which* heads on *which*, ὧν → *of them whom* on *whom*. Rule 22's instruction to head on the nominal word rather than the relativizer is scoped to a substantival **article**; applying it to a relative pronoun is the error this chapter made at 12.6 and corrected. Rule 17 decides it — *which* is what directly realizes ὅς — and the corpus agrees 23:4 overall and 8:3 for *that which*, including 2CO.11.17.
