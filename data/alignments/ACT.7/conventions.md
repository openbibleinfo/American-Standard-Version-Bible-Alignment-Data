# Acts 7 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Interrogative εἰ. A conditional particle introducing a direct question is decided by rules 44 and 64's English-side test, not by its COND tag: at ACT.7.1 Εἰ ταῦτα οὕτως ἔχει; -> Are these things so? the ASV marks the question by inversion alone, so εἰ is its own unrepresented unit and the copular Are stays with ἔχει. Do not give the particle a share of Are, and do not read the conditional gloss if off a question the ASV never introduces with one.
- Rule 73 collapse beyond a verb-plus-noun pair. Where the Greek expresses both elements and the ASV collapses them into a single English verb, the pair is one unit headed on the English verb and sourced on the content word, whether the Greek pair is verb plus noun (ACT.7.24 ἐποίησεν ἐκδίκησιν -> avenged, source head ἐκδίκησιν; ACT.7.60 θεὶς τὰ γόνατα -> he kneeled down, source head γόνατα) or a copular verb plus predicate adjective (ACT.7.32 ἔντρομος γενόμενος -> trembled, source head ἔντρομος). An article standing inside such a pair contributes no part of the collapsed English word, so it stays its own unrepresented unit, as τὰ does at ACT.7.60 on rule 93's principle.
- Repeated English of a function word already expressed in the sentence is folded, never added. Rule 86 names a conjunction repeated in a coordinated clause, and the chapter extends it to a preposition: ACT.7.6 gives the repeated complementizer that to the second verb δουλώσουσιν, and ACT.7.38 gives the repeated with to πατέρων, the μετά of μετὰ τοῦ ἀγγέλου having already been spent. Rule 103 is the contrast and it is scoped to full lexical content, which is why the repeated begat at ACT.7.8 and the repeated the Lord at ACT.7.59 are added instead.
- Heads inside drew-phrases. ACT.7.17 ἤγγιζεν -> drew nigh heads on nigh, the corpus spending the ἐγγ- word on nigh throughout, while ACT.7.31 προσερχομένου -> drew near heads on drew, προσέρχομαι being a compound of ἔρχομαι whose own sense is the motion verb (rule 104). The similar English is not evidence that these are one construction; rule 91's operational test is run on each lexeme separately.
- Ἄνδρες ἀδελφοί is analysed from the English the ASV wrote. Where it compresses the address to one word, Brethren answers to ἀδελφοί alone and Ἄνδρες is its own unrepresented unit (ACT.7.2); where it writes two, each token takes its own (ACT.7.26, Sirs and brethren). Joining the pair into one unit at 7:2 remains defensible, but the chapter follows rule 2.
- The ASV's the Nth time. Where the Greek is an articular dative, both the determiner and the supplied noun are the article's and the unit heads on the nominal: ACT.7.13 ἐν τῷ δευτέρῳ gives at to ἐν, the ... time to τῷ headed on time, and second to δευτέρῳ (rule 102). Where the Greek is a bare adverbial accusative, the supplied the ... time is a rule 7 helper on the ordinal and heads there: ACT.7.12 πρῶτον -> the first time heads on first. The identical English is not a split; the presence of an article is what decides it.
