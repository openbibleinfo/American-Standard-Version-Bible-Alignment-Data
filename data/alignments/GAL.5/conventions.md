# Galatians 5 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **GAL.5.17** — A reciprocal ἀλλήλων in the dative takes the whole English frame the ASV builds for it, and rule 17 heads it on the noun governed by 'to' — 'another' in 'one to another' (5.13), 'other' in 'the one to the other' (5.17) — never on the leading 'one'. EPH.5.21 and REV.11.10 head the same frame on 'another' without hedging, and every dative reciprocal in the corpus does the same.
- **GAL.5.17** — A generalizing ἐάν standing with a relative has two arms, and the ASV's English decides which. Where the ASV compresses the pair into a single -soever word the two tokens are one aligned unit under rule 50's test — 'ὅστις ἐὰν' → whosoever (5.10). Where the ASV writes no -soever and no other word for it, nothing answers to the particle and it is its own unrepresented unit — 'ἃ ἐὰν θέλητε' → the things that ye would (5.17), as REV.3.19, REV.11.6 and REV.13.15 already leave it. Rule 50's closing clause, that a reference comparison reporting an unrepresented particle is a correction, is scoped to ἄν: the corpus has no unrepresented ἄν and four unrepresented ἐάν, so the BSB disagreement at an empty ἐάν is a convention difference.
- **GAL.5.24** — A bare-genitive predicate carries a supplied copula exactly as rule 53's prepositional predicate does, the case-realizing 'of' being the genitive noun's own English. 'οἱ δὲ τοῦ Χριστοῦ Ἰησοῦ' → they that are of Christ Jesus gives 'are of Christ' to Χριστοῦ and leaves the substantival article only 'they that' under rules 22 and 32. JHN.12.43 is the corpus's one prior instance and it hedges, so the unit stays medium.
- **GAL.5.15** — A bare μή governing a clause after a verb of caution realizes both the subordination and the negation, whether the ASV spells it as one word or as a discontinuous frame: 'βλέπετε μὴ … ἀναλωθῆτε' → take heed that ye be not consumed gives 'that … not' to μή under rule 15, as the corpus gives a bare 'lest' to μή after φοβοῦμαι. Where a conjunction is expressed it takes the introducer under rule 40 and μή keeps only the negation, which is why 5.17's 'ἵνα μή' gives 'that' to ἵνα and 'not' to μή.
- **GAL.5.24** — Where the ASV writes a plain 'the' for an article and adds a trailing possessive no source word licenses, the article takes both as one discontinuous unit headed on 'the': 'ταῖς ἐπιθυμίαις' → the lusts thereof (5.24). Rule 20's displacement does not arise, because the possessive does not occupy the determiner slot the ASV has already filled. The corpus decides neither way — its twenty other 'thereof' units all align to an expressed genitive pronoun, as JHN.8.44 does, and its one added 'thereof' is a rule 37 versification case at JHN.7.21 — so the unit stays medium.
