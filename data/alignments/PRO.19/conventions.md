# Proverbs 19 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- `אַף כִּי` is aligned as one fused unit under rule 30, the particle and the conjunction sharing the single fixed English expression the ASV writes for the pair, of which neither morpheme owns a part. The source head is `אַף`, the governing first morpheme, under rule 23's sentence on a pair of particles in which neither carries content the other lacks; the target head is the content word the expression turns on by rule 31's subtraction, which is the comparative word. PRO.19.7 reads *How much more* headed on *more* and PRO.19.10 reads *Much less* headed on *less*; the two verses were decided independently on their own English and agree.
