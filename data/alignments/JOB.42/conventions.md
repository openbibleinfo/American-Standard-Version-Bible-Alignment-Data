# Job 42 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- `וְעַתָּה` at a clause head is divided by sense and not by position: `עַתָּה` takes the *now* that writes its own lexeme, and the waw takes whatever other connective the ASV has written beside it, whichever order the two words stand in. JOB.42.5 *But now* and JOB.42.8 *Now therefore* are one construction, and in the second the two units cross under rule 25. Rule 51 is the ground: distance from the waw, and the side of the clause a word falls on, are not evidence about which morpheme a connective belongs to.
- The idiom `נָשָׂא פָּנִים` → *accept* is fused where its morphemes are contiguous and split where they are not, which is rule 30's contiguity condition and not an inconsistency. JOB.42.8 `אִם־פָּנָיו אֶשָּׂא` → *him will I accept* is one `noncompositional` unit; JOB.42.9 `וַיִּשָּׂא יְהוָה אֶת־פְּנֵי אִיּוֹב` → *and Jehovah accepted Job* has the subject and the object marker standing between the two morphemes, so rule 2 splits it and `פְּנֵי` is `unrepresented`, as `גָּדַל ... מִן` → *exceeded* leaves its preposition. BSB attaches the noun to its phrase in both places; that is the documented convention difference behind the JOB.42.9 disagreement, not an error.
- JOB.42.3 writes two `וְלֹא` + finite-verb clauses that the ASV recasts differently, and they are aligned differently on that ground alone. *that which I understood not* writes an antecedent and a relative together, which is rule 46's headless shape, so both English words join the verb's own unit and that waw is `unrepresented`; *which I knew not* writes a bare relative after an antecedent the ASV has already written, so rule 17 gives the *which* to the waw. Read what the ASV supplied before reading the pair as a split.
