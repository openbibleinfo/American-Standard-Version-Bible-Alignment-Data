# 2 Chronicles 17 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **2CH.17.15** — עַל־יַד before a suffix, rendered next to, is one fused unit under rule 30 (preposition + construct noun), headed on יָד and on next; the suffix takes its own him. Applied identically in 17.15, 17.16 and 17.18.
- **2CH.17.7** — An appositional even is placed by whether a morpheme stands on the appositive: in 17.7 the לְ repeated on the first appositive (לְבֶן־חַיִל after לְשָׂרָיו) takes the even as the morpheme marking the apposition (rules 11, 16), while the later repeated לְ on plain list members are unrepresented; in 17.8 the even before Shemaiah, where no morpheme stands, is added. Not a split.
- **2CH.17.12** — In the periphrastic וַיְהִי X הֹלֵךְ וְגָדֵל → X waxed great, the linking verb waxed goes to הָיָה (rule 20's copula is whatever verb stands where a copula would), the progressive הֹלֵךְ and its waw are unrepresented; the reverse allocation is defensible and is named in the unit note.
