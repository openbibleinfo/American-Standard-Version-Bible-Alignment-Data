# Psalms 21 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Double-accusative verbs (שית + object suffix + second accusative in 21.6, 21.9, 21.12; קדם + suffix + accusative in 21.3): the verb keeps only its subject pronoun, auxiliary and lexical verb (make, meetest), and the whole English of the second accusative stays with that noun — most blessed (21.6, rule 31), as a furnace (21.9, כ written), turn their back (21.12, noncompositional under rule 2), with the blessings (21.3, supplied with to the bare nominal under rule 33 because the suffix already holds the direct-object role). The verb never absorbs the predicate complement.
