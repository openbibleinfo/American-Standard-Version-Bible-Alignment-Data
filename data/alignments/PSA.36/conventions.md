# Psalms 36 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Where two לְ + infinitive construct phrases stand asyndetically and the ASV coordinates them with and (plain in v2, italic in v3), the and is its own added unit under rule 14's closing sentences; the second לְ takes only what the ASV writes for it (to in v3, nothing in v2, rules 52/40) and is not read as the connective's home on the pattern of rule 26's בֵּין X לְ Y, which governs only that construction.
- **PSA.36.5** — עַד heading the verbless predicate of extent (אֱמוּנָתְךָ עַד־שְׁחָקִים) takes the italic reacheth together with unto as the construction-supplied linking verb (rules 20, 49), on the footing of לְ → endureth for ever: a bare is in its place says what the Hebrew says, so reacheth is a copula of extent and not a supplied lexical verb. Marking it added was the alternative considered.
