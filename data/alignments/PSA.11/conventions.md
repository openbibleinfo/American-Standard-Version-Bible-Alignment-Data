# Psalms 11 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in `AGENTS.md` once the chapter is finished.

- **PSA.11.1** — The MT writes this superscription into the verse it numbers 1 rather than numbering it separately, so PSA.11 has no verse 0. The ASV title's words are the opening of this packet's target side and are aligned against the superscription morphemes here, exactly as a numbered superscription is aligned in verse 0 elsewhere.
- **PSA.11.2** — Several anarthrous Hebrew nouns are rendered definitely by the ASV in this psalm (qeshet -> the bow, yeter -> the string, yishrei -> the upright). MACULA supplies no article token for them, so the English the is supplied and belongs to its noun's unit, never 'added', as at PSA.1.1.
- **PSA.11.1** — An English function word the ASV supplies for an adverbial accusative belongs to the noun whose case-function licenses it, never 'added': tsippor -> as a bird keeps the italicized as, and harkem -> to your mountain keeps to, exactly as a verbless clause's supplied copula joins its predicate. Reserve 'added' for English no morpheme or construction licenses at all, as with the supplied A Psalm in this superscription, which has no mizmor to license it.
