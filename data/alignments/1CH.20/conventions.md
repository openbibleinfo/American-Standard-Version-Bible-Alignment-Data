# 1 Chronicles 20 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- A waw opening a clause built on a suffixed noun (וְעֵץ חֲנִיתוֹ 20.5, וְאֶצְבְּעֹתָיו 20.6) that the ASV recasts as a *whose* relative clause: the suffix takes *whose* (rule 18) and the waw is `unrepresented` (rule 17), the ASV writing no connective. BSB represents both waws; this is a convention difference, not an error. Contrast 20.6 וַיְהִי → *where*, where the waw + finite verb has no pronoun for the relative to realize and the waw takes it.
