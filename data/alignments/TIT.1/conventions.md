# Titus 1 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **TIT.1.5** — The postposition χάριν takes the whole discontinuous English frame it governs — “For … cause”, “for … sake” — headed on the meaning-bearing noun, never on the English preposition. Applied at TIT.1.5 and TIT.1.11; matches EPH.3.1 and EPH.3.14.
