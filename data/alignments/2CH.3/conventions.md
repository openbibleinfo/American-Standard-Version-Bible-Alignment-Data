# 2 Chronicles 3 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **2CH.3.4** — Overlaying verbs with a bare material accusative (חפה, צפה + זָהָב / אֶבֶן): the ASV's supplied 'with' joins the verb's unit under rule 44, on the model of טָחוּ תָּפֵל → 'daubed ... with untempered mortar', and the material noun keeps only its own word. Applied in 3.4–3.10. Rule 33 keeps only a material or measure nominal the English attaches to a noun rather than to the verb — 'shekels of gold' (3.9), 'the veil of blue' (3.14), 'pillars of thirty and five cubits' (3.15).
- **2CH.3.11** — Measurement clauses: where a לְ heads the predicate (לְאַמּוֹת חָמֵשׁ 3.11, לִשְׁקָלִים חֲמִשִּׁים 3.9) the construction's copula goes to that לְ under rule 49 and 'cubits'/'shekels' stays bare; where no preposition stands, the copula joins 'cubits' under rule 20 and never the numeral (3.3, 3.4, 3.8, 3.11, 3.12, 3.15). A gapped second measure the ASV writes without a copula leaves 'cubits' bare.
- **2CH.3.8** — At 3.8 and 3.10, בֵּית קֹדֶשׁ הַקֳּדָשִׁים → 'the most holy house': the scoped retrospective judgments supersede the former separate holy/most allocation. The two lexical nouns jointly own most holy under rules 2 and 6; the independently rendered article and house remain separate. At 3.10 the fused unit is headed on the construct and holy under rule 23. These amendments are limited to the scoped lexical-noun units at 3.8 and 3.10.
- **2CH.3.5** — In וַיְחַפֵּהוּ recast as 'which he overlaid', the relative 'which' realizes the object suffix it denotes (rule 18) and the waw is unrepresented; the competing reading gives 'which' to the waw under rule 17 and leaves the suffix unrepresented. Noted on both units.
