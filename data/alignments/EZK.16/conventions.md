# Ezekiel 16 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה formula (16.3, 8, 14, 19, 23, 30, 36, 43, 48, 59, 63) is aligned in three units throughout: אֲדֹנָ takes the ASV's supplied *the* with *Lord*, its 1cs suffix is `unrepresented` because the ASV writes no *my*, and יְהוִה takes *Jehovah*. BSB represents that suffix in all eleven places, which is the whole of the chapter's Sp1cs disagreement count.
- Where the ASV renders a comparative by an English comparative adjective, both the מִן and its suffix go `unrepresented`: אֲחוֹתֵךְ הַגְּדוֹלָה מִמֵּךְ -> *thine elder sister* (16.46) and הַגְּדֹלוֹת מִמֵּךְ -> *thine elder sisters* (16.61) write no *than thee* at all. This is rule 30's גָּדַל ... מִן -> *exceeded* case extended to the suffix, and it is distinct from 16.47, 16.51 and 16.52, where the ASV writes *more ... than* and the preposition takes it under rule 25.
- An anarthrous noun takes a supplied possessive determiner into its own unit on the same terms as it takes a supplied *the* under rule 29: שָׁדַיִם -> *thy breasts* (16.7) and בְּרֹאשׁ -> *upon thy head* (16.43). No article morpheme is present in either phrase, so nothing else can carry the determiner and it is not `added`.
- Rule 31's subtraction was read as putting the head on the noun for שֵׁשׁ -> *fine linen* (16.10, 16.13) and סֹלֶת -> *fine flour* (16.13, 16.19), on the ground that *linen* and *flour* already name the substance and *fine* is the grade specifier added to it -- the תַּנִּין -> *sea monsters* half, not the יַבָּשָׁה -> *dry land* half. The same reading heads עָגִיל -> *ear rings* on *rings* (16.12). These stand beside the chapter's opposite group, where the ASV supplies the generic noun and the head stays on the modifier (רָמָה -> *lofty place*, גַּב -> *vaulted place*, קַדְמָה -> *former estate*, שְׂמֹאל/יָמִין -> *left/right hand*); the two groups are the two halves of one rule and the line between them is which word the ASV supplied.
- In בְּנֵי + gentilic proper name rendered by an English plural gentilic -- בְּנֵי מִצְרַיִם -> *the Egyptians* (16.26), בְּנֵי אַשּׁוּר -> *the Assyrians* (16.28) -- the construct בֵּן takes the ASV's single written *the* as a supplied one under rule 29 and the name takes the gentilic, בֵּן's own lexeme being absorbed into it. The construct is not left `unrepresented` and the two morphemes are not fused, since the ASV spends a separate word on each.
