# 2 Samuel 19 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Status of rule-30 fusions. Where two or more source morphemes are rendered by a single English rendering, this chapter records the shared unit as `noncompositional`, not as a multi-source `aligned` unit: לָמָה -> why / wherefore (19.10, 19.11, 19.12, 19.25, 19.29, 19.35, 19.36, 19.42), לִפְנֵי -> before and in the presence of (19.8, 19.13, 19.17, 19.18), כַּאֲשֶׁר -> as (19.3), בֶּן־יְמִינִי -> Benjamite (19.16), חֲמֵשֶׁת עָשָׂר -> fifteen (19.17), מַה־לִּי -> What have ... to do with (19.22), וְאַל -> neither (19.19), עָלֶיהָ -> thereon (19.26), כִּי אִם -> but (19.28), אַחֲרֵי אֲשֶׁר -> forasmuch as (19.30), כֹּל אֲשֶׁר -> whatsoever (19.38), כָּל־הַיָּמִים -> continually (19.13). A single source morpheme rendered by several English words stays `aligned` — תַּחַת -> in the room of (19.13), עוֹד -> any more (19.28, 19.29, 19.35), מִן -> out of (19.9). This is a chapter convention and not a contract ruling: the corpus reads the same expressions both ways (לִפְנֵי -> before is 218 noncompositional against 143 aligned; וְ לֹא -> neither is 105 against 103), so a later chapter is free to settle it the other way, but must settle it once.
- The בְּעֵינֵי X idiom. Where the ASV writes the idiom out — in thine eyes (19.27) — all three morphemes align: בְּ takes in, עַיִן takes eyes, the suffix takes the possessive. Where the ASV recasts it, the preposition and the noun both go `unrepresented` under rule 40 and the suffix takes whatever pronoun is written, including a subject pronoun: he thought good (19.18, suffix -> he), it had pleased thee well (19.6, suffix -> thee), shall seem good unto thee (19.37, 19.38, where בְּ takes the unto and only עַיִן is unrepresented). This accounts for four of the BSB disagreements, which read the noun as represented.
- A lexical word the ASV supplies inside a recasting is its own `added` unit and is never folded into the morpheme whose rendering it stands beside: cost and gift (19.42), right (19.43), the heart of and saying (19.14), the purpose phrase to bring him (19.11), the appositional even before the age phrase (19.32), and the object pronouns it at 19.19 and 19.36, where no object morpheme stands in the clause. Nine such units, and the policy is what makes them consistent; the candidate table cannot see it, since an `added` unit has no source token.
- The age formula בֶּן + numeral + שָׁנָה. The construct בֶּן is what the ASV renders by old, and it takes the clause's construction-supplied copula where the clause is verbless: fourscore years old (19.32), I am ... fourscore years old (19.35). The numeral and the counted noun keep their own English and the unit is discontinuous around them under rule 25. Do not mark בֶּן unrepresented and read old as construction-supplied.
