# Matthew 17 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- mellei governing an infinitive and rendered by the English modal 'shall' is aligned to that modal as its own unit, and the infinitive stands alone on its lexical verb: MAT.17.12 mellei ... paschein -> 'shall ... suffer' and MAT.17.22 Mellei ... paradidosthai -> 'shall be delivered up'. The modal is what answers to mellei's own sense of impending action, not an auxiliary of the infinitive under rule 9, so do not fold it into the infinitive's unit.
- kat' idian rendered by the single word 'apart' is one noncompositional unit under rules 45 and 94, headed on idian, at MAT.17.1 and MAT.17.19. Rule 93's judgment call is decided against rule 34's pleonastic arm here: idios by itself glosses 'one's own' and spells no part of 'apart', so the preposition is inside the English word and the pair joins rather than leaving kata unrepresented.
- heOs pote rendered by 'how long' is one aligned unit taking both English words, headed on pote and on 'how', at both occurrences in MAT.17.17. This follows rule 64's display of the pair as a single item; splitting 'long' onto heOs and 'how' onto pote remains defensible and is recorded in the verse notes.
- Versification split under rule 37 between MAT.17.14 and MAT.17.15: the projection places kai legOn at the head of 17.15, and the ASV translates it as 'and saying' at the end of 17.14. So 17.14 carries an added unit for 'and saying' headed on 'saying', and 17.15 leaves both kai and legOn as their own unrepresented units. A reference comparison reports 17.15 legOn as a disagreement, and that report is not a correction.
