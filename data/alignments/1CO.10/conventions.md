# 1 Corinthians 10 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Doubled article with a genitive possessor (τὸ τοῦ ἑτέρου at 10:24, τὴν τοῦ ἑτέρου at 10:29, τὸ τῶν πολλῶν at 10:33): where the ASV renders the possessor prenominally with 's, the inner article takes the determiner the English writes on the possessor ('his' at 10:24, 'the' at 10:29), the genitive takes only the possessive word, and the outer article is unrepresented under rule 20 unless the ASV supplies a nominal for it, which it takes under rule 102 ('good' at 10:24). Where the possessor follows the head ('of the many' at 10:33) nothing is displaced and each article takes its own determiner.
- Substantival article with a reflexive genitive (τὸ ἑαυτοῦ at 10:24, τὴν ἑαυτοῦ at 10:29, τὸ ἐμαυτοῦ at 10:33): the reflexive carries the English possessive ('his', 'thine', 'mine') and the article carries the supplied nominal 'own'. Emptying the article and folding 'own' onto the reflexive is the competing reading and was rejected in all three verses.
- Substantival article whose complement is an adjective recast as an English relative clause: the relativizer follows the verb. Where the adjective's own English stays adjectival and only a copula is supplied, the article keeps the relativizer and the adjective takes the copula — οἱ πολλοί → 'we who' / 'are many' at 10:17 (rules 22, 53). Where the adjective's own English is a lexical verb, that verb carries the relativizer — τῶν ἀπίστων → 'of them' / 'that believe not' at 10:27 (rules 31, 32), the case preposition riding with the article. Rule 22's carve-out names only a following participle and does not by itself decide an adjective.
- Supplied English in this chapter was decided by whether some token's own English is still incomplete, not by the ASV's italics: 'I say' at 10:20 is added because no token licenses it (rules 5, 86), while 'to a feast' folds onto καλεῖ at 10:27, the repeated 'for … sake' onto συνείδησιν at 10:28 (rules 86, 103), and the italic 'good' and 'profit' onto their own articles at 10:24 and 10:33 (rule 102).
