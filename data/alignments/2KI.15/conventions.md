# 2 Kings 15 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **2KI.15.17** — A supplied connective standing with the first word of the colon it introduces is one added unit headed on the content word: the italic 'and reigned' of 15.17, 15.23 and 15.27 (no verb or waw in the Hebrew) and the supplied 'and all' of 15.36 (no waw or כֹּל before אֲשֶׁר עָשָׂה). Two supplied words are split only where they belong to different clauses, as 15.16's 'it' (object of the preceding 'smote') beside the 'and' opening the next colon.
- **2KI.15.7** — תַּחְתָּיו → 'in his stead' divides as תַּחַת → 'in … stead', headed on 'stead' and discontinuous around the possessive, with the suffix taking 'his'; so in 15.7, 10, 14, 22, 25, 30, 38.
- **2KI.15.6** — The chronicles formula has two shapes here and is not a split: with הֲלֹא הֵם כְּתוּבִים (15.6, 21, 36) the fronted 'are' goes to הֲ and the participle keeps a bare 'written'; with הִנָּם כְּתוּבִים (15.11, 15, 26, 31) there is no interrogative and the participle takes 'are written'.
