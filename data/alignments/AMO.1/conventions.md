# Amos 1 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **AMO.1.3** — In Amos 1's recurring punishment refrain, the object suffix aligns to the full ASV expansion 'the punishment thereof' and heads on 'thereof', the word directly realizing the suffix's pronominal lexeme.
- **AMO.1.6** — In AMO.1.6 and AMO.1.9, the exile noun גָּלוּת is rendered metonymically as 'the people' and heads on 'people'.
