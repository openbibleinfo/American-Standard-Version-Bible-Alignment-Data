# Psalms 146 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- `לְדֹר וָדֹר` → *unto all generations* (v10) is read as rule 22's distributive repetition and not as a rule 30 fusion of the three morphemes: the single written `לְ` opens the phrase and takes *unto*, the first `דּוֹר` takes *all generations* together with the supplied determiner standing before it under rule 15, and the waw and the repeated `דּוֹר` are each `unrepresented`. A universal *all* before a plural noun is read as the distributive determiner rule 22 names alongside *every* and *each*, because the English noun is still a word the ASV spends on this noun in particular while the repetition and the coordination are left saying nothing either way. The corpus contains both treatments — the split at EXO.3.15 and PRO.27.24 among others, the fusion at PSA.106.31 and ISA.58.12 — so the choice is recorded here rather than promoted.
- Rule 43's count of the morphemes that could carry a supplied *have* was run twice in this chapter with opposite outcomes, and both stand. At v5 *hath* is `added`: the clause writes no `הָיָה` and no existential particle, and the `בְ` and the suffix have each already been spent on *for* and *his*, so nothing is left for the word to complete. At v2 no part of *have any being* is `added`: it is the whole of `עוֹד`'s own rendering, headed on *being* under rule 31, and dropping it leaves that morpheme with no English at all. A later verse should run the subtraction rather than reading either verse as the chapter's policy on a supplied *have*.
