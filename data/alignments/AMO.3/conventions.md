# Amos 3 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **AMO.3.7** — In the segmented title אדני יהוה, the ASV's supplied definite article belongs to the nominal host; the frozen 1cs suffix is unrepresented. A noncompositional title unit is defensible, but the smaller host/suffix split is used throughout the chapter.
- **AMO.3.3** — When the ASV gives an interrogative particle an explicit fronted question auxiliary in this chapter, the auxiliary aligns to the particle. Leaving the particle unrepresented and assigning the auxiliary to the predicate is a corpus-attested alternative, but AMO.3 uses the explicit-auxiliary analysis consistently.
