# Exodus 22 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **EXO.22.20** — When לְבַדּ plus a pronominal suffix is rendered by the single word ‘only,’ treat the fused unit as aligned and use the lexical noun בַד as source head.
- **EXO.22.21** — When waw plus a negative is fused as ‘neither’ or ‘nor,’ treat the unit as aligned and use the negative as source head.
