# Ecclesiastes 11 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Disjunctive protasis `אִם … וְאִם` where the ASV writes one connective for the waw and the repeated particle together (11.3: `וְאִם בַּצָּפוֹן` → *or toward the north*): the waw takes the written *or* and the repeated `אִם` is its own `unrepresented` unit, on rule 26's reasoning for `בֵּין X וּבֵין Y` — the connective is not transferred to the repeated element — with rule 22 for the repetition. The competing reading gives the *or* to `אִם` and leaves the waw unrepresented, and BSB aligns it that way; the corpus supports both (waw → *or* in 240 units, `אִם` → *or* in 33), so this is a chapter reading rather than a settled one. Where the ASV writes a word for each morpheme instead, as at 11.6 (*or whether*), each takes its own and nothing is repeated.
- Six of the ten verses carry ASV marginal notes — alternative renderings at 11.1 (*Send forth*), 11.2 (*Divide … into seven*), 11.3 (*in*, twice), 11.5 (*spirit*), 11.8 (*For*, *and remember*) and 11.10 (*vexation*), and a literal gloss at 11.1 (*Hebrew upon the face of the waters*). Every unit aligns the text as printed. The 11.1 gloss was used only as corroboration that `עַל־פְּנֵי` was rendered by one English word, which rule 30 already rules a fusion; no margin moved an edge or a head, and 11.3 keeps the printed *toward* on its `בְּ` rather than the marginal *in*.
