# Judges 6 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- A verb rendered by a lexical English verb completed by a particle or an adverb heads on that verb and never on the particle or adverb: `שָׁכַם` → *arose early*, *rose up early*; `זָעַק` niphal → *was gathered together*; `עָלָה` → *came up*; `יָצָא` → *brought forth*; `שָׁלַח` → *put forth*. The English verb there is not a bleached light verb, and the adverb alone names nothing. Rule 44's light-verb head is the other case and is decided by whether the English verb writes the source verb's own event: `מוּת` hophal → *let him be put to death* heads on *death*, `נָסָה` → *make trial* on *trial*, `דָּלַל` → *was brought very low* on *low*, `עָשָׂה` → *made ready* on *ready*.
- The entreaty particle `נָא` takes whatever the ASV writes for it and no more. Where the ASV writes the full formula the particle takes it whole — *I pray thee* at JDG.6.18 and JDG.6.39, headed on *pray* under rule 30 — and where a bare *now* is all that is written, that word is the particle's own aligned unit, at JDG.6.17 and JDG.6.39, and is never `added` nor folded into the imperative standing beside it.
- The Hebrew positive existential `יֵשׁ` takes whatever overt finite frame the ASV writes for it, as rule 50 directs for the Aramaic `אִיתַי`: `וְיֵשׁ יְהוָה עִמָּנוּ` → *if Jehovah is with us* gives *is* to the particle while the waw takes the *if*, and `אִם־יֶשְׁךָ מוֹשִׁיעַ` → *If thou wilt save* gives *wilt* to the particle, *thou* to its suffix under rule 18, and *save* to the participial predicate. This is recorded for this chapter only. The corpus's Hebrew `יֵשׁ` units were not examined, and a `יֵשׁ` clause made possessive by a `לְ` is rule 43's, not this one's.
