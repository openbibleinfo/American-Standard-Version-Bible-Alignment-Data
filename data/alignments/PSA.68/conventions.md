# Psalms 68 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- מִפְּנֵי is read two ways in this chapter by what the ASV wrote. Rendered *before* (68.1, 68.2 first occurrence) it fuses under rule 30, headed on the construct noun, as לִפְנֵי → *before* does (68.3, 68.4, 68.7). Rendered *at the presence of* (68.2 second occurrence, 68.8 twice) it splits under rule 2: מִן → *at*, פָּנֶה → *the presence* with the construct's supplied *the* (rule 29), and the rectum → *of God* (rule 19), because *presence* writes פָּנֶה's own lexeme and rule 30's subtraction test finds each morpheme holding a word of its own. Corpus: מִן → *at* before a construct פָּנֶה in 22 units, פָּנֶה → *(the) presence* headed on *presence* in 58, against one fused *at the presence of* unit.
