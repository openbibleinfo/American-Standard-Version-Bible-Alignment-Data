# Numbers 3 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **NUM.3.4** — The fixed expression liphne is consistently noncompositional when ASV renders the preposition and construct noun together by the single English preposition ‘before’; the source head is the construct noun and the target head is ‘before’.
