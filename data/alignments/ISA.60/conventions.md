# Isaiah 60 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- A waw the ASV renders by a postpositive 'also' is aligned to that 'also' at high confidence, however far right the ASV places it: ISA.60.11, .16, .17, .21. The corpus aligns a waw to 'also' in 33 units across 21 chapters and marks no 'also' added anywhere in the OT, so this is settled rather than a tie. Promoted to the Hebrew contract as rule 51.
- A purpose le + infinitive construct recast as a finite English clause gives the complementizer 'that' to the preposition and the supplied subject and modal to the infinitive, at high confidence: ISA.60.11 'that men may bring', ISA.60.21 'that I may be glorified'. This is rule 48's reasoning outside the privative min; the corpus puts the 'that' on the preposition in 28 units across 17 chapters and never on the infinitive. Promoted to the Hebrew contract as rule 52.
- Where a verb carries a pronominal object suffix and no preposition morpheme, and the ASV supplies an English preposition only to attach that object, the suffix takes the preposition with its pronoun and heads on the pronoun: ISA.60.7 and ISA.60.10 'unto thee' on the energic suffix of yesharethun, ISA.60.10 'on thee' on the suffix of richamtik. Aligned at high. Not promoted to the contract: the corpus reads it this way in 11 units across 5 chapters but PSA.18.49 gives the 'unto' to the verb unit instead, and no principle separates the two, so a contract rule would contradict a finished chapter.
