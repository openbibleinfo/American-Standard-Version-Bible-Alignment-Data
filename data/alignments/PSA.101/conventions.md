# Psalms 101 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- לְנֶגֶד before a noun (101.3, 101.7) is rendered by the single word 'before' and fuses under rule 30 as one unit on both prepositions, headed on נֶגֶד as the morpheme carrying the content (rule 23's first sentence). The competing reading heads on the opening לְ under rule 23's compound-preposition sentence (as מֵעַל and מֵאֵת do); the chapter took נֶגֶד in both verses and the notes name the alternative.
