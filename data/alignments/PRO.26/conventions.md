# Proverbs 26 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Proverbs' juxtaposed similes were counted rather than assumed. Where a comparative kaph, a correlative ken or a waw stands, it takes the As, like or So the ASV wrote for it (26.1, 26.2, 26.7, 26.8, 26.9, 26.10, 26.11, 26.14, 26.18, 26.21, 26.22); where the Hebrew juxtaposes the two cola with no such morpheme, the word is its own added unit and is not folded into the noun, the participle or the preposition standing beside it (As at 26.9, 26.10, 26.14, 26.21; like at 26.17, 26.23; So at 26.11). One verse may need both readings, as 26.9, 26.10, 26.14 and 26.21 do, where the supplied As opens the verse and a written waw carries the answering So. The genre is not evidence that a particle is present, and the ASV's italics corroborate without deciding (rule 7).
- be-eynaw rendered 'in his own conceit' is split three ways and not fused: be- takes in, ayin takes conceit, and the suffix takes the whole emphatic possessive his own under rule 18. Each morpheme holds an English word of its own, so rule 30's one-rendering condition is unmet and rule 2 governs, unlike the fused idioms of rules 30 and 40 where the preposition and its noun share a single word. Read identically at 26.5, 26.12 and 26.16, and the marginal literal 'Hebrew his own eyes' was read and set aside under rule 38 in all three. Where no suffix stands, a supplied possessive is added as usual (his own at 26.6, his at 26.26).
- In a construct chain the ASV renders attributively (rule 28), each noun takes the English word carrying its own lexeme, whichever of the two stands first in the English: leshon sheqer gives tongue to the construct and lying to the rectum (26.28), ish midyanim gives man and contentious (26.21), kesef sigim gives silver to the construct and dross to the rectum (26.23), and hadre vaten gives parts to heder and innermost to beten (26.22). Position in the English phrase decides nothing; 26.22 is the one place where the division was materially open, and the competing reading is named in that unit's note.
