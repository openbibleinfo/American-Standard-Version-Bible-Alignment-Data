# Job 40 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- A fronted English question word goes to a present הֲ or אִם where it is an auxiliary the clause would not otherwise write — *Shall* at v2, the distributed *Wilt ... Wilt* at v8 — but not where it is the lexical word another morpheme licenses: the *hast* of v9 is the possessive לְ's under rule 43, and the particle is `unrepresented` under rule 17, the question being marked by inversion alone. Where no interrogative morpheme stands, the fronted auxiliary is the verb's own: *Shall any take* at v24 and *canst thou thunder* in v9's second clause.
- A preposition the ASV supplies between a verb and a following bare nominal goes to the verb under rule 44 wherever that nominal or suffix is the verb's own object — *argueth with* (v2), *demand of* and *declare unto* (v7), *Deck ... with* and *array ... with* (v10), *look upon* (v11), *Look on* (v12), *confess of* (v14) — and to the noun under rule 33 only where the verb already carries its object, as at v22, where the object suffix on יְסֻכֻּהוּ leaves צִלֲלוֹ an adjunct and the *with* the noun's own. Two identical constructions in one verse are not to be divided between the two rules; v10 was corrected on that ground.
- The entreaty particle נָא is rendered by a bare *now* throughout this chapter (vv. 7, 10, 15, 16) and takes that word as its own aligned unit headed on it. Rule 30's *I beseech thee* formula is not written here, so nothing of it is to be supplied, and the particle is neither `unrepresented` nor fused into the imperative or presentative standing beside it.
- Where the ASV recasts a clause with a locative relative *where*, this chapter gives the word to the locative morpheme rather than to a connective or to nothing: תַּחְתָּם takes *where ... stand* at v12, its suffix taking *they*, and שָׁם takes *Where* at v20, the waw of that clause being left `unrepresented`. Rule 17's waw-to-*which* recasting is the competing reading at v20 and is named in that unit.
