# Psalms 134 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **PSA.134.1** — Superscription determiners. In `shir ha-ma'alot` -> *A Song of Ascents* the ASV writes an indefinite *A* before the construct noun's word and no determiner at all before the rectum's. The *A* belongs to `shir`, the noun that licenses it under rule 15, and the article on `ma'alot` is `unrepresented` under rule 29's closing sentence, because the ASV writes no *the* for the phrase at all. Nothing is fronted: rule 29's fronting answers to a phrase whose single *the* stands before the construct, and rule 29's indefinite-rectum paragraph governs an *a* standing before the rectum's own word, which is not this shape. Do not read the *A* as a determiner the article could take.
