# Psalms 148 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- An English coordinator written between two juxtaposed phrases goes to a preposition standing where the connective stands only where that preposition's own sense is what the coordinator states. The comitative preposition of `זְקֵנִים עִם־נְעָרִים` → *Old men* ***and*** *children* takes the written *and* under rule 11, since dropping the word leaves that preposition's relation unstated and nothing else diminished. The second `לְ` of `לָעַד לְעוֹלָם` → *for ever* ***and*** *ever* takes nothing and is `unrepresented` under rule 40 while the *and* is `added` under rule 14, since *and* states nothing of that preposition's duration relation; this is rule 14's own reading of `לְיוֹשְׁבֵי יְרוּשָׁלִַם אֶל־אַדְמַת יִשְׂרָאֵל` → *concerning the inhabitants of Jerusalem* ***and*** *the land of Israel*, where a second prepositional phrase with its own written preposition is juxtaposed and the supplied coordinator is still `added`. Rule 26's `בֵּין X לְ Y` and `מִן … עַד` carve-outs were considered for PSA.148.6 and set aside as naming their own constructions. BSB represents that `לְ` and this chapter does not; that is the documented convention difference and not a correction.
