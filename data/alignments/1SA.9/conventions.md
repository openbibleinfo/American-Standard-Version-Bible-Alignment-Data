# 1 Samuel 9 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Temporal clauses in this chapter are asyndetic as often as not, and the subordinator is placed by the count rules 14 and 55 require. Where a fronted `הֵמָּה` opens the clause with no conjunction morpheme in it, the subordinator the ASV supplies is its own `added` unit — *When* (9.5), *As* (9.11, 9.27) — and where the ASV writes a coordinator beside it the two words stand together and are one `added` unit headed on the subordinator (9.14 *and as*). Where a waw does stand at the clause head it takes the whole pair as one rendering, headed on the coordinator (9.17, 9.25 *And when*).
- Supplied English pronouns and nouns denoting a referent are placed by rule 25's condition read on the verse alone. Where the verse writes a nominal for the referent earlier than the clause in question, the further English realization joins that nominal's discontinuous unit: *he* of *he was higher* on `שָׁאוּל` (9.2), *he is a man* on `אִישׁ` (9.6), *it* of *hath it been kept* on `הַנִּשְׁאָר` (9.24), and both *is now called* and *was beforetime called* on the one `יִקָּרֵא` (9.9). Where the verse writes no such nominal, or writes it only in a later clause of its own, the supplied word is `added`: *they* of *there they were not* (9.4), the italic *he* of *he is before thee* (9.12), the italic *Samuel* (9.24). A supplied object pronoun with no object morpheme in the clause is `added` in either case (9.4 twice, 9.24 twice, rule 44).
- `יֵשׁ` is read on the terms rule 50 sets for an interrogative existential clause. In `הֲיֵשׁ בָּזֶה הָרֹאֶה` → *Is the seer here* the one fronted *Is* is the interrogative particle's under rules 17 and 20 and the written `יֵשׁ` is `unrepresented`, nothing being left for it (9.11); where no interrogative particle stands, `יֵשׁ` takes the whole overt frame the ASV writes for it and heads on it — *He is* (9.12).
