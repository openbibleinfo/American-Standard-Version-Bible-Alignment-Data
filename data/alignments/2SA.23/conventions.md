# 2 Samuel 23 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- A pronoun the ASV supplies for a referent the verse names is placed by what it completes, not by what it denotes. A subject pronoun filling a slot a verbless clause leaves empty joins the nominal's unit under rule 25 — 23.5 *For it is all my salvation* goes to `bərîṯ` — while a pronoun completing an English verb's valency where the Hebrew writes no object morpheme is `added` under rule 44, as 23.5 *he maketh it not to grow* and 23.16 *took it* and *brought it* are. A noun standing earlier in the verse is not evidence for the first reading, since a supplied object is supplied precisely because the verse has already named the thing.
- A supplied third-person subject pronoun in a verbless clause joins a participial predicate, whose gender and number are what the pronoun answers to: 23.19 gives *he* and 23.23 *He was* to `niḵbāḏ`. It is `added` under rule 20's closing sentence where the predicate carries no agreement at all — a comparative or other prepositional phrase — and no nominal in the verse denotes the referent, which is 23.4's *He shall be as the light*, the ruler being named in 23.3 and rule 10 making the verse the whole unit of work. The competing reading marks the participial cases `added` as well, on the ground that rule 17's permission names a finite verb.
- `bə` before a numeral rendered *one of* takes the whole partitive expression, the preposition being the morpheme that states the partitive relation: 23.9 `bišlōšāh` → *one of the three mighty men*, 23.24 `baššəlōšîm` → *was one of the thirty*, the copula joining the preposition there under rule 49. Rule 15's `added` partitive is not reached, since it governs a quantifier no morpheme in the clause licenses. The same preposition takes a bare *among* wherever the ASV writes one (23.18, 23.22).
