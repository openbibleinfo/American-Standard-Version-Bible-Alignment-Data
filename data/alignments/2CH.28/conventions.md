# 2 Chronicles 28 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- קָטַר is rendered *burn(t) incense* and heads on *incense* at 28.3, 28.4 and 28.25. The same rule 44 subtraction gives שָׁבָה two heads in this chapter, and they are one analysis: on *captive* where the ASV writes that complement (28.8 *carried away captive*, 28.11 *taken captive*) and on the verb where it writes only the phrasal particle (28.5, 28.17 *carried away*). Read which the ASV wrote before treating the two heads as a split.
- Where an existential הָיָה stands beside a locative שָׁם and the ASV writes a single *there* after the copula — 28.9 וְנָבִיא לַיהוָה הָיָה שָׁם → *But a prophet of Jehovah was there* — the adverb takes that word and הָיָה keeps only *was*. The dummy *there* that rules 20, 21 and 50 hand to an existential unit is a *there* no morpheme in the clause answers to; count the ASV's *there*s against the morphemes that could carry them, as rule 29 counts its *the*s.
