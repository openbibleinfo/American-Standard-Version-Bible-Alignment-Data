# Job 37 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Where a verse writes a nominal and the ASV resumes its referent as the supplied subject of a following clause that writes no subject of its own, both English realizations belong to that nominal's one discontinuous unit, headed on the first: אֱלוֹהַּ → *God ... him* (37.22) and שַׁדַּי → *the Almighty ... He* (37.23). The resuming pronoun is not added under rule 17 and does not join the predicate, which keeps only the construction-supplied copula. Where another morpheme in the clause already holds a pronoun of its own — the object suffix of מְצָאנֻהוּ, which takes the *him* of *find him out* — that morpheme is not a home for the second pronoun.
- Heads on this chapter's multi-word substantive renderings were settled by rule 31's subtraction test, with its tie clause deciding two of them. עָב → *the thick cloud* heads on *cloud* and תֵּבֵל → *of the habitable world* heads on *world*, because the ASV writes a bare *cloud* for עָנָן in the same verse (37.11) and a bare *world* for תֵּבֵל as its ordinary rendering, making the modifier its specifier. נִפְלְאוֹת and מִפְלְאוֹת → *wondrous works* (37.14, 37.16), דָּרוֹם → *the south wind* (37.17) and זָהָב → *golden splendor* (37.22) head on the modifier, their English nouns being supplied generics the ASV never writes alone for those words.
