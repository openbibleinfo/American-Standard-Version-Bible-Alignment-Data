# Matthew 1 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- ἐν γαστρὶ ἔχω is split per token and never taken as one unit: ἐν takes the English preposition and γαστρί the idiom's nominal *child*, while the verb keeps only what the ASV writes for it — *shall be* at 1:23, and nothing at 1:18, where εὑρέθη has already taken *she was found* and the participle ἔχουσα is its own `unrepresented` unit. Rule 94's count test forbids a compound unit where the ASV wrote separate words, and rule 36 gives *with* to the expressed preposition rather than to the verb. Reading the three tokens as one `noncompositional` idiom remains the competing reading and is named in both verse notes.
- Where a fixed two-token subordinator is rendered by a single English word that the first token already glosses by itself, the second token is its own `unrepresented` unit under rule 94's exclusion arm: πρὶν ἤ → *before* leaves ἤ empty at 1:18, and ἕως οὗ → *till* leaves οὗ empty at 1:25. Rule 50's join of ἕως ἄν → *till* is scoped in terms to ἄν, whose own rule forbids leaving that particle empty, so it is not precedent for a fossilized relative or comparative particle. Joining either pair into one `aligned` unit headed on ἕως or πρίν remains the competing reading; a reference comparison reports the empty token as a disagreement, and that report is not a correction.
