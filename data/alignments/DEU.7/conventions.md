# Deuteronomy 7 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Where an anarthrous prepositional phrase stands attributively to a noun and the ASV recasts it as a relative clause - KESEPH WE-ZAHAV ALEHEM to 'the silver or the gold *that is on* them' (DEU.7.25) - the supplied relative and the construction-supplied copula both join the preposition's unit, headed on the preposition. The relative realizes the Hebrew attributive relation exactly as it does for the articleless adjective or participle of rule 41's closing paragraph, and rule 49 places the copula; the word is not an added subordinator under rule 55. The competing reading, marking the 'that' added, is named in that verse's review notes.
