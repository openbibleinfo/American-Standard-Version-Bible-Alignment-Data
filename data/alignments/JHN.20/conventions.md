# John 20 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- τῇ μιᾷ (τῶν) σαββάτων → 'the first day of the week': the supplied 'day' is the article's own, headed there, and the numeral takes 'first' alone (rules 22, 49, as τὸ τρίτον → 'the part'/'third'). Any case preposition rides with the article (rule 21), so JHN.20.1's Τῇ takes 'on the … day'. JHN.20.1 and JHN.20.19 had split on this.
- A fused negative-temporal adverb (οὐδέπω, οὔπω) rendered by an English 'not … yet' pair heads on the negative element, however the ASV orders or separates the two words. JHN.20.9's 'as yet … not' and JHN.20.17's 'not yet' had split on the head.
- Εἰρήνη ὑμῖν → 'Peace be unto you': the supplied copula rides with the dative predicate (rule 53) and 'unto' realizes the case, but a bare dative is not one of the predicate types rule 53 names, so giving 'be' to the subject stays defensible and all three occurrences (20:19, 20:21, 20:26) are confidence medium.
- παρακύπτω rendered by an English doublet ('stooping and looking in', 'she stooped and looked') keeps the whole doublet on the verb, headed on the stooping element; the looking element is defensible, so both occurrences (20:5, 20:11) are confidence medium.
