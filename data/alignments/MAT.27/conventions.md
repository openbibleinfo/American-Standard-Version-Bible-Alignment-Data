# Matthew 27 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- A supplied English object or subject pronoun that English syntax requires of a verb is a rule 7 helper on that verb, not on a coreferential Greek pronoun or antecedent noun that already has its own English word. MAT.27.29, 27.31, 27.34, 27.40, 27.41, 27.43, 27.47, 27.48 and 27.65 all read it this way, and 27.31 and 27.43 do so with an expressed and separately realized αὐτόν standing in the same clause; 27.2 was corrected to match. Rule 103's resumptive-pronoun contrast is narrower than it looks: 3JN.1.13 folds *them* onto Πολλά because the fronted object is resumed in its own argument slot, which is not what a second verb's supplied object is.
- A supplied English preposition that only marks the case relation of an expressed complement belongs to that complement, not to the verb: 27.31 `αὐτόν` → *from him*, 27.2 and 27.58 `Πιλάτῳ` → *to Pilate*, 27.53 `πολλοῖς` → *unto many*, 27.56 `Ἰακώβου` → *of James*, and 27.22 `Ἰησοῦν` → *unto Jesus* after correction. A particle that is part of the English lexical gloss of the Greek verb stays with the verb: *ask for* at 27.20 and 27.58, *rail on* at 27.39 (rule 91's `βλασφημέω` precedent), *witness against* at 27.13 (the κατα- compound), *took off* at 27.31. The test is whether the corpus spends that English word on the lexeme, as rule 91 has it, not whether the phrase reads as one idiom.
- `ἀργύριον` → *pieces of silver* heads on *silver* at 27.3, 27.5, 27.6 and 27.9: rule 66's test makes *pieces* the generic completion and *silver* the gloss of the lexeme. Heading on *pieces* is named as defensible in the 27.3 note.
- Where a complex supplied English frame is carried by an expressed proper preposition, this chapter heads it on the prepositional word: `ἀπό` → *certain of* at 27.9 and `μετά` → *the day after* at 27.62. Rule 67 records that carrier class as genuinely divided, so this follows the JHN/REV/2CO run rather than the EPH/PHP reading, and both unit notes say so.
- An attributive demonstrative occupying the determiner slot under rule 38 does not stop rule 102 from giving the article a supplied nominal: `τῷ δικαίῳ ἐκείνῳ` → *that righteous man* at 27.19 and `τοῦ δικαίου τούτου` → *of this righteous man* at 27.24 give *man* (and its case preposition) to the article, *righteous* to the adjective and *this*/*that* to the demonstrative, as `τῇ δεξιᾷ` → *hand* does at 27.29 with a possessive in the slot. Anarthrous Greek is the contrast and keeps the supplied noun on the adjective: 27.38 `ἐκ δεξιῶν` → *on the right hand* heads on *right*.
- MAT.27.17 carries rule 97's mirror clause, apparently for the first time in the corpus: SBLGNT.MAT.27.17.v012 kept only r01 `Ἰησοῦν τὸν Βαραββᾶν` and r02, the projection carries the excluded base, which is longer than the ASV's bare *Barabbas*, and the surplus `Ἰησοῦν τὸν` is one `unrepresented` unit whose note carries the unit id and the readings that remained. A reference comparison will report both tokens as disagreements, and that report is not a correction.
