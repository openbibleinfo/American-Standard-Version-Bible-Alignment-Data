# Psalms 16 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **PSA.16.6** — Where the ASV recasts a Hebrew possessive clause as English have, the supplied verb belongs to the morpheme that expresses the possession, not to another verb in the clause. In 16:2 the verbless bal tovati alekha is recast as I have no good beyond thee, so the possessive suffix of tovati takes I have; in 16:6 nachalat shaphrah alay is recast as I have a goodly heritage, so al-plus-suffix takes have and shaphrah keeps only its lexeme goodly. 1KI.7.28 settles it: the possessive lamed of a verbless clause carries had. Where hayah is present it is the existential and takes the have itself, leaving the preposition unrepresented (1KI.11.3, 10.26, 4.24); shaphrah is not a copula and so does not.
