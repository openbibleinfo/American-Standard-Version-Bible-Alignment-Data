# 2 Samuel 4 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **2SA.4.5 — retrospective TS07 supersession** — The independently written source components of Ish-bosheth (אִישׁ + בֹּשֶׁת, tagged Np/Np or Ncmsc/Np) each retain their own English name component in 4.5, 4.8 twice, and 4.12. Apply the settled separate-name ownership under rules 2 and 30: each component is its own aligned unit, with its own lexical heads. Where the name is the rectum of a construct chain, the existing 'of' remains with the opening Ish component under rule 19. This supersedes only the former mandatory fusion; the original convention is preserved in data/audits/retrospective-new-chapters-2026-09-12/before/2SA.4/conventions.md. Independent scoped confirmations of 4.5 and 4.8 establish the conflict; other chapter conventions remain in force.
- **2SA.4.1** — Where the ASV writes the name Ish-bosheth for a Hebrew that has only בֶּן־שָׁאוּל (4.1, 4.2), the name is its own added unit headed on 'Ish' under rule 17, and בֶּן and שָׁאוּל keep 'son' and 'Saul's' (rule 29 possessive recast). It is not folded onto either noun.
- **2SA.4.12** — The chapter keeps every English phrasal particle with its verb under rule 44: 'took him up' (נשא, 4.4), 'take you away' (בער, 4.11) and 'hanged them up' (תלה, 4.12), the last read as the fixed English phrasal verb for hanging rather than a surplus direction; the supplied objects 'them' and 'it' in 4.12 are added, since the verbs carry no object suffix.
