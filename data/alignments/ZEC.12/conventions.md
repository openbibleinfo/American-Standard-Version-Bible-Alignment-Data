# Zechariah 12 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **ZEC.12.12** — Treat the reduplicated phrase מִשְׁפָּחוֹת מִשְׁפָּחוֹת noncompositionally as ‘every family,’ with the first source token as the deterministic display anchor. A split assigning ‘family’ to one occurrence and leaving the other unrepresented was rejected because the distributive ‘every’ is licensed by the reduplication as a whole.
