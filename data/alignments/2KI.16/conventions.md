# 2 Kings 16 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Compound prepositions and preposition-plus-suffix pairs are divided by the number of English words the ASV spends on them, not by lemma. One word for the pair fuses under rule 30 — מֵאֵת → from (16.14), לִפְנֵי → was before (16.14), מִפְּנֵי → because of (16.18), עָלָיו → thereon (16.12) — and a word for each morpheme splits under rule 2 — מִן + פְּנֵי → from before (16.3), מֵעֲלֵיהֶם and מֵעַל → from off (16.17), לִקְרַאת → to meet (16.10), עָלָיו → upon it (16.15). The same lemma appearing on both sides of that line is the ASV's doing and is not a split to reconcile.
- The ASV writes his for the article on הַמִּזְבֵּחַ where the Hebrew has no possessive morpheme at all (16.14, twice): nothing else licenses the word, so the article takes it and heads on it under rule 41. Where a possessive morpheme does stand in the clause the article is left with nothing and that morpheme takes the determiner — 16.13's אֲשֶׁר לוֹ is recast as his peace offerings, the suffix taking his while the relative, the לְ and the article are each unrepresented. Read the clause, not the noun phrase's edges, before placing a written his.
- The directive he is read by what the single English word carries. Where the ASV writes a word of its own for the motion it is its own unit (16.9, קִירָה → to Kir); where one English word carries the host's sense and the direction together it fuses under rules 30 and 32 (16.18, חִיצוֹנָה → without); and where the ASV renders the phrase with the host noun's own ordinary word and states no direction at all, the he is unrepresented (16.14, צָפוֹנָה → the north side, where north is the noun's own word and nothing carries the directional force).
- The ASV writes the title king Ahaz anarthrously throughout, so the article on הַמֶּלֶךְ before the name is unrepresented at 16.10 (twice), 16.11 (twice), 16.15, 16.16 and 16.17. An article before מֶלֶךְ standing without the name takes the written the as usual (16.12, three times). Do not supply a the for the title.
