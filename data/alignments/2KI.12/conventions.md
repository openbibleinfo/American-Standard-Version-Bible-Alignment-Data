# 2 Kings 12 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Bare בֵּית יְהוָה after a verb of bringing, coming or finding takes the ASV's supplied preposition into its own unit under rule 33 — *into the house* (12.4, 9, 13, 16), *in the house* (12.10), *for the house* (12.13), *at the house of Millo* (12.20) — with the supplied *the* joining it under rule 29. The one exception is 12.11, where the ASV recasts the Hophal participle הַמֻּפְקָדִים as *had the oversight of*: that *of* is the predicate's own valency and goes to פָּקַד under rule 44, leaving בֵּית the bare *the house*. Read which of the two the English verb is before placing the word.
- The italic *more* of *take no more money* (12.7, 12.8) is `added` in both verses: the negative (אַל in 12.7, בִלְתִּי in 12.8) takes the *no* the ASV recasts onto the object under rule 15, and neither it nor כֶּסֶף licenses the *more*.
