# Song of Solomon 3 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- סָבַב with a following בְּ is divided rather than read as an English phrasal verb: the verb takes *go* and the present preposition takes the written *about* (rule 11's priority of a present function morpheme over a supplied-word analysis). SNG.3.2 (אֲסוֹבְבָה בָעִיר) and SNG.3.3 (הַסֹּבְבִים בָּעִיר). The competing reading keeps *go about* whole on the verb and leaves the preposition unrepresented. Where the ASV instead spends no word on the preposition's own relation — רָאָה בְּ at SNG.3.11, *behold king Solomon* — that בְּ is `unrepresented` under rule 40; read what is written before dividing.
- עַד + שֶׁ is one `aligned` unit under rule 30, headed on עַד as the morpheme carrying the temporal content (rule 23), whichever single English word the ASV writes for the pair: *When* and *Until* at SNG.3.4, *Until* at SNG.3.5. The corpus reads the same pair `aligned` at SNG.8.4 and `noncompositional` at SNG.4.6; this chapter takes the `aligned` reading throughout, and the contradiction in the corpus is why no rule was promoted.
- In the refrain אֵת שֶׁאָהֲבָה נַפְשִׁי the relative שֶׁ takes the supplied antecedent with it — *him whom*, headed on *whom* (rule 46's first rider) — and the אֵת is `unrepresented` (rule 16). This holds even where a verb writing an object suffix of its own stands beside it: at SNG.3.1 the unsuffixed בִּקַּשְׁתִּי takes only *I sought* while the suffixed בִּקַּשְׁתִּיו takes *I sought him*, the two *him*s answering to different morphemes. Same division at SNG.3.2, SNG.3.3 and SNG.3.4.
- In the adjuration formula at SNG.3.5 the oath אִם is what carries the ASV's negation: the first takes *That … not* in one discontinuous unit (rule 25), headed on *That*, and the second fuses with its waw into the single *nor* under rule 35, headed on the particle. Heading the first unit on *not* instead is defensible, the negation being what the particle contributes.
- At SNG.3.7 the possessive שֶׁלִּשְׁלֹמֹה is divided: the לְ takes the written *of* (rule 49) and the relative שֶׁ is `unrepresented`, the ASV writing no relative, on the model of rule 49's אֲשֶׁר לַבָּיִת → *which belonged to the house*. The anticipatory suffix of מִטָּתוֹ is `unrepresented` under rule 22, the possession being written once. The competing reading fuses שֶׁ + לְ into the one *of* under rule 30, as the corpus does for שֶׁלִּי → *sake* at JON.1.12; BSB represents all three morphemes, by its own convention.
