# Acts 6 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Supplied English repeating an expressed token divides by word class in this chapter. A repeated full lexical noun is `added` under rule 103 — 6:9's the* synagogue*, synagoge being spent on t011 and the second attributive article barred by rule 31 from carrying anything — while a repeated preposition is a rule 7 helper on the coordinate member it introduces, 6:11's against* riding with theon under rules 21 and 40. Where the licensing token is an unspent article governing an expressed noun, neither applies and rule 102 takes the supplied words into the article's own phrase, as 6:1's the number of does. The ASV italicizes all three alike, so the italics decide nothing here.
- enopion is unrepresented at 6:5 and aligned to before at 6:6, and the difference is the ASV's. At 6:5 it makes pantos tou plethous the direct object of pleased and realizes the improper preposition nowhere, which is rule 36's absorbed-into-a-predicate arm; at 6:6 it writes the adverb out as a single word, so rules 42 and 71's frame question never arises and the governed article takes a bare the. BSB reports 6:5 as represented; that is a convention difference and not a correction.
