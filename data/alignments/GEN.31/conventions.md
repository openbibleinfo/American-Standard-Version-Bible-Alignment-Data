# Genesis 31 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **GEN.31.16** — When לְ plus a pronominal suffix is rendered by a possessive pronoun with a supplied copula (is ours/is mine), fuse the preposition and suffix in one aligned unit headed on the possessive pronoun.
- **GEN.31.27** — A clause-opening waw rendered by the ASV's displaced complementizer that takes that word; do not leave the waw unrepresented and label that added.
- **GEN.31.38** — A supplied lexical object with no source nominal or object morpheme is added, even when it stands inside a light-verb rendering.
