# Joshua 21 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Compound teen numerals remain aligned rather than noncompositional when their Hebrew components retain additive meaning; in verbless totals, the supplied copula belongs to the counted-noun predicate.
- The compound English preposition 'according to' belongs wholly to its source preposition and heads on 'according'; the following nominal keeps only its own rendering.
