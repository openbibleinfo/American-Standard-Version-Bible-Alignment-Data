# Numbers 16 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **NUM.16.3** — In NUM.16's verbless predicates, ASV's lexical frames ‘Ye take/ye take’ (16:3,7) and ‘seemeth it’ (16:9) are added; רַב retains ‘too much’ and מְעַט retains ‘but a small thing,’ following rule 20's lexical-verb boundary.
