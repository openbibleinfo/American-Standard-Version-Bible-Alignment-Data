# Revelation 19 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **REV.19.1** — The doxology's arthrous predicate nouns take no English determiner (ASV 'Salvation, and glory, and power'), so each unrendered article is its own unrepresented unit rather than riding inside its noun's unit. The same nouns recur in verses 6-7 and are analysed identically there.
- **REV.19.17** — Where the ASV supplies an italicized 'and' to join asyndetic Greek coordinates (19:8 and 19:14 'white and pure', 19:17 'Come and be gathered together'), the conjunction is an obligatory English coordination helper inside the unit of the second coordinate, not 'added'. The italics are evidence of the supply, not a verdict that no source construction licenses it.
