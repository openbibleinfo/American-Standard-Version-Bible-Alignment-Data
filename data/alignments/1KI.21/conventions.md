# 1 Kings 21 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **1KI.21.4** — A dative לְ before a pronominal suffix (לִי, לְךָ in *give me*, *give thee*) is unrepresented and the suffix alone takes the English object pronoun, since English marks the indirect object by word order. Verses 4, 6, and 15 follow this; verse 7 fuses the pair and needs review.
- **1KI.21.2** — בְּעֵינֵי + suffix is a fixed expression: preposition and noun share one unit headed on עֵינֵי for the ASV's compressed rendering (*to thee*), with the suffix keeping its own pronoun. The content noun is not left unrepresented while the function morpheme takes the English.
- **1KI.21.21** — בער אחרי is a fixed expression: the ASV's *utterly sweep away* covers verb and preposition together in one unit headed on the verb, since *utterly* appears only where אחרי is present (cf. 1KI 14:10, 16:3).
