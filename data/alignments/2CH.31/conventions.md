# 2 Chronicles 31 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- הִתְיַחֵשׂ (Vtc) is rendered throughout by the relative clause *(them) that were reckoned by genealogy*, headed on *reckoned*. Where a suffix stands on it (31.16) that suffix takes *them* under rule 18 and the infinitive keeps the rest; where none stands (31.17, 31.18, 31.19) the supplied antecedent and relative join the infinitive's own unit under rule 46. The alternative of heading on *genealogy* under rule 44 was considered and rejected: *reckoned* carries the lexeme.
- אֱמוּנָה is read by what the ASV writes for it, not by its form. בֶּאֱמוּנָה fuses with its preposition to the single word *faithfully* at 31.12 (rule 30), and splits as preposition plus *office of trust* at 31.15 and 31.18, where *office* is the supplied generic and *trust* carries the lexeme (rule 31). The possessive follows the morphology: *their* is `added` at 31.15, which writes no suffix, and is the suffix's at 31.18, which does.
- In this chapter's verbless clauses the copula goes to whichever side the Hebrew makes the predicate, which is not the English word order. 31.12 (וַעֲלֵיהֶם נָגִיד) writes a nominal predicate, so the fronted עַל phrase keeps only *over* and *was* joins נָגִיד under rule 49's closing limit; 31.14 (עַל נִדְבוֹת) and 31.15 (עַל־יָדוֹ) write no nominal predicate, so the preposition is the predicate and carries *was over* and *were ... under*. At 31.15 the preposition and its construct noun fuse to the one word *under*, and the copula joins that fused unit.
- Three distributive or resumptive repetitions are rendered once by the ASV and are marked the same way under rule 22: עֲרֵמוֹת עֲרֵמוֹת → *by heaps* (31.6), עִיר וָעִיר → *every city* (31.19, the repeated noun and its waw both unrepresented), and דְּבַר־יוֹם בְּיוֹמוֹ → *the duty of every day* (31.16, where the preposition and suffix of the resumptive phrase go unrepresented with the noun and the supplied verb *required* is `added`). BSB attaches each repeated element to its own phrase and disagrees on all three.
- An appositional or explicative English word is placed by counting what the morpheme beside it already holds. At 31.16 the לְ of לְכָל־הַבָּא holds nothing else, so it takes the *even* the ASV writes in its slot; at 31.3 the לְ of לְעֹלוֹת already holds *for* and no object marker stands on the phrase, so *to wit* is its own `added` unit under rules 16 and 51. The 31.16 reading is the hedged one of the two and its note names the alternative.
