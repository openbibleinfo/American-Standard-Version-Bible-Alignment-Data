# 2 Chronicles 36 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Regnal-age formula. In `בֶּן X שָׁנָה PN בְּמָלְכוֹ`, the construct `בֵּן` is the verbless clause's predicate and takes the construction-supplied copula together with *old* (rule 20), discontinuous around the numeral phrase under rule 25; `שָׁנָה` keeps its own *years*, the temporal `בְּ` takes *when* (rule 52), the infinitive keeps *began to reign*, and its suffix takes the subject pronoun *he* (rule 18). vv. 2, 5, 9, 11.
- `אַחַת עֶשְׂרֵה` → *eleven* is one fused unit under rule 30, since no English word answers to either morpheme alone, headed on `אַחַת` under rule 23, neither morpheme carrying content the other lacks. Do not split it or mark the second numeral `unrepresented` under rule 22. vv. 5, 11.
- `הַכֹּל` resuming a verb's object: the article takes whatever pronoun or demonstrative the ASV writes beside *all* — *them* at v.17, *these* at v.18 — under rule 41, and `כֹּל` takes only *all*. The article is not marked `unrepresented` and the extra word is not `added`.
