# 1 Kings 14 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **1KI.14.23** — The lexeme bamah, rendered high places, heads on high rather than places: high carries the Hebrew lexeme's content and places is the generic English noun supplied to expand a substantive, exactly as rule 31's dry land heads on dry and rule 24's living creature on living. This follows the convention 1KI.13's review recorded; note that the accepted data elsewhere in 1 Kings is split on this and the split is open work beyond this chapter.
- **1KI.14.9** — The hiphil of kaas rendered provoke ... to anger is one discontinuous unit under rule 25 (the object pronoun intervenes), headed on provoke: the causative verb is the lexical predicate, not a bleached support verb like done in done evil, so rule 24's tie-break keeps the head there rather than on anger. The purpose lamed keeps its own English to.
- **1KI.14.19** — The recurring regnal-summary formula sefer divrey hayyamim lemalkey X is aligned this way: divrey + ha + yamim is one rule 30 unit taking 'of the chronicles' (the article's the is inside it) and headed on divrey at medium confidence; the preceding construct sefer takes its own supplied 'the' under rule 29's no-article-morpheme clause; and the lamed is its own unit on 'of', separate from malkey's 'the kings', because 'of the kings' spends more than one English word and so fails rule 30's fusion condition.
- **1KI.14.25** — The narrative wayehi rendered And it came to pass ... that keeps the apodosis marker that in the verb's own unit, discontinuously (rules 21, 25), rather than marking it added: Hebrew has no morpheme between the temporal phrase and the main verb, so that is English supplied by the frame the verb licenses, exactly as rule 20's supplied copula is, and rule 30 reserves added for English no morpheme licenses at all. The conjunctive waw still takes And separately (rule 14) and the target head stays on came.
