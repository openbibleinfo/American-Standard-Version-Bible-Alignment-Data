# Job 35 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Supplied relative pronoun: read what the relative stands over. An articleless attributive participle takes it into its own unit under rule 41's closing sentence (35.10 נֹתֵן → *Who giveth*; 35.11 מַלְּפֵנוּ → *Who teacheth*), but a relative the ASV writes over a finite clause juxtaposed to a written antecedent answers to no morpheme and is `added` under rule 55 (35.5 שְׁחָקִים גָּבְהוּ מִמֶּךָּ → *the skies which are higher than thou*). The chapter writes both shapes within six verses; the morphology of the predicate word is what decides, not the English.
- Supplied English verb in a verbless clause whose predicate is a preposition: a copula, a *to be* frame or a relational verb joins that preposition's unit under rules 20 and 49 (35.2 לְמִשְׁפָּט → *to be*, headed on *to*; 35.14 לְפָנָיו → *is before*), but a full lexical event verb is `added` under rule 20's supplied-lexical-verb clause and leaves the preposition `unrepresented` under rule 40 (35.8 לְאִישׁ ... לְבֶן־אָדָם → *may hurt* ... *may profit*, both italic). The subtraction test is what separates them: *hurt* and *profit* introduce an event the Hebrew never predicates, whereas dropping *to be* or *is before* removes only the link English writes for a predication the Hebrew already made. Four of this chapter's six BSB disagreements ride on this line.
- Comparative מִן takes *more than* headed on *than* where the ASV writes a separate degree word (35.2, 35.3, 35.11 first), and a bare *than* where the English adjective already carries the comparison (35.5 *higher*, 35.11 second *wiser*). Count the degree words the ASV wrote rather than reading the construction off the Hebrew; 35.11 writes both shapes in one verse. Where the comparative phrase is the predicate of a verbless clause it also carries the construction-supplied copula under rule 49, as 35.2 מֵאֵל → *is more than God's* does.
- Rule-30 fusions of a preposition with its construct noun or negative substantive were left with the status each verse pass returned: מִפְּנֵי → *Because of* is `aligned` (35.12) while לְפָנָיו → *is before* (35.14) and בִּבְלִי → *without* (35.16) are `noncompositional`. Edges and heads are identical under either status, and the corpus uses both in quantity for these same expressions, so nothing in the contract or precedent prefers one. This is recorded so a later reader does not mistake it for a split and re-open it.
