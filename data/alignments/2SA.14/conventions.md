# 2 Samuel 14 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **2SA.14.4** — Gentilic הַתְּקוֹעִית recast as 'of Tekoa': the gentilic takes 'of Tekoa' as its own unit, headed on Tekoa, and its article is unrepresented because the ASV writes no 'the' for it (14.4, 14.9).
- **2SA.14.11** — Oath formula חַי־יְהוָה / חֵי־נַפְשְׁךָ followed by אִם: the adjective takes 'As … liveth' (rule 55) and the oath's אִם is what carries the negation the ASV writes for it — 'not' in 14.11, 'none' in 14.19 — so it is aligned to that word and not left unrepresented.
- **2SA.14.32** — לֵאמֹר rendered 'to say' (rather than 'saying') gives the written 'to' to the לְ under rule 52's written-word sentence; rule 27's unrepresented לְ governs only the bare 'saying' rendering, which stands in the same verse (s010) and is marked that way.
- **2SA.14.9** — Vocative אֲדֹנִי הַמֶּלֶךְ → 'My lord, O king': lord takes its own word with 'My' on the suffix, the article is unrepresented, and מֶלֶךְ takes 'O king' (14.9, 14.22); where the same phrase is not vocative ('my lord the king', 14.12, 14.15, 14.17, 14.18, 14.19) the article takes 'the' as usual.
