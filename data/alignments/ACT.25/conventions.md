# Acts 25 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- A supplied English juncture (*and*, *but*) coordinating a converted circumstantial participle with the clause beside it is folded onto the verb of the clause it introduces under rule 88 throughout this chapter — 25.5, 25.6 twice, 25.9, 25.13 and 25.17 twice — and is never `added`, whether or not the ASV renders the coordinated clauses finitely. Rule 98's `added` seam is not reached anywhere in ACT.25: no two coordinated constituents here are of the same class and each rendered in its own.
- μὲν οὖν stands twice with a single English word written for the pair, and the word decides which particle keeps it under rule 70, the other standing `unrepresented`. 25.4 gives the adversative *Howbeit* to μέν; 25.11 gives *then* to οὖν. This is rule 24's habit and not two analyses, and each unit's note names the competing reading.
- The article of a temporal dative phrase is decided by whether the ASV's noun glosses the adverb's lexeme. τῇ ἐπαύριον → *on the morrow* (25.6, 25.23) leaves *morrow* on the adverb and gives the article only *on the*; τῇ ἑξῆς → *on the next day* (25.17) gives the supplied nominal *day* to the article under rules 22 and 102, headed there, and leaves *next* bare on the adverb. Rule 66's test — whether the English noun glosses an element of the lexeme — is what separates them.
- *the principal men* is written twice from different Greek and is analysed twice accordingly. At 25.2 οἱ πρῶτοι has no expressed noun, so the article takes *the … men* under rule 102 and πρῶτοι keeps a bare *principal*. At 25.23 ἀνδράσιν τοῖς κατ' ἐξοχήν expresses the noun in front of its article, so under rule 31 ἀνδράσιν keeps *the … men*, τοῖς is `unrepresented` (no relative clause being written for it), and κατ' ἐξοχήν takes *principal* as one unit under rules 94 and 39.
