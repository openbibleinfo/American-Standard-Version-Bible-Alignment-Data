# Numbers 26 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The directive he of wamaalah shares its host's unit: maalah is one unit rendered upward, headed on maal (rule 30), in 26.2, 26.4 and 26.62. Rule 32 governs the paragogic and cohortative he only and does not reach the locative Sd, which is what the corpus does everywhere the ASV spends one English word on host and he together.
- In the clan formula the ASV writes an italic of before a bare name where the Hebrew has no le: 26.5 Hanoch, 26.23 Tola, 26.30 Iezer, 26.31 Asriel and Shechem, 26.32 Shemida and Hepher, and 26.40 of Ard, where both words are supplied. Count the le before placing the of. Where the preposition is written it takes the of; where it is not, the of answers to no morpheme, is its own added unit, and is not folded onto the name or onto the following mishpachat to avoid the added (rules 7, 30).
- The enumerated total of the census formula is a verbless clause wherever no hayah stands in it. The supplied were joins the numeral predicate (rule 20) and goes on the numeral the ASV's were stands before, which is the tens numeral in every case here: 26.34, 26.41, 26.43, 26.50. Where a finite hayah is written it takes the were instead (26.7, 26.20, 26.21, 26.40, 26.62), and where the ASV writes no were at all there is nothing to place (26.18, 26.22, 26.25, 26.27, 26.37, 26.47).
- ben in the age formula ben esrim shanah is rendered by the ASV's old and takes it: 26.2, 26.4, 26.62. The competing reading marks it unrepresented, but that leaves old licensed by no morpheme, since the numeral and the counted noun have their own English.
