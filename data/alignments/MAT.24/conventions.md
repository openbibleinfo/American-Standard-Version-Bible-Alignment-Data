# Matthew 24 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Supplied copulas: every verbless clause in this chapter puts the supplied copula on its predicate carrier — the preposition at 24:16, 24:17, 24:18, 24:26 and 24:38, the adverb at 24:23 and 24:32, the adjective at 24:46 — and on the subject only where rules 56 and 68 leave nothing else (τί at 24:3 against a noun-phrase predicate, ταῦτα at 24:8, ἡμέραι in the elliptical comparative at 24:37). Where the ASV repeats 'he is' in a parallel clause whose Greek is verbless (24:26), the repetition is that clause's own and folds onto its preposition; it is not `added`, rule 103 being scoped to lexical content while rule 7 names copulas and pronouns.
- Article and supplied possessive: the availability test of rule 76 is applied across the clause, not the verse, so a genitive pronoun standing in a different phrase does not displace the determiner. τήν → 'her' and τά → 'its' at 24:32 are the article's although αὐτῆς stands in the ὅταν clause; ὁ → 'his' at 24:45 has no genitive at all; τήν → 'their' at 24:45 is rule 76 proper, αὐτοῖς being already spent on 'them'. A free αὐτοῦ or μου in the same phrase empties the article as usual (24:17, 24:18, 24:35, 24:45 τῆς, 24:46).
- Negator with a quantifier against a negator with a plain indefinite: πᾶσα with οὐκ is joined on the single 'no' at 24:22 under rule 87, and the note records that giving 'no' to οὐκ alone stays defensible; μή with the plain τις is split at 24:4, μή taking the discontinuous 'that … no' and τις the generic 'man', because the ASV writes two English words where rule 59's join wants one and rule 69 heads such a pair on the determiner word. 24:6 analyses ὁρᾶτε μή exactly as 24:4 does, μή taking 'that … not'.
- ἕως ἄν → 'till' at 24:34 is kept as one aligned unit so the modal particle is not left unrepresented, rule 50 making that report a correction, although no part of 'till' visibly answers to ἄν as a '-soever' word would. Elsewhere in the chapter ἄν takes its own counterfactual auxiliary (24:22 'would', 24:43 twice), so this is the single compressed instance and not a second analysis.
