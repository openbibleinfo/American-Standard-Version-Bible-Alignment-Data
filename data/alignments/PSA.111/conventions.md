# Psalms 111 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- לָעַד and לְעוֹלָם are read by what the ASV writes for the pair: where it writes 'for ever' the לְ takes 'for' and the noun 'ever' (rule 2; vv.3, 8, 9, 10), and where it writes a lone 'ever' the preposition and noun share one unit headed on the noun under rule 40's adverb fusion (v5 'will ever be mindful', v8 'for ever and ever' second 'ever'). The 'and' the ASV writes between the two asyndetic phrases in v8 is added (rule 14). The alternative of leaving the לְ unrepresented beside a lone 'ever' was considered and set aside.
