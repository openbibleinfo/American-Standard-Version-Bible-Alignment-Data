# Ezra 5 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Aramaic genitive דִּי between two nominals takes the ASV's 'of' itself (5.2, 5.8, 5.13, 5.14, 5.16, 5.17), the construct noun keeping only its own word and a supplied 'the' under rule 29; within these overt relational דִּי constructions, the Td on the rectum is unrepresented where the ASV writes 'of God' / 'of Babylon' bare, and takes 'the' where one stands before its own word. That silence does not extend to the direct construct 'the house of God' in the scoped occurrences at 5.2, 5.15 and the inner chain at 5.16: under current rules 15 and 29 the rectum's Td takes the fronted 'the'. The outer foundations determiner and the overt relational דִּי at 5.16 remain unchanged. Where the ASV writes an English possessive (דִּי מַלְכָּא → king's, 5.17) דִּי shares the rectum's unit and the rectum's Td takes the phrase's 'the'; where the ASV renders the דִּי-phrase attributively ('the gold and silver vessels', 5.14) דִּי is unrepresented under rule 28 and the head noun's Td takes the single fronted 'the'.
- Proleptic pronominal suffixes — the anticipatory suffix before a דִּי-phrase (עַבְדוֹהִי דִּי־אֱלָהּ 5.11), the object suffix resuming a fronted object (בַּיְתָה דְנָה סַתְרֵהּ 5.12), and the object suffix resumed by a written דִּי relative (דִּי פֶחָה שָׂמֵהּ 5.14) — are their own unrepresented units where the ASV writes no pronoun for them; the noun, demonstrative or relative already carries the reference (rule 22). Where the ASV does spend an identifying word in the suffix's place (בֵּהּ־זִמְנָא → 'At the same time', 5.3) the suffix takes it, the competing reading being 'same' added.
- The construct עֲבַר of עֲבַר־נַהֲרָה is rendered by its own lexeme 'beyond' and takes that preposition (rule 19's directional-construct case, 5.3, 5.6); בַּעֲבַר → 'were beyond' fuses the בְּ with the construct under rule 30, headed on עֲבַר, the predicate copula joining the fused unit. The Td on נַהֲרָה takes the written 'the' of 'the River'.
- Aramaic copular independent pronouns in tripartite verbless clauses (מַן־אִנּוּן שְׁמָהָת 5.4, אֲנַחְנָא הִמּוֹ עַבְדוֹהִי 5.11) are unrepresented; the construction-supplied copula goes to the predicate (מַן → 'what ... were', עֲבַד → 'are the servants') under rule 20's tripartite sentence. A waw plus explicit הוּא recast as 'which' gives 'which' to the pronoun and leaves the waw unrepresented (5.8).
