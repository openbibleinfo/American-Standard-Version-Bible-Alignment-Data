# Ezekiel 27 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The trading formula natan plus a bare object: where the ASV writes a single 'for' and it stands before the verb's own Hebrew object, that word belongs to the verb's unit under rule 44 (27:12, 13, 14, 17, 22), and a be standing on the commodity phrase the ASV renders bare is unrepresented under rule 40 (27:13, 17). Where the ASV writes a preposition before the be's own phrase as well, each morpheme takes the word standing before its phrase (27:12, 16, 19, 22). Rule 44's limit about 'a preposition of the object's own' reaches a preposition on the object itself and not any preposition elsewhere in the clause, so count the ASV's prepositions against the morphemes that could carry them and read which phrase each stands before.
- arav plus ma'arav is decided by MACULA's form, not by the English. The construct participle 'orvei ma'aravekh (27:27) is a construct chain, so 'the dealers in thy merchandise' puts the 'in' on the rectum under rule 19; the infinitive construct la'arov ma'aravekh (27:9) takes an accusative object, so 'to deal in thy merchandise' keeps the 'in' with the verb under rule 44. Same English preposition, two analyses, and the verbal form is what separates them.
- machaziqei bedeq is rendered by the single English word 'calkers', so rule 30's one-English-word condition is met and the participle and bedeq share one unit headed on the participle, in both 27:9 and 27:27. The suffix stays outside the fusion and takes its own 'thy', since dropping that word loses the suffix's own rendering. The earlier reading, which left bedeq unrepresented beside the rendered participle and cited rule 22, was corrected in both verses.
- Where the ASV realizes one source item twice, both occurrences stay with that item. 27:21's resumed 'they' belongs to the one hemmah (rules 18, 20, 25), and 27:11's doubled 'were' to the one hayu that governs both coordinated clauses. On 27:11 the competing reading, which treats the first clause as gapped and puts its supplied copula on the preposition heading the prepositional predicate under rules 34 and 49, is recorded on the unit; the distributed reading was taken because the verb is actually written in that sentence.
