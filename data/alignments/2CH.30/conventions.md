# 2 Chronicles 30 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The adverbial `לָרֹב` occurs three times in this chapter and its `לְ` takes an English word only where the ASV writes one for the preposition's own adverbial relation: 30.5 *in great numbers* gives it *in*, while 30.13 *a very great assembly* and 30.24 *a great number of priests* recast the phrase as a nominal quantifier and leave it `unrepresented` under rule 40. The partitive *of* of 30.24 is not the preposition's — it stands before `כֹהֲנִים`, a bare noun with no preposition and no marker on it, and is that noun's under rule 33. Contrast 30.22, where `לַיהוָה` → *of Jehovah* keeps the *of* on the `לְ`, which there governs the very name the word introduces.
- Two of this chapter's recurring multi-word English phrases were decided by rule 31's subtraction and go the same way in every verse: `רֹב` → *great numbers*, *great*, *a great number* heads on *great* (30.5, 30.13, 30.24), and `מַצּוֹת` → *unleavened bread* heads on *unleavened* (30.13, 30.21). In both, rule 31's tie-breaker was considered and does not move the head: the ASV never writes a bare *number* for `רֹב` or a bare *bread* for `מַצָּה`, so the nouns are supplied generics rather than class nouns realizing the lexeme.
- The ASV's verse division cuts the phrase `בְּעַד כָּל־לְבָבוֹ הֵכִין` in two: *pardon every one* closes 30.18 and *that setteth his heart* opens 30.19, while the Hebrew `כָּל` stands in 30.19. Each verse partitions only its own ids, so 30.18 marks the supplied *every one* `added` and 30.19 gives `כָּל` the relative *that* alone, which joins the substantive's unit under rule 46. Nothing is missing and nothing is to be hunted for in the neighbouring verse; `בְּעַד` is `unrepresented` in 30.18 under rule 40, the ASV letting *pardon* reach its object bare.
- `מִן` before `יָד` is read by rule 30's one-rendering condition and so divides within this chapter: 30.6 `מִיַּד הַמֶּלֶךְ` → *from the king* fuses the preposition and the construct noun, because the ASV spends no word on *hand*, while 30.6 `מִכַּף מַלְכֵי אַשּׁוּר` → *out of the hand of the kings* and 30.16 `מִיַּד הַלְוִיִּם` → *of the hand of the Levites* are split, the ASV writing a word for each. This is not a split in the analysis; the count is read off the ASV in each verse.
