# Romans 3 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Versification: the projection places ἐν τῇ ἀνοχῇ τοῦ θεοῦ at the head of 3:26, where the ASV translates it at the end of 3:25. Under rule 37 the English *in the forbearance of God* is one `added` unit in 3:25 and the five source tokens are five `unrepresented` units in 3:26, each side naming the other verse in its note. This is the only place in the chapter where English and Greek cross a verse boundary; nothing else reaches across.
- Three configurations of the article before a modifier are kept apart and must not be levelled. (a) Substantival article before a participle with one English word written for the pair: the word is the article's (3:11 ὁ → *that*, twice, rule 90); with two words written the participle keeps the relativizer (3:22 τοὺς → *them*, πιστεύοντας → *that believe*, rule 32). (b) Second attributive article whose head noun is expressed and stands in front of it: the participle carries the relativizer and the article is `unrepresented` (3:5 ὁ θεὸς ὁ ἐπιφέρων → *God … who visiteth*, rule 31). (c) Article whose modifier is a verbless prepositional phrase: the article keeps the relativizer with any case preposition and the preposition takes the supplied copula or lexical verb (3:19 τοῖς → *to them that* with ἐν → *are under*; 3:24 τῆς → *that* with ἐν → *is in*; 3:26 τὸν → *of him that* with ἐκ → *hath*).
- An English preposition standing over an accusative object stays inside the verb's own unit and the object keeps a bare noun (rule 106's accusative arm, with rule 21 from the other side): 3:5 ἐπιφέρων → *visiteth with* with a bare *wrath*, 3:11 ἐκζητῶν → *seeketh after* with a bare *God*. The *of* before *Jews* at 3:9 is the same principle from the other direction: it is licensed by the supplied frame noun *charge* and stays inside προῃτιασάμεθα's unit under rule 21, not on the accusative Ἰουδαίους.
- Supplied English is decided by rule 103's licensing test in four places, and the outcomes divide: folded where a token's own English is still incomplete — *to be* onto ἱλαστήριον at 3:25 (rule 7) and *cometh* onto διά at 3:20 (rule 62, the standing prepositional phrase being the predicate) — and `added` where no token licenses it, the parenthetical *I say* at 3:26 and both italicized *the God* phrases at 3:29, where θεός is already spent on the subject and its article governs that expressed noun, so the repeat stands in a slot no token governs (GAL.3.20's frame).
