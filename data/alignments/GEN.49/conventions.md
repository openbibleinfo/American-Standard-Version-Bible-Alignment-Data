# Genesis 49 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- In a verbless predicate rendered with a referential it plus a copula, the unsupported it is added and only the copula stays with the predicate.
- Where the ASV recasts a finite clause as a relative and no explicit subject token is present, who belongs to the finite verb as its supplied subject; a waw with no separate connective is unrepresented.
- An unsupported English object noun phrase is added even when it occurs inside a verb's idiomatic rendering; the verb keeps only its licensed verb and particle.
