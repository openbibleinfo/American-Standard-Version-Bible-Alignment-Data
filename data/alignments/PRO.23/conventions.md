# Proverbs 23 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Wisdom substantives completed by a supplied generic noun are headed on the word carrying the lexeme throughout this chapter, under rule 31: `מֵישָׁרִים` → *right things* (23.16), `זָר` → *strange things* and `תַּהְפֻּכֹות` → *perverse things* (23.33), `חָכָם` → *a wise child* (23.24), `נׇכְרִי` → *a foreign woman* (23.27), `זָלַל` → *gluttonous eaters* (23.20, beside the bare *the glutton* the ASV writes for the same lemma in 23.21). The one contested member is `מִמְסָךְ` → *mixed wine* (23.30), headed on *mixed*; *wine* was named as the competing head, because rule 31's tie-break reads the ASV's bare *wine* for `יַיִן` standing in that same verse as evidence that *wine* is a class noun rather than a supplied generic. A later reader revisiting the family should start there and nowhere else.
- Where the ASV recasts a construct nominal of possession or quality as an English head noun with a clause of having, the supplied antecedent, the relative and the possessive verb all join that nominal's own unit and none of them is `added`: `בַּעַל נֶפֶשׁ` → *be a man given to appetite* gives *be a man given* to `בַּעַל`, headed on *given* because the possession is what that noun's own lexeme names (rule 43's closing paragraph), and `רַע עָיִן` → *of him that hath an evil eye* gives *of him that hath … evil* to `רַע`, headed on *evil* because the adjective's own lexeme is written (rules 19, 31, 46). This extends rule 43's `בַּעַל` carve-out to a construct chain headed by a quality word rather than a noun of ownership; the competing reading, marking the *hath* `added` under rule 43's count, was rejected because the whole English expression is one nominal's recasting and no clause stands in the Hebrew beside it.
