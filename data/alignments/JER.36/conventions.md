# Jeremiah 36 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **JER.36.13** — In 'the ears of the people/king,' the rectum article takes the second 'the', while the rectum noun takes the construct relation 'of' with its noun.
- **JER.36.23** — In the chapter's 'it came to pass ... that' frames, the complementizer belongs to the presentative verb when no apodosis waw intervenes.
