# 1 Samuel 22 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- galah + et + ozen + 1cs suffix ('uncover my ear') rendered 'disclose(th) to/unto me': the verb and ozen share one unit under rule 30 headed on the verb (with any supplied relative or auxiliary, 'that discloseth', 'did disclose'); the object marker takes the 'to'/'unto' the recast verb needs to reach its object under rule 16; the suffix takes 'me'; a supplied object 'it' (22.17) is added under rule 44. Applied at 22.8 (twice) and 22.17.
- ka-yyom ha-zzeh -> 'as at this day': the kaf takes 'as at' whole as one rendering headed on 'as' (alternative: 'at' added), the assimilated article and the agreement article are unrepresented, and zeh takes 'this' under rule 41. Applied at 22.8 and 22.13.
- sha'al lo b-YHWH/b-Elohim -> 'inquire of Jehovah/God for him': the bet takes 'of', the lamed takes 'for' and its suffix 'him'. Applied at 22.10, 22.13 and 22.15; le-'orev -> 'to lie in wait' gives 'to' to the lamed and 'lie in wait' to the participle headed on 'wait' (alternative head 'lie') at 22.8 and 22.13.
