# Ezekiel 32 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- `כֹּל` with a pronominal suffix: the construct takes only *all* and the suffix takes the whole *of them*, headed on the pronoun. The suffix fills the rectum slot, so rule 19 places the *of* there and leaves the construct noun its own word. Where the ASV writes no *of* at all, as in 32.12's *are they all*, the suffix takes only the pronoun. Applied at 32.12, 32.22, 32.23, 32.24, 32.25, 32.26 and 32.30.
- `שָׁמָּה` (D + Sd) at 32.29 and 32.30 is rendered by a bare *There*, which is `שָׁם`'s own word and not a fusion carrying the directive: the adverb takes it and the Sd is `unrepresented` under rule 32's last sentence. This matches the plain `שָׁם` -> *There is* / *is there* at 32.22, 32.24 and 32.26, so the whole `שָׁם` series reads alike. Rule 32's `שָׁמָּה` -> *thither* fusion is the other branch and the ASV does not take it here. BSB represents both Sd tokens; that is the documented convention difference, not an error.
- In the repeated `סְבִיבוֹת` grave formula, read whether `הָיָה` is written before applying rule 49. At 32.22, 32.25 and 32.26 no verb stands and the preposition carries the supplied *are round about* under rule 49; at 32.23 and 32.27 a written `הָיָה` takes the copula itself and `סָבִיב` keeps only *round about*. At 32.24 the ASV writes no copula at all, so there is nothing to place.
- The 1cs suffix of `אֲדֹנָי` is `unrepresented` throughout (32.3, 32.8, 32.11, 32.14, 32.16, 32.31, 32.32): the ASV writes *the Lord*, with the supplied *the* going to the title under rule 15 and no *my* anywhere for the suffix to take. These are seven of the chapter's BSB disagreements and none is a correction candidate.
