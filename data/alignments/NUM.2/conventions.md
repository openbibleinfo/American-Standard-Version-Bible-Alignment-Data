# Numbers 2 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- In the encampment formulas, which side of the verbless clause is the predicate decides where the construction-supplied copula goes, and the Hebrew decides that rather than the ASV's inversion (rules 20, 49). Where a participial phrase opens the clause — וְהַחֹנִים קֵדְמָה מִזְרָחָה דֶּגֶל מַחֲנֵה יְהוּדָה at 2.3, וְהַחֹנִים עָלָיו מַטֵּה X at 2.5, 2.12 and 2.27 — that phrase is the subject and the following nominal the predicate, so shall be joins דֶּגֶל or מַטֶּה and the עַל inside the subject phrase keeps only its own next unto. Where no participle stands and the definite construct chain opens the clause — דֶּגֶל מַחֲנֵה X <direction>ָה at 2.10, 2.18 and 2.25, וְעָלָיו מַטֵּה מְנַשֶּׁה at 2.20 — the construct chain is the subject and the locative is the predicate, so shall be joins the directive he or עַל and the nominal keeps only its own English.
- In the census figures the construction-supplied copula joins the numeral that opens the ASV's figure, not the one that opens the Hebrew's: אַרְבָּעָה וַחֲמִשִּׁים אֶלֶף → were fifty and four thousand puts were on חֲמִשִּׁים (so at 2.4, 2.6, 2.8, 2.11, 2.13, 2.15, 2.19, 2.21, 2.23, 2.26, 2.28, 2.30), while מְאַת אֶלֶף heading the figure takes were a hundred (2.9, 2.16, 2.24, 2.31) and שֵׁשׁ takes were six at 2.32. The tens and units words are then matched to each other across the ASV's reordering under rule 25, including where the ASV recomputes the figure: אַרְבָּעָה וְשִׁבְעִים → threescore and fourteen at 2.4 gives fourteen to אַרְבָּעָה and threescore to שִׁבְעִים, the only partition the two numeral tokens allow.
- The direction nouns קֵדֶם, תֵּימָן, יָם and צָפוֹן are rendered the east, south, west and north side (2.3, 2.10, 2.18, 2.25). Side is the ASV's supplied generic and the the is supplied, so both join the direction noun's own unit and the head stays on the direction word by rule 31's subtraction — the ASV writes no bare side for any of these words, so the noun is not a class noun realizing the lexeme. The directive he standing on them keeps whatever separate word the ASV writes for it, On or toward, under rule 32.
