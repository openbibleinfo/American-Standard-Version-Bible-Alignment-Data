# John 5 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **JHN.5.21** — A supplied 'even' is a helper on the word it introduces, never an added unit: ἕως -> 'even until' (5:17), οὕτως -> 'even so' (5:21, 5:26), καθώς -> 'even as' (5:23), Μωϋσῆς -> 'even Moses' (5:45). Recorded high after reconciliation found the chapter split on confidence alone — three verses medium, two high, for one judgment. The corpus settles it: 28 of the 29 NT units whose English begins 'even ' align it as a helper (καθώς x13, οὕτως at EPH.5.28 and JHN.3.14, μέχρι/ἄχρι x4, Χριστός at EPH.4.15), and the single 'added' unit, JHN.1.9's 'even the light', is a whole supplied phrase rather than a bare intensifier. Where the correlatives ὥσπερ … οὕτως stand apart, the 'even' belongs to whichever one it is adjacent to; carrying it back across the clause to ὥσπερ would absorb an unrelated word under rule 15.
- **JHN.5.19** — Where Greek doubles the negative and the ASV realizes it once in an English indefinite, that indefinite takes the whole negation and ου is its own unrepresented unit: ου δυναται … ποιειν … ουδεν -> 'can do nothing' (5:19), Ου δυναμαι … ποιειν … ουδεν -> 'can … do nothing' (5:30). Both verses reached this independently and JHN.3.27 already had it (Ου … ουδε εν -> 'nothing', with ου unrepresented), so the confidence was raised to high here and the rule promoted to the contract as rule 41. This is not rule 33's ου μη, where the two particles stand together and English realizes them jointly as one noncompositional unit; the corpus keeps that treatment in 16 units. BSB marks ου represented in both verses, which is a convention difference rather than a correction.
- **JHN.5.16** — δια τουτο is split, with the discontinuous 'For … cause' on the preposition and 'this' on the demonstrative (5:16, 5:18). The supplied 'cause' is the English preposition's own idiom, not the demonstrative's, and EPH.1.15 already renders Δια τουτο the same way; 5:18 was raised from medium to high to match. Elsewhere in the corpus δια takes 'because of' (10 units), which is the same judgment following the ASV's own words.
- **JHN.5.7** — A substantival article before a participle (rule 32) takes whatever pronominal or determiner slot the ASV leaves free, and the participle takes the relativizer and verb. The ordinary cases are 'He'/'he' (5:11, 5:13, 5:23, 5:24), 'they' (5:25, 5:29 x2, 5:39), 'him' (5:10, 5:24), 'of them' (5:3), 'of him' (5:30), 'who' (5:15) and 'one' (5:45), all matching the corpus (ο -> 'one' at JHN.3.8 and JHN.4.13; τοῦ -> 'of him' at JHN.4.34). Two cases the rule does not spell out were decided here. Where the ASV gives the phrase a determiner and no pronoun, the article surfaces as that determiner under rule 19 and the participle keeps its substantival expansion: ο ασθενων -> 'The' + 'sick man' (5:7). Where another source word has already taken the pronoun slot, the article realizes nothing: αλλος εστιν ο μαρτυρων -> 'It is another that beareth witness', with 'another' on αλλος and ο unrepresented (5:32). Both stay inside rule 32 rather than reverting to rule 22, which governs only where no participle follows.
