# 2 Peter 1 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **2PE.1.15** — A supplied English complementizer belongs to the expanded non-finite complement, not to the governing finite verb: 2PE.1.15 gives 'that' to echein and a bare 'I will give diligence' to spoudaso, as 2PE.1.19 gives 'that ye take heed' to prosechontes and a bare 'ye do' to poieite. PHP.4.14's kalos epoiesate plus supplementary participle is the same idiom and reads the same way, and the corpus is 11 unhedged units to 3 for the non-finite carrier. Rule 74's closing aside says the opposite, but it cites JHN.5.39 alone, which is medium and concedes the infinitive in its own note.
- **2PE.1.16** — A supplied English subordinator that no source token licenses is 'added', not folded onto the verb it stands next to. 2PE.1.16's 'when' before a finite indicative is 'added' because no Greek conjunction stands in the verse for rule 40 to realize; the corpus puts a supplied 'when' on a participle in 66 of its 67 units and on no other finite indicative, the subordination in such cases being the participle's own. Contrast 2PE.1.8, 1.10, 1.17, 1.18 and 1.19, where a participle carries its supplied subordinator as an ordinary rule 7 helper.
- **2PE.1.6** — Where a two-element Greek compound is rendered by two English words that each gloss an element, the target head is the word glossing the compound's own base element, not the modifier and not the first word: enkrateia -> 'self control' heads on 'control' and philadelphia -> 'brotherly kindness' on 'kindness', both the base element. English word order does not decide it, 1PE.1.22 rendering the same philadelphia 'love of the brethren' and heading on 'love', the same phil- element. Rule 66's stated test asks whether the English noun glosses the lexeme, which selects nothing when both words gloss an element.
