# 1 Samuel 15 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- חָרַם hiphil is rendered 'utterly destroy(ed)' throughout the chapter (vv. 3, 8, 9, 15, 18, 20). No infinitive absolute stands in any of them, so 'utterly' is the verb's own rendering and the whole is one unit headed on destroy; the ASV margin 'devote(d)' is read and set aside under rule 38.
- עֲמָלֵק is rendered by the ASV both as the bare name 'Amalek' (vv. 2, 3, 5, 20) and as the gentilic 'the Amalekites' (vv. 7, 18, 20, 32). Where the gentilic is written, the supplied 'the' joins the name's unit under rule 15, headed on Amalekites, exactly as it does on the true gentilic עֲמָלֵקִי (vv. 6, 15); where 'Amalek' is written the name takes only its own word and any 'of' the rectum's relation gives it.
- שָׁמַע בְּקוֹל → 'obey(ed) the voice' / 'obeyed their voice' (vv. 19, 20, 22, 24): the ASV writes no word for the בְּ in this chapter and it is unrepresented under rule 40 every time. The second 'in' of v. 22 ('delight in burnt offerings ... as in obeying') is the distributed repetition of the earlier בְּ of בְּעֹלוֹת under rule 25, not a rendering of the בְּ of בְּקוֹל.
- Toponyms carrying an article — הַכַּרְמֶלָה and הַגִּלְגָּל (v. 12), בַּגִּלְגָּל (vv. 21, 33), הָרָמָתָה (v. 34) — take no 'the' in the ASV, so the article is its own unrepresented unit under rule 29. A directive he standing on the name takes the written 'to' under rule 32 (Carmel v. 12, Ramah v. 34); where none stands, the supplied 'to' before the bare name is the name's under rule 33 (Gilgal v. 12, Gibeah v. 34).
