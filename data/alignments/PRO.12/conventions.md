# Proverbs 12 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Proverbs 12 is almost entirely verbless bicola, and the chapter reads the supplied copula by which side of the Hebrew clause is the predicate rather than by the ASV's English order. Where a prepositional phrase is the predicate the copula joins that preposition under rule 49 — `כְּרָקָב בְּעַצְמוֹתָיו` → *is as rottenness* (12.4), `בְּפֶשַׁע שְׂפָתַיִם ... מוֹקֵשׁ` → *In ... is a snare* (12.13), `עַד־אַרְגִּיעָה` → *is ... for* (12.19), `בְּלֶב־חֹרְשֵׁי רָע` and `לְיֹעֲצֵי שָׁלוֹם` → *is in* and *to ... is* (12.20), `בְּאֹרַח־צְדָקָה` → *In ... is* (12.28). A bare or indefinite nominal standing beside such a phrase is the clause's subject, not its predicate, wherever the clause writes no other subject and the ASV writes no separate English subject for it. Where instead the predicate is a nominal or adjective with no preposition on it, the copula joins that word under rule 20 — 12.1, 12.5, 12.9, 12.10, 12.15, 12.18, 12.22, 12.27, 12.28b.
