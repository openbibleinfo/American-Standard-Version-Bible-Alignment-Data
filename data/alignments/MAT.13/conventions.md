# Matthew 13 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Determinerless governed nouns. Where the ASV writes no determiner for a governed noun — *of heaven*, *of man*, *of fire*, *of teeth*, *of riches*, *of the world* at 13:39 — the governing article is its own `unrepresented` unit under rule 23 and the case preposition stays with the noun under rule 21's determinerless clause, never as a bare *of* on the article. This is the chapter's commonest single decision (13:11, 13:22, 13:24, 13:31, 13:33, 13:37, 13:41, 13:42, 13:44, 13:45, 13:47, 13:50, 13:52) and it accounts for sixteen of the twenty BSB disagreements.
- The similitude frame ὁμοία ἐστὶν ἡ βασιλεία τῶν οὐρανῶν + dative. ὅμοιος takes *like* and ἐστίν takes *is* as separate units in ASV order, and the dative noun carries both the *unto* realizing its case and any supplied *a*, headed on the noun: *unto a man* (13:24, 13:45, 13:52), *unto a grain* (13:31), *unto leaven* (13:33), *unto a treasure* (13:44), *unto a net* (13:47). The *unto* is not the verb's.
- ὁ σπείρων / τοῦ σπείραντος rendered by the agent noun *sower*. The article takes the English determiner (*the* at 13:3, *of the* at 13:18) and the participle takes *sower* alone. Rule 32's pronoun-only limit does not apply because the ASV writes no pronoun and no relativizer; the substantival expression brings its own determiner under rule 22 and the participle is the nominal word.
- An article whose complement is a substantival adjective takes both the determiner and the ASV's supplied generic noun under rule 102, leaving the adjective bare: *the … places* / *rocky* (13:5, 13:20) and *the … one* / *evil* (13:19, 13:38, the latter discontinuous with its *of the*). So *the evil one* is split rather than read as one indivisible title. Where the ASV supplies no noun the ordinary split stands, article to *the* and adjective to itself (13:43, 13:48, 13:49).
- δυνάμεις → *mighty works* heads on *mighty* (13:54, 13:58). *works* is the generic completion and *mighty* carries the δυναμ- sense, which the ASV's own marginal note confirms by glossing the word *powers*. Heading on *works* remains defensible and both verses concede it, but the chapter reads them alike.
- The ASV's *X side* is one English expression on the noun: θάλασσαν → *sea side* (13:1) and ὁδόν → *way side* (13:4, 13:19), with *side* the idiom's completion and the noun the head. Do not split *side* onto the preceding preposition or article, which keep their own *by* and *the*.
