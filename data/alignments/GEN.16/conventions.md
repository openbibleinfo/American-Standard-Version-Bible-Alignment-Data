# Genesis 16 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **GEN.16.3** — In the possessive-resultative sequence לְ + suffix + לְ + noun, the possessive lamed is unrepresented when its suffix alone supplies the possessive, while the following predicative lamed carries the supplied English result frame to be; GEN.12.19 is the exact parallel.
