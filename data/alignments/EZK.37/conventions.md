# Ezekiel 37 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Fused English pronominal adverbs take every morpheme of the Hebrew, including the resumptive one: asher + 'al + suffix -> 'whereon' (37.20), asher + be + suffix -> 'wherein' (37.23, 37.25), asher + sham -> 'whither' (37.21), 'al + suffix -> 'therein' (37.25). The suffix or resumptive adverb is not marked unrepresented under rule 22, because the single English word carries the resumption as well as the relation. Head on the relative where one stands, otherwise on the preposition.
- 'et: MACULA's To (H853) is unrepresented throughout (37.12, 37.13, 37.14, 37.17, 37.19, 37.21, 37.22, 37.23, 37.24, 37.26), the one exception being 37.26 s010, where the ASV spends 'with' on the object relation itself and rule 16's first family gives it to the marker. MACULA's R at 37.19 s030 is the homograph preposition H854 'with' and takes the 'with' written before its own phrase (rule 11); the appositional 'even' beside it is added, since no marker stands on that phrase.
- The predicate-complement lamed of hayah le is unrepresented wherever the ASV writes the complement bare - 'one nation', 'king', 'two nations', 'one shepherd', 'my people', 'their God', 'their prince' (37.17, 37.19, 37.22, 37.23, 37.24, 37.25, 37.27) - and takes the word only where the ASV writes one ('into' at 37.17 s010 and 37.22 s034). In the covenant formula the recasting to a possessive silences both prepositions: hayah keeps the copula, each le is unrepresented under rule 40, and the suffix takes 'my' or 'their'. At 37.24 the clause is possessive rather than copular, so rule 43 puts the 'have' on the present hayah, the le is unrepresented, and the suffix takes the subject 'they'.
- The ASV's continuative 'again' in the revelation formula is the waw's rendering, not an added word: 'Again he said unto me' at 37.4 and 'The word of Jehovah came again unto me' at 37.15, where English writes it after the verb and the unit is discontinuous under rule 25, as rule 51 reads a postpositive 'also'. Recorded because the two verses place the same word differently and a later reader could take the postpositive one for a supplied adverb.
- The divine title adonay YHWH is rendered 'the Lord Jehovah' throughout (37.5, 37.9, 37.12, 37.19, 37.21) and 'O Lord Jehovah' at 37.3: the noun takes the supplied article under rule 15, or the vocative 'O' under rule 36, and the first-person suffix has no English of its own and is unrepresented in all six. This accounts for the Sp1cs block of BSB disagreements, which renders it.
