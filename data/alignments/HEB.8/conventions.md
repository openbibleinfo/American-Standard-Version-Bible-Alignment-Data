# Hebrews 8 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Predicative εἰς in the Jeremiah quotation — the Hebraistic εἰς + accusative after a copula that the ASV renders by a bare predicate nominal (*I will be to them a God*, *they shall be to me a people*, 8.10) — is its own `unrepresented` unit under rule 36, the dative keeping its own *to them* / *to me*. Both instances are analysed alike under rule 24. A reference comparison reports each as a disagreement, and that report is not a correction.
- An italicized noun supplied to complete a substantival adjective belongs to the adjective, headed on the adjective under rule 17: *first covenant* on πρώτη at 8.7 (the article there already emptied by the demonstrative under rule 38) and *A new covenant* on Καινήν at 8.13. But where the supplied noun repeats a head noun already spent in the same verse it is `added` under rule 103 and the demonstrative keeps only its own word: *this* on τοῦτον and *high priest* `added` at 8.3, ἀρχιερεύς having been realized at the head of the verse. The test is whether the verse expresses the noun, not whether the English reads as a completion.
- In the LXX quotation the discourse-level καί that introduces the quoted content clause is aligned to the ASV's subordinating *That* (8.8) under rule 40's principle that a subordinating element takes whatever word introduces its English clause; where no conjunction stands in the clause the same supplied subordinator is `added` under rule 86 (*that thou make*, 8.5, γάρ being already spent on *for*). Hebrews quotes at length, so decide each such word by whether a conjunction is present, never by the shape of the English.
