# Luke 20 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- In a participle-plus-finite-verb chain the ASV renders as coordinate English clauses, an expressed Greek object pronoun goes to the verb that governs it in Greek, and each supplied English object goes to the verb that requires it in English. So 20:15 gives αὐτόν to ἐκβαλόντες and a supplied 'him' to ἀπέκτειναν, 20:10 gives αὐτόν to ἐξαπέστειλαν and a supplied 'him' to δείραντες, and 20:11 and 20:12 give supplied objects to ἀτιμάσαντες, ἐξαπέστειλαν and ἐξέβαλον while the fronted κἀκεῖνον and τοῦτον keep their own. A supplied subject goes to the first verb the ASV makes finite, which is normally the participle: 20:3, 20:20, 20:23 and 20:26.
- A Greek compound or derivative rendered by modifier plus noun heads on the noun that realizes the compound's nominal element: εὐαγγελίζω → 'preaching the gospel' on 'gospel' (20:1), ἀρχιερεύς → 'chief priests' on 'priests' (20:1, 20:19), πρωτοκαθεδρία → 'chief seats' on 'seats' and πρωτοκλισία → 'chief places' on 'places' (20:46), ἰσάγγελος → 'equal unto the angels' on 'angels' (20:36). Where the modifier is what glosses the lexeme and the English noun is the supplied completion, the head stays on the modifier: δεξιῶν → 'right hand' on 'right' (20:42), καταξιόω → 'accounted worthy' on 'worthy' (20:35).
- A genitive article whose English determiner is 'the' carries the case preposition even when the ASV writes a quantifier between them: παντὸς τοῦ λαοῦ → 'of all the people' gives the discontinuous 'of … the' to τοῦ and a bare 'all' to πάντος (20:45), matching the eight contiguous 'of the' units at 20:10, 20:13, 20:15, 20:20, 20:26, 20:27, 20:36 and 20:39.
- προσέθετο plus an infinitive (20:11, 20:12) is read as the Semitic iterative auxiliary: προσέθετο takes the English 'yet' with the supplied subject, discontinuously, and the infinitive πέμψαι carries the English finite verb 'sent'. Folding 'yet' into the infinitive's unit is the competing reading and is named in both verses.
- 20:27–28 is a rule 37 versification split. καὶ … ἐπηρώτησαν αὐτόν closes 20:27 in the projection and the ASV translates it at the head of 20:28, so ἐπηρώτησαν and αὐτόν are unrepresented in 20:27 and 'and they asked him' is the chapter's single added unit, in 20:28. The BSB comparison reports both source tokens as disagreements, and that report is not a correction.
- ἕως ἄν → 'Till' (20:43) is one aligned unit on both tokens. No part of the single English word answers separately to ἄν, but rule 50 forbids leaving the particle unrepresented and no modal or '-soever' word stands here for it, so rule 39's test joins the pair rather than emptying either. Aligning 'Till' to ἕως alone is the reading rule 50 rejects, and the unit says so.
