# Psalms 90 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **PSA.90.4** — Restrictive 'but' meaning 'only' ('Are but as yesterday' v4, 'is their pride but labor' v10) answers to no morpheme and is its own added unit under rule 30; it is not folded into the comparative kaf, the copula or the predicate noun beside it. Both occurrences in this chapter are treated alike.
- **PSA.90.15** — Where MACULA tags a noun construct with a finite clause standing under it (ימוֹת עִנִּיתָנוּ, שְׁנוֹת רָאִינוּ), the finite verb is the rectum: the ASV's 'wherein' together with the verb's subject and auxiliaries goes to that verb under rule 19, while the construct noun keeps only its supplied 'the' (rule 29) and its own word. Rule 52's absolute-noun reading, which marks such a relative added, does not arise because the tag is construct, and the asyndetic second phrase's 'And' is added under rule 14.
