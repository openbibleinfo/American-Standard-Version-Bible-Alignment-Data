# Mark 2 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- παραλυτικός is rendered by the ASV's phrase *sick of the palsy*, expanded at 2.3 to *a man sick of the palsy*. The whole phrase is one aligned unit on the adjective, headed on *palsy*, which glosses the lexeme, with *man* the generic completion under rule 66 and *sick of the* its supplied frame. Read this way at 2.3, 2.4, 2.5, 2.9 and 2.10; heading on *sick* remains defensible and is recorded in the 2.3 note.
- The chapter carries the ἐγένετο + infinitive frame twice and places the supplied complementizer *that* differently, because the Greek differs rather than the ASV. At 2.15 a preposition governs an articular infinitive (ἐν τῷ κατακεῖσθαι), so ἐν takes the clause-introducer under rule 61 and the article is unrepresented. At 2.23 the infinitive is bare and ἐν τοῖς σάββασιν is an ordinary temporal phrase, so the complementizer belongs to παραπορεύεσθαι under rule 85 and ἐν takes *on*. Rule 24 is not in question: it forbids following the surface where one construction is rendered two ways, and here two constructions are rendered alike.
- σάββατον rendered *sabbath day* is one aligned unit headed on *sabbath*, *day* being the supplied generic completion under rule 66. Read this way at 2.23 and 2.24; where the ASV writes a bare *sabbath* (2.27 twice, 2.28) nothing is supplied and the question does not arise.
