# Philippians 2 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **PHP.2.20** — τὰ περί + pronoun: where the ASV supplies an English preposition governing the phrase, it realizes περί and not the verb, even when English idiom makes the verb phrasal (care *for*). PHP.2.23 gives *with* to περί; PHP.2.20 now gives *for* to περί rather than to μεριμνήσει. Where the ASV supplies no preposition at all (PHP.2.19 *when I know your state*), περί stays unrepresented. The article keeps the supplied nominal (*state*, *how it will go*) in every case.
