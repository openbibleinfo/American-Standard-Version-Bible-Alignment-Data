# Psalms 84 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **PSA.84.0** — Title formulae: לַמְנַצֵּחַ → 'the Chief Musician' is one unit on the participle headed on Musician (the class noun; Chief is the defensible competing head); עַל in 'set to the Gittith' takes the whole fixed 'set to' and heads on the relational 'to', with the article and Gittith each taking their own word. Recorded so a later reader does not re-litigate the head choices in the title.
