# Joshua 7 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- JOS.7.3 compresses two Hebrew numeral phrases (ke-alpayim ish o ki-shloshet alafim ish) into one English phrase, about two or three thousand men, and the count runs word by word. The single written about stands before two, so the first comparative kaph takes it and the second is unrepresented under rule 22; the counted noun runs tail-to-tail, so the last ish takes men and the first is unrepresented; and the dual alpayim takes two while the single written thousand closes the whole figure on alafim. Read which numeral each English word stands before rather than preferring a position.
- The distributive la-gevarim is divided the same way in JOS.7.14, 7.17 and 7.18: the lamed takes the by of man by man, gever takes both occurrences of man as one discontinuous unit under rule 25, and the assimilated article is unrepresented because the ASV writes no the for the phrase.
- JOS.7.7 and 7.8 open the same prayer with different Hebrew, and each is divided by what the ASV wrote. In 7.7 ahah takes Alas and the vocative O joins the base noun of the divine title under rule 36. In 7.8 the entreaty particle bi takes the Oh written for it, a present morpheme taking its English before a supplied vocative reading does, and the base noun keeps the bare Lord. The 1cs suffix of the title is unrepresented in both under rule 57.
