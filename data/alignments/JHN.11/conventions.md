# John 11 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **JHN.11.21** — The modal particle ἄν is aligned, never unrepresented. In a counterfactual apodosis the English modal auxiliary is what realizes it — <had> in 'my brother had not died' (11:21, 11:32) — and the lexical verb stands alone. Where the ASV compresses a relative and ἄν into one word, the two tokens form one aligned unit: ὅσα ἄν -> whatsoever (11:22).
- **JHN.11.4** — An article standing before a proper name has no English realization and is its own unrepresented unit (rule 23). This chapter applies it in 22 verses; ὁ Ἰησοῦς, ἡ Μάρθα, ἡ Μαριάμ and τὸν Λάζαρον are all of them.
- **JHN.11.16** — Where the ASV supplies a possessive and no genitive pronoun is present to take it, the article is what the possessive realizes, with any case preposition riding along: τοῖς συμμαθηταῖς -> unto his (11:16), τοῦ ἀδελφοῦ -> their (11:19), τοὺς ὀφθαλμοὺς -> his (11:41). Where a genitive pronoun is present the article is unrepresented instead (rule 20).
- **JHN.11.37** — A substantival article before a participle supplies whatever nominal the ASV gives it and nothing else, and the relativizer rides with the participle: ὁ -> he / that believeth (11:25), ὁ -> even he (11:27), ὁ -> man under a preceding demonstrative (11:37), τοῦ -> of him / that was dead (11:39). Where the ASV supplies no nominal at all the article is unrepresented and the relativizer still goes to the participle (11:45). An adjectival complement is the contrast: τοῦ τυφλοῦ -> of him that / was blind keeps the relativizer on the article (11:37, rule 22).
