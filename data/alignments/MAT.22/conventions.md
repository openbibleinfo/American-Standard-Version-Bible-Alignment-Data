# Matthew 22 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- γάμος and its family take one head policy across the chapter. Where the ASV writes *marriage feast* for the single noun (22:2, 22:3, 22:4, 22:9) the phrase is one unit headed on *marriage*, *feast* being the generic completion under rules 66 and 91; where it writes a bare *wedding* the noun stands alone (22:10), and the attributive genitive γάμου is *wedding* alone (22:11, 22:12). The same test decides the verb: 22:30's γαμίζονται → *are given in marriage* heads on *marriage*, because the corpus spends the γαμ- root on *marry*, *marriage* and *wedding* and never on *give*, and rule 17 forbids choosing *given* merely because it is the English phrase's formal verb head. The ASV's *in* inside that phrase answers to no Greek preposition and stays inside the verb's unit.
- ἐπερωτάω: the object the English verb requires but the Greek leaves unexpressed rides inside the verb's unit as a rule 7 helper, headed on the *ask* word under rule 91 — *asked … a question* at 22:35 and 22:41, *ask … questions* at 22:46, and at 22:35 the supplied *him* as well under rule 74's supplied-object contrast rather than `added` under rule 103. 22:23's bare *they asked* is the same analysis with nothing supplied. Do not read *a question* as a light-verb complement to head on: the Greek expresses no second element, so rule 73 is not in question.
- Where the ASV recasts a Greek impersonal plus dative experiencer as a personal English verb, the dative is what the supplied English subject pronoun realizes and takes it as its own unit: σοι δοκεῖ → *thinkest thou* gives *thou* to σοι at 22:17, and ὑμῖν δοκεῖ → *think ye* gives *ye* to ὑμῖν at 22:42, the verb standing alone in both. Where the ASV writes no pronoun the dative has nothing to take and is `unrepresented`: 22:16's οὐ μέλει σοι → *carest not* leaves σοι empty, the English verb's inflection carrying the person. Folding σοι into μέλει's unit there remains defensible; a reference comparison reports it as a disagreement.
- *together* is decided by whether a Greek preposition stands to answer to it. At 22:10 and 22:41 nothing does, so it realizes the συν- preverb and rides inside the verb's own unit under rule 106. At 22:34 συνήχθησαν ἐπὶ τὸ αὐτό → *gathered themselves together* has one, so rule 106's scope clause hands the pair to rule 94: ἐπὶ τὸ αὐτό is one `noncompositional` unit taking *together* headed on the governed word, the article τό is its own `unrepresented` unit under rule 93, and the verb keeps only *gathered themselves*, the reflexive being a rule 7 helper.
- A substantival article before a participle is split by what the ASV wrote for the participle, not by the article's own class. Where the ASV renders the participle as a finite relative clause the article keeps only the pronoun under rule 32 — *them* / *that were bidden* at 22:3 and 22:4, *they* / *made light of it* at 22:5, *they* / *that were bidden* at 22:8, *they* / *that say* at 22:23, *that* / *which was spoken* at 22:31. Where the ASV renders it by a plain nominal instead, no relative clause exists for the article to absorb and the determiner is the article's own: 22:11's τοὺς ἀνακειμένους → *the guests* gives *the* to the article and *guests* to the participle, on the line rules 19, 51, 57, 58, 92 and 102 hold throughout — an article that is present takes whatever determiner the ASV writes. Rule 22's closing sentence, read flat, would empty that article; it is scoped here to the relative-clause rendering, and emptying the article and giving *the guests* whole to the participle remains the competing reading.
- 22:32 source-projection correction (documented 2026-09-07): commit `f9480c8fa` corrected the placement of SBLGNT.MAT.22.32.v021 r01 by anchoring the apparatus unit at its TEI position. The earlier convention's surplus-token and added-predicate conclusions relied on the mispositioned variant token and no longer describe this projection.
