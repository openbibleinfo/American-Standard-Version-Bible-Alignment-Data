# Genesis 33 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **GEN.33.12** — A supplied possessive with no source possessive morpheme is a separate added unit even when it intervenes inside a source verb's discontinuous light-verb rendering: 'our' in 'Let us take our journey' and 'their' in 'have their young'.
