# Psalms 3 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in `AGENTS.md` once the chapter is finished.

- **PSA.3.0** — The superscription is verse 0, aligned like any other verse: ASV's unnumbered descriptive title against the Hebrew verse the MT numbers 1.
- **PSA.3.5** — A paragogic or cohortative he, and a paragogic nun, have no English realization and are their own unrepresented units. These are the bulk of the BSB disagreements in the Psalms, which attaches them to their host word.
