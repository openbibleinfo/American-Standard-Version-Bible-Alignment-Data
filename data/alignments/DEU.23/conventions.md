# Deuteronomy 23 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- `נָשַׁךְ` in the interest laws is rendered *lend upon interest* and *lent upon interest*, and every occurrence heads on the English verb rather than on *interest*: the Hebrew verb's own lexeme is the lending, and the noun is the phrasal complement English needs in order to state it, not a bleached light verb's complement. All four occurrences are read that way — 23.19 *thou shalt not lend upon interest* and *is lent upon interest*, 23.20 *thou mayest lend upon interest* and *thou shalt not lend upon interest*. Rule 44's light-verb head was the competing reading and is named in both verses under rule 8.
- An English relative adverb goes to the morpheme carrying the local or directional sense it is built on, and a connective whose sense that word does not carry is left `unrepresented` beside it. At 23.12 `שָׁמָּה` with its directive `he` takes the whole of *whither* while the waw of `וְיָצָאתָ` is `unrepresented`, because *whither* says the direction but not the coordination; at 23.16 `בְּ` takes the bare *where* of *where it pleaseth him best*, no relative or resumptive morpheme standing in that clause. Where a relative morpheme is written it joins the fusion instead, as `אֲשֶׁר ... שָׁמָּה ה` → *whither* does at 23.20 under rule 22. Rule 17's sentence giving a recast relative to the waw was the competing reading at 23.12 and is named there.
