# 1 Samuel 31 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **1SA.31.9** — The bare recipient of piel בִּשַּׂר ('carry the tidings unto X') is the verb's direct object, not a goal accusative: the supplied 'unto' joins the verb's unit under rule 44's supplied-preposition clause (head stays on 'tidings'), while a marker on a coordinated recipient takes its own 'to' under rule 16. Contrast v10's בֵּית עַשְׁתָּרוֹת, a locative accusative of שׂים, where 'in' is the noun's under rule 33.
- **1SA.31.6** — Where the ASV writes a coordinator for גַּם standing with no waw before it (גַּם כָּל־אֲנָשָׁיו → 'and all his men'), the additive particle is the morpheme that licenses the word under rule 11 and takes it; it is not marked added under rule 14's asyndeton sentence. Where a waw does stand with גַּם, the waw takes the connective and גַּם takes the 'also'/'likewise' written for it, as in v5.
