# Deuteronomy 18 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Where the ASV renders a finite Hebrew verb adverbially and a complement infinitive carries the English predicate, the adverb belongs to the finite verb and the English verb to the infinitive: יָזִיד ... לְדַבֵּר דָּבָר -> 'shall speak a word presumptuously' (18.20) gives 'presumptuously' to יָזִיד and 'shall speak' to the infinitive, and לֹא אֹסֵף לִשְׁמֹעַ -> 'Let me not hear again' (18.16) gives 'Let me ... again' to אֹסֵף and 'hear' to the infinitive. Where no second verb morpheme stands, the English predicate the ASV supplies to complete that adverbial rendering stays inside the finite verb's own unit rather than being marked added: הֵיטִיבוּ אֲשֶׁר דִּבֵּרוּ -> 'They have well said that which they have spoken' (18.17) keeps 'said' with הֵיטִיבוּ, headed on 'well' under rule 44.
- In the divination list of 18.10-11 the ASV writes one English agent noun for a whole Hebrew phrase. חֹבֵר חָבֶר -> 'a charmer' and דֹרֵשׁ אֶל־הַמֵּתִים -> 'a necromancer' are read as rule 30 fusions headed on the participle, with the supplied indefinite article joining the nominal expression under rule 15. The article of הַמֵּתִים stands outside the fused unit as unrepresented, since the English word carries no article's sense — the division rule 30 already makes for דִּבְרֵי הַיָּמִים -> 'chronicles'.
