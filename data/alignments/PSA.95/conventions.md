# Psalms 95 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- A predicative לְ + pronominal suffix that the ASV renders by a bare English possessive with a construction-supplied copula — לוֹ → 'is his' (95.5), 'are his' (95.4) — is one fused unit of the preposition and the suffix under rule 49's closing paragraph, headed on the possessive, with the copula joining the fused unit; the לְ is the verbless clause's own predicate, which is the boundary rule 40 draws against an adjunct dative rendered 'his'. The split reading (לְ → 'is', suffix → 'his') exists in the corpus (e.g. DEU.32.35) and is named in each unit's note; this chapter took the fusion in both places.
