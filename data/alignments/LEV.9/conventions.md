# Leviticus 9 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- ka'asher is fused to the ASV's single 'as' under rule 30 and headed on the preposition ke, which is the morpheme carrying that lexeme; the relative asher has no 'as' of its own. Taken so at 9.7, 9.10 and 9.21. Heading the fused unit on the relative was the competing reading and is rejected chapter-wide.
