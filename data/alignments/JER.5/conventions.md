# Jeremiah 5 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Interrogative particles take the question auxiliary the ASV fronts for them, and the verb keeps its subject pronoun and lexical verb: הֲ → *do* (5.3, in a clause with no verb to take it), *Shall* (5.9, 5.29), and the אִם opening the following disjunctive question → *shall* (5.9, 5.29), *will* (5.22). Where the ASV marks the question by inversion alone and writes no auxiliary, the particle is `unrepresented`: הַאוֹתִי לֹא תִירָאוּ → *Fear ye not me* (5.22). Where no interrogative morpheme is present, the fronted *do* is the verb's own: לֹא שָׁפָטוּ → *do they not judge* (5.28). An interrogative that has an English word of its own — מָה → *what* (5.15, 5.31), אֵי → *How* (5.7) — leaves the auxiliary to the verb.
- בְּגוֹי אֲשֶׁר כָּזֶה (5.9 and 5.29, which are the same verse but for 5.9's extra waw) splits the correlative: כְּ → *such* and אֲשֶׁר → *as*, each present morpheme taking a word the ASV wrote. The competing reading gives *such ... as* wholly to כְּ and leaves אֲשֶׁר `unrepresented`. Both verses are aligned the same way and must be changed together if ever changed.
- עַל־כֵּן (5.6 *Wherefore*, 5.27 *therefore*) and לָכֵן (5.2 *surely*, 5.14 *Wherefore*) fuse under rule 30 and head on the first token, which is the one MACULA gives the compound lemma; heading on כֵּן is the competing reading. All four are headed the same way. Where the fused expression is instead an interrogative, the head is the wh-word: תַּחַת מֶה → *Wherefore* heads on מֶה (5.19), אֵי לָזֹאת → *How* on אֵי (5.7).
- A contiguous interrogative expression the ASV renders by one English word fuses whole under rule 30 even where the English carries only the wh-word's sense and the rest goes unrendered: תַּחַת מֶה → *Wherefore* (5.19) and אֵי לָזֹאת → *How* (5.7) are each one unit, not one rendered morpheme beside `unrepresented` neighbours. Rule 30's disclaimer that such an expression need not be idiomatic is general rather than local to its אִשָּׁה זוֹנָה example, and rule 35's *carries every morpheme's sense* governs fusion across distance, not this — בְּיַד → *by* fuses a lexeme the English drops. 5.7 was reconciled to 5.19 on this ground.
