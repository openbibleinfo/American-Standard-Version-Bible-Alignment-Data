# 2 Kings 25 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **2KI.25.3** — ASV supplements drawn from the Jer 52 parallel with no variant unit recorded are read by what stands in the slot. Where no token stands there at all — the italic 'fourth' of 25.3, answering to Jer 52:6's הָרְבִיעִי — the word is its own `added` unit; it is not folded into חֹדֶשׁ as a helper. Where a token does stand and the construction it heads would itself license the word — 25.10 'were with the captain', whose 'with' answers to Jer 52:14's אֶת before the bare predicate רַב — the word stays on that token under rules 33 and 53, with the copula of the verbless relative joining it under rule 20 and a note naming the divergence.
- **2KI.25.17** — In the measurement clauses שְׁמֹנֶה עֶשְׂרֵה אַמָּה קוֹמַת הָעַמּוּד → 'The height of the one pillar was eighteen cubits' and וְקוֹמַת הַכֹּתֶרֶת שָׁלֹשׁ אַמּוֹת → 'the height of the capital was three cubits', the numeral phrase is the predicate and the construction-supplied copula joins the counted noun אַמָּה in both cola, not the numeral; the compound numeral fuses to one word (rule 30) and keeps nothing else. Placement on the numeral is the competing reading.
- **2KI.25.30** — דְּבַר־יוֹם בְּיוֹמוֹ → 'every day a portion' reads the repetition on rule 22's distributive terms although it is joined by בְּ rather than a waw: the first יוֹם takes 'every day' with the supplied determiner under rule 15, and the בְּ, the second יוֹם and its suffix are each their own `unrepresented` unit. The four morphemes are not fused under rule 30, for the reason rule 22 gives about a distributive noun the ASV spends a word on.
- **2KI.25.30** — The topicalizing 'for' of 'and for his allowance' (וַאֲרֻחָתוֹ), standing before a fronted nominal that carries no marker and no preposition, is `added` on the terms rule 16 gives 'as for'; it is not placed on the noun under rule 33, since a nominal's fronting is not a case-function. The finite נִתְּנָה takes the presentative 'there was … given' under rule 17, so the appositional אֲרֻחַת keeps only 'a allowance'.
- **2KI.25.19** — סָרִיס אֶחָד → 'an officer' gives the 'an' to אֶחָד, the numeral used as an indefinite marker being the morpheme whose sense that word carries (rule 11), and leaves the noun its bare 'officer'. The competing reading — אֶחָד unrepresented and 'an' on the noun under rule 15 — is named in the note; the chapter's other two אֶחָד are ordinary 'one'.
- **2KI.25.2** — וַתָּבֹא הָעִיר בַּמָּצוֹר → 'So the city was besieged' gives the auxiliary 'was' to the finite verb and fuses the preposition and siege noun to 'besieged' under rules 30 and 40, with the assimilated article separately unrepresented. The earlier analogy to הַיּוֹם → 'to day' is superseded by the independently confirmed LS01 scoped review recorded below. The competing reading leaves the verb unrepresented with 'was besieged' whole on the phrase.
- **2KI.25.2** — Established-rule LS01 scoped review of 2KI.25.2: s005+s007 jointly render t005 'besieged', aligned and headed on the siege noun s007. Article s006 is unrepresented: this predicate expresses no definite determination, so rules 12/29/30 do not license the article-inclusive temporal/spatial adverb analogy. This supersedes only the earlier article-fusion clause; the finite verb's auxiliary allocation and all other chapter judgments were outside this review.
