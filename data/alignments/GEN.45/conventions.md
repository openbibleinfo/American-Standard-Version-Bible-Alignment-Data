# Genesis 45 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **GEN.45.6** — Rule 20 supplies only the copula in verbless predicate recastings; an unsupported presentative, cleft, or impersonal dummy is a separate added unit.
- **GEN.45.22** — אִישׁ rendered as ‘each man’ heads on ‘man,’ the word carrying the noun’s lexeme; ‘each’ supplies the distributive force.
- **GEN.45.6** — Supersedes the earlier GEN.45.6 entry, which was too wide. The presentative dummy “there” of a verbless clause is never `added`: it joins the unit bearing the construction-supplied copula under rule 20, whatever that unit is — in this chapter the adverb עוֹד of GEN.45.6 and GEN.45.11 (“there are yet five years”) — exactly as it joins the finite verb of GEN.45.1 (“there stood”) under rule 17 and the existential אַיִן of GEN.45.6 under rule 50. What stays `added` is a dummy rule 20 does not name: the cleft “it” of GEN.45.8 and GEN.45.12 and the impersonal “It” of GEN.45.28, for which the rule supplies the copula and nothing more.
