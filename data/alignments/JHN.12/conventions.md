# John 12 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- A supplied English object pronoun (it, them) is a helper on the verb that licenses it, never 'added': 12.28 'I have glorified it' and 'will glorify it', 12.29 'heard it', 12.42 'they did not confess it', 12.47 'keep them'. The corpus carries no 'added' object pronoun anywhere, so this is settled rather than a judgment call.
- A generic Greek article the ASV renders with the indefinite determiner is aligned to that determiner, not left unrepresented: 12.24 'ho kokkos' -> 'a grain' gives 'a' to the article. Rule 19 names only 'the', but the corpus aligns the article to 'a' in every instance it contains (JHN.6.60, JHN.7.51, JHN.8.44, REV.4.7).
