# Exodus 10 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **EXO.10.3** — Throughout EXO.10, שׁלח rendered as discontinuous let ... go heads on go, because let is the light verb and go carries the release lexeme.
- **EXO.10.15** — A single waw rendered by the correlative pair either ... or takes both words in one discontinuous unit, headed on or; the parallel prepositions remain unrepresented when ASV writes no preposition.
