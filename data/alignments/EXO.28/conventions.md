# Exodus 28 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- In the repeated בוא + אל construction rendered ‘go in unto,’ ‘in’ belongs to the phrasal verb and ‘unto’ belongs to אל.
- In חֹשֶׁן הַמִּשְׁפָּט rendered ‘the breastplate of judgment,’ the article on the nomen rectum takes the fronted ‘the’; the construct noun takes ‘breastplate.’
