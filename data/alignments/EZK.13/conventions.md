# Ezekiel 13 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה (8 occurrences: 13.3, 13.8 twice, 13.9, 13.13, 13.16, 13.18, 13.20) is aligned as three units and always the same way: the supplied the joins אֲדֹנָי as the nominal it determines (rules 15, 29), the 1cs suffix on אֲדֹנָי is unrepresented because the ASV writes no my, and יְהֹוִה takes Jehovah. Where the formula is the predicate of a verbless clause the copula joins אֲדֹנָי with the the (13.9, am the Lord). This is the chapter's largest BSB divergence and is convention, not error: BSB renders that suffix.
- The daub idiom (13.10, 13.11, 13.12, 13.14, 13.15) is read one way throughout. תָּפֵל is one substantive rendered untempered mortar, headed on untempered (rule 31). The with by which the English verb reaches that bare accusative joins the verb's or participle's unit, discontinuous around whatever stands between (rule 44). An English it after the verb goes to a present אֵת+suffix (13.10 s024, 13.15 s014) and is its own added unit where no suffix stands (13.11 t006, 13.12 t022); it is never folded into the verb's rendering.
- A supplied with before a bare accusative divides by what the Hebrew noun is, not by the English word. The second accusative of the double-accusative טוּחַ (13.10, 13.11, 13.14, 13.15) and the object of בָּקַע (13.13) take it into the verb's unit under rule 44; the adverbial accusative שֶׁקֶר of 13.22, whose verb already has its own rendered object, keeps it under rule 33. Read which the noun is before placing the word.
- Follow MACULA's article tokens rather than the pointing when placing the ASV's the after a pointed preposition. 13.5 (s004, s015) and 13.15 (s007, s011) carry a bracketed [הַ] that takes the written the (rule 12), while 13.4's בָּ is tagged R with no article token of any kind, so its supplied the belongs to the nominal expression it determines (rule 15) and rule 30 has nothing to fuse. This is a correction already applied to 13.4 and is the reading for the whole chapter.
- no more is split where both morphemes are written and whole where only one is. In 13.21 and 13.23 לֹא takes no and עוֹד takes more, including 13.23 where the ASV moves more forward into the earlier clause (rule 25). In 13.15, where no עוֹד stands, the existential אַיִן takes is no more entire and heads on the negation (rule 50).
