# Galatians 3 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **GAL.3.10** — Where a quantifier stands before an article governing a participle and the ASV supplies a generic nominal that no other source word takes, that nominal is the article's under rules 22, 32 and 49, the participle keeps its own relativizer, and the case preposition rides with the quantifier as the leftmost English word of the phrase. So πᾶσιν τοῖς γεγραμμένοις is 'in all' + 'things' + 'that are written' (cf. JHN.18.4, REV.22.18, PHP.1.1), matching πᾶς ὁ κρεμάμενος in 3:13 and τοῖς πιστεύουσιν in 3:22. Rule 31's unrepresented article is for the case where the ASV supplies no such nominal (REV.18.19, REV.18.24). This is recorded as a chapter convention and not promoted to the contract: the corpus contradicts itself nearby — REV.13.8 gives the relativizer to the article where REV.18.19 does not — and a substantival adjective, unlike a quantifier, is read as an expressed head at JHN.19.32.
- **GAL.3.15** — Where the ASV recasts a bare Greek phrase into a full English clause, the whole supplied frame — including a dummy subject and its copula — is a helper on the word that licenses the recasting, not on the noun the English turns into a predicate nominal. ὅμως takes 'Though it be … yet' whole, as ἀλλά takes 'But this cometh to pass' at JHN.15.25 and μήποτε takes 'Can it be that' at JHN.7.26; rule 63 forbids putting the copula on the predicate nominal, so διαθήκην keeps only 'covenant'.
- **GAL.3.20** — Where the ASV repeats a content word the Greek expresses once, this chapter marks the repetition added when it fills a second syntactic slot the Greek leaves elliptical, keeping any supplied determiner inside that unit because no source article stands in that slot (GAL.2.7, REV.21.12, REV.4.1, REV.20.12). The corpus writes this both ways — REV.1.14 and REV.10.8 absorb such a repetition — so the unit is medium with the competing reading conceded, and no general rule is claimed.
