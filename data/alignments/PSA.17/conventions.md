# Psalms 17 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **PSA.17.1** — A Hebrew verb the ASV renders as a light verb plus a noun takes its head on the noun, not on the light verb: 'Give ear unto' heads on 'ear'. The light verb is grammatical support; the noun carries the lexeme, exactly as in the contract's 'bear witness' and 'make straight'. This chapter had it both ways -- 17:1 headed 'Give' while 17:7 headed 'marvellous' in 'Show marvellous' -- and 17:1 was corrected.
