# 2 Chronicles 21 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Phrasal verbs in this chapter are classified as rule 44's verb-plus-particle, not as light verb plus complement, and every one of them heads on the English verb: passed over, was risen up, rose up, compassed about, carried away, brake into, came up, stirred up, fall out, fell out, led astray. What puts a rendering in the other class is a lexical complement that carries the source verb's lexeme, as in zanah hiphil rendered made ... to play the harlot (21.11, 21.13), headed on play. A directional or resultative adverb standing beside the verb is not such a complement, however imperfectly the bare English verb writes the lexeme: shavah rendered carried away and nadach rendered led astray both head on the verb. 21.11 was corrected from astray to led in the chapter reconciliation pass.
- The regnal accession formula be-malkho is rendered when he began to reign at 21.5 and 21.20 and is divided identically in both: the beth takes when under rule 52, the suffix on the infinitive construct takes the subject pronoun he under rule 18, and began is read as part of the infinitive's own rendering rather than as a supplied lexical verb, so the infinitive holds began to reign, headed on reign. The inchoative is what the ASV writes for this Hebrew formula everywhere in Kings and Chronicles; no corpus query was run for it in this pass, so a later chapter finding contrary precedent should record it rather than assume this.
