# 1 Chronicles 12 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The muster idiom עֹרְכֵי / לַעֲרֹךְ מִלְחָמָה -> that could set the battle in array is divided the same way throughout the chapter (12.33, 12.35, 12.36, and 12.38 with עֹדְרֵי -> that could order): the verb takes the supplied relative and modal and the English idiom's in array, its unit discontinuous around the object under rule 25, while the object noun takes the battle with a supplied the. Where the Hebrew has no object noun at all — 12.33's לַעֲדֹר — the ASV's italicised the battle array is added, since no morpheme's rendering it completes.
- The muster idiom יוֹצְאֵי צָבָא -> such as were able to go out in the host (12.33, 12.36) is divided so that the whole supplied antecedent phrase such as were able to joins the participle under rule 46, headed on the lexeme go, with out as the verb's particle under rule 44, while צָבָא takes in the host with its supplied the. The ASV writes in rather than of for this chain's relation, which the rectum takes by rule 19.
- The directive he (Sd) takes the English preposition the ASV writes for the goal, and the place noun keeps its own word with any supplied the: חֶבְרוֹנָה -> to Hebron (12.23, 12.38) puts to on the directive he, and מִדְבָּרָה -> in the wilderness (12.8) puts in on it. The competing reading, which marks the morpheme unrepresented and hands the preposition to the noun, is recorded on 12.8 and was not taken anywhere in this chapter.
