# Leviticus 3 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Sacrificial-term heads, fixed once for the whole chapter by rule 31's subtraction test with the competing head named in each unit's note: אִשֶּׁה -> *an offering made by fire* heads on *fire* (vv. 3, 5, 9, 11, 14, 16), שְׁלָמִים -> *of peace offerings* heads on *peace* (vv. 1, 3, 6, 9), עֹלָה -> *burnt offering* heads on *burnt* (v. 5), אַלְיָה -> *fat tail* heads on *fat* (v. 9). *offering*, *offerings*, *offering* and *tail* are the rejected readings; a later chapter in this ritual vocabulary should follow the same test rather than reopen the four.
- A possessive לְ whose relation the ASV writes inside the following word's English shares that word's unit and carries the clause's copula there: LEV.3.16 לַיהוָה -> *is Jehovah's* is one aligned unit headed on *Jehovah's* (rules 30, 49). This matches the chapter's fusion of לִפְנֵי -> *before* (vv. 7, 8, 12, 13) and לְעֻמַּת -> *hard by* (v. 9), and the corpus, which fuses לְ + the divine name in six units across five chapters. Recorded because the verse pass took the split reading and the lemma table showed it only as an ordinary *unto*/*is* difference.
- LEV.3.17's prohibition: the ASV's *neither ... nor* is divided between the written לֹא (*neither*) and the waw (*nor*) under rule 14's correlative limit, and that leaves both כֹּל tokens unrepresented, the ASV writing neither an *any* nor an *all* for them. BSB represents both; that is the documented convention difference and not an error. The same כָּל־חֵלֶב וְכָל־דָּם ... לֹא תֹאכֵלוּ formula recurs in Leviticus 7.
- Object markers: 27 of the chapter's 28 are unrepresented, the ASV writing the object bare or as a bare pronoun. The exception is LEV.3.7 אֶת -> *for*, where the ASV recasts the marked קָרְבָּנוֹ as a role phrase (*offer a lamb for his oblation*) and the subtraction test leaves only the object relation diminished (rule 16). The single *for* is not a chapter inconsistency, and v. 14's appositional *even* is `added` rather than given to a marker because no marker stands on אִשֶּׁה.
