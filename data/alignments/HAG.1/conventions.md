# Haggai 1 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **HAG.1.5** — In Haggai 1, שִׂימוּ לְבַבְ … עַל is the idiom rendered by the ASV as ‘Consider.’
- **HAG.1.1** — In the title הַכֹּהֵן הַגָּדוֹל, גָּדוֹל is rendered ‘high,’ forming ‘high priest.’
- **HAG.1.1** — בְּיַד is the fixed agency expression rendered by the ASV as ‘by.’
- **HAG.1.9** — נְאֻם is the utterance formula rendered verbally by the ASV as ‘saith.’
