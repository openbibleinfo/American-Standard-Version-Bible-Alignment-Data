# Colossians 2 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- A Greek compound noun the ASV renders by two English words that each gloss one of its elements heads on the word answering to the nominal core, not on the one answering to the modifying or prefixed element, and English word order does not move it: πιθανολογίᾳ → persuasiveness of speech heads on speech (COL.2.4, corrected in this pass) as ἐθελοθρησκίᾳ → will worship heads on worship (COL.2.23). Where the second English word is a generic completion rather than a gloss, rule 66's other arm applies and the head goes to the word carrying the sense.
- Where the ASV verbalizes εἰς plus a noun, the preposition takes the English word introducing the resulting clause and the noun takes the verb: εἰς ἐπίγνωσιν → that on εἰς and they may know on the noun (COL.2.2), εἰς φθοράν → to on εἰς and perish on the noun (COL.2.22). That is rule 61's placement holding where the preposition governs a nominal rather than an articular infinitive. Leaving the preposition unrepresented and giving the introducer to the noun remains defensible and is named in both verses.
- An English preposition standing over an accusative object stays inside the verb's or the frame's unit throughout this chapter — maketh spoil of (2.8), triumphing over (2.15), dwelling in (2.18), increaseth with (2.19) — and where that accusative carries an article of its own the article takes only the bare the the ASV wrote, never with the or of the. Rule 21 gives an article the preposition realizing its noun's own case and an accusative has none, so nothing is left for the article to carry beyond its determiner. COL.2.19 was corrected to this reading and its article now stands on a bare the.
- A supplied English correlative or intensifier standing with a comparative that has no answering καί rides with the comparative under rules 7 and 82 and is not added: Ὡς → As … so, discontinuously (COL.2.6), and καθὼς → even as (COL.2.7). Rule 82 gives such a word to the καί only where one stands, and none does in either verse.
