# 2 Corinthians 3 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **2CO.3.10** — εἵνεκεν is an improper preposition — it governs the genitive only and never forms a verbal compound — so its complex frame 'by reason of' is settled by rule 71 on the frame's lexical noun 'reason', and rule 67's standing division, which rule 71 confines to proper prepositions, does not reach it. The morph tag cannot decide this: the packet tags εἵνεκεν PREP, and tags rule 71's own leading example ἐνώπιον PREP in all 44 of its units, while πέραν, ἐγγύς and χωρίς are tagged ADV. Decide from the lexeme. REV.14.10 is the structural twin, giving ἐνώπιον the whole frame including its trailing 'of' and leaving the following article a bare 'the', which is why τῆς keeps a bare 'the' here. The corpus's six 'by reason of' units headed on 'by' are all proper prepositions (JHN.12.11, REV.9.2, REV.18.19, 2CO.9.14, 2PE.2.2, 2PE.3.12), so they belong to rule 67's division and are not counter-precedent; 2CO.3.10 is the corpus's only improper-preposition instance of this frame. Recorded high, since rule 71's class is 16-1.
- **2CO.3.7** — A supplied English 'and' coordinating two non-finite forms across an asyndetic Greek seam is 'added'. Rule 88's shield is scoped to a circumstantial participle the ASV converts into a coordinate FINITE clause; 'written and engraven' coordinates two attributive participles and converts nothing, so nothing licenses the juncture. This is the case rule 88 itself names in REV.15.6, 'stone pure and bright', where the supplied 'and' is 'added' unhedged. The two NT units that fold such a juncture, 2CO.8.13 and 2CO.10.16, are both medium and both concede 'added'.
- **2CO.3.7** — A supplied lexical noun repeating an antecedent inside a finite relative rendering is 'added', not a rule 7 helper. In 'which glory was passing away' the relativizer and copula are ordinary helpers on the participle, but δόξαν is already spent earlier in the verse on 'the glory of his face', so nothing licenses the second 'glory'. The corpus has no unit folding a repeated antecedent after a relativizer, and it marks supplied lexical content whose source token is realized elsewhere 'added' (REV.21.12 'the names', 2PE.3.11 'all'). Recorded medium, the helper reading remaining defensible.
- **2CO.3.7** — ἐν γράμμασιν rendered by the single word 'written' is one 'noncompositional' unit headed on the noun. Rule 93's test decides it against rule 34: the corpus never spends 'written' on γράμμα, which it renders 'letter', 'letters' and 'writings' in all five other units, so the noun cannot take the word alone and the preposition is not pleonastic; and no part of 'written' spells ἐν, so rule 35 cannot keep it 'aligned' — the reasoning 2CO.7.10, 2CO.9.6 and 2CO.11.28 state verbatim. The ASV margin 'Greek in letters.' glosses the one English word by the whole Greek phrase, corroborating one unit rather than a split.
