# 1 Peter 1 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **1PE.1.18** — A Greek noun rendered by the English frame *manner of X* heads on X, not on *manner*: rule 66's generic completion is *manner of*, and rule 17 forbids choosing the phrase's formal syntactic head. 1PE.1.15 (*manner of living* on *living*) and 1PE.1.18 (*manner of life* on *life*) agree, as 1PE.1.11 does in treating *what manner of* as a rule 7 completion. EPH.4.22 reads the same lemma this way; GAL.1.13 heads on *manner* but was aligned before rule 66 entered the contract and reasons from English composition.
- **1PE.1.17** — A privative adjective or adverb rendered by *without X* heads on the lexical noun X at high confidence, not on *without*. The corpus is 13-0, adverbs included and unhedged; the only heads on *without* are the lexemes it actually glosses. Applies to 1PE.1.17 *without respect of persons* and 1PE.1.19 *without blemish* and *without spot*.
- **1PE.1.14** — Where a rule 67 complex frame is carried by an expressed proper preposition, this chapter heads on the prepositional word and records medium, following 2CO.8.20's exact twin. Rule 67's division is genuinely live and 1PE's neighbours do not settle it: Revelation is 2-2 on *by reason of*. The article following such a frame is unrepresented on rule 20 proper wherever a free genitive pronoun carries the possessive, which is an independent ground and takes high.
- **1PE.1.19** — Supplied appositional English is folded where a source token opens the appositive and takes its own English word, and is marked added only where every token in the phrase is already spent. 1PE.1.9 folds *even* onto soterian, which opens *even the salvation of your souls*; 1PE.1.19 marks *even the blood* added, no token opening it and Christou already realizing *of Christ* (JHN.1.9, REV.16.12). A single source token scoping a coordination is the other line and always folds, as 1PE.1.11's repeated *time* does.
