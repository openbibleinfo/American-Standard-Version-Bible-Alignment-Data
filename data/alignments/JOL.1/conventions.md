# Joel 1 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **JOL.1.13** — A vocative article is unrepresented where the ASV writes no word for it: the supplied 'O'/'ye' belongs to the vocative noun under rule 36, not to the article. JOL.1.2 הַזְּקֵנִים → 'ye old men' and JOL.1.13 הַכֹּהֲנִים → 'ye priests' are now aligned the same way, matching 1KI.1.13, 1KI.1.24, 1KI.18.26 and 1KI.20.4 in the corpus.
- **JOL.1.9** — In an offering phrase, rule 31's subtraction decides the head separately for each lemma: מִנְחָה → 'meal offering' heads on 'offering', because the lemma alone is rendered 'offering'/'oblation'/'tribute' and 'meal' is the ASV's specifier (cf. 1KI.8.64), while נֶסֶךְ → 'drink offering' heads on 'drink', which carries the libation sense (cf. PSA.16.4). Applied at JOL.1.9 and JOL.1.13.
