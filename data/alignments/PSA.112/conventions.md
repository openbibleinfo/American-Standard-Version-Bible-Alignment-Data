# Psalms 112 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **PSA.112.3** — לָעַד → 'for ever' is split morpheme by morpheme: לְ → 'for', עַד → 'ever' (rule 2), as rule 26 splits 'and for evermore' and as the corpus splits לְעוֹלָם → 'endureth for' + 'ever'. The rule-40 fusion of preposition and absolute noun into one adverb does not reach it because the ASV writes two English words, one for each morpheme. Applied identically at PSA.112.9.
