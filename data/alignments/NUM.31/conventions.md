# Numbers 31 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **NUM.31.28** — In the coordinated partitive lists, the range-opening preposition carries the ASV correlative both of; both is not target-only.
- **NUM.31.35** — Where the repeated counted-noun formula begins with a construct, the construct occurrence receives persons and the trailing absolute occurrence is resumptive.
