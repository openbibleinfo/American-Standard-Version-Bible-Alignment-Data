# Jeremiah 8 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Demonstrative phrases. Where the phrase writes an explicit demonstrative pronoun — הָעֵת הַהִיא → At that time (8.1), הָעָם הַזֶּה → this people (8.5), הַמִּשְׁפָּחָה הָרָעָה הַזֹּאת → this evil family (8.3) — the ASV's single written determiner goes to the demonstrative pronoun, and every article morpheme in the phrase is `unrepresented`, including the agreement article standing on the demonstrative itself and the one on any intervening adjective. This is rule 29's הֶעָרִים הָאֵלֶּה → these cities ruling read together with rule 41's 'only where no other morpheme licenses that determiner': the demonstrative pronoun is that morpheme. Do not instead fuse the article with the demonstrative into a single rule 30 unit; the corpus has a few such joint Td+Pdxcp and Td+Pp3ms units, and this chapter does not follow them.
- A supplied English preposition standing before the pronoun that realizes an object suffix. Give it to the suffix where it carries a relation the ASV's recasting introduces and the host's own English is complete without it — יַעַבְרוּם → shall pass away *from them* (8.13), מְלוֹאָהּ → all that is *in it* (8.16). Give it to the host where it is part of that host's own English rendering — הֶחֱזִיקַתְנִי → *hath taken hold on* / me (8.21), 'take hold on' being the collocation the ASV writes for חָזַק. Test by asking whether the host's rendering is complete without the preposition, as rule 16 tests by subtraction; 'shall pass away' is complete, 'hath taken hold on' is one written idiom.
