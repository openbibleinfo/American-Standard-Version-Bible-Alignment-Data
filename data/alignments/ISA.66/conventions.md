# Isaiah 66 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **ISA.66.9** — The interrogative הֲ aligns to the fronted English auxiliary that realizes the question (*Shall*, *Hast*), not left unrepresented with the auxiliary folded into the verb. Verse 9 and 1KI.21.19-20 follow this; verse 8 was brought into line during review, where the paired disjunctive אִם is treated the same way.
- **ISA.66.19** — Where a waw and a following negative are fused by English into a single neither, they share one aligned unit rather than the negative carrying it beside an unrepresented waw; the head is the negative. Verses 19 and 24 agree.
