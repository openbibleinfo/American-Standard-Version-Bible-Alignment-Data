# Job 27 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Relative clauses the ASV supplies over asyndetic finite clauses with a written antecedent (27.2 who hath taken away, 27.2 who hath vexed, 27.13 which they receive, 27.18 which the keeper maketh) are added throughout. Where a waw precedes such a clause and the ASV writes its own connective for it (27.2 And the Almighty who…), the waw keeps that connective and the relative is still added; rule 17's waw → which reading applies only where the ASV writes no separate connective for the waw.
