# Numbers 13 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Preposition before a construct noun: the chapter applied rule 30's subtraction test uniformly and it divides four ways in this chapter. Fused, one unit headed on the construct noun — LIPHNE -> before (13.22), AL ODOT -> because of (13.24), BE + TAVEKH -> in (13.32, the suffix keeping its own it under rule 19). Split, each morpheme holding English of its own — AL PI -> according to / the commandment (13.3), AL YAD -> along by / the side (13.29). Do not mark the construct noun unrepresented to give the preposition the whole English; rule 22 reaches a repeated element, and a construct noun inside such a pair repeats nothing.
- Disjunctive indirect questions (13.18, 13.19, 13.20): the particle that opens the question takes the ASV's whether, and the particle standing at the head of the second member takes its or, whether that particle is a second interrogative HE or an IM. Rule 17 names IM in that slot; this chapter writes HE there too, at 13.18 (he-chazaq hu ha-rapheh -> whether they are strong or weak), and it is read the same way. The explicit subject pronoun keeps only its own they or it, and the copula goes to the predicate adjective under rule 20.
- Supplied English verbs: where a preposition stands whose own relation the ASV's supplied verb states, that verb — and any relative the recasting requires — joins the preposition's unit under rules 41 and 49, headed as rule 49 directs, on the English preposition where one remains and on the verb where none does: MIN -> who come of, headed on of (13.33), and LE -> to overcome, headed on overcome (13.30). Where no morpheme in the clause carries what the supplied verb states, it stays added under rule 20, with any subject supplied beside it: they brought in the asyndetic gapped clause at 13.23. Both readings are named in the units' own notes.
