# 2 Kings 7 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Price formula (7.1, 7.16, 7.18): סְאָה/סָאתַיִם are tagged absolute, so סֹלֶת and שְׂעֹרִים are appositional and take the supplied 'of' under rule 33, not rule 19. The copula belongs to הָיָה where it is written (7.16 'was', 7.18 'shall be') and to the בְּשֶׁקֶל predicate where it is not (7.1 'shall be for', rule 49); the ASV's 'sold' names an event the Hebrew never writes and is added in every verse (rule 20).
- רָמַס + object (7.17 vs 7.20): the supplied 'upon' of 'trod upon him' goes to the verb where the object is a bare verbal suffix (7.17 יִּרְמְסֻהוּ, rule 44) and to the object marker where one stands (7.20 יִּרְמְסוּ אֹתוֹ, rule 16 first family). The two verses differ because the Hebrew does, not the analysis.
- Reciprocal idiom 'one to another' (7.3, 7.6, 7.9): אִישׁ → one, רֵעַ/אָח → another, the possessive suffix unrepresented; the ASV writes no possessive for it.
- 7.4 apodosis waws before מָתְנוּ: 'we die also' gives also to the waw (rule 51); 'we shall but die' reads but as restrictive 'only', not a connective, so it is added and the waw unrepresented. The competing reading (but to the waw) is named in the note.
