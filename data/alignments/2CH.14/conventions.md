# 2 Chronicles 14 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Phrasal particles in this chapter's verbs of removal and plunder — סוּר hiphil → took away (14.3, 14.5), שָׁבַר piel → brake down (14.3), גָּדַע → hewed down (14.3), נָשָׂא → carried away (14.13), שָׁבָה → carried away (14.15) — are read as completing the verb's lexeme under rule 44 and kept in the verb's unit headed on the verb, not marked added as surplus; the notes name the competing reading.
