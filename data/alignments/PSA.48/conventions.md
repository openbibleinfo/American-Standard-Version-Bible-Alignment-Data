# Psalms 48 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- A bare nominal standing in apposition to a preceding בְּ phrase and rendered with a supplied preposition of its own — הַר־קָדְשׁוֹ → 'in his holy mountain' (48.1), יַרְכְּתֵי צָפוֹן → 'on the sides of the north' (48.2) — takes that supplied word on the noun under rule 33. It is not read as a distributed repetition of the earlier written בְּ under rules 25/33: those sentences govern a member coordinated under one written preposition, and an appositive is not coordinated. The alternative was considered in 48.1 and rejected for both verses.
