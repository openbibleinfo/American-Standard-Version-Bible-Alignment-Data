# 1 Samuel 16 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **1SA.16.1** — The gentilic בֵּית הַלַּחְמִי arrives as two Ngmsa tokens, the second carrying lemma הַ. It is aligned by token and not fused: בֵּית → Beth, and the article-bearing second token → the lehemite, headed on lehemite (16.1, 16.18).
- **1SA.16.2** — A construct chain the ASV renders by a single English class noun that writes the construct's lexeme and only implies the rectum's — עֶגְלַת בָּקָר → a heifer (16.2), גְּדִי עִזִּים → kid (16.20), and נֹשֵׂא כֵלִים → armorbearer (16.21), where both lexemes are written — is one unit under rule 30 headed on the construct, the supplied a joining it under rule 15; the rectum is not marked unrepresented beside it. Where the ASV writes a word for each (kid of the goats, bearer of armor) rule 2 splits the pair as usual.
- **1SA.16.7** — The three asterisked ASV supplies in this chapter — Jehovah seeth (16.7, one unit with its subject under rule 2), laden (16.20) and the first evil of 16.23, which stands before רוּחַ־אֱלֹהִים where the Hebrew writes no adjective — are each their own added unit; the packet records no variant unit in any of those slots, so rule 53's excluded-reading case does not arise. The second evil of 16.23 is רָעָה's own word.
- **1SA.16.16** — The impersonal predications טוֹב לְ and רָוַח לְ that the ASV recasts personally (thou shalt be well, Saul was refreshed, was well) give the verb its predicate with whatever auxiliaries the ASV writes, leave the לְ unrepresented under rule 40, and give the suffix the subject pronoun where one is written (16.16 ךְ → thou) and nothing where the ASV lets the clause share an earlier subject (16.23 לוֹ → and was well, suffix unrepresented).
- **1SA.16.6** — The presentative frame it came to pass / it shall come to pass is headed on pass in all three occurrences (16.6, 16.16, 16.23), with the apodosis that going to the apodosis waw under rule 45 and the temporal בְּ taking when under rule 52; came is the competing head and is named in the 16.6 note.
