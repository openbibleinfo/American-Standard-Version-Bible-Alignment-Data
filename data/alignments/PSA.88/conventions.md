# Psalms 88 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **PSA.88.0** — Title: עַל → set to is one unit on the preposition headed on set (rule 24 fixed phrase); לְעַנּוֹת → Leannoth fuses the preposition and the infinitive as one transliterated rendering (rule 30). Margin 'for singing' read and set aside.
- **PSA.88.15** — מִנֹּעַר → from my youth up: the fixed from … up belongs whole to the מִן, discontinuous under rule 25; the possessive my is added (no suffix). Marking up added was the competing reading and is named in the note.
