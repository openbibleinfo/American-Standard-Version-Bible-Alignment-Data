# Proverbs 31 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The chain דִּין כָּל־בְּנֵי X is written twice in this chapter (31.5, 31.8) and is divided the same way in both, the division following the ASV's word count rather than the Hebrew: כֹּל takes the recast construct relation together with its own quantifier — *of all* at 31.8, *due to any* at 31.5 — under rule 19; the final noun takes the supplied relative with its copula and predicate — *as are left desolate*, *that is afflicted* — under rules 46 and 20; and בְּנֵי takes an English word only where one is left over, which is *such* at 31.8 and nothing at 31.5, where it is `unrepresented` under rule 22. BSB represents that 31.5 בְּנֵי; the disagreement is this convention and not an error.
- Where one substantive is rendered by a multi-word English phrase, this chapter heads it by rule 31's subtraction and never on the generic English noun: שֵׁכָר → *strong drink* heads on *strong* (31.4, 31.6), סָדִין → *linen garments* on *linen* (31.24), מַרְבַדִּים → *carpets of tapestry* on *carpets* (31.22), שֵׁשׁ → *fine linen* on *linen* (31.22). The ASV writes none of *drink*, *garments*, *tapestry* alone for a word of that class here, so rule 31's tie-break does not reach them; each unit names the competing head under rule 8, and a later verse added to this chapter should follow the same reading.
