# Psalms 39 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- בְּ standing on a temporal adverb and rendered by one English word fuses with it under rule 30 and heads on the adverb: בְּעוֹד → While (39.1), בְּטֶרֶם → Before (39.13). The competing reading, the word on the בְּ under rule 52 with the adverb unrepresented, was considered and set aside for both.
- The ASV's 'held my peace' / 'Hold thy peace' for חָשָׁה and חָרֵשׁ is one idiomatic rendering of the verb: the possessive is part of the idiom and stays in the verb's unit, headed on 'peace' (rules 30, 44), not added under rule 18 (39.2, 39.12).
- A bare Hebrew direct object the ASV demotes to a 'with' instrument while the verb stays an English verb with a promoted object gives the 'with' to the verb (rule 33 direct-object paragraph, PRO.7.16 shape): 39.1 'keep my mouth with a bridle'. A genuine adverbial accusative keeps its supplied preposition (39.2 'with silence', 39.5 'as handbreadths', 39.6 'in vain').
