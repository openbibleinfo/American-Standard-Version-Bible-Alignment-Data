# Judges 7 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Where MACULA segments a compound place name into two tokens carrying the one compound lemma, each token takes the English element answering to it and neither is folded into the other nor marked unrepresented beside it: Beth + shittah for beyt ha-shittah (JDG.7.22), Beth + barah for beyt barah (JDG.7.24, twice), Abel + meholah for avel mecholah (JDG.7.22). A relational *of* the ASV writes before such a name is the enclosing chain's and goes to the first of the two tokens, as sephat avel mecholah -> *the border of Abel-meholah* does; the second token takes its bare word. But read what the ASV wrote for the first element rather than the shared lemma: where it translates that element instead of transliterating it, the pair is an ordinary construct chain under rules 19 and 29, so eyn charod -> *the spring of Harod* (JDG.7.1) gives *the spring* to eyn, with the determiner the nominal licenses under rule 15, and *of Harod* to charod.
