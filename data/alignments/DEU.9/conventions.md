# Deuteronomy 9 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Every *neither … nor* and bare *nor* in this chapter is divided by what stands in the line the English word opens. The opening *neither* of the twin abstinence couplets at DEU.9.9 and DEU.9.18 goes to the first clause's `לֹא` under rule 14; the closing *nor* of those verses and of DEU.9.23 goes to the waw fused with that clause's own written `לֹא` under rule 35, headed on the negative; and the two *nor*s of DEU.9.27, whose lines gap the negative, go to their waws alone. The competing division — *nor* to the waw by itself with the written second `לֹא` marked unrepresented — was considered and rejected in every case, and is named in the DEU.9.9 and DEU.9.18 unit notes.
- A periphrastic `הָיָה` plus participle is divided by the English the ASV writes for each morpheme. Where it writes a compound tense, the finite verb takes the subject pronoun with the auxiliary and copula and the participle keeps only its own word: `מַמְרִים הֱיִיתֶם` → *ye have been rebellious* at DEU.9.7 and DEU.9.24. Where it collapses the periphrastic into a simple past, the finite `הָיָה` keeps the subject pronoun alone and the participle takes the whole English verb phrase: `מַקְצִפִים הֱיִיתֶם אֶת־יְהוָה` → *ye provoked Jehovah to wrath* at DEU.9.22 gives *ye* to `הָיָה` and *provoked … to wrath* to the participle, headed on *wrath* under rule 44. `הָיָה` is not marked unrepresented in the collapsed case: the pronoun realizes its own person and number under rule 17.
