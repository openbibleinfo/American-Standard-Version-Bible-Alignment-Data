# Ecclesiastes 2 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Comparative frames: a comparative preposition takes the whole English frame the ASV writes for it into its own unit and heads on the relational word, never on a degree adverb or a supplied content word — מִן -> 'more than' (2.9) and 'than' (2.25) head on 'than', כְּ -> 'as far as' (2.13) heads on the opening 'as', כְּ -> 'As ... so' (2.15) heads on 'As', עִם -> 'even as' (2.16, twice) heads on 'as'. Where the ASV writes no comparative word at all because the comparison is inside a verb its recasting supplies ('wisdom excelleth folly', 2.13), the מִן is unrepresented rather than fused: it is not contiguous with יִתְרוֹן, so rule 30's condition is unmet.
- The 'I made me / I builded me' series (2.4-2.8) reads alike throughout: the dative לְ is unrepresented under rule 40 and its suffix takes the written 'me'. ECC.2.4's first clause writes no dative — הִגְדַּלְתִּי מַעֲשָׂי — and its 'me' goes to the possessive suffix on מַעֲשָׂי rather than being marked added; likewise the 'my' of 'in my house' at 2.7 goes to the suffix of לִי, recast as a determiner on the noun beside it. Where the ASV instead writes 'the' for such a suffix, as at 2.11 ('the works', with the one 'my' reserved for יָדַי), the suffix is unrepresented under rule 22 and the supplied determiner joins the noun under rule 15.
- טוֹב לִפְנֵי is one fused unit on 'pleaseth' at ECC.2.26, both times, on rule 40's רַע בְּעֵינֶיךָ -> 'if it displease thee' pattern: the adjective, the preposition and פָּנֶה share the single English word, the unit heads on the adjective, and a suffix standing on the construct keeps its own pronoun ('him'). Where no suffix stands, the anarthrous substantive additionally takes the supplied 'him that' under rule 46. These are the chapter's two three-morpheme units, and the validator's large-source-unit flag on them is answered here rather than in each verse.
