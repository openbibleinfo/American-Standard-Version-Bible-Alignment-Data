# Psalms 126 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **PSA.126.2** — הִגְדִּיל לַעֲשׂוֹת → 'hath done great things' (vv.2–3): the hiphil גָּדַל is the auxiliary verb whose whole contribution the ASV writes as the object phrase 'great things', one unit headed on 'great' with 'things' as a supplied generic (rule 56's auxiliary-verb clause, rule 31); the לְ before the infinitive is unrepresented (rules 52/40); the infinitive עֲשׂוֹת takes the English predicate 'hath done' with its auxiliary. עִם is 'for' in both verses.
