# Nehemiah 4 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Distributive אִישׁ is rendered every one and aligned as one unit on the noun headed on one, with every named as the competing head (4.15, 4.18, 4.22, 4.23). The reciprocal אִישׁ ... אָחִיו of 4.19 gives one to אִישׁ, another to אָח and leaves the suffix unrepresented. Where the ASV writes every one and no אִישׁ stands in the Hebrew (4.17) the pair is one added unit headed on one.
- The infinitive absolute הַרְבֵּה is read by its syntactic office: adverbial beside a finite verb it takes the degree word alone (4.1 great, beside took indignation); as the predicate of a verbless clause it takes the construction's copula and presentative frame (4.10 there is much, 4.19 is great).
