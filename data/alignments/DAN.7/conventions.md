# Daniel 7 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **DAN.7.10** — When דִּי נוּר is rendered by the single attributive 'fiery', the genitive marker and fire noun share one aligned unit headed on the noun.
- **DAN.7.12** — In the recasting 'their lives were prolonged', the prolongation noun takes 'prolonged' and the overt finite passive יְהִיבַת retains the auxiliary 'were'.
- **DAN.7.23** — In 'a fourth kingdom', the adjective alone takes 'fourth'; its separately segmented determined-state ending is unrepresented because the ASV writes no definite article.
