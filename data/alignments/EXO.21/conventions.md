# Exodus 21 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **EXO.21.3** — For בְּגַפּוֹ rendered “by himself,” the preposition takes “by” and גַּף plus its suffix fuse as “himself”; any extra directional “in” belongs to the governing verb.
- **EXO.21.7** — When עֶבֶד is expanded as “man servant” or “men servants,” “servant(s)” carries the slave lexeme and is the target head; “man/men” is the supplied gender specifier.
