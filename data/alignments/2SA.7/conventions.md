# 2 Samuel 7 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- לְמִן before a noun is one fused compound preposition in this chapter, headed on לְ under rule 23 and on the word carrying the ablative lexeme under rule 24: v.6 → 'since', v.11 → 'as from'. The ASV's italic 'as' in v.11 is read as part of that one two-word rendering under rule 30 rather than as an added unit; the competing reading (מִן → 'from', 'as' added) is named in the v.11 note and was not adopted.
