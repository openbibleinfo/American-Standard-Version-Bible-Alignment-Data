# 1 Samuel 17 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The contiguous encounter expression לִקְרַאת is one noncompositional unit when ASV renders the whole expression by the single word ‘against’.
- The separately rendered place-name pieces בֵּית לֶחֶם align to ‘Beth’ and ‘lehem’ individually; the two-piece gentilic in verse 58 remains grouped because the source edition marks both pieces as one compound gentilic lexeme.
