# 1 Samuel 4 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **1SA.4.13** — A coordinating 'and' the ASV writes where a purpose le plus infinitive construct stands is its own added unit, and the le is unrepresented. 1SA.4.13 'and came into the city and told it' renders lehaggid by a coordinated finite clause: no conjunction morpheme stands in that clause, so rule 14's closing sentences make the 'and' added, and rule 14 forbids the preposition as a home for a coordinator, leaving it unrepresented under rule 40. Rule 52's 'no word, not no complementizer' reaches a complementizer or an ordinary preposition written in that slot (by, in, for, of, when), not a clause coordinator. The competing reading, which gives the 'and' to the le, is recorded in the verse's review notes and set aside; the corpus's 32 units aligning le to 'and' are lemma-wide and predate rule 14's closing sentences, and rule 52's own counts record no le-before-infinitive aligned to 'and'. The verse's apodosis waw (s031) is separately unrepresented, the ASV opening 'all the city cried out' bare, so the added 'and' is not that waw reordered either.
