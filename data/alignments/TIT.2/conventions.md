# Titus 2 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **TIT.2.5** — Attributive ἴδιος between an article and its expressed head noun carries the supplied English possessive, and the article is unrepresented under rule 20: τοῖς ἰδίοις ἀνδράσιν → “to their own husbands” gives “their own” to ἰδίοις and “to husbands” to ἀνδράσιν. This matches anarthrous TIT.2.9 and TIT.1.3 and arthrous EPH.5.22, JHN.1.41, JHN.4.44, EPH.4.28. Where ἴδιος is substantival with no head noun, rule 22 applies instead and the article supplies the pronoun (JHN.8.44 ἐκ τῶν ἰδίων → “of his own”).
- **TIT.2.2** — The English compound “sober minded” is headed on “sober” wherever it renders σώφρων or σωφρονέω, with or without a supplied copula, following TIT.1.8. Applied to TIT.2.2, TIT.2.5 and TIT.2.6.
- **TIT.2.12** — ἵνα is headed on the word introducing the English clause, so the expanded “to the intent that” heads on “that” as the bare “that” does, following EPH.3.10 and rule 24.
