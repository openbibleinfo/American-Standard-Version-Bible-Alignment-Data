# 1 Samuel 19 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- גַּם + explicit pronoun after a finite verb (19.20, 19.21, 19.22, 19.24): the verb takes only its lexical English, גַּם takes 'also', and the pronoun הוּא/הֵמָּה takes the ASV's 'he'/'they' as its own word (rule 20), not the verb under rule 17. Where a pronominal suffix in the same clause already holds the single English pronoun — 19.23 עָלָיו גַּם הוּא → 'upon him also' — the suffix keeps 'him' and the emphatic הוּא is unrepresented (rule 22).
- וַתְּהִי עַל … רוּחַ אֱלֹהִים → 'the Spirit of God came upon …' (19.20, 19.23): הָיָה takes 'came' as its own rendering and עַל takes 'upon'; the verb is not read as a copula joining the preposition under rule 49, since the ASV wrote a lexical verb for it and the preposition keeps its own word.
