# Psalms 52 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **PSA.52.0** — Verse boundary drawn differently (rule 54): the ASV closes the heading with the Doeg clause ('when Doeg the Edomite came ... house of Ahimelech') that WLC carries at the head of the next verse. PSA.52.0 holds that English as one added unit with a null head; PSA.52.1 holds the eighteen Hebrew morphemes (s001-s018, בְּבוֹא ... אֲחִימֶלֶךְ) each as its own unrepresented unit. Nothing is missing from either verse and nothing is folded across the boundary.
- **PSA.52.5** — In both cola 'pluck thee out of thy tent' and 'root thee out of the land', the 'out of' is read as the compound rendering of the written מִן (headed on out, rule 24), and the verbs נָסַח and שָׁרַשׁ take bare pluck / root. The competing reading, out as the verb's phrasal particle with only of on the preposition, was rejected consistently in both cola.
