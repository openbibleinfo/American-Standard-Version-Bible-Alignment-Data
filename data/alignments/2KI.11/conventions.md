# 2 Kings 11 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Construct chain with an arthrous rectum the ASV renders with no determiner while writing one 'the' before the construct — שָׂרֵי הַמֵּאוֹת → 'the captains over/of hundreds' (vv. 4, 9, 10, 15, 19), בַּת הַמֶּלֶךְ יוֹרָם → 'the daughter of king Joram' (v. 2), בֵּית/כֹּהֵן הַבַּעַל → 'the house/priest of Baal' (v. 18), זֶרַע הַמַּמְלָכָה → 'the seed royal' (v. 1): the 'the' is fronted from the rectum's article, which takes it, and the construct keeps its bare noun (rule 29). The verse passes supplied it on the construct and left the article unrepresented in seven verses; reconciliation reversed them. Where the ASV writes a 'the' before the rectum's own word as well (עַם הָאָרֶץ → 'the people of the land') or recasts as a possessive ('the king's house/son/sons'), the count is arthrous or possessive and no fronting arises.
- Participial and finite בּוֹא / יָצָא rendered 'come in', 'cometh in', 'go out', 'goeth out', 'go forth', 'brought out', 'Have ... forth' (vv. 5, 7, 8, 9, 12, 15) keep the particle with the verb under rule 44 as completing the entering/exiting lexeme; the competing reading that marks the particle added was rejected throughout.
