# Acts 15 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Recitative ὅτι is aligned to a supplied *saying* where no Greek verb of speaking stands to license that word (15:1, ἐδίδασκον … ὅτι Ἐὰν μή → *taught the brethren, saying, Except*), and is its own `unrepresented` unit where an expressed λέγων or λέγοντες has already taken *saying* (15:5). Where the ASV writes *that* it is aligned in the ordinary way (15:7, 15:24). Rule 43's test is the presence of an English word and rule 86's is whether a source token licenses it; ὅτι is the token that marks what follows as discourse. Reading *saying* as `added` with ὅτι `unrepresented` remains defensible and is named in 15:1's note.
- Supplied English is folded onto the token whose own expression it completes and marked `added` only where no token licenses it, on rule 103's test and not on the ASV's italics. Folded in this chapter: the supplied subject *the brethren* on the third-plural ἔταξαν (15:2), the cataphoric discourse-marker *thus* on γράψαντες (15:23), and *saying* on ὅτι (15:1). Marked `added`: the locative *there* (15:33), which answers to no token, the appositional *namely* (15:22), whose carrier ἄνδρας has already spent its English on *men*, and the supplied predicate *and see* (15:36), ἐπισκεψώμεθα having already spent its English on *visit*.
- Distributive κατά: *every* belongs to πᾶς wherever πᾶς is expressed, and κατά keeps only the preposition word the ASV wrote. `κατὰ πόλιν` → *in every city* gives the whole *in every* to the preposition, no quantifier standing (15:21); `κατὰ πόλιν πᾶσαν` → *in every city* gives *in* to the preposition and *every* to πᾶσαν (15:36); `κατὰ πᾶν σάββατον` → *every sabbath* gives *every* to πᾶν and leaves the preposition its own `unrepresented` unit, the ASV having written no word for it (rule 36). The three are one analysis.
- `Ἄνδρες ἀδελφοί` → *Brethren*: the ASV compresses the vocative pair into one English word, which ἀδελφοί takes, and Ἄνδρες is its own `unrepresented` unit (15:7, 15:13). A reference comparison reports Ἄνδρες as a disagreement, and that report is not a correction.
- Versification: the projection carries λέγει κύριος ποιῶν ταῦτα at the end of 15:17, and the ASV translates it at the head of 15:18 (*Saith the Lord, who maketh these things known*). Those four tokens are `unrepresented` in 15:17 and the English answering to them is `added` in 15:18, each side naming the other under rule 37. The resumptive Semitic ἐπ᾽ αὐτούς at the end of 15:17 is untranslated and is two `unrepresented` units, one per token, which is a separate matter from the versification. A reference comparison reports all six tokens as disagreements, and that report is not a correction.
- `ἀφ᾽ ἡμερῶν ἀρχαίων` → *a good while ago* is split token-wise under rule 2 — ἀπό → *ago*, ἡμερῶν → *a … while*, ἀρχαίων → *good* — rather than joined into one `noncompositional` unit, as `ἐκ γενεῶν ἀρχαίων` → *from generations of old* is split at 15:21. The two renderings differ because the ASV's phrasing differs, not because the analysis does; the joined reading for 15:7 is named in that unit's note.
