# Matthew 8 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Where the ASV renders one Greek word by a frame of the shape sick of the X or possessed with X, the head is the noun that glosses the lexeme and never the supplied participle or adjective: παραλυτικός → sick of the palsy on palsy (8:6), πυρέσσουσαν → sick of a fever on fever (8:14), and δαιμονίζομαι → possessed with demons on demons (8:16, 8:28, 8:33). δαιμονίζομαι is a denominal -ίζω verb formed on δαίμων, so the supplied possessed answers to no element of it while demons is the root gloss, which is how rules 66, 91 and 104 read ξενίζω → think it strange and φλογίζω → setteth on fire. Heading on sick or possessed remains defensible and each unit's note says so.
- προσέρχομαι's English complement is placed by whether the Greek expresses a dative, not by the English. Where it does, the dative takes the case preposition under rule 106 and the verb keeps only its verb word: προσῆλθεν αὐτῷ → there came and unto him (8:5), as προσήνεγκαν αὐτῷ → they brought and unto him (8:16). Where it does not, the supplied to him answers to no token and is a rule 7 helper inside the verb's own unit: προσελθών → there came to him (8:2) and προσελθόντες → they came to him (8:25), the αὐτῷ of 8:2 and the αὐτόν of 8:25 being already spent on the following verb's object and so unavailable under rule 76.
