# Nehemiah 1 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **NEH.1.1** — The vayehi frame straddles the verse boundary: 1.1 keeps 'it came to pass' on haya with no 'that', and the apodosis 'that' opening 1.2 belongs to the waw of vayyavo there (rules 45, 14). Neither verse reaches into the other.
- **NEH.1.2** — The ASV's supplied 'certain' before a bare plural noun ('certain men' 1.2, 'certain days' 1.4) is an indefinite determiner standing where 'some' would and joins the noun's unit under rule 15, headed on the noun; it is not added.
