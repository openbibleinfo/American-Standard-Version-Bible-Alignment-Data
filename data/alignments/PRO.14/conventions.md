# Proverbs 14 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Bare nominal predicates in the antithetical couplet: where an anarthrous participle, adjective or noun is the predicate of a verbless colon, this chapter puts the construction-supplied copula (rule 20), the preposition rule 33 supplies before the bare nominal, and the antecedent-and-relative rule 46 supplies for a substantive all into that one unit, headed on the word carrying the lexeme — 14.22 *shall be to them that devise*, 14.29 *is of great*, 14.35 *against him that causeth shame*, and the *He that is soon/slow/hasty* units of 14.17 and 14.29. The competing reading, splitting the supplied copula off as its own `added` unit, was considered at 14.22 and rejected throughout the chapter.
- Supplied English verbs were placed by the subtraction tests of rules 34 and 43 rather than by the ASV's italics, which corroborate and decide nothing (rule 7). *findeth it* (14.6), *tendeth* (14.23), *causeth* (14.25) and *hath* (14.20) are each their own `added` unit, because no morpheme in their clauses is left short when the words are dropped. Against them, *shall be satisfied* (14.14) joins the verb the Hebrew writes once and the ASV realizes in both cola, as one discontinuous unit (rules 25, 34), and *hath a refuge* (14.32) joins the participle the ASV renders by nothing else (rule 43).
