# Amos 9 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **AMO.9.1** — In Amos 9, segmented אֲדֹנָי before יהוה is rendered by ASV's lexicalized the Lord or of the Lord on the noun; its first-person suffix has no separate English realization.
- **AMO.9.7** — The repeated utterance formula נְאֻם יהוה is split as נְאֻם to saith and יהוה to Jehovah.
