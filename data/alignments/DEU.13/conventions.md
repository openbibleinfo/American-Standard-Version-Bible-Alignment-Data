# Deuteronomy 13 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- In the Egypt formula, the מִן of מֵאֶרֶץ מִצְרַיִם and מִבֵּית עֲבָדִים takes the whole *out of* and heads on *out* under rule 24, while the Hiphil participle of יָצָא or פָּדָה keeps only *brought* or *redeemed*. The competing reading, which takes *out* as the verb's phrasal particle under rule 44 and leaves *of* to the preposition, is rejected chapter-wide. DEU.13.5 (three occurrences) and DEU.13.10 (two).
- An adverbial בְּ plus article plus substantive that the ASV renders by a single English adverb — בַּסֵּתֶר → *secretly*, בָּרִאשֹׁנָה → *first*, בָּאַחֲרֹנָה → *afterwards* — fuses the preposition with the substantive under rule 30, headed on the morpheme carrying the lexeme, while the separately segmented article, bracketed or not, stands outside the fusion and is `unrepresented`: the one English word carries the relation and the lexeme but not the definiteness, which is why rule 30 does not absorb it. DEU.13.6, DEU.13.9 (twice).
- שָׁמַע בְּקוֹל is divided by what the ASV writes for the preposition and not by the idiom. וּבְקֹלוֹ תִשְׁמָעוּ → *and obey his voice* leaves the בְּ `unrepresented` under rule 40, the noun and suffix keeping their own English; כִּי תִשְׁמַע בְּקוֹל יְהוָה → *when thou shalt hearken to the voice of Jehovah* gives it the written *to*. DEU.13.4 and DEU.13.18 are not a split, and the BSB disagreement on the first is a convention difference.
- In the range מִקְצֵה הָאָרֶץ וְעַד־קְצֵה הָאָרֶץ → *from the one end of the earth even unto the other end of the earth*, the whole *even unto* is the עַד's rendering under rule 16 and heads on *unto*, and the waw is `unrepresented`. Rule 26's waw-takes-the-connective shape does not arise, because the ASV writes no *and* here; and rule 14's bare *even* is not what was written, the corpus aligning a waw to *even unto* in no unit. The two supplied contrastive determiners *the one* and *the other* join their construct nouns under rule 15. DEU.13.7.
