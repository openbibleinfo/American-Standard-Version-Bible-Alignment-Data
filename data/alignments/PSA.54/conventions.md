# Psalms 54 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **PSA.54.0** — The Ziphite historical note ('when the Ziphites came and said to Saul, Doth not David hide himself with us?') stands in the ASV superscription PSA.54.0 but in WLC at the head of PSA.54.1 (s001–s014). Under rule 54's verse-boundary paragraph it is one added unit with null head in 54.0 and fourteen unrepresented units in 54.1; neither verse reaches into the other.
