# 1 Samuel 29 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The evaluative idiom at 29.7 תַעֲשֶׂה רַע בְּעֵינֵי → displease retains its previously recorded contiguous fusion under rules 30 and 40. At the favor occurrence in 29.6, the scoped retrospective judgment supersedes the former discontinuous predicate fusion: favor is aligned separately from the nominal experiencer fusion licensed by rule 6. Rules 30 and 40's specific predicate-contiguity boundary governs; rule 35's negative and function-word fusions do not remove it. Where the ASV writes a word for each morpheme (good in my sight, 29.6 and 29.9), the previously recorded ordinary split remains. This amendment concerns only the scoped favor occurrence at 29.6.
- מִיּוֹם follows what the ASV writes for the noun: fused to since where no day is written (29.3, headed on the construct under rule 30); split since / the day where the ASV writes the day (29.6); the former whole-phrase treatment of 29.8 is superseded by the scoped correction recorded below.
- שָׁכַם is headed on the English verb wherever one is written (rise up early 29.10, rose up early 29.11) and on the predicate up where the ASV writes only are up early (29.10); early is named as the competing head in each note. Phrasal particles the ASV writes for a verb's own lexeme (gathered together, passed on, fell away, going out, coming in, go up, go down, go back) stay with the verb throughout, heads on the verb.
- **1SA.29.8** — Established-rule LS15 correction: in 29.8, the explicit relative אֲשֶׁר separately owns the closing as. מִן + יוֹם jointly own “so long” as a noncompositional duration rendering, headed on the temporal noun יוֹם (rules 23 and 40), with English head long. This supersedes only the prior 29.8 whole-fusion and first-token-anchor treatment above; the 29.3 and 29.6 judgments remain as recorded. Fresh review and two independent confirmation passes established the final partition and head.
