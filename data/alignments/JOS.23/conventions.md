# Joshua 23 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- מפני and מלפני are read apart in this chapter. Where the ASV writes *from before you* (vv.5, 9) each morpheme holds an English word and rule 2 splits the phrase three ways; where it writes *from out of your sight* (vv.5, 13) the two prepositions מִ + לִּ share one unit as the compound *from out of*, headed on מִ and on *from*, while פני keeps *sight* and the suffix its possessive. The same verse (v.5) writes both shapes, so the division follows the morphemes present and not the lemma.
- The cleft הוא ה+participle לכם (vv.3, 10, *he it is that hath fought* / *that fighteth*) is divided: *he* to the explicit pronoun under rule 20, the dummy *it* its own added unit, the copula together with the participle's own English under rules 20 and 41, and *that* to the participle's article under rule 41. Rule 20's ruling that a cleft's dummy, copula and relative are all added governs a clause whose predication a written finite verb carries; here an articular participle carries it, so a present morpheme takes each word first.
- The ויהי frame of v.1 writes no complementizer in its own verse: the *that* opening v.2 is carried by that clause's apodosis waw under rule 45. In v.15 the same frame keeps its own *that*, because the ASV writes it immediately after *it shall come to pass* and ahead of the כאשר clause, and the apodosis is opened by כן with no waw to compete for it. A later reader checking v.1 alone will find its frame apparently short a word.
- The dative לכם after נתן is unrepresented in vv.13 and 15, where the ASV writes *hath given you* with a bare indirect object (rule 40), and aligned to *unto* in v.16, where the ASV writes *hath given unto you*. Follow the English word written; do not level the three.
- In v.4 the ASV's *even unto the great sea* stands where the Hebrew writes ו + הים and no עד or other limit preposition at all. The waw takes the bare *even* under rule 14, and the supplied *unto* belongs to the accusative of extent הים under rule 33. Rule 16's whole-expression reading of *even unto* presupposes a preposition for the two words to render, so count the phrase's prepositions before applying it.
- In v.15 the supplied *of* of *of which Jehovah your God spake* belongs to דבר, the preposition by which the English verb reaches its bare object אשר (rule 44), discontinuous under rule 25; the relative takes only *which*. The same relative clause at v.14 is written bare, so the *of* answers to the ASV's recasting and not to the relative. The competing reading, *of which* taken whole on אשר, is named in that unit's note.
- In v.1 מסביב is read as ordinary rule 40: the fixed compound *round about* belongs whole to סביב, which is that noun's own rendering in the corpus, and the מן is unrepresented, no English word being spent on its relation. Rule 40's בֶּאֱמֶת → *faithfully* fusion asks for a single English word standing for the preposition and its noun together, and a two-word compound that is the noun's own rendering is not that.
