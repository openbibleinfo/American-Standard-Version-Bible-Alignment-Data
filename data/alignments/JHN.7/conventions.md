# John 7 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **JHN.7.8** — οὔπω rendered 'not yet' is headed on 'not', not on 'yet' (JHN.7.6, 7.8, 7.30, 7.39, and οὐδέπω at 7.39). JHN.7.8 alone had headed it on 'yet' and was changed in reconciliation. The finished John chapters agree: JHN.2.4, JHN.3.24, JHN.6.17 all head on 'not'. REV.17.10 and 17.12 head on 'yet', so the corpus is split across books and this is a chapter-and-book convention rather than a promotable rule.
- **JHN.7.31** — Interrogative μή — the particle marking a question that expects a negative answer — is its own 'unrepresented' unit wherever the ASV marks the question by word order alone: JHN.7.31, 7.35, 7.47, 7.48, 7.51, 7.52. Where the ASV does supply an English word for it, the particle is aligned to that word instead: 'Μὴ γὰρ' at JHN.7.41 gives 'What' to Μή, and μήποτε at JHN.7.26 takes the whole supplied frame 'Can it be that'. The BSB comparison reports every unrepresented one as a disagreement; that report is not a correction.
- **JHN.7.4** — ἐν παρρησίᾳ → 'openly' at JHN.7.4 is kept as one aligned PREP+N unit rather than an unrepresented ἐν plus 'openly' on the noun. The corpus is genuinely split on this shape: REV.1.1 merges ἐν τάχει → 'shortly' into one unit while REV.22.6 leaves ἐν unrepresented and gives 'shortly' to τάχει alone. The merged reading was kept because the single English adverb answers to the phrase as a whole; the confidence stays 'medium' and the alternative is named in the note. Because the corpus contradicts itself here, no contract rule was promoted.
