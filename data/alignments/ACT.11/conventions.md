# Acts 11 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Supplied English lexical content is folded onto the token whose own English it completes rather than marked `added`: *the matter* on ἐξετίθετο at 11:4, *mine eyes* on ἀτενίσας at 11:6, and *he did* on the dative ἡμῖν of the elliptical comparative at 11:17. The chapter therefore carries no `added` unit anywhere, and each fold names the competing rule 5 reading in its own note.
- A σύν-compound verb's dative complement takes the English *with* on rule 106's oblique arm — 11:3 συνέφαγες αὐτοῖς → *with them*, 11:12 συνελθεῖν αὐτοῖς → *with them* — while an **expressed** σύν whose sense the ASV's verb absorbs whole is `unrepresented`, as 11:12 ἦλθον σὺν ἐμοί → *accompanied me* is. What decides between them is whether a preposition is expressed for the English to answer to, never the preverb; one verse writes both.
- ἕως is classified an improper preposition under rule 95's lexeme test, against a morph tag that neighbours it with the proper prepositions, so its supplied frame *as far as* is headed on *far* under rule 71 at both 11:19 and 11:22.
