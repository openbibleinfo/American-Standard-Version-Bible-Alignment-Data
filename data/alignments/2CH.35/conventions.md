# 2 Chronicles 35 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **2CH.35.12** — In ka- plus the assimilated article plus the passive participle katuv, the substantival article takes whichever pronoun the ASV writes in that slot and heads on it under rule 41, while the participle keeps the supplied copula and the lexical verb: 35.12 as it is written gives it to the article and is written to the participle, and 35.26 according to that which is written gives that which the same way. Rule 20's ruling that an impersonal it is added does not reach it, because a present article morpheme licenses the pronoun (rule 11); the finished corpus reads the same assimilated article as it at 2CH.30.5 and 2CH.30.18, both kakkatuv.
- **2CH.35.8** — Where the ASV completes a compound numeral figure with a counted noun the Hebrew does not write (the italic small cattle at 35.8 and 35.9), that supplied noun joins the closing numeral's unit and the head stays on the numeral (rule 31), tail to tail with rule 22's reading of a counted noun distributed across a compound numeral. Joining it to the opening numeral was the competing reading and is recorded here so that later figures in the book divide the same way; where the Hebrew does write the counted noun, as with baqar in the second figure of each verse, that noun is its own unit as usual.
