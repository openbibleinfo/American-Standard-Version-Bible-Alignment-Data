# Daniel 2 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Aramaic possessive recasting of a construct chain: where the ASV writes the rectum as an English possessive (millat malka -> 'the king's matter', shallita di malka -> 'the king's captain', rab tabbahayya di malka -> 'the captain of the king's guard'), the phrase's one written 'the' stands before the possessor and belongs to the determined-state ending on that noun; the construct noun keeps only its own word, and takes a supplied 'the' only where a second one is written. 2.10 and 2.23 were corrected to this, which 2.14 and 2.15 already had.
- kol-qobel (di / dnah): where the ASV renders the whole expression by one indivisible connective - 'because' (2.8), 'forasmuch as' (2.10, 2.40, 2.41, 2.45), 'Therefore' (2.24) - it is one noncompositional unit headed on qobel and on the English anchor. Where the ASV instead spends a word on dnah itself, that morpheme is its own unit and the remaining kol-qobel is an ordinary aligned unit: 2.12 'For this cause' gives 'this' to dnah and 'For ... cause' to kol-qobel.
- Fusion status in this chapter: a rule-30 fusion is recorded aligned unless corpus precedent for that particular expression is uniformly noncompositional. Noncompositional here: kol-qobel di, mah di -> 'what', ke di -> 'as', ki dnah -> 'such', di + suffix -> 'whose', di + le + suffix -> 'unto whom', ahare dnah -> 'hereafter', ka hadah -> 'together', le + suffix -> 'his'. Aligned here: be-edayin -> 'Then', la + bi -> 'without', min qodam, lipne, ad di, al mah, le-qobel, min qesat, be-kol di, hazeh hawayta. The corpus is itself divided on fusion status generally (lipne -> 'before' is 230 noncompositional to 201 aligned), so this is a chapter convention, not a contract rule.
- Haphel yeda (and hawi) with a pronominal object suffix: where the ASV writes 'make known unto/to X', the supplied preposition belongs to the verb's unit under rule 44, and the suffix takes only its pronoun. 2.5 and 2.9 were corrected to this, which 2.23 (twice), 2.26 and 2.29 already had, and which is how the corpus reads DAN.5.15, 5.16 and 5.17.
