# 2 Corinthians 1 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **2CO.1.9** — Intensive nominative αὐτός standing with a first-person plural finite verb takes the whole English *we ourselves* as one unit, headed on *ourselves*, and the verb keeps only its lexical realization. 2CO.1.4 and 2CO.1.9 write the identical construction and rule 24 requires one analysis; GAL.2.17's αὐτοί → *we ourselves* head *ourselves* is the exact string, unhedged, and the corpus keeps the subject pronoun with the intensive in nine such units against one. Do not read *we* off the verb's inflection under rule 9 merely because it stands next to it.
- **2CO.1.7** — A verbless clause's supplied subject and copula ride with the predicate, and the carrier is the expressed Greek preposition where one stands (2CO.1.6's ὑπέρ → *it is for*, twice; 2CO.1.20's ἐν → *in is* and δι → *through is*) and the predicate noun or adjective where none does (2CO.1.7's παρακλήσεως → *are ye … comfort*, 2CO.1.21's θεός → *is God*). A determiner-carrying article is never that carrier: it keeps only rule 21's own *of the*, as the same verse's τῶν παθημάτων does. No corpus unit puts a supplied subject pronoun on an article that does not itself realize a pronoun, and rule 67 warns that a preposition-carrier precedent does not jump carrier classes.
