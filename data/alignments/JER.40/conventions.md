# Jeremiah 40 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **JER.40.10** — In the chapter’s produce-gathering uses, קיץ rendered ‘summer fruits’ heads on ‘fruits,’ which names the referent; ‘summer’ specifies it, while heading on ‘summer’ as the broader seasonal lexeme remains the competing reading.
