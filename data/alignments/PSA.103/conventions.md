# Psalms 103 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Simile apodoses in this psalm are asyndetic: the 'So' opening the second colon of v11, v12 and v13 answers to no waw and is its own added unit under rule 14, while the 'so' of v15 is the written כֵּן and is aligned to it. Read the Hebrew for a conjunction before placing any 'So' in the chapter.
- Where the ASV opens a כְּ simile with a two-word equative frame — 'As far as' in v12, 'Like as' in v13 — both English words belong to the single comparative כְּ (v12 discontinuous around 'far'), headed on 'as', on the terms rule 24 gives a comparative מִן rendered 'too … for'; the alternative of marking the second word added was considered and named in the v12 note.
- v5 בַּטּוֹב → 'with good things': the supplied generic 'things' joins the substantival adjective under rules 31/46, headed on 'good', and the assimilated article is unrepresented because no 'the' is written; rule 41 was not reached because the ASV supplied a generic noun and not a pronoun-plus-relative.
