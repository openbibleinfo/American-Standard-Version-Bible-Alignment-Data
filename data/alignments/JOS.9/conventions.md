# Joshua 9 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The Gibeonite deception is narrated in 9.4-5 and then repeated in the envoys' own words in 9.12-13, and the two tellings do not write the same Hebrew, so an apparent split between them must be checked against the source before it is treated as one. The clause yabesh ... haya niqqudim is asyndetic in 9.5, where the ASV's second 'and' is `added` under rule 14, and writes a waw in 9.12, where that waw takes the same word; the participles of 9.5 (bal, metulla'ot) become a finite balu in 9.13, so the copulas fall differently. Read the two speeches against each other rather than assuming the chapter has one practice.
