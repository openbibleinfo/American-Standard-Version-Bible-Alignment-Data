# Psalms 51 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **PSA.51.0** — Superscription boundary: the ASV prints the whole title in verse 0, but WLC carries its second clause (בְּבוֹא־אֵלָיו נָתָן הַנָּבִיא כַּאֲשֶׁר־בָּא אֶל־בַּת־שָׁבַע) at the head of the packet's PSA.51.1 (s001–s013). Verse 0 holds the English as one added unit with null head; verse 1 marks each of those thirteen morphemes unrepresented (rule 54's verse-boundary paragraph). Nothing is missing from either verse.
- **PSA.51.16** — A supplied 'in' the ASV writes to reach the bare direct object of חָפֵץ or רָצָה ('delightest in sacrifice', 'delight in the sacrifices', 'hast pleasure in burnt offering') joins the verb under rule 44 (PSA.51.16, PSA.51.19); the colon-initial 'In' before the appositional עוֹלָה of PSA.51.19 was instead given to that bare nominal under rule 33, with rule 25's repeated-supplied-preposition reading (to the verb) named as the alternative.
