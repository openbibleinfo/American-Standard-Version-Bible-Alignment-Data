# Jeremiah 50 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **JER.50.3** — When בְּ plus a pronominal suffix is rendered by the single word “therein,” treat the indivisible fusion as noncompositional.
- **JER.50.9** — Renderings of ערך as “set … in array” head on “array”; a reflexive pronoun realizing the verb’s voice remains inside the verb unit.
