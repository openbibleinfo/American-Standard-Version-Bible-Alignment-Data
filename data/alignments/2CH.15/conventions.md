# 2 Chronicles 15 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- A resumptive pronominal suffix pointing back at a fronted nominal the ASV renders once — עָלָיו after the fronted subject Azariah (15.1), the object suffix of הֱסִירָהּ after the fronted Maacah (15.16) — is its own unrepresented unit under rule 22; the nominal takes the English and the suffix is not folded into the verb or preposition.
- The agent לְ + suffix after the Niphal יִמָּצֵא rendered as an English passive ('he was found of them/you') takes the ASV's 'of' as its own unit (15.2, 15.4, 15.15); it is not silenced under rule 40 because the ASV spends a word on the agent relation.
- In the לְמִן־קָטֹן וְעַד־גָּדוֹל / לְמֵאִישׁ וְעַד־אִשָּׁה ranges (15.13) rendered 'whether small or great, whether man or woman', the fronting לְ is unrepresented, מִן takes 'whether' as the opening correlative, the waw takes 'or', and עַד is unrepresented beside the waw's connective (rules 14, 26).
