# 1 Corinthians 12 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Supplied English connectives in this chapter divide by whether a source token licenses them. At an asyndetic seam between like members of the gift lists the ASV's *and* is `added` under rule 98 — once at 12:9 and four times at 12:10 — and where the ASV writes no juncture nothing is supplied, as before ἑτέρῳ at 12:10. Where an expressed conjunction governs two coordinate members and the ASV repeats its English, the repetition is a rule 7 helper on the member it introduces and is never `added`: 12:25's second *that* belongs to μεριμνῶσι, the ἵνα it repeats standing in the same sentence, which is rule 103's function-word contrast. A chapter writing both is not inconsistent.
- 1CO.12.26 source-projection correction (documented 2026-09-07): commit `f9480c8fa` corrected the placement of SBLGNT.1CO.12.26.v022 r01 by anchoring the apparatus unit at its TEI position. The selected projection has one numeral in each clause: s004 before the first μέλος and s012 before the second μέλος. The former assertion that both numeral tokens stand in the first clause no longer describes this projection, so its proposed cross-clause matching is not needed to explain the token positions. This corrects the historical source premise without introducing an alignment rule or replacing independent review. The original convention and its rationale are preserved in `data/audits/corpus-qa-bookkeeping/1CO.12.before-conventions.md`.
- Where a verbless clause's subject is a substantival quantifier or adjective rather than a pronoun, rule 63's pronoun exception does not reach it and the supplied copula is the predicate nominal's helper: 12:17 gives *were hearing* to ἀκοή under the subject ὅλον, and 12:29 gives *are apostles*, *are prophets*, *are teachers* and *are … miracles* to the predicate nouns under πάντες. Rule 68's demonstrative arm was not extended to a quantifier, its reason being that a demonstrative is no more a lexical noun than ὑμεῖς is, which would also carry to πάντες; the reading giving *are all* to the quantifier is therefore defensible and was not taken.
