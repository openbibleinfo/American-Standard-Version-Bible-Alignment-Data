# Jeremiah 49 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **JER.49.30** — When יעץ has an overt cognate object עצה and the ASV writes ‘take counsel,’ split the support verb ‘take’ from the noun ‘counsel’; do not absorb the written object into the verb.
- **JER.49.31** — When the paired second member writes waw plus לא and ASV renders the two together as ‘nor,’ fuse those two morphemes; the earlier לא independently takes ‘neither.’
