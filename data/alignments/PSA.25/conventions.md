# Psalms 25 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- לְמַעַן rendered 'For ... sake' (25.7, 25.11) is one discontinuous unit on the preposition headed on 'sake', the content word the phrase turns on (rules 24, 25); the construct noun keeps only its own 'name's' / 'goodness'.
- Where an articled substantival participle is the subject and the ASV fronts its pronoun before the verb — 'They shall be put to shame that deal treacherously' (25.3) — the article takes both 'They' and 'that' as one discontinuous unit headed on the pronoun (rule 41), and the finite verb keeps only its auxiliaries and lexical phrase (rule 17).
