# 1 Chronicles 18 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **1CH.18.4** — An indefinite article inside the English numeral itself — 'a thousand chariots', 'a hundred chariots' — is the numeral's own rendering (English writes 'a thousand' for 'one thousand') and joins the numeral's unit, not the counted noun's; rule 15's supplied-determiner sentences govern an article that determines the noun, and a plural counted noun takes no 'a'. Consistent with rule 42's own 'a hundred and twelve'.
- **1CH.18.13** — The refrain 'Jehovah gave victory to David' is written twice with different Hebrew: v.6 with לְדָוִיד, where the לְ takes 'to', and v.13 with אֶת־דָּוִיד, where the object marker takes the 'to' the light-verb phrase 'gave victory' needs to reach its object (rule 16, first family). Do not mark the v.13 marker unrepresented on the strength of v.6.
