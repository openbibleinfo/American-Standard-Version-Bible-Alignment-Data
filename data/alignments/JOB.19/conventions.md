# Job 19 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Compound מֵעַל + suffix: where the ASV writes one English preposition for it (JOB.19.13 'far from me'), מִן and עַל fuse into that word headed on the opening מִן (rules 23, 30); where the recasting promotes the suffix's referent to the English verb's direct object and spends no word on the relation (JOB.19.9 'stripped me of my glory'), each of the two prepositions is its own unrepresented unit (rules 2, 40), the suffix takes the object pronoun, and the 'of' that reaches the verb's own bare object (כְּבוֹדִי) is the verb's under rule 44.
