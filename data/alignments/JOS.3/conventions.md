# Joshua 3 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- מִלְמַעְלָה (3.13 s029-s032, 3.16 s007-s010) divides three ways: מִן takes from, the adverb מַעַל and its directive he share the single above under rules 30 and 32, and the intervening לְ is its own unrepresented unit, since the ASV writes only two words for the compound and each is already owned (rule 40).
- The chapter's frame and apodosis connectives are counted across verse boundaries, and 3.16 cannot be read for them from inside itself. 3.2 gives the הָיָה frame's that to the apodosis waw (rule 45); 3.13 gives it to הָיָה, because כְּ already holds the temporal clause's when and the apodosis writes no waw of its own; 3.14 writes no that at all, so there is none to place, and the waw before the priests is unrepresented because the ASV opens that clause bare; and the that opening 3.16 is the apodosis connective of the temporal clause begun at 3.15's וּכְבוֹא, taken by the waw written for it.
- תָּמַם standing beside a following verb is rendered adverbially by the ASV throughout this chapter — wholly at 3.16 s034, clean at 3.17 s027 — and takes that adverb as its own unit, while the verb beside it keeps the finite predicate. Giving the adverb to that neighbouring verb would leave תָּמַם unrendered.
- נֵד אֶחָד → in one heap (3.13 s035-s036, 3.16 s012-s013): the supplied in goes to the numeral אֶחָד, the word it stands before, under rule 33's placement sentence, and נֵד keeps the bare heap. Both verses were brought to this reading; attaching in to נֵד as the English phrase's head noun is the named alternative and is what 3.16 originally had.
