# Ezekiel 4 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- An English *even* is aligned to an `אֵת` only where that marker stands on the phrase the word introduces. EZK.4.1's `אֵת־יְרוּשָׁלִָם` → *portray upon it a city, even Jerusalem* takes it under rule 16; the appositional *even* before *three hundred and ninety days* in EZK.4.5 and EZK.4.9 is `added` under rule 30, because each clause's only markers stand on earlier objects the ASV renders bare, and neither is reached across the clause to avoid the `added`. EZK.4.13's *Even thus* is not this construction: *thus* alone is the whole of `כָּכָה`'s rendering and *Even* completes the ASV's emphatic expression rather than introducing an apposition, so it joins that morpheme's unit under rule 31; that verse's note names the competing `added` reading.
- `מִסְפַּר הַיָּמִים` → *according to the number of the days* (EZK.4.4, EZK.4.9) is read as one compound judgment in both verses. The italicized *according to* goes to the construct `מִסְפָּר` as the function words its adverbial accusative licenses under rule 33, and is not `added`; the ASV's two *the*s are then distributed by rule 29's arthrous-rectum clause, the present article on `הַיָּמִים` taking the one standing before *days* and the construct taking a supplied one. Nothing is fronted here, unlike EZK.4.11's `שִׁשִּׁית הַהִין` → *the sixth part of a hin*, where the ASV writes a single *the* and the present article takes it.
