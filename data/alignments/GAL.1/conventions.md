# Galatians 1 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- A Greek preposition the ASV renders by a multi-word English preposition takes the whole expression as one unit, headed on the contentful element and never on the bleached linking word: παρ᾽ ὅ → “other than” heads on “other”, as “out of” heads on “out” and “according to” on “according”. GAL.1.8 and GAL.1.9 write the identical construction and are headed alike.
- **GAL.1.20** — A relative pronoun the ASV expands with a supplied nominal noun is headed on that noun, not on the relativizer: ἃ → “touching the things which” heads on “things”. Where the expansion supplies only a pro-form and no nominal, the relativizer is still the head, so ὅ → “that which” heads on “which” in GAL.1.8 and GAL.1.9. This is rule 22's determiner clause holding for a relative pronoun, which the contract states only for the article.
