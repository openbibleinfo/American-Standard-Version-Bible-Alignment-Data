# Acts 16 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- A preverb particle the ASV writes as a separate word stays inside the compound verb's own unit where the governing preposition is ἀπό, whose English here is a bare of or from and does not carry the particle: ἐξελθεῖν ἀπ᾽ αὐτῆς → to come out of her (16.18) gives out to the verb and of to ἀπό, as ἀπελθεῖν ἀπὸ τῆς πόλεως → to go away from the city (16.39) and ἐξελθόντες ἀπὸ τῆς φυλακῆς → they went out of the prison (16.40) do. Rule 106's contrary arm is scoped to a preposition whose own gloss carries the particle, as ἐκ → out of does; where the preposition's gloss is the whole English expression the verb goes bare, which is why εἰσελθόντες εἰς → come and into (16.15) and εἰσῆλθον πρός → entered and into (16.40) leave the verb only its verb word.
- τις with an anarthrous noun keeps whatever indefinite word the ASV writes for it, and the noun receives a supplied determiner only where a second English word is left over for it. a certain disciple (16.1) and a certain maid (16.16) give certain to τις and the discontinuous a … disciple and a … maid to the noun under rule 51; a man of Macedonia (16.9), where the ASV writes only one determiner, gives a to τις and leaves ἀνήρ bare. Leaving τις unrepresented and reading the single a as a rule 51 helper on the noun remains defensible, but no contentful indefinite in this chapter is left empty.
