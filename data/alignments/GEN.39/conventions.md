# Genesis 39 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **GEN.39.12** — Unsupported English object pronouns with no source object morpheme are separate added units, including ‘him’ in ‘got him’ (39:12, 15) and ‘it’ in ‘the doer of it’ (39:22) and ‘made it to prosper’ (39:23).
- **GEN.39.21** — When שַׂר בֵּית הַסֹּהַר is rendered ‘keeper of the prison,’ intermediate בֵּית is unrepresented and final סֹהַר takes discontinuous ‘of … prison,’ headed on ‘prison,’ under rule 19.
- **GEN.39.23** — The compositional fusion בְּ + אֲשֶׁר → ‘because’ is aligned, not noncompositional; both morphemes contribute to the single English connective under rule 30.
