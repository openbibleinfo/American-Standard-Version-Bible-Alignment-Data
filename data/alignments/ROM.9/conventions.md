# Romans 9 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Versification: the ASV renders ROM.9.12's opening clause, οὐκ ἐξ ἔργων ἀλλ᾽ ἐκ τοῦ καλοῦντος, at the end of 9:11. Under rule 37 those seven source tokens stand unrepresented in 9:12 and their English is one added unit at the end of 9:11; neither verse reaches across, and the BSB comparison reports all seven as disagreements, which is not a correction.
- Where the ASV supplies a copula, or a copula with an expletive subject, for a verbless clause, the supplied words ride with the predicate word and never with the clause's negator, however adjacent the English writes them: Οὐχ οἷον → *it is … as* on the correlative at 9:6, οὐ τὰ τέκνα τῆς σαρκός → *it is … children* on the noun at 9:8, οὐ τοῦ θέλοντος → *it is of him* on the substantival article at 9:16, each leaving a bare *not* on the negator. 9:7, 9:14, 9:27 and 9:32 place a supplied subject, an existential *there* and a supplied lexical verb the same way. A clause with an expressed finite Greek verb is the contrast: the corpus keeps such a cleft frame on the negator, and 9:27's cleft over σωθήσεται puts it on the focused noun because no negator stands.
- The ASV's supplied appositional *even* is added at 9:10 and 9:24, where the appositive is carried by a name and by a pronoun. Rule 105 folds such a word only onto an expressed preposition, and the repeated English *by* of 9:10, which rule 103 assigns to πατρός, is not one. The unitalicized *even* of 9:30 is a different case and answers to the connective δέ, which stands unspent; that rendering is unattested across the corpus's 2022 δέ units, and it is kept because rule 70's English-side test leaves the particle nothing else to take.
