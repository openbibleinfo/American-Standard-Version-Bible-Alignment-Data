# Joshua 5 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- אֲשֶׁר with a resumptive preposition and suffix is divided by how many English words the ASV writes for the three morphemes, not by the construction. JOS.5.6 'unto whom Jehovah sware' writes two, so the relative takes 'whom', the resumptive לְ takes the 'unto' the ASV fronts with it (rule 25), and the resumptive suffix is unrepresented (rule 22); JOS.5.15 'the place whereon thou standest' writes one, so אֲשֶׁר, עַל and the suffix share one unit headed on the relative (rules 22, 30). The two are one policy, not a chapter split.
- שַׂר צְבָא יְהוָה is rendered twice and placed by rule 29's count of the ASV's determiners. JOS.5.14 'as prince of the host of Jehovah' supplies a 'the' on the middle construct צָבָא and gives each rectum its own 'of'; JOS.5.15 'the prince of Jehovah's host' recasts the inner relation as an English possessive, and the phrase's single written 'the' stands before 'prince', so it is the supplied determiner of the outer construct שַׂר and the divine name keeps only 'Jehovah's'. Rule 29's sentence putting a left-edge 'the' on the possessor governs a possessive that is the whole phrase (the king's gate), not one nested inside a larger chain.
