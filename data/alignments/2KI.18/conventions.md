# 2 Kings 18 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- רַב־שָׁקֵה is one fused unit rendered *Rabshakeh* at every occurrence (17, 19, 26, 27, 28, 37), headed on רַב; the coordinate title רַב־סָרִיס standing beside it in 17 is split, because the ASV writes *Rab saris* in two words. The shared רַב is not evidence either way: the ASV's word count decides each title on its own under rule 30.
- In the title הַמֶּלֶךְ חִזְקִיָּהוּ the article is `unrepresented` throughout (9, 13, 17), because the ASV writes *king Hezekiah* with no determiner; the noun keeps only its own word and nothing is supplied for the article.
- A gentilic adverbial of language takes the supplied *in the* and the supplied generic noun *language* into its own unit, headed on the gentilic: אֲרָמִית → *in the Syrian language* and יְהוּדִית → *in the Jews language* (26 twice, 28). Neither the preposition nor the generic is `added`, and neither is the head.
- Where the ASV rewrites a preposition over a member the Hebrew coordinates under one written preposition, the repetition belongs to that written morpheme and its unit is discontinuous: בְּכֹל מַלְכֵי יְהוּדָה וַאֲשֶׁר הָיוּ לְפָנָיו → *among ... nor among* (5), and בַּחְלַח וּבְחָבוֹר ... וְעָרֵי מָדָי → *in ... on ... in* (11), where the second *in* is the first בְּ's across the second member's own *on*. Where instead the bare nominal stands before any written preposition of its phrase, or none is written, the supplied word is the nominal's under rule 33: בֵית־יְהוָה → *in the house* (15), the two bare accusatives of Jerusalem (17), the language adverbials (26, 28), and קְרוּעֵי בְגָדִים → *with ... rent* (37).
- Where the ASV compresses a construct chain into a single English noun that writes only one member's lexeme, that member takes the word and the other is `unrepresented` rather than fused: מֵימֵי רַגְלֵיהֶם → *their own water* leaves רֶגֶל nothing (27), and אֶרֶץ זֵית יִצְהָר → *a land of olive trees* leaves יִצְהָר nothing (32). Rule 30's one-rendering condition is unmet in both, because the English noun is a word the ASV spends on the other morpheme in particular. BSB represents both; the disagreement was reviewed and is a convention difference.
