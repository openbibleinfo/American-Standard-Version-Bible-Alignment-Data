# Psalms 46 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **PSA.46.7** — The refrain יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת עִמָּנוּ מִשְׂגָּב־לָנוּ אֱלֹהֵי יַעֲקֹב (vv. 7, 11) and the opening אֱלֹהִים לָנוּ מַחֲסֶה (v. 1) are aligned alike: יהוה → Jehovah with `of hosts` on צָבָא; עִם → `is with` carrying the rule-49 copula; the predicate noun (מִשְׂגָּב, מַחֲסֶה) takes the copula `is` discontinuous around `our`; the possessive לְ is unrepresented and its suffix takes `our` (rule 40). Margin 'high tower' set aside.
- **PSA.46.9** — A subject pronoun the ASV supplies for a participial predicate with no nominal in the verse denoting it (`He maketh wars to cease`, מַשְׁבִּית) is added, not folded into the participle: rule 17's pronoun reaches a finite verb's person and number only, and rule 25's 1SA.2.8 example (מֵקִים → He raiseth up) is the precedent. Finite verbs in the same verse keep their `He` under rule 17.
