# Mark 12 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Versification: the projection carries δῶμεν ἢ μὴ δῶμεν at the end of MRK.12.14 and the ASV translates it at the head of MRK.12.15 ('Shall we give, or shall we not give?'). Rule 37 keeps each side in its own verse — the four tokens are their own unrepresented units in 12.14 and the English is one added unit in 12.15, each carrying a note naming the other verse. The BSB comparison reports three of those tokens as disagreements and that report is not a correction.
- Where the ASV writes an English object pronoun for each of two coordinated verbs and the Greek expresses it once, the expressed pronoun is aligned to the English pronoun standing with the verb that governs it in Greek, and the other is a rule 7 helper on the verb English requires it of: MRK.12.3 λαβόντες αὐτὸν ἔδειραν gives the first 'him' to αὐτόν and the second to ἔδειραν, while MRK.12.8 λαβόντες ἀπέκτειναν αὐτόν gives the first 'him' to λαβόντες and the second to αὐτόν. Supplied objects with no Greek pronoun at all fold the same way: 12.1 'set ... about it', 12.15 'see it', 12.16 'brought it', 12.34 'ask ... any question'.
- βάλλω rendered 'cast in' where the Greek verb is simplex and no preposition follows: the particle 'in' is a rule 7 helper inside the verb's own unit, neither a preverb realization under rule 106 nor added (12.41, 12.42, 12.43, 12.44 twice). Where εἰς is expressed it takes its own 'into' and the verb keeps a bare 'cast' (12.41, 12.43).
- Rule 67 leaves the head open where a supplied complex frame rides on an expressed proper preposition. MRK.12.26 takes the prepositional-word arm in both of its frames: περί -> 'as touching' heads on 'touching', the word that realizes the preposition, and ἐπί -> 'in the place concerning' heads on 'in', the supplied 'place' staying inside the unit. Read the chapter as that arm in both; the περί unit's note calls 'touching' the lexical word and names 'as' as the alternative, but 'as' is a frame modifier and not a preposition, so nothing here is precedent for heading such a frame on its supplied noun.
- A supplied English juncture rides with the constituent it introduces throughout this chapter, including the one configuration rules 88 and 98 leave open. At MRK.12.28 the ASV converts προσελθών and ἀκούσας into finite 'came and heard' but leaves εἰδώς a participle, and the supplied 'and' before 'knowing' is that participle's helper rather than an added seam: rule 98's added arm is scoped to two like constituents both rendered alike, which these are not. Marking it added remains defensible.
- The adverbial *in* of *cast in* is a rule 7 helper on the verb it completes and never a realization of an expressed preposition: 12:41, 12:42, 12:43 and 12:44 all keep it inside ballo's own unit, and 12:41 and 12:43 write both words, giving *into* to the expressed eis and *in* to the verb. No Greek preverb stands there, so rule 106 is not in question either.
- A Hebraistic eis with the accusative that the ASV absorbs into an English predicate is its own `unrepresented` unit under rule 36's absorbed-into-a-predicate clause: egenethe eis kephalen gonias at 12:10 gives *was made* to the verb and *the head* to the anarthrous noun under rules 51 and 58, and blepeis eis prosopon at 12:14 gives *regardest* to the verb and *the person* to the noun. A reference comparison reports both prepositions as represented, and that report is not a correction.
- A supplied complex frame carried by an expressed proper preposition is headed on the prepositional word in this chapter: epi at 12:26 takes *in the place concerning* headed on *in*, with rule 67's competing lexical-noun reading named in the unit. Where such a frame carries no lexical noun at all, rule 67's divided head does not reach it: peri at 12:26 takes *as touching* headed on *touching*, the word that realizes the preposition, and *as* is a supplied particle rather than the prepositional word.
- The closing domen e me domen of 12:14 stands in that verse of the projection and is translated at the head of 12:15 (*Shall we give, or shall we not give?*). Rule 37 keeps each side in its own verse: the four tokens are `unrepresented` in 12:14 and the English is one `added` unit in 12:15. Do not re-align either verse across the boundary to close the gap, and do not read the reference comparison's four disagreements at 12:14 as corrections.
