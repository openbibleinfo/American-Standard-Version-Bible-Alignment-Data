# Psalms 144 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- בְּנֵי נֵכָר -> *aliens* (144.7, 144.11) is one fused unit under rule 30, headed on נֵכָר under rule 23, since the single English word writes that noun's lexeme alone; the chain's *of* is the rectum's under rule 19 and בֵּן is not marked unrepresented beside it.
- Where a Hebrew substantive is rendered by a two-word English phrase closing on a class noun, this chapter read the class noun as rule 31's supplied generic and headed on the modifier: יָמִין -> *right hand* on *right* (144.8 twice, 144.11 twice), מִשְׂגָּב -> *My high tower* on *high* (144.2), זָוִית -> *corner stones* on *corner* (144.12). The competing head is named in each unit's note under rule 8. Corpus precedent agrees: יָמִין -> *right hand* heads on *right* in 42 units against two on *hand*.
- 144.3 writes two waws and three ASV connectives (*that*, *Or*, *that*). Both waws take the *that* standing where they stand, and the disjunctive *Or*, which opens a colon the Hebrew juxtaposes with no conjunction in it, is the surplus and is `added` under rule 14's closing sentences. The competing allocation gives *Or* to the second waw and marks the second *that* added; the first colon's identical waw -> *that* decides it.
- The datives לִי (144.2, beside מָגִנִּי, which carries the one written *My* on its own suffix) and לּוֹ (144.15, the ASV writing *in such a case* for כָּכָה alone) are each unrepresented together with their suffixes, the ASV's recasting writing nothing for either morpheme. These four tokens are four of the five BSB disagreements.
