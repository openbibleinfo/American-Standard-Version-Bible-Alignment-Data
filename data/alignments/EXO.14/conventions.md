# Exodus 14 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- In a construct chain whose article-bearing nomen rectum is rendered anarthrously, the rectum article takes the single English article fronted before the construct head; the construct head remains bare (as in 'the angel of God' and 'the pillar of cloud').
