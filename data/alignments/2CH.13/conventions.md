# 2 Chronicles 13 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The idiom הִתְחַזֵּק לִפְנֵי (hithpael of חָזַק with לִפְנֵי) rendered by the single English verb withstand is one three-morpheme unit under rule 40's fused-predicate sentence, headed on the verb, in both 2CH.13.7 (could withstand, with not left to לֹא) and 2CH.13.8 (withstand); the object or suffix after לִפְנֵי stays its own unit. The competing reading, leaving לִפְנֵי unrepresented beside the verb, was considered and rejected because no English word is spent on the preposition or its noun apart from the verb and the three morphemes are contiguous.
