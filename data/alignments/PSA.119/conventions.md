# Psalms 119 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **PSA.119.8** — The ASV prints the Hebrew letter name at the head of each of the psalm's 22 stanzas (ALEPH, BETH, DALETH, ...). No source morpheme licenses it: it is the edition's acrostic apparatus, not translated text, so each heading is its own `added` unit. This is the chapter's one recurring judgment that the testament contract does not already carry.
