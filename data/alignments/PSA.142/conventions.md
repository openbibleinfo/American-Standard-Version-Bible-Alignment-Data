# Psalms 142 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The two Sh tokens of this chapter — the paragogic he of הַקְשִׁיבָה (142.6) and of הוֹצִיאָה (142.7) — are each their own `unrepresented` unit under rule 32, and MACULA's glosses on them (*attention*, *out*) are the host word's gloss distributed across its segments in reverse order, the display defect rule 39 describes. The second is the trap: *out* stands in 142.7's target, but it is the English phrasal particle of הוֹצִיא → *Bring ... out* and belongs to the verb under rule 44, the ablative מִן taking the *of* beside it. Read the morphology tag and not the gloss; the he takes nothing in either verse.
