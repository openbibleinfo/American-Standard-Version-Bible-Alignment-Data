# Numbers 34 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Boundary itinerary: a supplied English to or at before a bare place name is licensed by that name's own directional or locative accusative and joins the name's unit under rule 33, never the verb of motion under rule 44 — to Hazar addar (34.4), at Hazar enan (34.9), to Riblah (34.11), at the Salt Sea (34.12), at Jericho (34.15). Where a directive he stands on the name instead, that morpheme takes the written word and the name keeps only its own (34.4, 34.5, 34.8, 34.9, 34.10, 34.12, 34.15). Read whether an Sd token is present before placing the word.
- The לָכֶם of the boundary formula is recast three ways, and the determiner count follows the recasting. Where the ASV writes a bare possessive, the לְ is unrepresented under rule 40 and the suffix takes your (34.3 twice, 34.4, 34.7, 34.9, 34.12); where that your is the nominal's only determiner, an article standing on the noun is unrepresented under rule 41 (34.4 הַגְּבוּל, 34.12 הָאָרֶץ). Where the ASV writes ye shall have (34.6), rule 43 gives shall have to הָיָה and the subject pronoun ye to the suffix, so no possessive determiner is spent and the article on הַיָּם keeps the written the. Where the ASV writes a preposition of its own, the לְ takes it (34.2, 34.7, 34.17).
- נָחַל rendered as a light verb plus complement is headed on the complement throughout the chapter: shall divide for inheritance (34.17), divide for inheritance (34.18), divide the inheritance (34.29), each headed on inheritance under rule 44, with heading on divide named as the competing reading. The single-word ye shall inherit (34.13) has no complement and heads on the verb.
