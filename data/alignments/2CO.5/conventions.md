# 2 Corinthians 5 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **2CO.5.15** — A fused negative adverb (mekéti, ouketi) rendered by a two-word English negation is one aligned unit headed on the negation, and the unit is recorded medium. Rule 24 forbids keying the head off the English surface phrase, so 5:15 'no longer' and 5:16 'no more' are analysed alike; the corpus head is genuinely contested, 14 units on the negation against 7 on the temporal word with unhedged units on both sides, so medium is what rule 16 asks for in both verses.
- **2CO.5.2** — Intensifying kai in kai gar is high, not medium: gar keeps 'For' and kai takes the intensifier the ASV writes ('verily' 5:2, 'indeed' 5:4). The corpus splits every kai gar this way unhedged and never renders gar by an intensifier in 225 units, so assigning 'For' to kai is not a materially different analysis under rule 16.
- **2CO.5.16** — PREP + article + ADV compressed into one English word: the article is always its own unrepresented unit, since no noncompositional unit in the corpus contains an article token. Which of two readings the phrase takes is decided on the English -- apo tou nyn -> henceforth is noncompositional on apo+nyn (rule 45), because nyn alone is 'now' in 54 of 62 corpus units and never 'henceforth', whereas 2CO.13.2's eis to palin -> again leaves eis unrepresented (rule 34) because 'again' is palin's own ordinary gloss.
