# 2 Samuel 20 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- MACULA tags the name שֶׁבַע (Sheba son of Bichri) as a numeral (Acfsa) throughout this chapter. It is the proper name and is aligned as one: Sheba, of Sheba, was Sheba, never as a number.
- Where the ASV renders an אַחֲרֵי whose verb is gapped or elided by an English verb of following — 'from following David', 'and followed Sheba' (20.2), 'let him follow Joab' (20.11) — that verb, with any volitive frame, is the preposition's own rendering of its relation, on rule 49's 'belonged to' reasoning, and is not a supplied lexical verb under rule 20.
