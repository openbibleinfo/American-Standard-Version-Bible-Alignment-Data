# Psalms 61 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **PSA.61.6** — דֹר וָדֹר rendered 'as many generations' is read as rule 22's distributive repetition though 'many' is a quantifier rather than a distributive: 'generations' is the noun's own word, so the first דֹר takes 'many generations' (headed on generations), the waw is unrepresented (rule 14) and the second דֹר unrepresented (rule 22). Contrast יוֹם יוֹם → 'daily' in 61.8, fused under rule 30 because the lone adverb spends nothing on either occurrence. The corpus carries both the split and the noncompositional shape for this phrase, so this is a chapter choice and not a rule.
