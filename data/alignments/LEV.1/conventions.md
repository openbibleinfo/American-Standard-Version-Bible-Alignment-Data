# Leviticus 1 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- אִשֶּׁה → *an offering made by fire* is one unit headed on *fire* (LEV.1.9, 1.13, 1.17). *fire* carries what distinguishes this offering from the others named beside it, as *burnt* does in *burnt offering* under rule 31; heading on *offering* was the competing reading and is defensible if *offering* is read as the lexeme-bearing class noun, and each verse's note names it.
- In אִשֵּׁה רֵיחַ נִיחֹחַ → *an offering made by fire, of a sweet savor* (LEV.1.9, 1.13, 1.17), רֵיחַ takes *of a … savor*, discontinuous around the attributive, and נִיחֹחַ takes *sweet* under rule 28. The *of* is the construct relation between אִשֵּׁה and its rectum רֵיחַ under rule 19; the verse notes cite rule 33's supplied preposition instead, which places the same word on the same token with the same head, so the edges are unaffected.
- בֶּן הַבָּקָר → *bullock* (LEV.1.5) is one rule 30 fusion of the two nouns, headed on בָּקָר, with the article taking the written *the*; the competing reading marks בֵּן unrepresented beside a bare בָּקָר → *bullock*. Contrast בְּנֵי הַיּוֹנָה → *young pigeons* (LEV.1.14), where the ASV spends a word on each, so rule 30's one-English-word condition is unmet, rule 2 splits the pair, and בְּנֵי takes the attributive *young* under rule 28 while the article goes unrepresented.
- The three parallel אִם protases place the copula on different morphemes because the predicate differs, and this is not an inconsistency. LEV.1.3 אִם עֹלָה קָרְבָּנוֹ מִן־הַבָּקָר → *If his oblation be a burnt offering of the herd* and LEV.1.14 אִם מִן־הָעוֹף עֹלָה קָרְבָּנוֹ → *if his oblation … be a burnt offering of birds* have a nominal predicate, so the supplied *be* joins עֹלָה under rule 20 and מִן keeps only its *of*; LEV.1.10 וְאִם־מִן־הַצֹּאן קָרְבָּנוֹ → *if his oblation be of the flock* has a prepositional predicate and no עֹלָה in the clause, so the *be* joins מִן and heads there under rule 49.
- הַמִּזְבֵּחָה is divided three ways throughout — article → *the*, מִזְבֵּחַ → *altar*, and the directive he → the locative preposition the ASV writes (*on* in LEV.1.9, 1.15; *upon* in LEV.1.13, 1.17) under rule 32. קֵדְמָה → *on the east part* (LEV.1.16) is divided on the same model, the he taking *on* and קֶדֶם keeping *the east part* headed on *east*; the competing reading gives *on the east part* whole to the adverb and marks the he unrepresented. צָפֹנָה → *northward* (LEV.1.11) is the other case, where one English word covers both morphemes and rule 30 fuses them.
