# Exodus 30 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **EXO.30.10** — When קֹדֶשׁ קָדָשִׁים is rendered ‘most holy,’ split the distinct English words: the construct head takes ‘holy’ and any predicate copula, while the plural nomen rectum takes ‘most.’
- **EXO.30.21** — An appositional ‘even’ is added when no source marker or connective licenses it; a following לְ separately takes ‘to.’
