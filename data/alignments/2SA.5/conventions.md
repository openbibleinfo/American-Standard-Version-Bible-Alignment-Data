# 2 Samuel 5 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- A bare place name standing after bo' as the goal of motion (Jerusalem 5.6, Gezer 5.25) is an adverbial accusative of direction: the ASV's supplied to belongs to the name under rule 33 and not to the verb under rule 44's supplied-preposition clause, which reaches only a direct object.
- mashach/hekhin X le-melek rendered 'anointed/established X king': the le before melek is its own unrepresented unit (rule 40) and melek takes the bare king; do not give the le an as.
- bekha'im → 'the mulberry trees': trees is the ASV's supplied generic and the unit heads on mulberry (rule 31); the supplied the joins the noun (5.23) or, where the article is written, goes to the article (5.24). The margin's balsam-trees is read and set aside (rule 38).
