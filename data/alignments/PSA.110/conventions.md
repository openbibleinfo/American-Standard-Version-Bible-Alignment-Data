# Psalms 110 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- yamin → 'right hand' (vv.1, 5) is one unit headed on 'right': the ASV never writes bare 'hand' for this noun, so 'hand' is the supplied generic under rule 31's tie-break and 'right' carries the lexeme. Heading on 'hand' is the named alternative in both notes.
