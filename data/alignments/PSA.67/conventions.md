# Psalms 67 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **PSA.67.3** — Appositional כֻּלָּם after a bare plural noun (עַמִּים כֻּלָּם) rendered 'all the peoples': כֹּל takes 'all', the noun takes 'the peoples' with its supplied the, and the 3mp suffix is unrepresented because the ASV writes no 'of them' (rules 19, 40). Applies identically in 67.5; BSB attaches the suffix to 'all' and disagrees by convention. The gloss on s008/s009 is reversed (rule 39).
