# Deuteronomy 8 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- A relative clause resumed by a pronominal suffix and rendered by a single *whose* is one unit on the relative and that suffix, headed on the relative, exactly as `אֲשֶׁר ... בָּהּ` → *wherein* is (DEU.8.9, both). The suffix is not `unrepresented`, and the noun between them keeps its own English. Where a further clause is coordinated under the same written `אֲשֶׁר` and the ASV rewrites *whose*, that second word goes to the coordinated clause's own resumptive suffix under rule 18, not to the relative as a distributed repetition.
- Supplied English subordinators in this chapter are counted against the conjunction morphemes standing in each clause, and the chapter writes both outcomes. Where a waw stands at the clause's left edge and the ASV writes coordinator and subordinator contiguously, the pair is one rendering of that waw and neither word is a surplus: *and when* (DEU.8.13), *and lest* (DEU.8.17, italic). Where the clause's only conjunction is `פֶּן`, which already holds its own *lest*, a further *when* is subordinator beside subordinator, no morpheme answers to it, and it is `added` (DEU.8.12). The same count leaves DEU.8.5's apodosis *so* `added` for want of any conjunction, while DEU.8.14's *then* goes to the waw written for it.
- The ASV writes *fed thee with manna* twice from different Hebrew, and the two are deliberately aligned differently: DEU.8.3 has `אֵת` on `הַמָּן` and the marker takes the *with* (rule 16), while DEU.8.16 writes the object bare and the *with* joins the verb's unit (rule 44). Do not harmonise them; the present marker is what decides.
