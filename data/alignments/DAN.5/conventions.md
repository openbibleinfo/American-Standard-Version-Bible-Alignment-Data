# Daniel 5 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **DAN.5.3** — In Aramaic genitive chains, a separately written 'of' belongs to the genitive linker and a separately written 'the' belongs to the determined-state ending; when the genitive is recast possessively, the linker is unrepresented while any separate article remains with the determined-state ending.
