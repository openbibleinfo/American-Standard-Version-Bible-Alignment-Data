# Psalms 60 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **PSA.60.0** — Superscription split (rule 54): the ASV prints the historical title 'when he strove ... twelve thousand' in verse 0, while WLC carries its Hebrew at the head of PSA.60.1 (s001–s023 there). Verse 0 holds that English as one added unit with a null head; verse 1 marks each of those 23 morphemes its own unrepresented unit. Neither verse reaches into the other.
- **PSA.60.1** — Phrasal particles the ASV writes for a single Hebrew verb stay in the verb's unit under rule 44 throughout the chapter — 'cast off' (זָנַח, v1, v10), 'broken down' (פָּרַץ, v1), 'goest forth' (יָצָא, v10), 'mete out' (מָדַד, v6), 'tread down' (בּוּס, v12) and 'restore ... again' (שׁוּב polel, v1) — discontinuous around an object suffix where one intervenes (rule 25). 'again' is kept because the return it states is what שׁוּב writes, not a repetition the Hebrew leaves unwritten; the added reading is named in the v1 note.
