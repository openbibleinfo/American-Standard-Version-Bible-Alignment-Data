# Proverbs 16 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Where one Hebrew substantive is rendered by a multi-word English phrase, this chapter settles rule 31's tie-break by asking whether the Hebrew word names a member of the English noun's class. מַלְקוֹשׁ → *the latter rain* (16.15), אַלּוּף → *chief friends* (16.28) and מָקוֹר → *a well spring* (16.22) head on the class noun, because the ASV writes *rain*, *friend* and *spring* alone for other words of those classes and the second word is its specifier. קֶסֶם → *A divine sentence* (16.10), עָמֵל → *the laboring man* (16.26), תַּהְפֻּכוֹת → *perverse things* (16.30) and שֵׂיבָה → *The hoary head* (16.31) head on the modifier, because the English noun is a generic the ASV supplied to complete the phrase and the Hebrew word is no kind of sentence, man, thing or head. Each of the seven units names its competing head under rule 8.
- Both rule 30 fusions in this chapter write more than one morpheme's sense inside the single English word — צוּף־דְּבַשׁ → *honeycomb* (16.24) and לֹא מִשְׁפָּט → *injustice* (16.8) — so neither is settled by rule 23's content sentence, and both head on the morpheme standing first and governing the rest. 16.8 was corrected at reconciliation from מִשְׁפָּט to לֹא: that the one English word writes a noun's own lexeme is a target-head criterion under rule 24 and not a source-head one.
