# Genesis 41 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **GEN.41.27** — Across GEN.41.6, .23, and .27, קָדִים rendered 'east wind' heads on 'east': loss of 'east' loses the lexeme, while 'wind' is the generic completing noun.
- **GEN.41.27** — The identical רַע rendering 'ill favored' in GEN.41.20 and .27 heads on 'ill', which carries the negative lexeme; 'favored' completes the phrase.
- **GEN.41.46** — Where עבר בְּ is rendered 'went throughout' (GEN.41.29 and .46), 'went' belongs to the verb and 'throughout' to the preposition as the smallest meaningful units.
