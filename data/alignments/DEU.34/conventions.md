# Deuteronomy 34 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Compound preposition renderings in this chapter: the ASV's *over against* is one rendering no morpheme owns a part of, so `al-penei` (34.1) fuses under rule 30 with the verbless clause's copula joining the whole expression, headed on the construct noun and on *over*, and the single-morpheme `mul` (34.6) takes the same compound whole. `al-pi` (34.5) is split instead, because the ASV spends *according to* on the preposition and *the word* on the construct noun, so each morpheme has a rendering of its own; the head of the preposition's unit is *according*, not the connective *to*. Read which of the two shapes the ASV wrote before reaching for either outcome.
- The age idiom `ben` + numeral + `shanah` (34.7, *Moses was a hundred and twenty years old when he died*) is divided with the construct `ben` taking both the construction-supplied copula and *old*, discontinuous around the numeral phrase (rules 20, 25), while each numeral keeps its own English, the *a* of *a hundred* belongs to the numeral rather than to the plural counted noun, and `shanah` keeps *years*. Neither *old* nor the copula is `added`, and neither belongs to `shanah`.
