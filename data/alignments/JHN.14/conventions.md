# John 14 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Where a Greek clause is verbless and the ASV supplies a copula before a prepositional predicate, the copula rides with the preposition, not with the subject pronoun: 'ego en to patri' -> *I am in the Father* gives *I* to the pronoun and *am in* to 'en' (JHN.14.10, 14.11, 14.20). Matches JHN.10.38 and thirteen corpus units on 'en'.
