# John 2 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The article before a personal or place name is unrepresented, whether the ASV gives the name no determiner at all (τῆς Γαλιλαίας → 'of Galilee', τοῖς Ἱεροσολύμοις → 'Jerusalem') or the name stands as a bare subject (ὁ Ἰησοῦς → 'Jesus'). Applied at JHN.2.1, 2.2, 2.3, 2.4, 2.7, 2.11, 2.13, 2.22, 2.23.
- ἀρχιτρίκλινος is rendered by the fixed English phrase 'ruler of the feast', which has no independently recoverable parts, so it stands as one unit headed on 'ruler'. Applied at JHN.2.8 and twice at JHN.2.9.
- **JHN.2.5** — A supplied English object pronoun is a helper on the verb that licenses it, not an added unit: ποιήσατε → 'do it' (JHN.2.5) and ἤνεγκαν → 'bare it' (JHN.2.8), both at high confidence.
