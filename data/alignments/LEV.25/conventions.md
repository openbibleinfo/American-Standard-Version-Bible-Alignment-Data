# Leviticus 25 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **LEV.25.26** — When the ASV renders the hand-reaching or hand-finding-sufficiency idiom as 'be waxed rich' or 'be able,' the lexical idiom tokens form one noncompositional unit, while the possessive suffix separately takes the recast English subject pronoun.
- **LEV.25.31** — In the recurring redemption-plus-hayah recasting, the redemption noun takes the lexical English redeem(ed), hayah takes the written modal/copular material, the dative remains unrepresented when English spends no preposition on it, and its suffix takes the recast subject pronoun.
- **LEV.25.46** — In the reciprocal construction where a kinship noun plus possessive suffix is rendered by the single English word 'another,' treat the source phrase as noncompositional rather than as an ordinary multi-source aligned unit.
