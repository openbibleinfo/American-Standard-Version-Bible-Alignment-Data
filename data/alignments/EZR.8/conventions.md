# Ezra 8 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Census refrain וְעִמּוֹ N הַזְּכָרִים → 'and with him N males': the ASV writes no copula and no the, so עִם takes bare 'with', the suffix 'him' (or 'them' at 8.14, aligned as written), the numeral its own word, and the article on הַזְּכָרִים is unrepresented under rule 29. The anarthrous לִזְכָרִים of 8.3 → 'of the males' is the one exception, לִ taking 'of' and the noun the supplied 'the'. Where the ASV does write a copula with עִם (8.33 'with him was Eleazar') it joins the preposition under rule 49; the refrain's bare 'with' is not a second treatment of the same construction.
