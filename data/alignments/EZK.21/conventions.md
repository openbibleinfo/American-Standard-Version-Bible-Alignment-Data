# Ezekiel 21 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The suffixal ending MACULA segments off ADONAI is unrepresented throughout this chapter (21.7, 21.9, 21.13, 21.24, 21.26, 21.28). The noun itself takes the ASV's the Lord, with the supplied the on the anarthrous rectum under rule 15, except in 21.9 where the ASV writes Jehovah alone for it and the noun takes that single word. This is what the six Sp1cs BSB disagreements are, and it is a convention difference rather than an error.
- Where the ASV renders a bare LO by no more and no OD stands in the clause, both words belong to the negative's unit, headed on no: shall be no more (21.13), shall be no more the same (21.26), this also shall be no more (21.27), thou shalt be no more remembered (21.32). Where OD is present the two are split, the negative taking only its own not and OD taking any more headed on more (21.5). Do not read the four as rule 35's never fusion; the contrast is decided by whether the temporal morpheme stands in the clause.
- The prophetic word-formula verses 21.1, 21.8 and 21.18 carry identical source text and are aligned identically apart from the waw: 21.1 and 21.8 give it the ASV's And, and 21.18, where the ASV writes no And but writes again at the clause's close, gives it again under rule 14's open list. The competing reading marks that waw unrepresented and again added, and is recorded in the unit's note. HAYAH takes only came in all three, since the word of Jehovah is an explicit subject and no presentative frame is written.
- A possessive the ASV supplies before an anarthrous body-part or abstract noun where neither a suffix nor an article stands is its own added unit: thy loins (21.6), thy thigh (21.12), thy hands (21.14), their heart (21.15). Where an article does stand and the ASV writes a possessive in its place, the article takes it under rule 41 - their stumblings for HAMMIKSHOLIM in 21.15, in the same verse as the added their of their heart. Read the phrase for an article or a suffix before placing such a word.
- Rule 30 fusions in this chapter are recorded as aligned multi-source units, not as noncompositional: LAKEN to therefore (21.4, 21.12, 21.24), EL-KAF to together (21.14, 21.17), AL-MAH to Wherefore (21.7), and ASHER plus a possessive suffix or LO plus suffix to whose (21.25, 21.27, 21.29). The chapter contains no noncompositional unit; the corpus uses both statuses for such fusions, and the choice here is uniform so that a later reader does not have to infer it unit by unit.
- HALAL rendered deadly wounded heads on wounded in every occurrence (21.14 twice, 21.25, 21.29), since dropping wounded loses the lexeme and deadly alone does not name it (rule 31). On the of in those phrases the chapter reads two configurations: in 21.14 the chain HEREV HALAL HAGGADOL has one construct link, so the written of belongs to that link's rectum HALAL even though the ASV puts it before the great one; in 21.29 the chain TSAVVEREI HALLEI RESHAIM has two links and the ASV writes only one of, so it goes to RESHAIM, the word it stands before, exactly as rule 29 counts the ASV's the's. Both units carry their competing reading in a note.
