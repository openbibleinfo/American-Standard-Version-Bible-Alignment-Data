# James 3 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **JAS.3.3** — Where the ASV supplies a possessive and every genitive pronoun in the verse is already spent on English of some other role, the article takes the possessive under rule 20's second arm as rule 76 reads it. Applied at JAS.3.3 (τὰ → *their*, ἵππων on *horses'*, αὐτούς on *they*, αὐτῶν on *their body*) and JAS.3.13 (τῆς → *his*, αὐτοῦ on *his works*). The contrast is a genitive that is free, which empties the article under rule 20 proper: JAS.3.3 τὸ, JAS.3.6 τοῖς, JAS.3.13 τὰ, JAS.3.14 τῇ. Both verses write both treatments, as 2PE.1.5 does. A shared genitive scoping over coordinated nouns is not this case and empties both articles (REV.1.14, EPH.2.1).
- **JAS.3.5** — Where one Greek word is rendered by an English phrase, this chapter heads the word the corpus spends on that lexeme, never the phrase's formal English head, and where the lexeme is unattested it queries the neighbouring class rather than decomposing the Greek. ἡλίκην → *how much* on *much* and ἡλίκον → *how small* on *small* (parity with τηλικαῦτα → *so great* on *great*, JAS.3.4); ἑρπετῶν → *of creeping things* on *creeping* (רֶמֶשׂ, five units); ἐναλίων → *things in the sea* on *sea*; συκῆ → *a fig tree* on *tree* (four units); εὐπειθής → *easy to be entreated* on *entreated* (εὐ- compounds and the πειθ- root); φλογίζουσα → *setteth on fire* on *fire* (the set-plus-complement frame). Rule 99's *what manner of* frame is scoped to a quality interrogative with a supplied sortal noun and does not reach a degree correlative whose completion is a dimension adjective.
