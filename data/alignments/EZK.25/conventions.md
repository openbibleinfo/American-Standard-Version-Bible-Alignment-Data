# Ezekiel 25 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The divine title אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה (9 occurrences) is aligned as three units throughout: the ASV's supplied 'the' goes on the construct אֲדֹנָי together with 'Lord' (rule 29), the 1cs suffix is unrepresented because the ASV writes no possessive for it, and יְהוִה takes 'Jehovah'. Where the title is itself the rectum of a further construct — EZK.25.3 'Hear the word of the Lord Jehovah' — אֲדֹנָי additionally takes the relational 'of' and the second written 'the' under rules 19 and 29, the first 'the' standing on דְּבַר. The corpus's competing noncompositional reading, which fuses אֲדֹנָי with its suffix into 'the Lord' (11 units, Isaiah), was not followed; the aligned reading matches the corpus's dominant 140 units.
- יַעַן rendered 'Because that' (EZK.25.8, EZK.25.12) is one compound subordinator fused into the particle's single unit under rule 30, headed on 'Because'. It is not 'Because' beside an added 'that' under rule 55: a conjunction does stand in the clause, the two English words are contiguous, and dropping 'that' diminishes only the expression as a whole. Where the ASV writes 'Because' alone (25.3, 25.6, 25.15) the particle takes just that word.
- In the idiom שְׁאָט בְּנֶפֶשׁ (EZK.25.6 'with all the despite of thy soul', EZK.25.15 'with despite of soul') the בְּ is what the ASV renders 'of', and נֶפֶשׁ keeps only 'soul'. The 'of' is not a construct relation to be placed by rule 19 — no construct chain is written there — and the preposition is the only morpheme standing before 'soul'.
