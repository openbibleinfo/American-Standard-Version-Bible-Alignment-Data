# Leviticus 18 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Determiner precedence in this chapter, for a determiner the ASV writes once for a whole phrase: the morpheme that is itself what the word says takes it, and any other determiner morpheme in the phrase goes unrepresented. For any: a present כֹּל takes it (18.23 בְכָל־בְּהֵמָה any beast, 18.24, 18.26, 18.29), the partitive מִן taking only its own of; with no כֹּל standing, the partitive מִן takes the whole any of, headed on of (18.21 מִזַּרְעֲךָ any of thy seed, 18.30 מֵחֻקּוֹת any of these abominable customs), since the following noun already carries a determiner of its own and cannot be what the any determines; with neither morpheme present, the supplied any joins the noun's unit under rule 15 (18.23 אִשָּׁה any woman). For these: a written אֵלֶּה takes it and both articles of the phrase are unrepresented (18.26, 18.27, 18.29), while with no demonstrative written the article itself takes the these standing before the phrase (18.30 הַתּוֹעֵבֹת these ... abominable).
- Supplied English connectives across the chapter: the word goes to a waw standing in the clause where one is present and has not already been spent, and is added where none is. Rendered on a waw: even (18.7, 18.29), or (18.17), therefore (18.5, 18.25, 18.26, 18.30), neither ... nor as one correlative pair (18.26), that (18.28), and that (18.30). Added: the appositional even of 18.9 and 18.10, where the clause holds neither a waw nor an object marker for it to rest on (rule 16); the conditional if of 18.5, the protasis writing no conjunction at all while אֲשֶׁר already holds its which (rule 55); and the also of 18.28, where the verse's one waw has already been spent on that. Do not fold any of these into a neighbouring noun, negative or verb to avoid the added.
- The idiom נָתַן שְׁכֹבֶת (18.20, 18.23) is divided so that the finite verb carries the whole English finite verb — thou shalt lie, with auxiliary and subject pronoun under rule 17 — even though the ASV's lie recasts give. שְׁכֹבֶת then takes whatever single word is left for it (carnally at 18.20) and is unrepresented where the ASV writes none (18.23), as are its suffix in both verses and the לְזָרַע phrase of 18.20 (rule 40). The competing reading, which puts lie on the noun and leaves the verb only thou shalt, is what BSB's four disagreements in these two verses reflect.
- In the kinship chains of 18.8–18.17 the ASV's one relational of goes to the rectum of the outermost construct and every inner rectum is rendered as an English possessive, whatever the depth of the chain: עֶרְוַת אֵשֶׁת אָבִיךָ → The nakedness of thy father's wife gives of ... wife to אִשָּׁה and father's to אָב (18.8), and the three-level עֶרְוַת בַּת־אֵשֶׁת אָבִיךָ → of thy father's wife's daughter gives of ... daughter to בַּת with wife's and father's inside it (18.11). The outer construct עֶרְוָה takes the supplied the under rule 29, and the unit holding the of is discontinuous around the English possessives under rule 25.
