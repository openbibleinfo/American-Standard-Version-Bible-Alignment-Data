# Job 9 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **JOB.9.26** — A supplied relative before a finite clause with an explicit antecedent is added when no article or relative morpheme licenses it; the articleless-attributive treatment applies to adjectives and participles, not finite verbs.
