# Job 31 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Job 31 writes both `מִי יִתֵּן` and `אִם לֹא` in both of rule 30's shapes, and each occurrence is decided by the ASV's rendering rather than by the lemma pair. Where the ASV writes one fixed English expression for the pair, the two morphemes share one `noncompositional` unit — `מִי יִתֵּן` → *Oh that* in v35, `אִם לֹא` → *Surely* in v36 — and rule 6's fallback supplies the source head, since neither morpheme's own lexeme is written there. Where the ASV instead spends a word on each morpheme, rule 2 splits them and each takes what is written for it — `מִי` → *Who* beside `יִתֵּן` → *can find* in v31, and `אִם` → *If* beside `לֹא` → *not* in v20 and v31. Rule 17's sentence that a present `מִי יִתֵּן` takes its own fixed English formula is read as it stands, under rule 30, and does not license fusing an occurrence the ASV has rendered as an ordinary interrogative or protasis.
- In this chapter's interrogative verbless clauses the single written copula belongs to the interrogative morpheme, not to the predicate nominal standing beside it. `הֲ` takes the fronted *Is* of v3, where rule 20's fronted-copula sentence makes that one word both question auxiliary and copula, and `מָה` takes the *is* of v2, where it is itself the clause's predicate over a definite construct-chain subject. The impersonal *it* the ASV writes in v3 is not a presentative dummy and is its own `added` unit.
- Where one Hebrew morpheme's English is rewritten at the head of a clause coordinated under it, this chapter distributes every occurrence to that morpheme rather than marking the later one `added`: the one `אִם` of v7 and of v20 takes both of its *if*s, and the one `הֲ` and the one `לֹא` of v15 and the one `לֹא` of v20 take both of theirs. The same reading was extended to the italicized supply of v35, where *that I had* repeats the optative formula, the possessive `לְ` and its suffix over the coordinated second clause; an ASV italic corroborates an `added` reading but does not decide it (rule 7), and the competing reading is named in that verse.
