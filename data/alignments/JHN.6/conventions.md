# John 6 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **JHN.6.23** — Improper preposition (an adverb governing a case): the adverb keeps only the English phrase that renders it, and where the ASV supplies a determiner the accompanying preposition realizes the case and rides with the article under rule 21. peran tes thalasses -> 'on the other side' | 'of the' | 'sea' (JHN.6.1, 6.22, 6.25); engys tou topou -> 'nigh' | 'unto the' | 'place' (JHN.6.19, 6.23). Where the adverb is itself rendered by a single English preposition the article takes a bare 'the': peran tes thalasses -> 'over' | 'the' | 'sea' (JHN.6.17).
- **JHN.6.31** — The ASV gives 'heaven' no determiner, so the article of ek/apo tou ouranou is its own unrepresented unit and the preposition stays with the noun (rule 23): JHN.6.31, 6.32 (x2), 6.33, 6.38, 6.41, 6.42, 6.50, 6.51.
- **JHN.6.14** — Recitative hoti introducing direct speech has no English realization and is its own unrepresented unit (JHN.6.14, 6.42).
