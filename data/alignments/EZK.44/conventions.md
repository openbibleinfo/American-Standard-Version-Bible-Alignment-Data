# Ezekiel 44 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Rule 30 fusions of several morphemes into a single English word are recorded aligned in this chapter, not noncompositional: therein at 44.3 and 44.14, theirs at 44.29, wherein at 44.19. The chapter carries no noncompositional unit. This is recorded as a chapter judgment rather than proposed as a contract ruling because the corpus is genuinely divided on the neighbouring case - therein runs 33 aligned against 26 noncompositional - while for the relative-plus-preposition wherein it shows 20 aligned units of the identical shape and no noncompositional one among the commonest treatments.
- The divine-speech formula is divided the same way in all five of its occurrences (44.6, 44.9, 44.12, 44.15, 44.27): adon takes the supplied the with its own noun under rule 15, its lexicalized 1cs suffix is unrepresented because the ASV writes no possessive for it, YHWH takes Jehovah, and where the formula is introduced by neum that noun takes the finite saith. BSB represents the suffix, which is the documented convention difference and not an error.
- The compound me-alay is divided by rule 40 in all three of its occurrences (44.10 twice, 44.15): min takes the single written from and al is unrepresented, since the ASV spends no English word on what al contributes. The pair is not fused under rule 30, because from renders min alone while al's own sense is dropped, which is the split rule 2 requires.
