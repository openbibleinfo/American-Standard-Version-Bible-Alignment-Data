# 1 Chronicles 9 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **1CH.9.13** — Construct-chain ownership follows rules 19 and 29: relational ‘of’ belongs to the nomen rectum; an article on that rectum takes a written fronted ‘the’ and otherwise remains unrepresented.
- **1CH.9.23** — A supplied lexical predicate in a verbless prepositional clause is added; the preposition retains only a separately written relation such as ‘of’ or ‘to,’ and is unrepresented where the recasting leaves none.
