# 1 Kings 13 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **1KI.13.2** — The lexeme bamah, rendered high places, heads on high rather than places: high carries the Hebrew lexeme's content and places is the generic English noun supplied to expand a substantive, exactly as rule 31's dry land heads on dry and rule 24's living creature on living.
