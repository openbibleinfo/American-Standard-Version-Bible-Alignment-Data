# Nehemiah 2 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **NEH.2.4** — `אֱלֹהֵי הַשָּׁמַיִם` → *the God of heaven* (2.4, 2.20): the ASV renders *heaven* anarthrously and writes one *the*, so the article on the rectum takes that fronted *the* under rule 29 and `אֱלֹהֵי` keeps the bare *God*; the construct takes no supplied *the*. The verse pass was first tempted to leave the article unrepresented and supply the *the* on the construct; that is the competing reading and it was rejected.
- **NEH.2.13** — Periphrastic `הָיָה` + participle rendered by a single English finite verb — `אֱהִי שֹׂבֵר` → *viewed* (2.13, 2.15), `אֱהִי עֹלֶה` → *went I up* (2.15) — is one fused unit under rule 30, headed on the participle, which carries the lexeme; `הָיָה` is not marked unrepresented beside it. The competing reading marks `הָיָה` unrepresented and is named in each note.
- **NEH.2.5** — The pleasing idiom is read by contiguity. `אִם־עַל־הַמֶּלֶךְ טוֹב` → *If it please the king* (2.5, 2.7) gives *it please* to `טוֹב` as its fixed rendering under rule 20 and leaves `עַל` unrepresented under rule 40, because the article and `מֶלֶךְ` stand between the preposition and the adjective, so rule 30/40 fusion cannot reach it; `וַיִּיטַב לִפְנֵי הַמֶּלֶךְ` → *it pleased the king* (2.6) fuses the verb, the `לְ` and `פְּנֵי` in one unit headed on the verb, as `רַע בְּעֵינֵי` → *it displease* does, because there the three are contiguous and none has English of its own.
