# 1 Chronicles 21 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- גֹּרֶן → 'threshing floor' heads on *threshing* in every occurrence (15, 18, 21, 22, 28): rule 31's subtraction ties, and the tie is broken by the ASV never writing a bare 'floor' for גֹּרֶן, so 'floor' is the supplied generic. The competing head on 'floor' is named in the notes.
- אָמַר rendered 'commanded' (17, 18, 27) takes that word alone, and the preposition standing on the addressee — אֶל in v18 ('commanded Gad'), לַ in v27 ('commanded the angel') — is unrepresented under rule 40 because the ASV writes the addressee as a bare object. Where the same verb is 'said' the addressee's אֶל/לְ takes its own 'unto'/'to'.
- וְאִם in the three-option list of v12 is counted word by word under rule 14: where the ASV writes only 'or' the waw is unrepresented and אִם takes it; where it writes 'or else' the waw takes 'else' and אִם 'or'. The opening אִם takes 'either'.
