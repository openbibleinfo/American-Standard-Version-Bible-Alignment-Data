# Numbers 21 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- An ad rendered *even unto* is one aligned unit on the preposition, headed on *unto*; the *even* is not `added` (21.24 s017, 21.26 s026, 21.30 s006 and s010). No waw and no object marker stands in any of those clauses to answer for the word, and the corpus aligns a preposition to *even unto* in 33 units and a waw to *even unto* in none. Where the ASV writes a bare *unto* instead, the preposition takes only that (21.24 s015, 21.30 s013).
- derek plus a place name rendered *by the way of X* (21.1, 21.4, 21.33): the supplied *by* is the adverbial accusative's own under rule 33 and stays with derek, which keeps its English noun *way*, so rule 19's directional-construct rider does not reach it and the relation still goes to the rectum. The phrase's single *the* is then placed by rule 29 on the evidence of the rectum: fronted onto a present article the ASV renders anarthrously (21.1, 21.33), and supplied on the construct where the chain writes no article morpheme at all (21.4). Where the ASV instead spends its own noun on the construct — 21.22 *the king's highway* — rule 29's possessive case governs the *the*.
