# 2 Chronicles 32 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Rule 31's class-noun tie guidance was applied by the test the rule states and not by the shape of the English phrase. Where the ASV writes the generic noun alone for a Hebrew word of that same class in this chapter, the head stays on that noun: מִסְכְּנוֹת -> *store houses* heads on *houses*, because בַּיִת -> *house* stands at 2CH.32.21, and תִּירוֹשׁ -> *new wine* heads on *wine*, which is what the word is. Where it does not, the noun is a supplied generic and the head is the modifier: מִגְדָּנוֹת -> *precious things*, חֲסָדָיו -> *good deeds*, גִּבּוֹר -> *mighty men*, רְחוֹב -> *broad place*, בָּמָה -> *high places*, מַעֲרָב -> *west side*, זֹאת -> *this manner*, יְהוּדִית -> *the Jews language*.
- Where the ASV subordinates to a written antecedent something the Hebrew writes as a phrase rather than a clause — an appositional nominal or a bare prepositional phrase — the supplied relative answers to no morpheme and is its own `added` unit under rule 55, while the construction-supplied copula joins the predicate morpheme: מַעֲשֵׂה יְדֵי הָאָדָם -> *which are the work of men's hands* at 2CH.32.19 leaves *which* added and gives *are* to מַעֲשֶׂה under rule 20, and כָּל בִּלְבָבוֹ -> *all that was in his heart* at 2CH.32.31 leaves *that* added and gives *was in* to the בְּ under rule 49. Rule 46's nominal-recasting paragraph was read and set aside in both: it reaches a noun the ASV recasts as head noun and relative clause together, and here the antecedent is a different written word that already holds its own English.
- An emphatic הוּא standing before a written proper name takes the whole determiner expression the ASV writes for it and the name keeps only its own word: הוּא יְחִזְקִיָּהוּ -> *the same Hezekiah* at 2CH.32.12 and *This same Hezekiah* at 2CH.32.30. Head on the demonstrative word where the ASV writes one (*This*), and on the identifying word where it writes only an article beside it (*same*); neither word is `added` and the name is not a home for either.
