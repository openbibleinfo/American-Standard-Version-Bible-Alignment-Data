# Isaiah 58 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **ISA.58.6** — When the ASV writes 'and that' or 'and if' for a clause with only one waw, the waw takes 'and' and the surplus subordinator is added under rule 55.
- **ISA.58.7** — When ASV intensifies a possessive suffix as 'thine own', both words belong to the suffix; where no suffix or other possessive morpheme exists, the supplied possessive expression is added.
- **ISA.58.5** — In this chapter's yes/no questions, interrogative he is unrepresented when ASV expresses interrogativity only by inversion; the copula or auxiliary remains with the predicate that licenses it.
