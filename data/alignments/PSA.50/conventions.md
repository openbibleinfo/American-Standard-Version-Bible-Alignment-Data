# Psalms 50 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **PSA.50.11** — A prepositional predicate with a 1cs suffix that the ASV renders by the English genitive 'mine' with a copula — לִי → 'is mine' (vv10, 12), עִמָּדִי → 'are mine' (v11) — is one unit of preposition + suffix + copula, headed on 'mine', under rule 49's closing paragraph; no word is spent on the preposition's own relation, so עִמָּד is read exactly as לְ. The competing reading (copula on the preposition alone, 'mine' on the suffix) is named in the v11 note.
