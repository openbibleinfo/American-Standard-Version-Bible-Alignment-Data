# Philippians 4 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **PHP.4.6** — A genitive or dative article whose noun the ASV renders with no English determiner is unrepresented, and the English case preposition stays with the noun: τῇ προσευχῇ → “by prayer”, τῆς εἰρήνης → “of peace”, τῷ θεῷ → “to God”. Rule 21's article-carries-the-preposition unit needs an English “the” to anchor it (τοῦ εὐαγγελίου → “of the gospel”, 4.15; τοῦ κυρίου → “of the”, 4.23).
