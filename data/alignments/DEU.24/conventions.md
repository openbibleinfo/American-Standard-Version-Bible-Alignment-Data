# Deuteronomy 24 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- לוֹ לְאִשָּׁה is aligned by the morphemes each verse actually writes. At v3, where the Hebrew has no verb, the resultative frame *to be* goes to the לְ of לְאִשָּׁה (rule 49) and the לְ of לוֹ is unrepresented; at v4 the written לִהְיוֹת supplies both words, so *to* is the לִ's and *be* the infinitive's, and the לְ of לוֹ and the לְ of לְאִשָּׁה are both unrepresented (rules 40, 52). The two readings differ because the source differs, not because one construction was analysed twice.
- אַחֲרֶיךָ is aligned as the ASV wrote it in each verse: *again* at v20, with the suffix unrepresented under rule 22, and *after thee* at v21, with the suffix taking *thee*. Identical Hebrew rendered two ways by the ASV is not a split, and neither verse should be moved to match the other.
- ASV italics in this chapter fall both ways under rule 7, decided by whether a morpheme licenses the word rather than by the markup. *due to* (v17) joins the rectum גֵּר as the construct relation the ASV recast (rule 19), and *the grapes of* (v21) joins בָּצַר as the completion of that verb's own lexeme; *wife* (v2), *a man's* (v6) and *or* (v17) are `added`, no morpheme in their clauses answering to them. v17 writes one of each.
- Heads on a verb rendered by more than one English word: where the English verb is light, the head is the complement carrying the lexeme — חָבַל → *take ... to pledge* (v6 twice, v17) heads on *pledge* and מוּת hophal → *shall be put to death* (v16 three times) on *death*; where the English verb itself carries the action and the supplied noun only spells out its domain, the head stays on the verb — פִּאֵר → *go over the boughs* (v20) heads on *go* and בָּצַר → *gatherest the grapes of* (v21) on *gatherest*. Rule 44's subtraction test is what separates them.
