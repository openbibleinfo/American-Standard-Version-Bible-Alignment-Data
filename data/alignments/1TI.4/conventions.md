# 1 Timothy 4 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- τῇ + διδασκαλίᾳ occurs twice with different English and is analysed two ways on purpose: at 4.13 the ASV supplies no determiner, so the article is unrepresented (rule 23) and the case preposition stays on the noun (rule 21); at 4.16 the ASV writes *to thy teaching*, and since σεαυτῷ has already been spent on *thyself* no source word is available for the possessive (rule 76), so the article takes *to thy* under rule 20's second arm. The three datives of 4.13 are treated alike under rule 24.
- Substantival πιστός is decided by whether a Greek article stands. With an article the unit splits — τοῖς / πιστοῖς → *by them* / *that believe* (4.3), τῶν / πιστῶν → *to them* / *that believe* (4.12) — the article taking the pronoun and case preposition under rules 21 and 32. Anarthrous, the adjective takes the whole supplied expression: πιστῶν → *of them that believe* (4.10). The head is *believe* in all three.
- Where the ASV renders a bare-case Greek object with a supplied English preposition because its verb frame requires one, the preposition rides with the governed nominal, not with the verb: Ταῦτα → *of these things* against the frame *put … in mind* (4.6), ταῦτα → *in these things* against *Be diligent* (4.15), αὐτοῖς → *in these things* against *Continue* (4.16). Each heads on the demonstrative or pronoun under rule 17's generic-noun clause.
- A supplied English expansion folds onto the source lexeme where it unpacks that lexeme's own meaning — ψευδολόγων → *men that speak lies* and κεκαυστηριασμένων → *branded as with a hot iron* (4.2) — and is `added` where the lexeme's English is already complete: *commanding* at 4.3 (κωλυόντων spent on *forbidding*, rule 103) and the supplied adverbial *until now* at 4.6 (παρηκολούθηκας spent on *thou hast followed*). Reading *until now* as part of the perfect's rendering remains defensible.
- προσέχω and ἐπέχω rendered by an English support-verb frame head on *heed* (4.1 *giving heed*, 4.13 *give heed*, 4.16 *Take heed*). 4.15's ἴσθι → *give thyself wholly* departs from that shape and heads on *give*, because no English word in the frame glosses εἰμί and the corpus spends only *be*, *is*, *are* on it; heading on *wholly* is recorded there as the competing reading.
