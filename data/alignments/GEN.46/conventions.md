# Genesis 46 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **GEN.46.27** — When the ASV renders a single Hebrew numeral with no waw as 'threescore and ten,' the surplus English 'and' is a separate added unit; the numeral keeps the supplied copula and 'threescore ... ten.'
- **GEN.46.32** — The repeated occupational construct אַנְשֵׁי מִקְנֶה is split compositionally as 'keepers' plus 'of cattle'; by contrast, רֹעֵי/רֹעֵה צֹאן shares one noncompositional unit with the single English word 'shepherd(s).'
