# 1 Samuel 27 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Piel חָיָה rendered 'saved … alive' (27.9, 27.11) is one discontinuous unit headed on 'alive', the complement carrying the lexeme under rule 44; 'saved' is the competing head and was not taken. The clause's לֹא takes the opening 'neither' and the waw before the second noun the closing 'nor' in both verses.
- The temporal infinitive-construct formula בּוֹאֲךָ (27.8, 'as thou goest to Shur') keeps the supplied 'as' with the bare infinitive under rule 33's adverbial reasoning rather than marking it added under rule 55; the suffix takes 'thou' under rule 18 and the directive he takes 'to' under rule 32.
