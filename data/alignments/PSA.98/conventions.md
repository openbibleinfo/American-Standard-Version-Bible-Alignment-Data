# Psalms 98 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Heads for the psalm's three multi-word imperatives, applied wherever each recurs in the chapter: רוע hiphil → Make a joyful noise heads on noise (rule 44 complement; joyful named as the alternative), זמר → sing praises heads on praises (rule 44, sing named as the alternative), and רנן → sing for joy heads on sing, for joy being read as an adjunct rather than a light-verb complement (joy named as the alternative, following the corpus majority at 1CH.16.33, JER.51.48, PSA.149.5).
