# Colossians 3 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- A supplied English verb resuming a Greek ellipsis is folded only where its own clause still has a token to carry it. 3:13's οὕτως καὶ ὑμεῖς expresses a subject and no predicate, so 'do' rides with ὑμεῖς under rule 56, which rule 62 has already freed from being a copula. 3:14 and 3:17 have no such token: every source token's English there is complete, and the licensing verb is spent — at 3:12 for 3:14's 'put on', at t004 for 3:17's 'do' — so the supplied verb is its own `added` unit under rules 5 and 103. An expressed object or adjunct is not a carrier: rule 62 wants a prepositional predicate, and ἐπὶ πᾶσιν τούτοις (3:14) and ἐν ὀνόματι (3:17) are adjuncts. The ASV's italics agree here but did not decide it (rule 11).
- Where one English *-soever* word covers πᾶν ὅ τι ἐάν (3:17), the relative cluster takes it whole as one `aligned` unit under rules 50 and 72 and the quantifier πᾶν is left `unrepresented`, as ὃ ἐάν takes it at 3:23. Rule 80's opposite result is scoped to πᾶς governing a substantival article, which rule 32 limits to a pronoun and nothing else; a relative pronoun with its generalizing particle has its own claim on both halves of the word, 'what-' answering to the relative and '-soever' to the particle. Giving 'whatsoever' to πᾶν and emptying the other three tokens remains defensible and is how BSB reads it.
