# Genesis 18 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **GEN.18.10** — When a waw precedes an explicit subject pronoun and ASV recasts that pronoun as who or which, the pronoun takes the relative and the waw is unrepresented if ASV writes no separate connective.
- **GEN.18.29** — In ויסף עוד לדבר rendered he spake yet again, the finite יסף takes the subject pronoun, עוד takes yet again, the infinitival ל is unrepresented, and דבר takes spake.
