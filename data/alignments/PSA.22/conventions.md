# Psalms 22 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **PSA.22.23** — מִמֶּנּוּ is tagged Sp1cp by MACULA in 22.23 and 22.24 but the ASV writes him for both; the suffix takes the pronoun written for it (rule 18) and the tag is not read as evidence of a first-person rendering.
- **PSA.22.16** — כָּאֲרִי → They pierced follows the versions against the pointed text and no variant unit is recorded in the packet, so it is aligned under rule 53 as printed: the noun אֲרִי carries They pierced, the כָּ and assimilated article are unrepresented, and no textual decision is taken inside the alignment file.
- **PSA.22.1** — A subject pronoun the ASV supplies for a later verbless clause is given to the nominal written earlier in the verse that it resumes (22.1 thou → vocative אֵלִי; 22.28 he → יהוה), not marked added, with the added alternative named in the note (rule 25).
