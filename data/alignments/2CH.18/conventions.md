# 2 Chronicles 18 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **2CH.18.6** — מֵאֹתוֹ after דָּרַשׁ, where MACULA tags the אֵת as the object marker (To) and not the preposition (R): מִן takes whatever preposition the ASV writes for the relation (of in 18.6, by in 18.7), the marker is its own unrepresented unit under rule 16, and the suffix takes its pronoun or relative (him, whom). Do not fuse the marker into מִן as a compound preposition; rule 23's מֵאֵת compound is the R-tagged form, as in 18.23 מֵאִתִּי → from.
