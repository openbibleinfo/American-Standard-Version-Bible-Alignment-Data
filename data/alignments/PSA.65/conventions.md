# Psalms 65 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Verbs of clothing and girding (לָבַשׁ 65.13, חָגַר 65.12, עָטַף 65.13) take the thing worn as a bare accusative; where the ASV recasts them passive (are clothed with, are girded with, are covered over with) the supplied with is the English verb's reach to its own object and joins the verb's unit under rule 44, the noun keeping only its own word. Rule 33's instrumental reading was considered and rejected in each verse because the noun is the verb's direct object. The phrasal about/over of girded about and covered over is kept with the verb as its fixed phrasal rendering, competing reading added.
