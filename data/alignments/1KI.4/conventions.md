# 1 Kings 4 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **1KI.4.1** — The article on הַמֶּלֶךְ in the title הַמֶּלֶךְ שְׁלֹמֹה is unrepresented: the ASV writes 'king Solomon' with no definite article, so the article morpheme gets its own source-only unit rather than being folded into the noun's unit.
