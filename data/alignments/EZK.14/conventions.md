# Ezekiel 14 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- מֵעַל and מֵאַחֲרֵי rendered by a single *from*: מִן takes the *from* and the second preposition is its own `unrepresented` unit (rules 2, 40), the suffix taking its pronoun. Not fused under rule 30, because the ablative *from* carries only the מִן's sense and drops the עַל's or אַחַר's, which is rule 35's test. Corpus: 43 units mark a preposition after מִן `unrepresented` and 155 align מִן to *from* before another preposition; but the corpus fuses מֵאֵת → *from* into one unit in ~20–44, so this is a chapter decision and not a general rule. Where the ASV writes a word for the second preposition (*from off*, *from pursuing*, *above*) that preposition takes it. Applies at 14.5, 14.6 (twice), 14.7, 14.11.
- בְּתוֹכָהּ → *in it* / *were in it*: בְּ + תָוֶךְ are one fused unit under rule 30's one-English-word condition, headed on תָוֶךְ, which carries the lexeme; the suffix takes *it* separately, and in the verbless clauses of 14.16, 14.18 and 14.20 the construction-supplied copula *were* joins the fused unit under rule 49, as לִפְנֵי → *was before* does. In 14.14 the written הָיוּ takes *were* and the fused unit holds only *in*. Contrast מִתּוֹךְ עַמִּי at 14.8 and 14.9, where the ASV writes *the midst* and each morpheme has an English word of its own, so rule 2 splits them.
- A phrase whose demonstrative or possessive supplies the ASV's single determiner leaves its article morphemes `unrepresented`: הָאֲנָשִׁים הָאֵלֶּה → *these men*, הָאִישׁ הַהוּא → *that man*, הָאָרֶץ הַהִיא → *that land*, and שְׁפָטַי הָרָעִים → *my … sore judgments*. The written demonstrative pronoun or suffix licenses the determiner (rule 41's limit) and the agreement article takes nothing (rules 29, 47). This is the whole of the Td×16 BSB disagreement. Applies at 14.3, 14.8, 14.9, 14.14, 14.16, 14.17, 14.18, 14.19, 14.21.
- Supplied subordinators and connectives are counted against the clause's conjunctions (rule 55). Where כִּי or a waw stands it takes the ASV's word — כִּי → *if* (14.9), *when* (14.13, 14.21, 14.23), *but* (14.18); waw → *though* (14.14, 14.18, 14.20), *but* (14.16), *so that* (14.17), *but that* (14.11), *that* recasting a clause as relative (14.7). Where the clause writes no conjunction at all the word is its own `added` unit: *though* at 14.16 (the verse opens with the bare numeral), *if* at 14.17 and 14.19 (the only conjunction is אוֹ, which already has its *Or*), and the restrictive *but* at 14.14 and 14.20. Those five plus the supplied *unto him* of 14.10 are the chapter's six `added` units.
- The oath correlatives → *neither … nor*: the opening word goes to the morpheme that is itself what it says — the oath אִם (14.16, 14.20) or the written לֹא (14.18) — and the closing *nor* goes to the waw where one stands (14.16, 14.18) and to the second אִם where none does (14.20). A second אִם standing beside a waw that has taken the *nor* is `unrepresented` under rule 22 (14.16). Read which morphemes are written before placing the pair, as rule 14 directs.
- אֲדֹנָי יְהוֹה → *the Lord Jehovah*: no article morpheme stands, so the supplied *the* belongs to the nominal expression אֲדֹנָי determines (rule 15), and the 1cs suffix is `unrepresented` because the ASV writes no *my* (rule 22). Eight verses, and the whole of the Sp1cs×9 BSB disagreement.
