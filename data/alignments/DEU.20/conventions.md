# Deuteronomy 20 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The exemption formula of vv. 5-8, מִי־הָאִישׁ אֲשֶׁר ... יֵלֵךְ וְיָשֹׁב לְבֵיתוֹ, is aligned the same way in all four verses. The interrogative מִי is the clause's predicate and takes the whole supplied frame *is there* - the construction's copula together with its presentative dummy under rule 20 - discontinuous around the subject noun, which keeps only *man*. The article on אִישׁ is `unrepresented`, since the ASV writes *What man* with no *the* (rule 29). And the dismissal apodosis puts the whole jussive frame *let him go* on the first verb, the coordinated יָשֹׁב taking the bare *return*, because the ASV writes the frame once (rules 22, 25).
