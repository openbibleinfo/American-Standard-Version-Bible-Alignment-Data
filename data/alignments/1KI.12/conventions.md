# 1 Kings 12 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **1KI.12.10** — יְלָדִים "young men" (vv. 8, 10, 14) heads on *young*, the word carrying the lexeme's youth, not the supplied generic *men*; the parallel זְקֵנִים "old men" heads on *old* the same way.
- **1KI.12.27** — The ASV's appositional *even* before a repeated preposition ("even unto Rehoboam" v. 27, "even unto Dan" v. 30) joins that preposition's unit rather than being marked added.
- **1KI.12.32** — In the date formula BA-HAMISHSHAH ASAR YOM (vv. 32, 33), where MACULA tags the pointed preposition as R with no article morpheme, the English the the ASV supplies belongs to the noun YOM, not to the compound numeral. Rule 30's fusion covers only the two numeral morphemes that fifteenth realizes; the supplied article definitizes the noun phrase, exactly as the article on the month takes the the of the eighth month while the ordinal takes only its own word.
