# 2 Chronicles 34 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The construct chain OSEH/OSE (HA)MELAKHAH is fused to the single English word 'workmen' under rule 30 at 34:10 (twice) and 34:17, headed on the participle, with the separately segmented article taking the written 'the' beside the fused unit and the chain's relational 'of' going to the fused rectum where the phrase is a genitive. Where the ASV instead writes a word for each morpheme, as 'all that did the work' at 34:13, the one-rendering condition is unmet and rule 2 splits it, the participle taking the supplied relative and the noun its own English.
- In the double-accusative clause HESHIV ET-HAMMELEKH DAVAR, rendered 'brought back word to the king' at 34:16 and 34:28, the object marker takes the supplied 'to': the unmarked accusative holds the English direct-object slot and the English verb reaches the marked object through the preposition, which is rule 16's first family and the route rule 44's first limit sends such a word by. The same reading gives the marker the 'for' of 'to make beams for the houses' at 34:11. Do not leave such a preposition with the noun under rule 33 or mark the marker unrepresented.
- The chapter writes three topicalizing or appositional expressions and only one of them answers to a morpheme: the 'even' of 34:24 is the marker's, because that marker stands on the appositive phrase the word introduces, while the 'even' of 34:33 and the 'As touching' of 34:26 are added, the clause's marker in the first standing on the divine name rather than on the infinitive phrase and no marker or preposition standing on the fronted nominal in the second. The count was run phrase by phrase, so a later reader finding two 'even's treated differently is seeing rule 16's limit applied, not a split.
