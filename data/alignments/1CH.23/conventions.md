# 1 Chronicles 23 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- `לְמָעְלָה` is one unit. The preposition, the adverb and the directive `he` are carried by a single English word, so rules 30 and 32 fuse all three, headed on `מַעַל` — *upward* at 23:27 and *very* at 23:17. Where the Hebrew writes no preposition, as `וָמָעְלָה` at 23:3, the adverb and the `he` fuse alone. Do not mark the `לְ` `unrepresented` beside the adverb: no English word in either verse is spent on it in particular, which is the subtraction test rule 40 runs.
- The chapter's multi-level `בַּיִת` chains are written with a *the* before every English noun, and rule 29's count allocates them. Where no article morpheme stands in the chain — `עֲבֹדַת בֵּית יְהוָה` → *the service of the house of Jehovah* (23:24, 23:28, 23:32) — each construct takes a supplied *the*, which rule 29 allows for the second. Where one does — `מַעֲשֵׂה עֲבֹדַת בֵּית הָאֱלֹהִים` → *the work of the service of the house of God* (23:28) — the article's *the* is the one standing before *house*, the word its own noun immediately depends on, and the two outer constructs take supplied *the*s.
- The prepositions BSB represents and this chapter leaves `unrepresented` are all recastings that spend no English on the relation, and none is an error to be repaired against BSB: `הָלַל`/`יָדָה` with `לַיהוָה` → *praise Jehovah* (23:5, 23:30) and `נָצַח` with `עַל` → *oversee the work* (23:4), where the object is promoted to a bare direct object; the `לְ` before a bare name in the genealogical list (23:6); the resultative `הָיָה לְ` (23:11) and the possessive `לְ` beside a written `הָיָה` (23:17, 23:22), which takes the *have* itself; the dative `לְ` whose object the ASV makes the English subject of *have need* (23:26); and the prepositions and article of the distributive `בַּבֹּקֶר בַּבֹּקֶר` → *every morning* (23:30), where only the first occurrence is rendered.
