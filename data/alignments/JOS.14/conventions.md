# Joshua 14 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- `וְעַתָּה` at a clause head (vv. 10 twice, 12): `עַתָּה` takes the English *now* wherever the ASV writes one, and the waw takes whatever other connective stands beside it — *And now* in v. 10, *Now therefore* in v. 12, where the waw's word stands second and its unit is reordered under rule 25. Do not divide the pair by the order of the English words.
- The idiom `מִלֵּא אַחֲרֵי` → *wholly followed* (vv. 8, 9, 14) is split, not fused: the verb takes the *wholly* and the preposition `אַחַר` takes the *followed*, since the ASV writes an English word for each and rule 2 splits where each morpheme owns one. The subject pronoun goes to the verb under rule 17 where no explicit pronoun stands (vv. 9, 14) and to `אָנֹכִי` where one does (v. 8).
- The age idiom `בֶּן` + numeral + `שָׁנָה` (vv. 7, 10) puts the *old* on the construct `בֵּן`, which is also the predicate carrying the verbless clause's supplied copula under rule 20 — *old was* in v. 7, *am old* in v. 10, discontinuous across the numeral phrase — while the numeral and the counted noun keep their own words and an explicit `אָנֹכִי` keeps only its *I*.
