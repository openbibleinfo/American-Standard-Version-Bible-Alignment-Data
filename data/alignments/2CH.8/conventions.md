# 2 Chronicles 8 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Construct chains whose arthrous rectum the ASV renders with no determiner of its own give the phrase's single *the* to the rectum's article (rule 29 fronting) throughout the chapter, and this includes אִישׁ הָאֱלֹהִים → *the man of God* (8.14), where *God* is read as an anarthrous rendering of הָאֱלֹהִים and the construct keeps bare *man*; the same reading covers *the store cities* (8.4, 8.6), *the chief officers* (8.10), *the feast of unleavened bread / weeks / tabernacles* (8.13) and *the sea shore* (8.17).
- The distributive repetitions דְבַר־יוֹם בְּיוֹם(וֹ) → *the duty of every day required* (8.13, 8.14) and לְשַׁעַר וָשָׁעַר → *at every gate* (8.14) are read under rule 22 with the first occurrence taking the noun and *every*, the second preposition, waw, noun and suffix unrepresented, and the supplied *required* its own added unit rather than folded into דְבַר; the *of* is the construct relation of דְבַר and goes to the first יוֹם as its rectum.
- מֶלֶךְ שְׁלֹמֹה with the article → *king Solomon* leaves the article unrepresented (8.10, 8.18); הֵמָּה takes *they* where the ASV writes it (8.9), is unrepresented as a resumptive pronoun the relative already carries (8.7), and takes the lexical expression *the places* where the ASV writes that for it (8.11, margin 'Hebrew they are' read and set aside).
