# Judges 5 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Where a colon gaps its verb and the ASV writes that verb's English twice, both occurrences are the one written verb's discontinuous unit under rules 25 and 34, headed on the first English occurrence even where that occurrence is the ASV's supplied italic: JDG.5.14 gives the italic *came down* of the Ephraim colon and the written *came down* of the Machir colon to the one יָרְדוּ, headed on the italic, and JDG.5.28 gives *cried* and the italic second *cried* to the one תְּיַבֵּב, headed on the plain first. The italic corroborates and decides nothing (rule 7), and being italic does not disqualify a token from being the display anchor.
