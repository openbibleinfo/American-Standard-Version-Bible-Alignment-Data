# Numbers 5 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Cognate accusative with מָעַל: where the ASV writes a single English word for the verb and its cognate noun — לִמְעֹל מַעַל → *so as to trespass* (5.6) — the verb takes that word and the noun is its own `unrepresented` unit under rule 22, because the word is a rendering the verb owns outright rather than one no morpheme owns a part of. Where the ASV writes a word for each — וּמָעֲלָה בוֹ מָעַל → *commit a trespass* (5.12), וַתִּמְעֹל מַעַל → *have committed a trespass* (5.27) — rule 2 splits them and the noun takes its own English with the indefinite article. The competing reading is rule 30's fusion on the one-English-word condition, which fuses a verb with its object noun in הֵשִׁיב פָּנִים → *deny*; BSB represents the noun at 5.6 and so reads it that way. The chapter took rule 22 throughout.
- Where a written אִישׁ and a coreferent pronominal suffix are rendered by one English possessive, the noun takes it and the suffix is `unrepresented` under rule 22: אִישׁ אֶת־קָדָשָׁיו → *every man's hallowed things* (5.10) and אִישׁ אִישׁ … אִשְׁתּוֹ → *any man's wife* (5.12) both put *man's* on the noun. The reverse division is defensible and is what BSB records; the chapter did not take it.
- A coordinator and a subordinator the ASV writes together at the left edge of a clause it has recast as subordinate are one rendering of the single conjunction morpheme standing in that clause, whatever that morpheme is: a waw at 5.27 (וְהִשְׁקָהּ → *And when he hath made her drink*) and a disjunctive אוֹ at 5.14 (אוֹ־עָבַר → *or if the spirit of jealousy come upon him*), each headed on the coordinator. Where a morpheme of its own stands for the subordinator it takes it instead and nothing is fused, as אֲשֶׁר does for the *when* of 5.30.
