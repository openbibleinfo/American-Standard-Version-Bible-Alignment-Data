# Acts 20 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The supplied English 'and' that the ASV must write once it has converted a Greek circumstantial participle into a finite clause is a rule 88 helper on the finite verb it introduces, and never `added`, even where that verb stands several tokens away behind a subordinate clause the ASV placed in front of it. ACT.20 carries nine such junctures — 20:1 ἐξῆλθεν, 20:3 γενομένης, 20:5 ἔμενον, 20:9 ἔπεσεν, 20:10 ἐπέπεσεν, 20:14 ἤλθομεν, 20:17 μετεκαλέσατο, 20:36 προσηύξατο, 20:37 κατεφίλουν — and not one is `added`. Rule 98 is the contrast and its test is the constituents joined, not the word standing immediately in front of the juncture: at 20:9 a participial phrase stands there and the seam is still clause-level, joining the converted 'there sat' to the expressed ἔπεσεν.
- Rule 73 leaves the source head of a collapsed light-verb pair open, and ACT.20 puts it on the noun in all three of its pairs: ἐγένετο γνώμης → 'determined' at 20:3, θεὶς τὰ γόνατα → 'kneeled down' at 20:36, and ἐγένετο κλαυθμός → 'wept' at 20:37. In each the single English verb realizes the noun's lexeme and not the general verb's, which is the test rule 73 states. The article and the genitive pronoun inside 20:36's phrase stay their own `unrepresented` units under rules 23 and 93, the collapsed unit absorbing neither.
