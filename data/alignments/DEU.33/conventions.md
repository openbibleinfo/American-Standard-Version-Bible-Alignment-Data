# Deuteronomy 33 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- In a verbless poetic line that fronts a prepositional phrase before an indefinite nominal, the construction-supplied copula goes to the preposition under rule 49, not to the nominal standing after it: `מִימִינוֹ אֵשׁ דָּת` -> *At his right hand was a fiery law* (33.2) and `וּמִתַּחַת זְרֹעֹת עוֹלָם` -> *And underneath are the everlasting arms* (33.27) both put the copula on the preposition, and 33.27's compound `מִתַּחַת` is fused and headed on the governing first morpheme under rules 23 and 30. Rule 49's closing adjunct carve-out was read as not reaching these, because the Hebrew writes no definite nominal predicate for the copula to join; the competing reading, which puts *was*/*are* on `דָּת` and `זְרוֹעַ`, is named in both verses' notes. The chapter treats an ordinary prepositional predicate the same way (33.3, 33.8, 33.29) and puts the copula on the nominal wherever the predicate is nominal (33.1, 33.17, 33.20, 33.22, 33.24, 33.25, 33.27, 33.29).
- In the repeated blessing formula of 33.13-16 the ablative `מִן` takes the ASV's written *for* (`מִמֶּגֶד` -> *for the precious things*), and `מֶגֶד` takes *the precious things* headed on *precious*, *things* being the supplied generic under rule 31 and the *the* supplied under rules 15 and 29. `רֹאשׁ הַרְרֵי־קֶדֶם` -> *the chief things of the ancient mountains* (33.15) follows the same division, headed on *chief*, and the `קֶדֶם` and `עוֹלָם` of 33.15 and 33.27 are rendered attributively under rule 28 rather than taking an *of*. The five `מֶגֶד` units and the one `רֹאשׁ` unit are one judgment, so a verse aligned alone from this stretch should not re-decide the head.
- `יַחַד` at 33.5 takes only the written *together*, and the *All* standing before *the tribes of Israel* is `added`: no `כֹּל` stands in the clause, the determiner slot before the noun already holds the supplied *the* of rule 29, and *All ... together* cannot be one rendering under rule 30, which wants one English word and contiguity. `יָחְדָּו` at 33.17 instead takes the whole *all of them* headed on *all*, because there the ASV writes no separate adverb for it and the alternative would leave the morpheme unrepresented beside three added tokens. The apparent split between the two verses is the ASV's and not the analysis's. Where a `כֹּל` is present it takes the word written for it, as at 33.3 (*All his saints*) and 33.12 (*all the day long*).
