# Psalms 30 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- זָמַר rendered 'sing praise' (30.4 imperative, 30.12 'may sing praise to') is one verb's rendering headed on 'sing', both words writing the lexeme on the terms rule 44 gives 'did work'; the supplied 'to' reaching the suffix object in 30.12 joins the verb. Heading on 'praise' as a light-verb complement is the competing reading and is the corpus majority (about 27 units to 7), so a later chapter should choose and name the other reading rather than treat either as settled.
