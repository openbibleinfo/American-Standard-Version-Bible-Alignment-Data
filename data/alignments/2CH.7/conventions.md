# 2 Chronicles 7 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **2CH.7.3** — The doxology כִּי לְעוֹלָם חַסְדּוֹ (7.3, 7.6) divides as כִּי → for, לְ → 'endureth for' (copula on the prepositional predicate, rules 20/49, headed on for), עוֹלָם → ever, חֶסֶד → lovingkindness, suffix → his. In 7.3 the supplied 'he' of 'For he is good' resumes the יהוה written earlier in the verse and joins that name's unit under rule 25, headed on Jehovah; 'saying' there is a supplied lexical verb with no לֵאמֹר and is added. 7.6 writes no such pronoun.
