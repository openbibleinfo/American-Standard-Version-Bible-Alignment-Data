# Ephesians 1 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in `AGENTS.md` once the chapter is finished.

- Reconstructed after the fact: this chapter was adjudicated before the conventions log existed, so these entries were written from the accepted files rather than as the decisions were taken. Later chapters must record them live.
- **EPH.1.1** — An article realized in English takes the case preposition with it: dative τοῖς → 'to the', headed on 'the'. An anarthrous noun that English makes definite keeps 'the' inside its own unit.
- **EPH.1.1** — An attributive article before a participle supplies the English relative: τοῖς οὖσιν → 'that' + 'are', split across the two source tokens.
- **EPH.1.3** — When a possessive displaces the article ('of our Lord'), the article is unrepresented, the possessive belongs to the genitive pronoun, and 'of' stays with the noun.
- **EPH.1.3** — A substantival adjective expanded by a generic English noun heads on the adjective: ἐπουρανίοις → 'heavenly places', head 'heavenly'.
- **EPH.1.5** — A single Greek word expanded into an English phrase heads on the word that realizes the lexeme, not the expansion: υἱοθεσίαν → 'adoption as sons', head 'adoption'.
- **EPH.1.7** — Where no Greek possessor exists, an article ASV renders as 'our' is aligned to that word rather than marked unrepresented; ASV does not italicize it.
- **EPH.1.10** — Articular τὰ πάντα splits: the adjective heads 'all' and the substantivizing article supplies the generic noun 'things'. Anarthrous πάντα stays one unit headed on 'all' (verse 22), and where ASV gives no generic noun the article is unrepresented (verse 23).
- **EPH.1.10** — A substantival article rendered as a nominal expression heads on the nominal word, not the relativizer: 'the things', 'of him who', 'we who', 'that which'.
- **EPH.1.11** — A support-verb or light-verb rendering heads on the meaning-bearing complement: ἐκληρώθημεν → 'were made a heritage' heads on 'heritage'; ὑπέταξεν → 'put in subjection' heads on 'subjection'.
- **EPH.1.12** — εἰς τὸ + infinitive: the purposive preposition carries the English connector ('to the end that', or a bare 'that' in verse 18) and the nominalizing article is unrepresented. Revised during review after the BSB manual alignment read both verses this way.
- **EPH.1.19** — Where ASV's word order contradicts the usual glosses, follow ASV's own consistent rendering: τοῦ κράτους τῆς ἰσχύος αὐτοῦ → 'the strength of his might', positionally, as ASV also renders it at Eph. 6:10.
