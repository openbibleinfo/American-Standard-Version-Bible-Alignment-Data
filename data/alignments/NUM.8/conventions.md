# Numbers 8 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- mul penei rendered by a single English front (8.2, 8.3) is one fused unit under rule 30, headed on mul, and penei is not marked unrepresented: rule 22 reaches a repetition of the same element, not the parts of an idiom. What is spent on the pair is one word only, since the in belongs to el and the of to the nomen rectum under rule 19. Heading on penei, as the chapter's six lifnei units do, was the competing reading.
- la'avod 'eth is aligned two ways in this chapter and the difference is in the Hebrew, not in the analysis. Where the Hebrew writes 'avodah (8.11, 8.19, 8.22) the infinitive takes do, the noun takes the service, and the object marker is unrepresented; at 8.15 no 'avodah is written, so do the service is the light verb's whole rendering of 'avad (rule 44) and the object marker takes the of through which it reaches 'ohel mo'ed (rule 16). 8.26's sheret 'eth 'echaw -> minister with their brethren is the same reading of the marker. Do not level these into one treatment.
- li rendered by a single mine (8.14 shall be mine, 8.17 are mine) is one fused unit of preposition and suffix under rule 30, on the reasoning rule 22 gives for bah -> therein: the one English word carries both the relation and the pronoun. The lamed is not marked unrepresented under rule 40, which governs a recasting that leaves the preposition nothing to say, and here nothing is left over.
