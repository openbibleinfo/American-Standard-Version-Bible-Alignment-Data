# Isaiah 10 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **ISA.10.12** — The identical presentative rendering 'it shall come to pass that' heads on 'pass': 'come' is the light verb and 'pass' is the lexeme-bearing complement under the subtraction test. Outside-chapter precedent is split (21 'come', 18 'pass'), so the contract's head rule decides the chapter-wide treatment.
