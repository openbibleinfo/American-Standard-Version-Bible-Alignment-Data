# 2 Samuel 18 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- שָׁלוֹם as a one-word question or exclamation: where no interrogative particle stands (v29 שָׁלוֹם לַנַּעַר → Is it well with the young man) the whole fixed frame Is it well stays on the noun, headed on well, under rule 20's fixed-rendering it; where הֲ stands (v32 הֲשָׁלוֹם → Is it well) the particle takes the fronted Is and the noun keeps it well; and where the ASV writes a lexical subject (v28 שָׁלוֹם → All is well) the All is added as a supplied subject and is well joins the noun, since the ASV writes peace and well for the bare word without an All. The three are one reading of rule 20, and the difference is what stands in the Hebrew.
- An emphatic independent pronoun doubling a verb's own subject is the unrendered occurrence where the ASV writes one pronoun: v22 אָרֻצָה־נָּא גַם־אָנִי → let me … also run keeps me with the cohortative and marks אָנִי unrepresented; v33 מוּתִי אֲנִי → I had died gives I to the infinitive's subject suffix (rule 18) and marks אֲנִי unrepresented. Where the ASV writes a separate word for the pronoun (v2 אֲנִי → myself, v13 אַתָּה → thou thyself) the pronoun takes it.
- לְבַד with a 3ms suffix → alone: the preposition and construct noun fuse under rule 30 and the suffix is its own unit, unrepresented where the ASV writes no pronoun (v24, v26 a man running alone) and taking the subject pronoun where it does (v25 אִם־לְבַדּוֹ → If he be alone, with the construction copula joining the fused unit).
- נַעַר → young man is headed on man throughout (vv5, 12, 15, 29, 32): the rule 31 subtraction ties, and man is the class noun the ASV writes bare for אִישׁ in the same verses, so young is the ASV's specifier; young remains the competing head.
- The appositive לַנַּעַר לְאַבְשָׁלוֹם: the second לְ takes with where the ASV repeats the preposition (v5 with the young man, even with Absalom, the even added under rule 16) and is unrepresented where the ASV writes the appositive bare (v29, v32 with the young man Absalom).
