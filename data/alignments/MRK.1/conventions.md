# Mark 1 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Where a compound verb's preverb is echoed by an expressed cognate preposition, the whole English prepositional expression belongs to the preposition, headed on the prepositional word, and the verb keeps only its verb word: `ἔξελθε ἐξ αὐτοῦ` → *come* and *out of* (1:25), `ἐξῆλθεν ἐξ αὐτοῦ` → *came* and *out of* (1:26), `ἐξελθόντες ἐκ` → *when they were come* and *out of* (1:29), `εἰσελθὼν εἰς` → *entered* and *into* (1:21, 1:45), `ἀπῆλθεν ἀπ᾽ αὐτοῦ` → *departed* and *from* (1:42). Where the preverb is not part of the following preposition's own gloss the particle stays inside the verb's unit under rule 106, which is why 1:10 gives *up* to ἀναβαίνων and *out of* to ἐκ in one clause, and 1:12 gives *forth* to ἐκβάλλει and *into* to εἰς. 1:5, 1:28, 1:34, 1:35, 1:38, 1:39, 1:43 and 1:45 keep the particle on the verb because no preposition stands there at all.
- A periphrastic ἦν + participle keeps the finite copula's own English on ἦν and the lexical verb word on the participle: *was* at 1:6 (*was clothed*) and 1:33 (*was gathered together*), and the supplied subject *he* at 1:22, where the ASV collapses the periphrasis into the simple past *he taught*. The differing surface is the ASV's, not two analyses (rule 24). Do not read the collapse as leaving ἦν `unrepresented`, and do not give the supplied subject to the participle.
- Where a denominative Greek verb is rendered by a generic English verb plus the noun of its own root, the head is that noun: `σπλαγχνισθείς` → *being moved with compassion* on *compassion* (1:41), `πυρέσσουσα` → *sick of a fever* on *fever* (1:30), `δαιμονιζομένους` → *that were possessed with demons* on *demons* (1:32). Where the root is itself verbal the head is the verb word, as `ἀμφιβάλλοντας` → *casting a net* on *casting* (1:16), `ἐμβριμησάμενος` → *he strictly charged* on *charged* (1:43) and `ἐπισυνηγμένη` → *gathered together* on *gathered* (1:33) do. `ἤγγικεν` → *is at hand* on *hand* (1:15) is not an exception but rule 71's recorded corpus reading of the ἐγγ- family.
- Two of the chapter's three `added` units rest on textual decisions rather than on rule 86, and a later reader should not read them as ordinary supplied English. *the Son of God* at 1:1 is rule 97's fallback case, SBLGNT.MRK.1.1.v001 being marked FALLBACK EXCLUDED BY EVIDENCE with r01 and r02 carrying υἱοῦ θεοῦ. *kneeling down* at 1:40 is the selected-reading case, SBLGNT.MRK.1.40.v057 having selected r02, which carries no participle of kneeling though the ASV translates one; the packet marks such a unit in no way at all. The ASV's own margin corroborates both but licenses neither by itself. Only 1:9's *that* is rule 86's case: a finite indicative the ASV subordinates with no conjunction anywhere in the verse.
