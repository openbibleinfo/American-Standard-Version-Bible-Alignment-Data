# 1 Chronicles 4 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **1CH.4.3** — When ASV expands a bare genealogical father expression with the lexical nominal 'the sons of' and no source son noun is present, mark that supplied nominal added; keep the verbless copula and father rendering with the Hebrew father noun.
