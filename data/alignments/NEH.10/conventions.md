# Nehemiah 10 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Offering compounds head on the word carrying the lexeme rather than 'offering(s)'/'things': מנחה → meal, עלה → burnt, חטאת → sin, תרומה → heave, קדשים → holy (10.33, 10.37, 10.39). Where the ASV's modifier is a specifier on a noun that already names the thing, the head stays on the noun: חדשים → new moons on 'moons', מועדים → set feasts on 'feasts', תירוש → new wine on 'wine' (10.33, 10.37, 10.39). The competing head is named in each note.
- 'first fruits' is headed by lemma: בכורים heads on 'fruits' (10.35), because the ASV writes 'fruit' alone for פרי in the same verse and rule 31's tie-break makes 'fruits' the class noun; ראשית heads on 'first' (10.37), because dropping 'first' loses that lexeme and 'fruits' is the supplied generic. The two verses are not in conflict.
- The subject of the participial clause opening 10.29 (מחזיקים 'they clave') is the list of 10.28; under rule 10 nothing carries across the verse, so the ASV's 'they' is added in 10.29, while in 10.37 the same 'they' is the written הם. The previously recorded finite-participle allocations at 10.31 s005 and 10.37 s036 remain outside this scoped qualification. At 10.34 and 10.36, both independent deferred-investigation judgments give the written *it* to the substantival article in כַּכָּתוּב, while the participle retains *is written* under rules 20 and 41. Finite English recasting does not remove that expressed nominal reference. This qualification changes only those two scoped occurrences; the original convention is archived in the deferred-investigation audit.
