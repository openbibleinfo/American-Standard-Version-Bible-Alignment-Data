# 1 Thessalonians 2 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Versification. The projection and the ASV divide this chapter differently at two places, and both are handled under rule 37 with each side recorded. The ASV renders δυνάμενοι ἐν βάρει εἶναι ὡς Χριστοῦ ἀπόστολοι (1TH.2.7 s001-s007) at the end of its verse 6, so those seven tokens are unrepresented in 2:7 and the English 'when we might have claimed authority as apostles of Christ' is added in 2:6. The ASV renders παρακαλοῦντες ὑμᾶς καὶ παραμυθούμενοι καὶ μαρτυρόμενοι (1TH.2.12 s001-s006) at the end of its verse 11, so those six tokens are unrepresented in 2:12 and 'exhorting you and encouraging you and testifying' is added in 2:11. Neither verse reaches across, and a reference comparison reports all thirteen source tokens as disagreements without that being a correction.
- καθώς and an answering καί. Where the ASV writes a bare 'even as' with no correlative 'so', the 'even' is the καί's and the comparative keeps a bare 'as' (2:14, καθὼς καὶ αὐτοί -> 'even as they did'). Where no καί stands, nothing else can claim the supplied 'even' and the comparative keeps 'even as' whole (2:4). The chapter's other comparatives are bare 'as' (2:2, 2:5, 2:13, and καθάπερ at 2:11).
- Possessive expressions. A reflexive ἑαυτοῦ or the adjective ἴδιος rendered by a two-word English possessive takes that whole expression as one unit headed on 'own', never on the pronominal word: 'her own' (2:7), 'our own' (2:8), 'his own' (2:11, 2:12), 'your own' (2:14). The pronominal is the possessor form English syntax requires, and 'own' is what realizes the reflexive or the ἴδιος sense; the governing article is displaced under rules 20 and 47 in every one of these five units.
