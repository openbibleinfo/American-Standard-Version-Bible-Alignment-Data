# Joshua 24 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Impersonal English *it*. Where it stands inside the fixed English rendering of a Hebrew predicate word or interjection that the ASV never writes without it, it joins that morpheme's unit: רַע → *it seem evil* at 24.15, following rule 40's decided *it displease*, and חָלִילָה → *Far be it* at 24.16, headed on *Far*. Where every morpheme of the clause already holds its own English and the word merely opens a cleft the ASV has built, it is `added`: *he it is that brought us up* at 24.17, where הוּא keeps its own *he*, the article keeps the relative *that* under rule 41 and the participle keeps the copula. A referential supplied subject that no morpheme in the verse denotes is `added` on the ordinary terms, as *We* is at 24.22, both occurrences of אַתֶּם having already been spent.
- *out of* after a verb of bringing. Where מִן stands in the clause it takes the whole *out of*, headed on *out* (24.6, 24.10, 24.17, 24.32), and the hiphil keeps only its own verb together with any second particle, as *brought ... up* does at 24.17 and 24.32. Where no preposition stands, the particle is the verb's own under rule 44, as *I brought you out* is at 24.5. Read whether the preposition is present before placing the word: what differs between 24.5 and 24.6 is the morpheme, not the English.
