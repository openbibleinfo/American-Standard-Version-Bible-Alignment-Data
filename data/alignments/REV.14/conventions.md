# Revelation 14 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **REV.14.1** — Inside a compound numeral ('a hundred and forty and four thousand'), the juncture 'and' has no Greek counterpart but is obligatory English idiom: attach each to the numeral token it introduces rather than marking it added.
