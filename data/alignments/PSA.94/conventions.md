# Psalms 94 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **PSA.94.9** — Double interrogative particles in one colon (vv. 9–10): where הֲ stands on the participial subject and a second הֲ stands on לֹא before the finite verb, the ASV's single fronted 'shall' is given to the הֲ on לֹא and the הֲ on the participle is unrepresented. In v9 the second colon additionally opens with אִם; the auxiliary still goes to that colon's own הֲ, parallel to the first colon, and אִם is unrepresented (competing reading: 'shall' to אִם, noted on the unit).
- **PSA.94.8** — Where a single supplied 'ye' stands immediately after an imperative and immediately before a vocative (Consider, ye brutish), the ASV's comma and the parallel colon's 'ye fools' resolve rule 36's adjacency tie in favour of the vocative; the verb keeps only its own word.
