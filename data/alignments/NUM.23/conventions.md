# Numbers 23 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- מִשָּׁם is read by what stands with it. At 23.13 the ASV writes from whence for אֲשֶׁר ... מִשָּׁם, and the relative, the preposition and the resumptive adverb are one unit headed on the relative under rule 22; the bare מִשָּׁם of the same verse and of 23.27 is from thence and splits, from on מִן and thence on שָׁם. The competing reading — from on מִן with אֲשֶׁר and שָׁם fused as whence — is named in the verse note and was not taken.
- גַּם ... לֹא rendered by a single Neither or nor (23.25, twice) is one fused unit headed on the negative, discontinuous around the intervening infinitive absolute, on rule 35's reasoning for waw plus a negative: the one English word carries both the additive and the negation. Do not give the word to גַּם and mark לֹא unrepresented.
- The chapter's rule 30 fusions and their heads, each a lexical decision a later verse would otherwise re-argue: בָזֶה → here headed on the demonstrative (23.1, 23.29); כַּאֲשֶׁר → as headed on כְּ by rule 23's governing-first-morpheme test (23.2, 23.30); לְבָדָד → alone headed on the noun (23.9); כָּעֵת → Now headed on the noun (23.23); עַל־פְּנֵי → upon headed on the construct noun (23.28); נַפְשִׁי → me headed on the suffix (23.10); and יִישַׁר בְּעֵינֵי → please headed on the verb (23.27), where the rectum הָאֱלֹהִים keeps its own God and its article is unrepresented.
