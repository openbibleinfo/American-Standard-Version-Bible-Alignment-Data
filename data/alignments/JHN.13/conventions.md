# John 13 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The lexeme of ἀνάκειμαι carries the whole locative phrase the ASV renders it by: 13:23 ἀνακείμενος → 'at the table reclining' and 13:28 τῶν ἀνακειμένων → 'at the table', with no separate source token for 'table'. Where the ASV supplies no partitive pronoun, the substantival article governing that participle is 'unrepresented' (13:28), which is the exception to rule 32's article → pronoun in this chapter.
