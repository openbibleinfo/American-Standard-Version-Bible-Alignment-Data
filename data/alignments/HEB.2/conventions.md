# Hebrews 2 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- brachy ti -> 'a little' is split at 2:7 and 2:9, the indefinite ti taking the determiner 'a' and the adjective brachy taking 'little'. Rule 51's anarthrous clause is not read as making 'a' a rule 7 helper on the adjective: an expressed indefinite pronoun is a source word able to take the English indefinite, exactly as an article is under rule 51's first clause, so the determiner is ti's own to take.
- pas is decided by the presence of the Greek article and never by the English: anarthrous panta -> 'all things' is one unit headed on 'all' under rule 17 (2:8 s001, 2:17 s004), and articular ta panta gives 'things' to the article and 'all' to the quantifier under rules 22 and 102 (2:8 s012/s013 and s023/s024, 2:10 s006/s007 and s011/s012). 2:8 writes both.
- The nominalizing article of an articular infinitive is unrepresented where the ASV writes it no determiner (en to hypotaxai at 2:8, eis to hilaskesthai at 2:17) and aligned where it writes one. At 2:15 that determiner is the supplied possessive 'their' of 'all their lifetime', taken as a determiner of the article's own under rule 61's closing clause because rule 76 leaves no source word free for it: toutous is spent on 'them' and hosoi on 'all ... who'. In the same phrase dia is its own unrepresented unit, the ASV spreading dia pantos tou zen over three English words that each answer to another token, so rule 94 forbids a compound unit and no English word is left for the preposition.
- Support-verb and resultative renderings head on the complement throughout the chapter -- 'didst put in subjection' (2:8), 'give heed' (2:1), 'doth he give help' and 'giveth help' (2:16), 'bring to nought' (2:14), 'make propitiation' (2:17), 'madest lower' (2:7) and 'made lower' (2:9), 'make perfect' (2:10), 'put my trust' (2:13), 'are sharers' (2:14). The one head on the verb word, hymneso -> 'will I sing thy praise' at 2:12, is not an inconsistency: rule 91's test finds the lexeme's own sense in 'sing', where 'give', 'put' and 'make' carry none of theirs.
