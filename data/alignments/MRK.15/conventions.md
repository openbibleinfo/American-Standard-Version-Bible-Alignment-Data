# Mark 15 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- In the ASV's 'accuse X of Y' frame for κατηγορέω, the English 'of' belongs to the token realizing the thing accused of, never to the genitive of the person and never to the verb: MRK.15.3 gives 'of many things' to πολλά and a bare 'him' to αὐτοῦ, and MRK.15.4 gives the clause-final 'of' to πόσα discontinuously under rule 15 and a bare 'thee' to σου. Stranding the preposition at the end of the English clause does not move it, rules 53 and 63 having already refused to read a helper off English word order.
- A supplied English preposition that the ASV's verb idiom requires, where no Greek preposition is expressed and no Greek case answers to it, is a rule 7 helper on the verb that licenses it: 'clothe … with' at 15.17 (double accusative, no preposition), 'asked of him' at 15.6, 'calling unto him' at 15.44, 'part … among them' at 15.24. Where the English preposition realizes an expressed Greek case it rides with its own noun or pronoun instead, under rules 21 and 23's determinerless clause: 'with a reed' at 15.19 and 'with a … voice' at 15.34 (instrumental dative), 'of vinegar' at 15.36, 'of God' at 15.39. A supplied lexical verb is not such a helper: it is 'added' under rule 5 together with its own complement, as 'to go with them' is at 15.21, where ἀγγαρεύω's complement stands nowhere in the projection, and as the repeated 'to do' is at 15.8 under rule 103.
- Rule 34's pleonastic reading of ἀπὸ μακρόθεν is scoped to the ASV's 'afar off', where no English word answers to the preposition separately. Where the ASV instead writes 'from afar', as at 15.40, the 'from' answers to ἀπό and rule 2 splits the pair, the ASV's phrasing deciding as it does for an improper preposition under rule 42's closing clause. The competing reading is named in that unit's note.
