# Psalms 129 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- PSA.129 reads the ASV's *from my youth up* (129.1, 129.2) as one discontinuous rendering of the ablative `מִן`: both *from* and *up* belong to that preposition's unit under rules 24 and 25, headed on *from*, which carries the relation. The competing reading, marking *up* `added` under rule 30, is named in each unit's note. This does not reach an English particle that completes a verb's own lexeme: the *up* of `שָׁלַף` → *groweth up* (129.6) and the *asunder* of `קִצֵּץ` → *cut asunder* (129.4) stay with their verbs under rule 44, headed on the verb. Read what the particle closes — a preposition's relational frame, or a verb the bare English word leaves short — before placing it.
