# Jeremiah 6 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- An enclitic זֶה bound by maqqef to an interrogative is decided by rule 30's one-English-rendering condition, not by its own class. Where the ASV writes a single word for the pair it shares the interrogative's unit, headed on the interrogative: אֵי־זֶה → 'where is' (JER.6.16, the copula joining under rule 20). Where the ASV spends a word on each of the other morphemes the condition is defeated and the זֶה is unrepresented: לָמָּה־זֶּה → 'To what purpose' gives 'To' to לְ, 'what' to מָה, and 'purpose' to מָה as rule 31's supplied completion (JER.6.20).
- Where a demonstrative phrase carries an article on the noun and another on the demonstrative and the ASV writes one determiner for the whole — הָעָם הַזֶּה → 'this people' (JER.6.19, JER.6.21) — the demonstrative takes that determiner because it is the morpheme that licenses it, and both articles are unrepresented (rules 22, 41, 47). Rule 41's 'determiner written in the article's place' does not reach it, since another morpheme licenses the word.
- The ASV's 'every one' heads on different words in this chapter and the difference is rule 31's subtraction, not an inconsistency: אִישׁ → 'every one' heads on 'one' (JER.6.3), because אִישׁ carries the substantive and 'every' is supplied; כֹּל → 'every one' heads on 'every' (JER.6.13), because כֹּל carries the quantifier and 'one' is supplied. In the כֹּל case the supplied 'one' joins that unit and the word's own suffix is left unrepresented, since no English remains for it.
