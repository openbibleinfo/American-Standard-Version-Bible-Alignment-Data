# Matthew 21 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- A Greek adverb the ASV expands into a complex English prepositional frame keeps that frame whole as one aligned unit and heads on the frame's lexical noun: Πρωΐ → 'in the morning' heads on 'morning' at 21.18 and ὡσαύτως → 'in like manner' heads on 'manner' at 21.36. No Greek preposition or article stands for the frame's other words to answer to, so nothing is split off and nothing is `added`. This is rule 104's route rather than rule 91's, the corpus spending 'likewise' on ὡσαύτως (as 21.30 writes it) and so returning no word inside the frame; the analogy taken is rules 71 and 84, which head a supplied frame on its noun. Heading 21.36 on 'like' remains defensible and is recorded in that verse's note.
