# Acts 3 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Versification: the ASV translates ὅπως ἂν ἔλθωσιν καιροὶ ἀναψύξεως ἀπὸ προσώπου τοῦ κυρίου at the end of 3:19, not in 3:20. Under rule 37 that English is one `added` unit in 3:19 headed on *come*, and the nine source tokens ὅπως … κυρίου are each their own `unrepresented` unit in 3:20; both sides carry a note naming the other verse, and a reference comparison reports all nine as disagreements. The *that* of 3:20's *that he may send* is not `added`: ὅπως governs both subjunctives, so under rule 103's repeated-function-word clause the repetition is a rule 7 helper on ἀποστείλῃ even though the conjunction's own English stands in the previous verse.
- ἀτενίζω rendered by the frame *fasten one's eyes* (3:4 *fastening his eyes*, 3:12 *fasten ye your eyes*) is headed on the verb word, with the supplied *his eyes* / *your eyes* folded in as rule 7 helpers. Heading on *eyes* as rule 17's meaning-bearing complement remains defensible; the chapter writes one analysis in both verses.
- The article before θεός is aligned only where the ASV writes a determiner in the phrase — 3:13 *The God*, 3:22 *the Lord God* — and is its own `unrepresented` unit under rule 23 wherever the ASV writes a bare *God* (3:8, 3:9, 3:15, 3:18, 3:21, 3:25, 3:26). In 3:22's κύριος ὁ θεός the phrase's one *the* is given to the expressed article rather than to the anarthrous κύριος, an article being present in the phrase for the determiner to belong to (rules 51, 57 and 92); giving it to κύριος as a rule 51 helper and emptying the article is the competing reading and is named in that unit's note.
- Where the ASV renders ἐκ by the two-word *from among* (3:22, 3:23), both words ride whole with the preposition and *among* is not split off onto the following article or noun. The article of that phrase is decided independently — displaced by ὑμῶν at 3:22, aligned to *the* at 3:23.
