# 1 Chronicles 10 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **1CH.10.4** — נֹשֵׂא כֵלִים → armorbearer (10.4 twice, 10.5): the construct chain fuses into the one English word under rule 30; both lexemes are written in it, so the head is the construct נֹשֵׂא under rule 23; the 3ms suffix takes 'his' separately.
- **1CH.10.1** — הַר גִּלְבֹּעַ → mount Gilboa (10.1, 10.8): attributive rendering under rule 28; הַר takes 'mount', the name takes 'Gilboa', and no 'of' is placed.
- **1CH.10.12** — 1CH.10.11 is a temporal protasis ('And when all Jabesh-gilead heard…') whose apodosis is the whole of 1CH.10.12. The waw opening 10.11 takes 'And when' as one rendering (rule 55, head 'And'); the waw opening 10.12 is the apodosis waw for which the ASV writes no connective and is unrepresented (rule 14). Neither verse is short of anything.
- **1CH.10.3** — הַמּוֹרִים בַּקָּשֶׁת → the archers: the participle, the preposition and קֶשֶׁת share one unit headed on the participle (rules 30, 40), because 'archers' alone carries the shooting, the instrument relation and the bow. The assimilated article on the bow stays outside the fusion as its own unrepresented unit, since 'archers' says nothing of the bow's determination (as the article of דִּבְרֵי הַיָּמִים → chronicles does). The bare הַיּוֹרִים later in the verse is article + participle as usual.
- **1CH.10.1** — Phrasal particles in this chapter stay with the verb under rule 44's clause-level reading: 'fell down' (10.1, falling is downward) and 'took away' (10.12, the removal is what the clause narrates, נָשָׂא being carry/bear rather than mere taking). The competing reading that marks the particle added is named in each unit's note; 'thrust through' (10.4) is not in question, since the particle completes the lexeme of דָּקַר.
