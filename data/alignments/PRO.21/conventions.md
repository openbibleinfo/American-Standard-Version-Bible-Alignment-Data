# Proverbs 21 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- A preposition the ASV supplies before a bare written nominal joins that nominal's unit under rule 33 and is never `added`, whatever preposition it is: the comparative *as* of 21.1 פַּלְגֵי מַיִם → *as the watercourses*, the agent *by* of 21.6 מְבַקְשֵׁי מָוֶת → *by them that seek death*, and the *with* and *in* of 21.9 and 21.19 before the members of a מִן comparison. The boundary the chapter drew the other way twice is rule 16's: an *as for* before a topicalized phrase carrying no object marker and no preposition is `added` (21.8, 21.29), a fronting being no case-function. Read whether a Hebrew nominal stands under the supplied word, not which English preposition it is.
- Where a construct chain is rendered attributively under rule 28, a determiner the ASV supplies joins the morpheme its English head noun renders, whichever side of the chain that is, and the unit is discontinuous around the attributive. The construct carries it in אִישׁ מַחְסוֹר → *a poor man* (21.17), אֵשֶׁת מִדְיָנִים → *a contentious woman* (21.9, 21.19), עֵד־כְּזָבִים → *A false witness* (21.28) and לְשׁוֹן שָׁקֶר → *a lying tongue* (21.6); the rectum carries it in רוּם עֵינַיִם → *A high look* (21.4), where the ASV's head noun is *look* and the construct supplies the attributive. This is rule 15's *the noun of the expression, not its leftmost word* read for a chain rather than for a numeral.
