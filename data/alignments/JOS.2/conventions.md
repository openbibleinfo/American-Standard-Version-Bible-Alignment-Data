# Joshua 2 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **JOS.2.6** — The ASV inverts the chain pishtei ha-ets as 'the stalks of flax', making the construct noun the English dependent. The chain's 'of' is placed on the noun it stands before, so pishtei takes 'of flax' and ets keeps 'stalks'; rule 19's default direction does not survive an inversion, exactly as rule 28 directs for an attributive rendering. The article on ha-ets takes the single written 'the', which stands before 'stalks', the word its own noun is rendered by (rule 29). The competing reading, imposing rule 19's direction on the inverted English, is named in the verse's note.
- **JOS.2.5** — The compound temporal expression 'about the time of' is given whole to the le before lisgor, headed on 'time' under rule 24, rather than marking 'about the time' added under rule 33 and leaving the preposition only the 'of'. The infinitive keeps 'the shutting' as a gerund with its supplied determiner (rules 15, 52), and the infinitive's subject noun shaar takes the second 'of' as a supplied nominal relation (rule 33).
- **JOS.2.2** — The ASV's hyphenated 'to-night' arrives as two target tokens. It is read as one rendering of ha + laylah under rule 30's one-rendering condition, headed on the noun, rather than giving the 'to' to the article separately: no morpheme owns a part of the hyphenated word. This is a tokenization artifact of one English word, not a construction that generalises.
- **JOS.2.1** — The 'whose' of 'whose name was Rahab' is given to the pronominal suffix of shemah under rule 18's relative-pronoun sentence, and the waw is left unrepresented because the ASV writes no separate connective for the recast clause. Rule 17's recasting clause, which would put the relative on the waw, reaches only a clause with no pronominal morpheme in it to take the word first. This is the source of the BSB disagreement recorded at WLC.JOS.2.1.030.
