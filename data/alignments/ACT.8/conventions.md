# Acts 8 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- εὐαγγελίζομαι: where the ASV renders the lexeme by a single English word, that word takes the whole unit and the head (8:4 preaching, 8:35 preached); where it writes a light verb plus a noun, the head is the noun that glosses the lexeme under rule 91 — tidings at 8:12 (preaching good tidings), gospel at 8:25 and 8:40 (preached the gospel). Heading such a unit on preaching or preached is the competing reading and was not taken.
- Periphrastic finite verb plus participle splits into two units: the finite verb keeps the English auxiliary and any supplied subject pronoun, the participle keeps the lexical verb — 8:1 ἦν → was / συνευδοκῶν → consenting, 8:16 ἦν → it was / ἐπιπεπτωκός → fallen and ὑπῆρχον → they had been / βεβαπτισμένοι → baptized, 8:28 ἦν → he was / ὑποστρέφων and καθήμενος. Where the ASV collapses the periphrasis into a simple tense and writes no auxiliary at all, the finite verb keeps only the supplied subject: 8:13 ἦν → he, προσκαρτερῶν → continued. Joining the pair into one unit headed on the lexical verb is the competing reading and was not taken.
- A supplied juncture and converting a Greek participle into a coordinate member rides with the member it introduces and is never added, in this chapter for coordinate finite clauses (8:5, 8:24, 8:27, 8:30, 8:34) and equally at 8:31, where the ASV coordinates the participle ἀναβάντα with the infinitive καθίσαι and the and goes to καθίσαι. Rule 98's added reading was weighed and refused there, its scope being an asyndetic seam between two like constituents both rendered alike.
