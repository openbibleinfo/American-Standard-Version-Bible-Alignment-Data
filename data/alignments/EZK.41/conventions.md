# Ezekiel 41 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Doubled סָבִיב סָבִיב, which the ASV renders once, is aligned on the first member of the pair and the second is unrepresented (rule 22), on rule 47's reasoning about a repeated article; a further lone סָבִיב standing on the other side of לַבַּיִת takes the second English expression the ASV writes. So 41.5's pair takes round about and its lone third takes on every side, while 41.10's lone first takes round about and the following pair takes on every side — the two look opposite in a lemma table and are one analysis, because the Hebrew orders differ. The same first-occurrence reading gives 41.23's coordinated possessive לְ its single had. BSB represents every occurrence, which is the whole Ncbsa column of disagreements and part of the Rd column.
- מִפֹּה ... מִפֹּה is split under rule 2 into מִן → on and פֹּה → the one side / the other side, headed on one or other rather than on the generic side (rule 31); the ten units in 41.1, 41.2, 41.15, 41.19 and 41.26 all read this way. EZK.40 aligned the same two morphemes as one unit, on this side / on that side headed on on, where the ASV writes no article; that precedent was read and not followed here, because the ASV's the one side gives each morpheme English of its own and rule 30's one-English-rendering condition is unmet.
- In the chapter's measurement clauses the numeral is the predicate and carries the construction-supplied copula — was five cubits, were three (rule 20) — while רֹחַב and אֹרֶךְ take only the word the ASV writes for them: the breadth, a breadth, broad, thick, long, The thickness. Where the ASV makes רֹחַב itself the predicate the copula goes there instead (41.1, which was the breadth of the tabernacle), and where the predicate is wholly elided in a gapped closing clause (41.20 thus was the wall, 41.26 thus were the side chambers) it falls back onto the explicit subject under rule 20's elided-predicate exception. The measure phrase's supplied of goes to the numeral, not to רֹחַב (41.8, 41.10).
- לְ is read off the ASV in four ways here and they are not a split: unrendered before הַבַּיִת in the round about the house formula and for the second of two coordinated possessives (rule 40, 41.5/41.7/41.10/41.23); had or belonged to where the clause is possessive or a belonging relative (rules 43, 49); of where the ASV writes the relation itself (41.7 the breadth of the house); and its own directional word — for, toward, upon, by, on — elsewhere. In 41.7 the same לְמַעְלָה fuses whole to higher where the ASV writes one word and splits where it writes a supplied frame (went higher, continued upward), the frame going to the preposition under rule 49. The five Rd entries in the BSB column are this convention difference, not candidates.
