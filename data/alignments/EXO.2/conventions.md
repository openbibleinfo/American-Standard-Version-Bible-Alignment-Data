# Exodus 2 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Partitive מִן rendered ‘one of’ owns the complete phrase, including a supplied copula when it is the verbless predicate, and heads on the quantifying anchor ‘one’; a following construct noun does not take the partitive ‘of’.
