# Nehemiah 9 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Waw + finite verb the ASV recasts as a relative clause is read by what the English relative realizes. Where the relative is the recast clause's subject and no pronoun morpheme answers to it, the relative is the waw's connective (9.5 וּמְרוֹמַם → 'which is exalted', 9.27 וַיָּצֵרוּ → 'who distressed', וְיוֹשִׁיעוּם → 'who saved', rule 17). Where the relative is instead the object and an object suffix on the verb denotes the antecedent (9.22 וַתַּחְלְקֵם → 'which thou didst allot'), the suffix takes the relative under rule 18 and the waw, for which the ASV writes no connective, is unrepresented. These are one analysis, not a split; the 9.22 note names the competing reading.
- שׁוּב is aligned three ways in this chapter, each licensed by the Hebrew form. Hiphil as the clause's lexical verb keeps the ASV's 'again' in its own unit (9.26 לַהֲשִׁיבָם → 'to turn them again', 9.29 → 'thou mightest bring them again', rule 44), since 'turn'/'bring' alone do not write the returning. Qal יָשׁוּבוּ used as an auxiliary before לַעֲשׂוֹת (9.28 → 'they did evil again') takes 'again' alone under rule 56, the infinitive taking the subject pronoun and 'did' and its לְ going unrepresented. Qal שָׁבוּ as a full verb takes 'turned' (9.35).
