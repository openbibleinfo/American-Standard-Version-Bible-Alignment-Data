# Acts 4 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **ACT.4.1** — An ἐπι- compound verb governing a bare dative — ἐπέστησαν αὐτοῖς at 4:1, ἐπέβαλον … αὐτοῖς at 4:3 — is read under rule 106's closing clause: the English upon or on realizes the dative's case government and rides with the governed pronoun, and the verb keeps only its verb word. Do not read it as the preverb's own realization, which would leave the pronoun with no case preposition at all.
- **ACT.4.26** — The συν- preverb of συνάγω is realized by together inside the verb's own unit (4:27 συνήχθησαν → were gathered together, 4:31 συνηγμένοι → gathered together). Where ἐπὶ τὸ αὐτό stands in the same clause and the ASV writes together once (4:26), that word is the prepositional phrase's instead, as one noncompositional unit on ἐπί + αὐτό with the intervening article unrepresented under rule 93, and συνήχθησαν keeps only were gathered. MAT.22.34 and ACT.2.44 are the precedent.
- **ACT.4.35** — In τοὺς πόδας τῶν ἀποστόλων → the apostles' feet (4:35, 4:37) the article governing πόδας is unrepresented, the English possessive already occupying its determiner slot under rule 20, while the article governing the genitive possessor keeps its own the. A reference comparison reports the first article as represented in BSB; that report is not a correction.
- **ACT.4.21** — A substantival article before a participle keeps only the pronoun even where the ASV writes that which: τῷ takes that and γεγονότι takes which was done (rule 32). Rule 90 relaxes this only for a single relativizer, and rule 77's finding that τὸ → that which heads on that is a claim about the head, not licence to take the pair whole as one unit.
