# Psalms 118 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **PSA.118.10** — The refrain בְּשֵׁם יְהוָה כִּי אֲמִילַם (vv.10-12) reads the asseverative כִּי as unrepresented: the ASV writes In the name of Jehovah I will cut them off with no word for it. אֲמִילַם → I will cut ... off keeps off with the verb (rule 44), since cut alone does not write the destroying the hiphil of מוּל writes, and סְבָבוּנִי → compassed me about keeps about with the verb, because it restates the surrounding סָבַב itself writes and adds nothing the Hebrew lacks; both are discontinuous around the object pronoun under rule 25.
- **PSA.118.6** — יְהוָה לִי (vv.6-7) → Jehovah is on my side: the copula joins the prepositional predicate (rule 49), the fixed phrase on ... side is the לְ's rendering headed on side under rule 24's content-word sentence (competing head on), and my is the suffix's.
- **PSA.118.15** — יְמִין יְהוָה (vv.15-16) → The right hand of Jehovah heads on right under rule 31, since the ASV never writes a bare hand for יָמִין (that is יָד's word); the competing head hand is named in each note. The supplied The is the construct's under rule 29.
- **PSA.118.14** — וַיְהִי־לִי לִישׁוּעָה (v.14, and תְּהִי־לִי לִישׁוּעָה v.21) → he is become my salvation: הָיָה takes the become frame with its subject pronoun (rules 17, 21); the first לְ is a dative the ASV recasts as the possessive my, which goes to the suffix while the preposition is unrepresented (rule 40); the second לְ is the resultative one, unrepresented beside a written הָיָה (rule 21). v.22 הָיְתָה לְרֹאשׁ פִּנָּה reads the same way.
