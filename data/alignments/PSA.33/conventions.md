# Psalms 33 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **PSA.33.9** — עָמַד rendered 'stood fast' (33.9) and 'standeth fast' (33.11) keeps 'fast' with the verb: the standing-firm sense is the verb's own lexeme in this psalm, so 'fast' is not a surplus particle under rule 44. Competing reading marks 'fast' added; applied uniformly in both verses.
- **PSA.33.5** — A participial predicate with no written subject in the verse (אֹהֵב 33.5, כֹּנֵס and נֹתֵן 33.7) takes the ASV's supplied 'He' into its own unit under rule 17, since the participle is the clause's predicate and no other morpheme in the verse denotes the subject. Competing reading marks 'He' added under rule 20's closing sentence; the chapter applies the rule-17 reading throughout.
- **PSA.33.9** — וַיֶּהִי 'and it was done' is read as the whole rendering of הָיָה 'came to be' (rule 21), whereas the italic 'done' of 33.4 'is done in faithfulness', a verbless clause, is added under rule 20. The difference is the presence of the verb, not a split.
