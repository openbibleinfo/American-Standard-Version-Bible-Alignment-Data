# Numbers 19 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- mey (ha)niddah is aligned as a construct chain in all five places: niddah takes the relation the ASV recasts as for impurity (rule 19), and the phrase's single the goes to the article morpheme where one stands and the rectum is rendered anarthrously (19.21, twice), and to the construct as a supplied determiner where no article morpheme is in the chain (19.13, 19.20). 19.9's for a water for impurity gives the indefinite article to the construct noun under rule 15 and writes a for for each preposition.
- tame hu' is aligned by what the ASV writes for the pronoun and not by the shared Hebrew: 19.20 writes he is unclean, so hu' takes he and the copula joins the predicate adjective; 19.15 writes only is unclean after the long fronted subject every open vessel which..., so the resuming hu' is the unrendered occurrence and is unrepresented (rules 20, 22). BSB represents the 19.15 pronoun; that is its convention, not an error here. Do not harmonise the two.
- Every English object pronoun the ASV supplies where no object morpheme stands in the clause is its own added unit and is never folded into the verb beside it: cast it (19.6), lay them up (19.9), dip it and sprinkle it (19.18), toucheth it (19.22). Where an object marker or a suffix is present the pronoun goes to that morpheme instead (19.3, 19.5, 19.19). The cross-verse candidate table is blind to added units, so this is the chapter's only record that they were checked against each other.
