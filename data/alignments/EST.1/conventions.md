# Esther 1 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **EST.1.8** — For distributive X-and-X rendered once as every X, the first noun and conjunction are unrepresented and the closing noun carries the English expression.
- **EST.1.3** — The לְ־פָּנֶה expression remains fused when it is a verbless prepositional predicate; a supplied copular participle joins the fused unit.
- **EST.1.17** — A supplied deictic demonstrative is added when neither an article nor a demonstrative morpheme licenses it; the bare construct noun retains only its lexical rendering.
