# Psalms 85 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **PSA.85.3** — A reflexive or reciprocal pronoun the ASV writes for a verb whose Hebrew has no object morpheme is kept in the verb's unit under rule 44's reflexive clause rather than marked added: 85.3 Hiphil הֱשִׁיבוֹתָ → 'turned thyself' (italic thyself), 85.10 Qal נָשָׁקוּ → 'have kissed each other' and Niphal נִפְגָּשׁוּ → 'are met together'. The competing reading (a supplied object, added) is named in each note; the chapter takes the reflexive reading throughout.
- **PSA.85.5** — The one interrogative הַ opening the verse takes both 'Wilt's: the second colon תִּמְשֹׁךְ אַפְּךָ is asyndetic with no particle of its own, and the ASV rewrites the auxiliary at its head under the first particle's scope (rule 25's distributed interrogative, applied where no waw stands in the second colon). Giving the second Wilt to the verb under rule 17 was the competing reading.
