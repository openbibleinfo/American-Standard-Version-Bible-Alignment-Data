# Deuteronomy 1 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **DEU.1.21** — The fixed לְ+פָנֶה expression rendered by the single word “before” is noncompositional; because it has no uniquely defensible internal source head, use the first token לְ as the deterministic source anchor.
- **DEU.1.29** — Waw plus a negative fused as “neither,” “nor,” or “lest” is an aligned multi-source unit, headed on the negative, because both morphemes remain compositionally represented.
- **DEU.1.44** — The fixed כְּ+אֲשֶׁר expression rendered by the single word “as” is noncompositional throughout the chapter.
- **DEU.1.30** — In כְּ+כֹּל rendered “according to/unto all,” split the compound preposition “according to/unto” onto כְּ and “all” onto כֹּל.
- **DEU.1.12** — In לְבַדִּי rendered “myself alone,” split “myself” onto the pronominal suffix and “alone” onto לְ+בַד.
- **DEU.1.20** — The Hebrew הַר expanded as “hill country” heads on “hill,” the word carrying the lexeme.
