# Revelation 18 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **REV.18.15** — ἀπὸ μακρόθεν ("afar off", vv. 10, 15, 17): the preposition is pleonastic and English shows no separate realization of it, so μακρόθεν takes the whole idiom "afar off" headed on "afar" and ἀπό is unrepresented. Do not split "off" onto ἀπό; verse 10 was adjudicated that way before this was settled and needs the reviewer's correction.
- **REV.18.21** — οὐ μή with a future/aorist-subjunctive ('no more at all', 'in no wise'): keep the two negatives as one noncompositional unit taking the whole English emphatic negation, headed on οὐ / the English 'no'. Do not split 'at all' or 'in no wise' across the two particles. Verse 7 was adjudicated as a plain two-source aligned unit before this was settled; the reviewer should reconcile it.
