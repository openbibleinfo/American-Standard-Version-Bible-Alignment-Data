# Exodus 29 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **EXO.29.26** — Treat lexicalized לִפְנֵי as one noncompositional unit; because its internal source head is not uniquely defensible, use the first source token as the deterministic anchor, while English 'before' is the target head and may carry a supplied copula in a predicate.
- **EXO.29.27** — In the chapter's wave-, heave-, and peace-offering expansions, head the substantive on the lexeme-bearing English modifier ('wave', 'heave', or 'peace'), not the generic supplied noun 'offering(s)'.
