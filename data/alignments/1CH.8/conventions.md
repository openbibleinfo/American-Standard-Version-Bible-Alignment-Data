# 1 Chronicles 8 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **1CH.8.33** — Two-token proper names that MACULA segments and the ASV also writes in two tokens — מַלְכִּי שׁוּעַ → Malchi shua (8.33), מְרִיב בַּעַל → Merib baal (8.34) — are matched surface to surface, one unit per token, rather than fused as one two-token unit; where the name is a verbless-clause predicate the construction's copula joins the first element (was Merib). 1CH.9.39-40 repeats both names and should follow this.
- **1CH.8.29** — וְ + noun + suffix recast by the ASV as a relative clause (whose wife's name was Maacah 8.29, whose names are these 8.38): the suffix takes whose (rule 18), the waw is unrepresented because no connective is written for it, and the copula joins the Hebrew predicate (was Maacah on the name; names are on שְׁמוֹת) whatever order the ASV writes. 1CH.9.35 repeats the first clause.
