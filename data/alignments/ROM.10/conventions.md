# Romans 10 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **ROM.10.12** — A supplied English noun standing inside a phrase a Greek article governs is the article's under rule 102 even where the same lexeme is expressed elsewhere in the verse: ὁ … αὐτὸς κύριος πάντων → *the same Lord is Lord of all* gives the discontinuous *the … Lord* to the article headed on *Lord*, a bare *same* to the intensive pronoun and *is Lord* to κύριος. Rule 103's `added` reading was taken first and is corrected here; GAL.3.20 is not the frame, its article governing an expressed noun. ROM.10.15's τὰ ἀγαθά → *things* and *good* is the same judgment where no lexeme recurs.
- **ROM.10.13** — *Whosoever* is assigned by which source tokens stand under it, and the chapter writes both cases. At 10:11 πᾶς ὁ πιστεύων gives the one English word to the quantifier and leaves the article `unrepresented` (rule 80). At 10:13 πᾶς ὃς ἂν ἐπικαλέσηται gives it to ὃς ἄν as one unit, rule 50 forbidding an `unrepresented` ἄν and rule 80's own clause reserving the `-soever` for a particle standing to answer to it, so πᾶς is `unrepresented` instead. BSB represents πᾶς there; that report is not a correction.
- **ROM.10.4** — A substantival article before a participle carries its own case preposition — τῶν εὐαγγελιζομένων → *of them* at 10:15, τοῖς … ἐπερωτῶσιν → *unto them* at 10:20 — unless another case-marked source token stands in front of it and the ASV writes the preposition there: παντὶ τῷ πιστεύοντι → *to every* on the dative quantifier and a bare *one* on the article at 10:4, the article's English being a nominal rather than a determiner for rule 21 to anchor. Where an expressed preposition governs the phrase it keeps its own word and the article stays bare, as ἐν τοῖς … ζητοῦσιν → *of* and *them* at 10:20.
