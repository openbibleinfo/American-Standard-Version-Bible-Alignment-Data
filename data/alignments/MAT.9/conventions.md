# Matthew 9 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- παραλυτικός → *sick of the palsy* heads on *palsy* in all three units (9:2 twice, 9:6). *sick* is the generic word and the phrase's formal English head only, which rule 17 forbids as a ground of choice; the chapter spends a bare *sick* on κακῶς at 9:12, and that is what shows under rule 91's operational test which word the ASV gives this lexeme.
- An articular substantival adjective yields the supplied nominal to its article only where the ASV writes a generic completion: οἱ τυφλοί → *the … men* (9:28) and ὁ κωφός → *the … man* (9:33) give that noun to the article under rule 102 and leave the adjective bare. Where the ASV instead renders the adjective by a multiword expression of its own, τῷ παραλυτικῷ → *unto the sick of the palsy* (9:2) and *to the sick of the palsy* (9:6) give the article only the determiner with its case preposition and keep the whole expression on the adjective, no word of it being supplied. The anarthrous contrast is δύο τυφλοί → *two blind men* (9:27) and παραλυτικόν → *a man sick of the palsy* (9:2), where no article competes and rule 17 keeps the supplied noun in the adjective's own unit.
