# Judges 12 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- An italic partitive the ASV supplies before a definite construct chain — any of the fugitives of Ephraim (v5), one of the cities of Gilead (v7) — answers to no morpheme in this chapter: no מִן, כֹּל or numeral stands in either clause, and the chain's own of belongs to the rectum under rule 19. The quantifier and its of are one added unit headed on the quantifier, while the construct noun keeps the supplied the rule 29 gives it. Do not read the quantifier as a supplied determiner under rule 15: the ASV writes its own the after it, so it determines nothing.
- MACULA segments the toponym בֵּית לֶחֶם as two Np tokens sharing one lemma. The ASV writes Beth lehem in two words, so each token takes the word written for it and neither is fused or left unrepresented (rule 2). JDG.12.8 and JDG.12.10 are aligned alike.
