# Ezekiel 12 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה is split three ways wherever it stands (12.10, 12.19, 12.23, 12.25, 12.28): אֲדֹנָי takes *the Lord* with the supplied *the* under rule 15, its 1cs suffix is `unrepresented` because the ASV writes no pronoun for it, and יְהוִה takes *Jehovah*. In נְאֻם אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה the ASV recasts נְאֻם as the finite *saith*, so no *of* is written for the construct relation and none is placed. BSB represents that suffix and this is the documented difference, not an error.
- לְעֵינֵיהֶם, seven times across 12.3–12.7, is split rather than fused: לְ takes *in*, עַיִן takes *sight*, the suffix takes *their*. The ASV spends a word on each morpheme, so rule 30's one-English-word condition is unmet and rule 2 governs; do not read *in the sight of* as one rendering.
- The ethical dative לְךָ / לִי on an imperative or a first-person verb (12.3, 12.5, 12.7): the preposition is `unrepresented` in every case under rule 40, and its suffix takes an English pronoun only where the ASV writes one — *thee* in *prepare thee stuff* (12.3), nothing in *Dig thou through the wall* (12.5) or *I digged* (12.7). The *thou* of *Dig thou* and of *Say thou* (12.10) is the imperative's own subject pronoun under rule 17 and is never the suffix's. BSB represents these prepositions and suffixes; that is the convention difference the validation report counts.
- בֵּית הַמֶּרִי is rendered attributively throughout (rule 28), and the article's placement follows what the ASV writes. Where the ASV writes two *the*s (12.2 *the midst of the rebellious house*, 12.9 *the house of Israel the rebellious house*), the article takes the one standing before its own word's rendering *rebellious* and the construct noun takes a supplied one, per rule 29's arthrous-rectum clause; nothing is fronted. In the vocative *O rebellious house* (12.25) the ASV writes no *the* at all, so the article is `unrepresented` and the noun holds the whole *O … house* under rule 36.
- כַּאֲשֶׁר is deliberately treated two ways in this chapter and neither is to be harmonised to the other: fused into one unit for the single *as* of 12.7, split into *like* (כְּ) and *as* (אֲשֶׁר) in 12.11, where the ASV spends a word on each morpheme. Rule 30's one-English-word condition, not the Hebrew phrase, decides which.
