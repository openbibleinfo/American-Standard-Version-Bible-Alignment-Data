# Ezekiel 45 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- A function word the ASV supplies before a bare Hebrew nominal goes to the noun or numeral it stands before under rule 33 and is never `added`, whatever the italic says (rule 7): the *of* of *of five and twenty thousand* (45:1), *of one measure* (45:11) and *of the bath* (45:14), and the *for* of *for twenty chambers* (45:5) and *for a sin offering* (45:23). Only where the ASV supplies the head noun as well is the whole phrase `added`, there being no Hebrew noun in the clause to carry it: the italic *in length* and *in breadth* of 45:2, and the italic *reeds* of 45:1.
- אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה → *the Lord Jehovah* is divided the same way at every occurrence (45:9, 45:15, 45:18): the supplied *the* joins the title under rule 15, אֲדֹנָי takes *Lord*, the possessive suffix is `unrepresented` under rule 22, and יְהוִה takes *Jehovah*. This is the source of the four Sp1cs disagreements with BSB, which renders the suffix.
- In the date formulae the ordinal or numeral is what the ASV determines. Where the Hebrew writes no יוֹם or חֹדֶשׁ, the italic noun joins the ordinal's own unit — *the first month* (45:18, 45:21), *the seventh day* (45:20), *the seventh month* (45:25) — and where יוֹם is written it takes *day* while the numeral keeps only the supplied *the*: *the fourteenth day* (45:21), *the fifteenth day* (45:25). A compound numeral the ASV renders by one English word shares a unit under rule 30, including its waw where one stands: *fifteen* (45:12), *fourteenth* (45:21), *fifteenth* (45:25).
- Rule 31's subtraction test placed the head on the lexeme-bearing word in every multi-word rendering of a single substantive, and that word is not always the same part of speech. It is the attributive in קֹדֶשׁ → *holy portion* and *holy place*, חַטָּאת → *sin offering*, מִנְחָה → *meal offering*, עֹלָה → *burnt offering*, שֶׁלֶם → *peace offerings*, נֶסֶךְ → *drink offerings*, חֹדֶשׁ → *new moons*, מוֹעֵד → *appointed feasts*, מַצָּה → *unleavened bread* and מַשְׁקֶה → *well watered pastures*; it is the noun in חֹק → *the set portion* (45:14) and מְזוּזָה → *the door posts* (45:19), where the ASV's added word is a specifier and the noun carries the lexeme, as the bare *the posts* later in 45:19 confirms.
