# Deuteronomy 6 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **DEU.6.12** — The וְהָיָה כִּי protasis opened at DEU.6.10 runs through DEU.6.11 and finds its apodosis at DEU.6.12, which the ASV opens with *then*. No conjunction morpheme stands in DEU.6.12 — the clause begins with the imperative הִשָּׁמֶר — and rule 10 keeps the הָיָה of DEU.6.10 inside its own verse, so that *then* is its own `added` unit under rule 14. Do not restore it to the הָיָה frame of DEU.6.10, which holds only *it shall be* because the ASV writes no *that* (rule 45), and do not fold it into the imperative or into the לְ of לְךָ: the only morpheme that could have licensed it stands in another verse.
- **DEU.6.11** — Supplied English prepositions in this chapter are placed by what licenses them, not by position. One written before a nominal whose own case-function licenses it joins that nominal under rule 33 — אֶרֶץ → *in a land* (6.3), מָחָר → *in time to come* (6.20), יוֹם → *at ... day* (6.24, the כְּ beside it already holding its own *as*). The *of* of מְלֵאִים כָּל־טוּב → *houses full of all good things* instead joins מָלֵא, on rule 44's reasoning that a preposition the ASV writes to reach a governing word's complement belongs to that word's unit: מְלֵאִים is absolute (Aampa), so no construct relation stands for rule 19 to place the *of* on, and English *full* selects the word rather than the nominal stating what it contributes. The competing reading, giving the *of* to כֹּל as rule 33's opening word of the phrase, is recorded in that verse's note.
- **DEU.6.24** — כָּל־הַיָּמִים → *always* is aligned as a two-morpheme fusion of כֹּל and יָמִים, headed on יָמִים, with the separately segmented article its own `unrepresented` unit because the ASV writes no *the* for the phrase. That follows rule 30's explicit ruling on דִּבְרֵי הַיָּמִים → *chronicles*, which keeps a separately segmented article out of such a fusion. Corpus precedent for the same Hebrew phrase rendered *for ever* runs the other way, reading all three morphemes as one `noncompositional` unit headed on *ever* in seven units including DEU.4.40 and 1KI.11.39. The binding rule was followed over the precedent here; anyone revisiting this should change the rule and the corpus together rather than one chapter at a time.
