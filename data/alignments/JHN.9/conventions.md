# John 9 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **JHN.9.11** — ἀναβλέπω is rendered by the support phrase 'receive(d) (his) sight'. The head is the meaning-bearing complement 'sight', which realizes the βλέπ- lexeme, never the light verb 'received' (rule 17). Applies at JHN.9.11, 9.15, and both units in 9.18.
- **JHN.9.14** — An article standing before a name — ὁ Ἰησοῦς, τοῦ Σιλωάμ, τοῦ Μωϋσέως, ὁ θεός — has no English realization and is its own unrepresented unit (rule 23); any case preposition stays with the name.
- **JHN.9.2** — Where a genitive pronoun stands with the noun, the ASV's possessive (his, mine, thine, your, our) belongs to that pronoun and the article is displaced and unrepresented (rule 20). Recurs on ὀφθαλμούς and γονεῖς throughout the chapter.
- **JHN.9.12** — An inflected verb carries its supplied English subject pronoun as a helper, and the head is the lexical verb, not the pronoun or auxiliary (rules 7, 9, 17).
- **JHN.9.28** — The verse-opening *And* is `added` because SBLGNT.JHN.9.28.v029's fallback dropped the connective, not because the ASV supplied one freely: 'And they reviled him' excludes the asyndetic r00-base, but r01 (καὶ) and r02 (οἱ δὲ) are both rendered 'and they', so the unit stays underdetermined and the projection carries a base the evidence excluded. Rule 97 is the general statement; PHP.3.12 is the other live instance.
- **JHN.9.9** — Where the ASV recasts a demonstrative-plus-expressed-copula clause as an English cleft with an expletive subject — Οὗτός ἐστιν → 'It is he', ἐκεῖνός ἐστιν → 'he it is' — the demonstrative takes the predicate pronoun that realizes it and the expressed copula takes the expletive together with 'is' as an ordinary rule 9 helper. Do not align the demonstrative to the expletive, and do not read rule 68's subject-takes-the-copula arm here: that arm governs a verbless clause with a supplied copula, whereas the copula is expressed in both of these and the English subject is the expletive rather than the demonstrative. Applies at JHN.9.9 and JHN.9.37.
- **JHN.9.21** — The idiom ἡλικίαν ἔχει, which the ASV renders 'is of age', splits rather than joining: the verb takes the supplied subject pronoun with 'is', and the noun takes 'of age' headed on the noun. Rule 73 does not join it, the ASV having spread the pair over separate English words rather than collapsing it into a single verb, and 'is' answers to ἔχει rather than being a supplied copula for rule 53 to place. A single noncompositional unit over both tokens remains defensible; the chapter takes this reading at JHN.9.21 and JHN.9.23 and both verses must move together.
