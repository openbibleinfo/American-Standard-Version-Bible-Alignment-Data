# Joshua 6 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The chapter's five הָיָה frames are read by where the ASV wrote the complementizer, not by whether the apodosis has a waw: 6.5 and 6.8 keep *that* with the frame, because it stands immediately after *it shall be* / *it was so* and ahead of the temporal material; 6.15 and 6.20 give *that* to the apodosis waw, because it stands at that clause's head; and 6.16 writes none for the apodosis, so its waw is `unrepresented` and the *when* it supplies for the asyndetic תָּקְעוּ clause is `added`. Where a כְּ stands (6.5, 6.8, 6.20) it takes the *when* under rule 52.
- תַּחְתֶּיהָ → *flat* (6.5, 6.20): the one English word goes to תַּחַת and the 3fs suffix is its own `unrepresented` unit, since *flat* carries nothing of the suffix and rule 30's condition is unmet for it. The ASV's marginal *Hebrew in its place* is what the fused reading would rest on; it is not taken. BSB represents the suffix in both verses.
- A jussive the ASV expands with a *let* frame keeps that frame with the verb whose mood it renders, discontinuous around an intervening subject (rule 17): יִשְׂאוּ → *let ... bear* (6.6), יַעֲבֹר → *let ... pass on* (6.7), תַשְׁמִיעוּ → *let ... be heard* (6.10). The *let* is not rule 20's supplied lexical verb and is never `added`.
- The italic *the priests* the ASV supplies as subject of the closing participial clause is `added` in each of 6.9, 6.13 and 6.20 (rules 7, 17); it is not folded into the infinitive absolute or the participle standing beside it.
- The infinitive absolute of הָלַךְ is read by what the ASV spends on it: recast as a finite clause it takes the whole clause as its own unit, הָלוֹךְ → *as they went* (6.9, 6.13), headed on the lexical verb; written as a distinct adverb it is rule 56's, הָלוֹךְ → *continually* (6.13). The waw between two infinitives absolute is `unrepresented` where the ASV writes no connective (6.9, 6.13).
- Rule 31's tie-break is settled inside this chapter for two lemmas: the ASV writes a bare *ram's* for יוֹבֵל (6.5) and a bare *young* for נַעַר (6.21), so *rams horns* heads on *rams* (6.4, 6.6, 6.8, 6.13) and *young men* on *young* (6.23), the accompanying noun being the supplied generic.
