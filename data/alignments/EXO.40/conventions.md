# Exodus 40 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The Piel כָּהַן is rendered *minister in the priest's office*, and the whole English expression is one unit on that verb, headed on *minister* rather than on *priest's*: the office phrase is the idiom's own body and not a light verb's complement under rule 44. Its supplied subject pronoun and modal stay with it under rule 17, and the unit is discontinuous around the dative *unto me*, whose two words belong to the לְ and its suffix (rules 18, 25). EXO.40.13 and EXO.40.15 are read alike; each verse's note names the competing head.
- Where the hiphil of לָבֵשׁ is recast so that the garments become the English direct object and the person clothed is reached by *upon*, the object marker standing on the person takes that *upon* under rule 16's first family, and heads on it. EXO.40.13 gives *upon* to the marker on אַהֲרֹן and leaves the second marker, standing on the garments the ASV writes bare, unrepresented; EXO.40.14 gives *upon* to the marker on the suffixed object while the suffix takes *them*. This is the reading to carry into the parallel investiture formulas.
- Where עָרַךְ and its cognate noun עֵרֶךְ both stand in one clause, each morpheme takes one English expression, and which of them carries *in order* follows from what the other's own rendering leaves rather than from a fixed preference. EXO.40.4 gives *the things* to the suffixed עֶרְכּוֹ, so *set in order* is the verb's light-verb rendering headed on *order* (rule 44); EXO.40.23 gives *the bread* to the rectum לֶחֶם, so *in order* is the construct עֵרֶךְ's own adverbial rendering (rule 33) and the verb keeps *set*. The divergence is the ASV's and is not a split.
- EXO.40.2 and EXO.40.17 write the same בְּאֶחָד לַחֹדֶשׁ, but the ASV collapses 40.2's two date phrases into the single *On the first day of the first month*, so that verse's second בְּ, its לַ, its assimilated article and its second חֹדֶשׁ are unrepresented under rules 22 and 40, while 40.17 renders each of them. In 40.2 the two written ordinals are distributed one to אֶחָד and one to רִאשׁוֹן, each standing beside the noun it modifies in English; the reverse distribution is defensible and is named in that unit's note. A later reader should expect the candidate table to flag this pair and should not read it as a divergence of analysis.
