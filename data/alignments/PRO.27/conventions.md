# Proverbs 27 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- PRO.27 italics: this chapter's ASV marks eight supplied words, and each was counted against the morphemes of its own clause rather than accepted or rejected as a class (rule 7). Where no morpheme answers, the word is `added`: the three italic connectives of 27.12 and the italic *And* of 27.23, none of which has a waw in its clause, and the italic *answereth* of 27.19 and *tried* of 27.21, whose Hebrew lines write no verb. Where a morpheme answers, the italic joins it: the *doth* of 27.9 and the repeated *that is surety for* of 27.13 are second realizations of a verb the Hebrew writes once, and the *there will be* of 27.27 is the construction's copula and dummy on the לְ heading the predicate.
- PRO.27 draws the rule 20 boundary between a linking verb and a supplied lexical verb twice, in the same construction and opposite ways. At 27.24 *endure* in *doth the crown endure unto all generations* joins the לְ under rules 20 and 49, because it links the subject to a prepositional predicate the Hebrew writes and adds no participant. At 27.21 *is tried* in *a man is tried by his praise* is `added` whole, auxiliary included, because it introduces an event and an agent the Hebrew never wrote and the לְפִי beside it has already spent its word on *by*.
- PRO.27 renders four construct chains attributively rather than with *of* (rule 28): גֹּאֶה-type pairs at 27.9 (*hearty counsel*), 27.15 (*a very rainy day*, *a contentious woman*) and 27.27 (*goats milk*). In each the rectum keeps only its attributive word and no relational word is placed, while the chains rendered with *of* at 27.9, 27.10, 27.17, 27.19, 27.20, 27.23, 27.25, 27.26 and 27.27 put that word on the rectum. The chapter reads which shape the ASV wrote rather than the Hebrew's construct state.
- PRO.27 marks one unit `noncompositional` — שִׁית לֵב → *look well* at 27.23, where neither English word writes either morpheme's lexeme, so rule 6's fallback anchors the source head on the first token. The chapter's four preposition-plus-construct fusions (27.1, 27.4, 27.21, 27.22) are `aligned`, following rule 30 rather than rule 6. The corpus records both statuses for such fusions, so the split here is a chapter convention and not a finding about either verse.
