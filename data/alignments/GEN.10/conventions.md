# Genesis 10 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **GEN.10.12** — When a definite noun and its attributive adjective both carry articles but the ASV writes one determiner for the phrase, assign that determiner to the first article and leave the later agreement article unrepresented, even when English reorders the determiner before the adjective.
- **GEN.10.19** — A supplied English subordinator such as as is added when a finite recasting of a bare infinitive construct has no source conjunction; the infinitive keeps the verb and its suffix keeps the subject pronoun.
