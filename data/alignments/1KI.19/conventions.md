# 1 Kings 19 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **1KI.19.5** — Asyndetic imperative pairs (קוּם אֱכֹל in verses 5 and 7): the ASV's coordinating *and* answers to no Hebrew morpheme and is marked added, not folded into the second imperative's unit.
- **1KI.19.14** — In *and I, even I only, am left* (verses 10 and 14) the emphatic *even* belongs to the pleonastic independent pronoun אֲנִי, not to the 1cs suffix of לְבַדִּי, whose morphemes are already the fixed expression rendered *only*.
- **1KI.19.4** — רֹתֶם is the shrub itself, so *juniper-tree* heads on *juniper* and *tree* is the ASV's supplied specifier (verses 4 and 5).
- **1KI.19.4** — The numeral אֶחָד after רֹתֶם is the indefinitizing *a certain*, and aligns to the ASV's article *a* rather than being marked unrepresented (verses 4 and 5).
