# Deuteronomy 4 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **DEU.4.28** — In DEU.4, waw plus lo rendered by the single compositional connective nor or neither remains one aligned unit, headed on the negative source morpheme and the English connective.
- **DEU.4.8** — In DEU.4, le plus paneh rendered before is one aligned compound-preposition unit, with paneh as source head and before as target head, regardless of the external complement.
