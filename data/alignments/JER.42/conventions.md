# Jeremiah 42 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **JER.42.2** — When כַּאֲשֶׁר is rendered by the single word 'as', treat it as noncompositional and use the first source token as the deterministic source head.
- **JER.42.11** — When ASV fronts 'to', 'unto', or 'of' before 'whom' from a written resumptive preposition, assign the preposition to that relation, אֲשֶׁר to 'whom', and leave the resumptive suffix unrepresented.
- **JER.42.17** — Treat a rendered מִן־פָּנֶה compound as an aligned one-rendering fusion under rules 19 and 30; contextual 'of' versus 'from' does not change its status.
