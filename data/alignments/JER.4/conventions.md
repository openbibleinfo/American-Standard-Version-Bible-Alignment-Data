# Jeremiah 4 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Article in a demonstrative phrase. Where בַּיּוֹם הַהוּא, לָעָם הַזֶּה, בָּעֵת הַהִיא write two article morphemes beside a demonstrative pronoun and the ASV writes one determiner — that day, this people, at that time — the determiner goes to the demonstrative pronoun and both articles are unrepresented. Rule 41's rider, that a determiner is the article's only where no other morpheme licenses it, is read here to cover a demonstrative pronoun exactly as it covers a possessive suffix. Six article morphemes across JER.4.9, JER.4.10 and JER.4.11 go this way, and none of them is aligned to that or this.
- מִפְּנֵי is decided by rule 30's subtraction at each occurrence rather than once for the chapter. It fuses where the ASV writes a single rendering — because of in JER.4.4, before in the second half of JER.4.26 — and splits where the ASV spends at on the preposition and the presence on פָּנֶה, in the first half of JER.4.26. The candidate table shows this lemma with two treatments in one verse; that is the ASV's own doing and is not a split to repair.
- A single Hebrew substantive rendered by an attributive plus an English noun heads on the attributive where the English noun is generic — נִיר → fallow ground, כַּרְמֶל → fruitful field, שְׁפִי → bare heights — and on the noun only where the noun itself carries the lexeme, as כָּלָה → a full end. All four were reconciled to rule 31's subtraction test in this chapter; the English phrase's syntax was not allowed to decide any of them.
