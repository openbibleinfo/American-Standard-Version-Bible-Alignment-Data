# Ecclesiastes 12 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- `עַד אֲשֶׁר לֹא` rendered by a single *before* is one unit of all three morphemes under rule 30, headed on `עַד` under rule 23: the one English word carries the temporal limit, the subordination and the negative's 'not yet', so nothing is left over to mark. ECC.12.1, ECC.12.2 and ECC.12.6 are all decided this way; the competing reading gives *before* to `עַד` alone and marks the relative and the negative `unrepresented`, and is rejected because rule 22 forbids marking a morpheme unrendered whose sense the ASV wrote inside a fused word.
- Where a locative `בְּ` phrase follows a verb the ASV renders with a directional particle, decide the particle by subtraction on the verb's lexeme rather than by the particle's shape. `הָרֹאוֹת בָּאֲרֻבּוֹת` → *those that look out of the windows* gives *out of* whole to the preposition under rule 24, because *look* already renders `רָאָה` and dropping *out* leaves only the relation unstated; `וְסָבְבוּ בַשּׁוּק` → *the mourners go about the streets* keeps *about* with the verb under rule 44, because *go* alone loses `סָבַב`'s 'go around', and the `בְּ` is then `unrepresented` under rule 40 with no word spent on its own relation.
- A subject the ASV supplies for a verbless clause is divided by what it denotes. An impersonal *it* completing the copula joins the predicate under rule 20's own `כִּי־טוֹב` → *that it was good* example, so `אִם־טוֹב וְאִם־רָע` → *whether it be good or whether it be evil* puts *it be* on each predicate adjective at ECC.12.14. A referential demonstrative denoting something no morpheme in the clause denotes is `added`, so the *This* of `סוֹף דָּבָר` → *This is the end of the matter* at ECC.12.13 is its own `added` unit while the copula still joins the predicate nominal.
