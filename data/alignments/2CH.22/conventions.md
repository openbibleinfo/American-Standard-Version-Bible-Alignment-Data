# 2 Chronicles 22 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Royal filiation formula. In `X בֶּן־Y מֶלֶךְ Z` → *X the son of Y king of Z*, the construct בֵּן takes a supplied *the* under rule 29 and the apposed construct מֶלֶךְ is written bare, taking no supplied determiner, because the ASV writes one *the* for the phrase and it stands before *son*. So 2CH.22.1, 22.5, 22.6 (twice) and 22.7. Where an article morpheme stands in such a chain instead, the fronting sentences of rule 29 govern and the construct is the bare one — 22.11's `בַּת־הַמֶּלֶךְ יְהוֹרָם` → *the daughter of king Jehoram*.
- The determiner count of rule 29 is run phrase by phrase, and one verse may contain both shapes. 2CH.22.11 writes `בַּת־הַמֶּלֶךְ` → *the daughter of the king*, where the rectum is arthrous, nothing is fronted, the article takes the *the* before its own word and the construct takes a supplied one; and `בַּת־הַמֶּלֶךְ יְהוֹרָם` → *the daughter of king Jehoram*, where the rectum is anarthrous, the phrase's single written *the* is the article's fronted to the left edge, and the construct keeps only *daughter*. 22.10 (`זֶרַע הַמַּמְלָכָה` → *the seed royal*) and 22.12 (`בֵית הָאֱלֹהִים` → *the house of God*) are the fronting shape as well. Read which determiner stands before the rectum's own word; do not read the left edge as evidence of fronting, and do not mark a present article unrepresented while a supplied *the* holds the written word.
- 2CH.22.6 s007: the ASV's *of the wounds* answers to the מִן of the parallel at 2 Kings 8:29, where the projection carries כִּי, and the packet records no variant unit in that slot. Under rule 53's closing paragraph the printed English is aligned against the token that is there, so the כִּי holds the *of* and the divergence is named in the unit's note. Do not renormalize this to an unrepresented conjunction beside a supplied preposition on `הַמַּכִּים`: rule 9 forbids taking a textual decision here, and the ASV margin (*The text has because the wounds which etc.*) corroborates the divergence without deciding it, by rule 38.
