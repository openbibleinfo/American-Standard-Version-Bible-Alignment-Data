# 2 Chronicles 26 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- An appositional or explicative לְ standing on the phrase an English *even* introduces takes that word, together with whatever relational word the ASV writes beside it, as one rendering of the one morpheme, headed on the relational word where one is written and on *even* where it stands alone: לְגִבּוֹרֵי חָיִל → *even* (26.12) and לְכָל הַצָּבָא → *even for*, headed on *for* (26.14). Rule 16's ruling that an appositional *even* is `added` reaches a phrase on which no morpheme stands at all; a written preposition on that phrase is not that case, and a lamed already holding its own *for* is still the only morpheme the *even* answers to.
- Compound prepositional expressions built on עַד and מִן with a following לְ or כִּי are read as one fused unit wherever the ASV writes a single rendering for the group, never split so that one member holds the whole English word and the rest go `unrepresented`: עַד לְ → *even to* (26.8) and → *so that* (26.16), עַד לְמַעְלָה → *exceeding* (26.8), עַד לְמֵרָחוֹק → *far abroad* (26.15), מֵעַל לְ → *beside* (26.19), עַד כִּי → *till* (26.15). Head on the morpheme carrying the lexeme where one stands in the group (מַעַל, רָחוֹק), and otherwise on the governing first morpheme under rule 23.
- In the regnal age formula בֶּן N שָׁנָה, בֵּן takes the construction-supplied copula and heads on *old* — בֶּן שֵׁשׁ עֶשְׂרֵה שָׁנָה → *was ... old* (26.1) and *old was* (26.3) — while the numeral morphemes fuse into the single English numeral and שָׁנָה keeps its own *years*. The unit is discontinuous around the numeral and the counted noun under rule 25, and the ASV's word order does not move the head.
