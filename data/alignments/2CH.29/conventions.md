# 2 Chronicles 29 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- `עַד` + `לְ` is read by what the `לְ` governs. Before a noun, where the ASV writes a single English word for the pair, the two fuse under rule 30 and the unit heads on `עַד`: `עַד־לְשִׂמְחָה` → *with* (29.30 s025+s026). Before an infinitive construct the `לְ` is that verb's own marker and not half of a compound preposition, so `עַד` takes the written *until* or *till* by itself and the `לְ` is `unrepresented` under rules 40 and 52: `עַד לִכְלוֹת הָעֹלָה` → *until the burnt offering was finished* (29.28 s016, s017), aligned exactly as the `לְ`-less `עַד־כְּלוֹת הַמְּלָאכָה` → *till the work was ended* (29.34 s023) is. Both units name the competing rule 30 fusion in their notes; the chapter took the split, and the corpus was not consulted for `עַד לְ` before an infinitive.
- `פְּנִימָה` takes only what the ASV writes for it, and the discriminator is whether that rendering is already complete without the supplied words. At 29.16 the adverb's whole rendering is the phrase *the inner part*, which joins its unit under rule 31 headed on *inner*, since dropping *part* leaves no rendering of the lexeme. At 29.18 the ASV writes a complete *within* for the adverb and then supplies an italic *the palace* — both the determiner and a head noun the Hebrew never writes — which is its own `added` unit under rule 33's supplied-head-noun case. Do not fold that noun phrase into the adverb on the strength of the 29.16 unit.
