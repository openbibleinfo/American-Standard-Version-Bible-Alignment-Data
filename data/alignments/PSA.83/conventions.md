# Psalms 83 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Where the ASV writes one volitive frame over a run of coordinated jussives or prohibitives (v.1 *keep not thou … Hold not thy peace … be not still*; v.17 *Let them be put to shame and dismayed … let them be confounded and perish*), the frame joins the first verb of the coordination under rule 17 and each later verb takes only its own lexical word and the negative or connective written for it; the frame is not repeated onto later verbs and no part of it is `added`.
