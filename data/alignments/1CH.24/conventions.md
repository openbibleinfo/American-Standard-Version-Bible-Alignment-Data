# 1 Chronicles 24 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Where the ASV recasts a Hebrew prepositional predicate or appositional phrase so that the preposition carries its own written English and the copula is left standing beside a predicate the ASV has supplied, this chapter marked the copula `added` together with that supplied predicate rather than giving it to the spent preposition under rule 49. 1CH.24.1 `וְלִבְנֵי אַהֲרֹן מַחְלְקוֹתָם` → *And the courses of the sons of Aaron* ***were these*** puts *were these* in one `added` unit, the `לְ` keeping the *of* the ASV wrote for it; 1CH.24.6 `מִן־הַלֵּוִי` → *the scribe,* ***who was*** *of the Levites* does the same with *who was*, the `מִן` keeping its *of*. Both notes name rule 49's competing reading, which would give the copula to the preposition as `בְכַפֵּיהֶם` → *is in their hands* does. What tipped both units the other way is that the recast English leaves the preposition inside a subject or a modifier rather than heading the clause's predicate, which is the case rule 49's own closing paragraph excludes.
