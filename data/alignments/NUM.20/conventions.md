# Numbers 20 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The mount-Hor phrase Hor ha-har (vv 22, 23, 25, 27): the English preposition belongs to Hor, the noun the Hebrew phrase opens with, and not to the appositive the ASV fronts ahead of it. vv 23 and 27 write that preposition on Hor itself (be-Hor ha-har -> in mount Hor, 'el-Hor ha-har -> into mount Hor) and the ASV renders it before mount, which is what settles the supplied unto of vv 22 and 25 under rule 33; the English order is not evidence against the Hebrew's. har takes only mount, and its article is unrepresented in all four verses because the ASV writes no the for the phrase (rule 29). Contrast v 28's rosh ha-har -> the top of the mount, where the ASV writes a the before each word and the article takes the one standing before its own.
- In the double-accusative stripping and clothing clauses of vv 26 and 28, the marker on the first object is unrepresented and the marker on the second takes the preposition the English verb reaches it through — of in strip Aaron of his garments, upon in put them upon Eleazar — under rule 16's first family, the object noun keeping only its own English. The 'ot + suffix pair of vv 25 and 28 goes the ordinary way beside them: the suffix takes them and the marker has nothing.
