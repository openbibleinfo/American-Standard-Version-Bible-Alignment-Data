# Galatians 4 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **GAL.4.17** — Where the ASV renders one Greek verb by an English adverb plus a generic verb, the head is the adverb that realizes the lexeme, not the formal lexical verb: zeloo -> zealously seek (v17) and to be zealously sought (v18) both head on zealously, the corpus glossing the zel- root zeal and jealous and never seek, which is zeteo's gloss. Compare EPH.6.20 speak boldly and REV.18.9 lived wantonly. This does not reach a contentful English verb that glosses the lexeme itself: euengelisamen -> I preached the gospel (v13) heads on preached, the gospel being a supplied cognate object, as at GAL.1.8 and GAL.3.8.
- **GAL.4.8** — Where the ASV expands a single Greek adverb into the temporal frame preposition + determiner + time, the head is the noun, not the determiner: tote -> at that time (v8) and pantote -> at all times (v18) head on time and times. Neither word alone glosses the lexeme, and the corpus reads this frame the same way at JHN.11.6, JHN.1.18, JHN.5.37 and JHN.11.39, the last having considered and rejected the determiner. This is narrower than adverb-phrase renderings in general, where the corpus is divided and the modifier takes the head when it is itself a gloss of the Greek: kalos -> in good way (v17) stays on good.
