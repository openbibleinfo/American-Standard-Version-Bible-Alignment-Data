# 1 Samuel 26 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- מְרַאֲשֹׁתָיו (v7, v11, v12, v16) is a bare adverbial noun with no preposition on it: it takes the ASV's supplied at or from under rule 33 and the suffix takes his; where it is the predicate of a relative clause (v11 that is at his head, v16 that was at his head) the construction's copula joins the noun's unit under rule 20.
- בַּמַּעְגָּל (v5, v7) → within the place of the wagons: the assimilated article takes the first the, and place of the wagons is one unit on מַעְגָּל headed on wagons under rule 31, the second the and the of being the ASV's own expansion of the single substantive rather than a construct relation.
- The oath חַי־יְהוָה כִּי (v16) and חַי־יְהוָה כִּי אִם (v10): the adjective takes As ... liveth (rule 55), the name its own word, and the oath particles כִּי and אִם are each unrepresented, the ASV writing nothing for them; the v10 margin Or, either was read and set aside.
