# Isaiah 59 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Privative min + infinitive construct recast as a negative result clause: the preposition takes only the English realizing its own sense — the complementizer and the negation, including a cannot that fuses that negation with the modal — while the supplied subject pronoun and any separable modal go with the infinitive. Settled for vv. 1 and 2 alike; the corpus agrees (1KI.21.3 min -> that beside should give, 1KI.2.1 he should die, PSA.119.4 we should observe).
- A verbless clause with a prepositional predicate takes its supplied copula on the preposition at high confidence, not medium: 103 corpus units across 39 chapters read it that way and none the other. Applied to vv. 6, 7, 12, 21.
- The existential ayin heads on the negation word and not on the supplied there, at high confidence: all 28 corpus occurrences head on no, none, not, neither or nothing. Applied to vv. 8, 15, 16.
