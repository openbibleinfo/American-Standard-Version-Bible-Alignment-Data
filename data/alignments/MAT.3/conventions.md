# Matthew 3 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- MAT.3.1-2: the ASV places 'saying' at the end of 3:1, so λέγων (MAT.3.2 s002) is its own unrepresented unit under rule 37 and the target 'saying' (MAT.3.1 t015) is added, each carrying a note naming the other verse. Do not stretch 'saying' onto κηρύσσων in 3:1, and do not stretch λέγων onto Μετανοεῖτε in 3:2. A reference comparison reports λέγων as a disagreement and that report is not a correction.
- An English phrasal-verb particle the ASV writes with its verb rides inside that verb's own unit and is never added, and the head stays on the verb word, whether or not the particle answers to a Greek preverb. ἐξεπορεύετο → 'went out' (3.5), ἀνέβη → 'went up' (3.16) and διακαθαριεῖ → 'thoroughly cleanse' (3.12) realize ἐκ-, ἀνα- and δια- under rule 106; ποιήσατε → 'Bring forth' (3.8), ποιοῦν → 'that bringeth not forth' (3.10), ἐγεῖραι → 'to raise up' (3.9), ἐκκόπτεται → 'is hewn down' (3.10) and κατακαύσει → 'he will burn up' (3.12) answer to no preverb and are folded under rules 7 and 27 with the head fixed by rule 17. No expressed Greek preposition competes for any of them, so rule 106's preposition arm is not in question; ἀπό at 3.16 takes its own 'from' beside the participle's 'up', as that rule's own ἀναβαίνων ἐκ τοῦ ὕδατος example has it.
