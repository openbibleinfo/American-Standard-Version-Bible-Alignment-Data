# John 1 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Where the ASV renders both emprosthen mou and protos mou as *before me* in the same clause pair (1:15, 1:30), align each collocation separately and do not read either as a second realization of the other; the ASV's own gloss (Greek first in regard of me) is what fixes protos as the temporal-priority word.
- ek rendered by the two-word *out of* (1:32, 1:46) is one aligned unit headed on *out*: the *of* belongs to the preposition's own English expression and is not a case marker to be split onto the governed noun.
