# Proverbs 3 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- A single English Neither or Nor is read off what stands in the colon it opens. Where a waw and a prohibitive both stand there, the one word is their rule 35 fusion, one unit headed on the negative (PRO.3.11). Where the negative is gapped in that colon and only a waw stands, the word goes to the waw alone under rule 25's carve-out, and the earlier colon's al is not distributed into it (PRO.3.25).
- A supplied English relative over a finite clause the Hebrew juxtaposes to a written antecedent is added: PRO.3.13 marks two thats, separate units because rendered material stands between them, and PRO.3.12 marks whom. A relative or antecedent the ASV writes inside its recasting of a single Hebrew nominal, or one licensed by an article or by an anarthrous substantive, joins that morpheme's unit and is never added: PRO.3.27 be'alav -> them to whom it is due, PRO.3.15 hafatzeykha -> the things thou canst desire, PRO.3.18 them that (article, rule 41) and every one that (anarthrous participle, rule 46).
- A supplied English preposition before a bare nominal is placed by what governs that nominal in the Hebrew, and the chapter runs the test both ways. An accusative of material completing a passive or recast-intransitive predicate gives the word to the verb (PRO.3.10 shall be filled with plenty, shall overflow with new wine); a nominal whose own case-function or recast contribution the word states gives it to the nominal (PRO.3.23 in thy way, PRO.3.35 of fools).
- The adverbial phrase lamed plus betach is one fused unit under rules 30 and 40, headed on the noun, since the ASV writes securely for the pair and no word for the preposition apart from it (PRO.3.23, PRO.3.29). The preposition is not marked unrepresented beside a noun holding the whole word.
