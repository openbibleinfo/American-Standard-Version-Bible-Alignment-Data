# Acts 24 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Versification: the ASV renders the opening of ACT.24.19 (τινὲς δὲ ἀπὸ τῆς Ἀσίας Ἰουδαῖοι) at the end of ACT.24.18. Under rule 37 the English 'but there were certain Jews from Asia' is one `added` unit in 24.18 and s001-s006 of 24.19 are their own `unrepresented` units, each carrying a note naming the other verse. Neither verse reaches across, and a reference comparison reports all six 24.19 tokens as disagreements; that report is not a correction.
- Where the ASV compresses a substantival τά plus a preposition plus a pronoun into an English possessive, as rule 36's τὰ περὶ ἡμῶν → 'our state' has it, this chapter empties the preposition and gives the possessive to the pronoun in both instances - τὰ περὶ ἐμαυτοῦ → 'my defence' at 24.10 and τὰ καθ' ὑμᾶς → 'your matter' at 24.22 - and decides the article by rule 103's licensing test rather than by the construction. At 24.22 the article takes the supplied nominal 'matter', no other token standing to claim it; at 24.10 it is `unrepresented` too, because 'defence' is what glosses ἀπολογέομαι under rule 91 and is already spent on the verb.
- Where the ASV converts a Greek relative clause into a coordinate one, the supplied juncture is folded onto the relative pronoun's own unit and is not `added`: οἵτινες ἐνεφάνισαν → 'and they informed' at 24.1 gives 'and they' to οἵτινες. Rule 86's test is what licenses it, the relative pronoun being the source token that carries the subordination the ASV converted, and rule 88 places the word on the clause it introduces, as the chapter's participle-to-finite junctures at 24.23, 24.24, 24.25 and 24.26 are placed. Reading the juncture `added` under rule 86 remains defensible and the unit's note records that.
