# Numbers 14 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **NUM.14.42** — Where לִפְנֵי is rendered by the single English preposition ‘before,’ the preposition and construct noun form one noncompositional unit; a following suffix or nominal complement remains separate.
- **NUM.14.21** — In the oath formula ‘as I live’ (and its parallel clause), English ‘as’ is added when the Hebrew clause has no subordinating conjunction; any waw separately takes its written ‘and.’
