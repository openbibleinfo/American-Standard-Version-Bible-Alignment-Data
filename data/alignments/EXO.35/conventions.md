# Exodus 35 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Construct kol takes only its own quantifier word (every, all, any); a noun or pronoun the ASV supplies as the phrase's antecedent joins the substantive kol governs, under rule 46 - kol-hakham-lev gives 'wise ... man' to the adjective (35.10), kol-'ish gives 'one' to the noun (35.21), kol-merim gives 'one that did offer' to the participle (35.24). Absolute kol has no substantive under it and so takes the supplied noun phrase itself: 'every one' (35.21), 'as many as' (35.22), 'every man' (35.24). A following 'asher clause does not make it construct. The fixed quantifier phrase 'all manner of' is kol's own rendering under rule 31, since the noun it governs keeps its own English word (35.31, 35.33, 35.35).
- tola'at shani is rendered by the single word 'scarlet' and fuses under rule 30, headed on shani, which carries the lexeme (35.6, 35.23, 35.25, 35.35); an article standing inside the chain is read separately under rule 29 and takes a written 'the' (35.25) or goes unrepresented where none is written (35.35). The compounds 'orot tehashim to 'sealskins' (35.7, 35.23) and lehem happanim to 'showbread' (35.13) fuse on the same terms, headed on the construct noun whose sense the English compound spells out, with the intervening article again read separately.
- 'asher together with the possessive suffix on the following subject noun is rendered by the single word 'whose' and is one fused unit under rules 30 and 35, headed on the relative, the unit standing across the intervening words (35.21, 35.26, 35.29). Where the ASV instead writes a preposition and 'whom' - 'with whom' for 'asher ... 'itto - the preposition takes its own word under rule 11 and the resumptive suffix is unrepresented under rule 22 (35.23, 35.24).
- Multi-morpheme fusions under rule 30 are recorded here with status aligned rather than noncompositional, throughout the chapter (whosoever at 35.2, throughout at 35.3, scarlet, sealskins, showbread, whose, 'asher-lo to 'its' at 35.16, beyad to 'by' at 35.29). The corpus does both - it records waw plus lo' as neither in 106 noncompositional units and 103 aligned ones - so this is a chapter-level choice and not a reading of the rule.
- A correlative 'both ... and' belongs to the morpheme that does the joining, which need not be a waw. At 35.22 no waw stands in the clause and the preposition 'al joins the two nouns, so it takes the pair and heads on the closing 'and' (rule 11); at 35.34 a waw is present and takes it under rule 14, the explicit pronoun hu' keeping only its own 'he'.
