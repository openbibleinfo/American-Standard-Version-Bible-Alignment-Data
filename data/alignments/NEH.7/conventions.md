# Nehemiah 7 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- A construct chain whose ASV rendering carries a single *the* at the phrase's left edge gives that *the* to an article morpheme present anywhere in the chain, including one standing on a second, coordinated nomen rectum that the ASV renders bare: `אַנְשֵׁי בֵית אֵל וְהָעָי` → *The men of Beth el and Ai* (v.32) puts the *The* on הָעָי's article and leaves אַנְשֵׁי with *men* alone, exactly as `אַנְשֵׁי הָרָמָה וָגָבַע` → *The men of Ramah and Geba* does in v.30. Supply a *the* on the construct only where no article morpheme stands in the chain at all (vv.33-34 `אַנְשֵׁי נְבוֹ אַחֵר`, v.39 `בֵית יֵשׁוּעַ`, v.60 `בְנֵי עַבְדֵי שְׁלֹמֹה`), and mark an article `unrepresented` only where the ASV writes no *the* for the phrase at all (v.2 `אֶת הָאֱלֹהִים` → *God*, v.65 `הַכֹּהֵן` → *a priest*).
- רִבּוֹא is fused or split by how many English words the ASV spends on it, not by the construction. `אַרְבַּע רִבּוֹא` → *forty* (v.66) meets rule 30's one-English-word condition and is one unit headed on the counting numeral; `שְׁתֵּי רִבּוֹא`/`רִבּוֹת` → *twenty thousand* (vv.71, 72) does not, so rule 2 splits it, the counting numeral taking *twenty* and the multiplier noun taking *thousand*.
- In a verbless enumeration clause the supplied copula joins the numeral the English copula stands before, not a head noun standing later in the predicate: *were three hundred* (v.60), *there were seven thousand* (v.67), *were seven hundred* (v.68), and *was twenty thousand darics of gold* (v.72), where *was* goes to שְׁתֵּי and דַּרְכְּמוֹנִים keeps *darics*.
- An ASV italic joins the unit of the morpheme whose sense it spells out, and is `added` only where no morpheme carries that sense. *houses* in `רָאשֵׁי הָאָבוֹת` → *the heads of fathers houses* (vv.70, 71) joins הָאָבוֹת, which in this idiom names the ancestral houses, and heads on *fathers* by rule 31; *their* before the absolute `גְּמַלִּים` and `חֲמֹרִים` (v.69) answers to no suffix and is `added`, as is the *and* of a numeral figure that holds no waw (vv.11, 24, 42, 66) — v.24's is not italicised, and that is not evidence against it (rule 7).
