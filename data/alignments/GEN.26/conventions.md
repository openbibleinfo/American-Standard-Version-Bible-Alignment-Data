# Genesis 26 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **GEN.26.2** — Directional phrasal verbs head on the English verb, not the particle; thus ‘Go down’ heads on ‘Go,’ consistently with ‘went up’ and ‘sent away.’
- **GEN.26.33** — In verbless naming clauses, an explicit name-subject takes only its nominal rendering and the supplied copula belongs to the lexical name-predicate.
- **GEN.26.34** — When a temporal recasting has two Hebrew waws and ASV ‘And … when,’ the clause-linking waw licenses the fronted ‘when’ rather than leaving it added.
