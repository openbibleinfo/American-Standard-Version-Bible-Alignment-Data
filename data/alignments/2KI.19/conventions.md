# 2 Kings 19 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Where a construct chain is rendered attributively under rule 28 and the ASV writes one the for the phrase with no article morpheme standing in it, that the goes to the construct noun, whose own English word it stands before, and the rectum keeps only its own word: קוֹמַת אֲרָזָיו -> the tall cedars thereof, מִבְחוֹר בְּרֹשָׁיו -> the choice fir trees thereof (19.23), יְרַק דֶּשֶׁא -> as the green herb (19.26), בְּתוּלַת בַּת־צִיּוֹן -> The virgin daughter of Zion (19.21). Rule 15's direction to read the noun of the expression rather than its leftmost word governs a numeral and the noun it counts and does not reach a chain, whose determiners rule 29 counts. 19.23's first chain was corrected in reconciliation; do not read the rectum's position as the English head noun as grounds to move the determiner back.
- הַמֶּלֶךְ חִזְקִיָּהוּ is read by the the the ASV writes and not by the phrase it stands in. At 19.1 the ASV writes king Hezekiah with no the and the article is unrepresented under rule 29's last sentence; at 19.5, inside עַבְדֵי הַמֶּלֶךְ חִזְקִיָּהוּ -> the servants of king Hezekiah, the rectum is rendered anarthrously and the phrase's single the is fronted to the left edge, so the article takes it and the construct עַבְדֵי stays bare. The two verses are not a split, and a supplied the on the construct is permitted only where no article morpheme is present.
- לְבַדְּךָ is counted verse by verse against the pronouns the ASV writes. At 19.15 -> thou art the God even thou alone, two thou's answer to two morphemes: the explicit אַתָּה takes the first and the suffix the second, while לְ + בַד fuse into alone. At 19.19 -> thou Jehovah art God alone, the one thou is the explicit אַתָּה's, nothing is left for the suffix, and it is unrepresented; rule 30 excludes a suffix on the construct noun from the fusion. BSB represents that suffix, which is the documented difference.
- A serial auxiliary verb the ASV renders adverbially takes the adverb, and the verb it governs keeps its own English: שׁוּב at 19.9 (וַיָּשָׁב וַיִּשְׁלַח מַלְאָכִים -> he sent messengers again) and יָסַף at 19.30 (וְיָסְפָה ... שֹׁרֶשׁ -> shall again take root) each take again, with the auxiliary carrying the finite frame at 19.30. Do not fold such an again into the following verb as a phrasal particle under rule 44, and do not mark it added: the auxiliary verb is the morpheme it renders. At 19.9 the ASV writes no connective for either waw of that clause, and both are unrepresented under rule 14.
