# Psalms 42 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **PSA.42.3** — כָּל־הַיּוֹם → continually (v.3, v.10): the single adverb carries quantifier, article and noun, so all three morphemes are one aligned unit under rule 30 (as כָּל־הַיָּמִים → for ever), headed on the governing construct כֹּל under rule 23; יוֹם is the competing head and is named in the notes.
- **PSA.42.5** — Refrain (v.5, v.11): the appositive יְשׁוּעוֹת פָּנָיו is recast two ways by the ASV and the chapter follows each. v.5 'For the help of his countenance' places the italic For on the bare nominal under rule 33; v.11 'Who is the help of my countenance' leaves Who is as one added unit under rules 2 and 55 (no relative and no verbless clause stands in the Hebrew; the phrase is in apposition to the suffix him). This is a licensed difference, not a split. v.5's italic second why is מַה's distributed realization under rule 25, while v.11 writes a second מַה of its own.
