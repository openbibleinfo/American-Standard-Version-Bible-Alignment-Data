# Song of Solomon 2 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The comparative lamed after damah is `unrepresented`: the ASV spends its one word on the verb's own lexeme, as `domeh` -> *is like* and `demeh` -> *be thou like*, so both prepositions of `litzvi o le'ofer` go unrendered, at SNG.2.9 and SNG.2.17 alike. The competing reading gives *like* to the first preposition and only the copula to the verb; it is named in each unit's note and was not taken, because dropping *like* leaves the verb with no rendering at all.
- An ethical dative lamed with its suffix -- lakh, lo, lekha -- is rendered by nothing in this chapter, and both morphemes are their own `unrepresented` units: SNG.2.10 twice, SNG.2.11, SNG.2.13 twice, and SNG.2.17. The *up* of *Rise up*, the *away* of *come away* and the *gone* of `halakh lo` -> *is over and gone* are the English verbs' own words under rule 44 and are not the dative's rendering.
- `hinneh-zeh` is split rather than fused: the particle takes *behold* and the demonstrative `zeh` takes the English subject pronoun *he*, at SNG.2.8 and SNG.2.9, because the ASV writes a word for each. Where no demonstrative stands beside a participle, the supplied subject pronoun joins the verb's unit under rule 17 instead, as it does for both later participles of SNG.2.9.
- In the comparative `ke- X ... ken Y` the fronted preposition takes only *As*, and the verbless clause's one construction-supplied copula joins the correlative `ken`, giving *So is*, at SNG.2.2 and SNG.2.3. The competing reading places that copula on the fronted comparative phrase; it is named in both units' notes and was not taken, because the ASV writes the copula beside the correlative.
- `semadar` as the predicate of a verbless clause takes the ASV's whole *are in blossom* -- the construction-supplied copula and the supplied *in* alike -- headed on *blossom*, at SNG.2.13 and SNG.2.15. The supplied preposition is that predicate nominal's own under rule 33 and is not `added`.
- A relative the ASV supplies to recast a modifying expression stays with the expression it recasts, while a coordinator the ASV supplies between juxtaposed clauses is `added`. `bechagvei hassela` -> *that art in the clefts of the rock* keeps *that art* with the preposition heading the prepositional predicate at SNG.2.14, and `mechabbelim` -> *That spoil* keeps *That* with the articleless attributive participle at SNG.2.15 under rule 41; the *and* of `chalaf halakh` -> *is over and gone* at SNG.2.11 and of `sov demeh` -> *Turn ... and be thou like* at SNG.2.17 is `added` under rule 14. The 2.14 extension of rule 41's reasoning from an attributive to a prepositional predicate rests on this one occurrence and is recorded here rather than promoted.
