# 1 Chronicles 29 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- A possessive לְ with a pronominal suffix that the ASV renders by a single English possessive pronoun is rule 49's fused genitive and not a split pair: לְךָ → *Thine* (29.11, twice) and *thine own* (29.16) put the preposition and the suffix in one unit, headed on the suffix and on the English pronoun, since no English word is spent on either morpheme alone. The clause's construction-supplied copula joins that fused unit, exactly as it joins לַיהוָה → *is Jehovah's*, and an intensifying *own* belongs to it under rule 18. The vocative and the predicate nominal standing beside it keep only their own English.
