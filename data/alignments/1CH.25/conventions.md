# 1 Chronicles 25 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The chapter's teen numerals — `שְׁנֵים עָשָׂר` → *twelve* and its fellows at 25.5 and 25.18-19 — are each one `aligned` unit of the two numeral morphemes under rule 30, headed on the governing first morpheme under rule 23, and never `noncompositional`. The corpus writes both treatments for this very string (24 `aligned` against 15 `noncompositional`), so the choice is recorded rather than left to be rediscovered; all 24 of the chapter's teen numerals follow it.
- In the lot roster the numeral stands substantivally with no noun to determine, so a supplied *the* joins the numeral's own unit under rules 15 and 31 (vv. 18, 20-27), and where the numeral is compound it joins the unit of the numeral the *the* stands before — *the one and twentieth* gives *the one* to `אֶחָד` and *twentieth* to `עֶשְׂרִים`, the waw taking the *and* under rule 42 (vv. 28-31). Verse 19 is the exception the source licenses: there an article morpheme stands on the numeral and takes the written *the* under rule 47, a present morpheme taking its English before a supplied reading does.
- The compound name `רֹמַמְתִּי עֶזֶר` is aligned by what each verse's ASV text writes, not by the source pair. At 25.4 the ASV transliterates the two tokens as *Romamti* and *ezer*, so each morpheme has a rendering of its own, rule 30's one-rendering condition is unmet and rule 2 splits them; at 25.31 it writes the single word *Romamtiezer*, which writes both lexemes, so the two share one unit headed on the first token under rule 23. Rule 6's fallback anchor arises in neither verse.
