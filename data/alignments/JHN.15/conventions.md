# John 15 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **JHN.15.16** — Where one ἵνα governs coordinated subjunctives and the ASV repeats 'that' before the second, the repeated marker is an ordinary helper on that verb at high confidence, not 'added' (rules 7, 11, 40): JHN.15.11 and JHN.15.16 are the same construction.
