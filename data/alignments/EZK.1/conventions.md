# Ezekiel 1 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- כְּעֵין is one unit wherever the ASV spends no separate English word on עַיִן — *as it were* (1.4, 1.27), *like* (1.7), *like unto* with the verbless clause's copula (1.16) — headed on the lexeme-bearing עַיִן. It is split only at 1.22, where the ASV writes *to look upon* for the noun and *like* for the preposition, so rule 30's one-rendering condition is unmet and rule 2 divides it.
- The chapter's verbless possessive clauses are read three ways, by what stands in the Hebrew and what the ASV wrote. Where a possessive לְ is present and the ASV writes *had*, the לְ carries it and the suffix takes the subject pronoun (1.6, 1.8, 1.10, 1.16, 1.18, 1.23). Where the לְ is present but the ASV recasts the predicate — *a brightness round about it*, *the fire was bright*, *they were high*, *there was brightness* — the לְ is `unrepresented` under rule 40 and its suffix still takes the pronoun (1.4, 1.13, 1.18, 1.27). Where the clause holds no possessive morpheme at all, the supplied *they had* is `added` (1.8, 1.10); this is what distinguishes the first four-faces clause from the three that follow it.
- Where the ASV recasts a pair of waw-consecutives as a temporal clause plus its apodosis — *Now as I beheld ... behold* (1.15), *And when I saw it, I fell* (1.28) — the opening waw takes the coordinator written for it (*Now*, *And*), the supplied subordinator (*as*, *when*) is `added` under rules 42 and 55, and the second waw is `unrepresented` because the ASV writes no connective for the clause it opens. The corpus elsewhere aligns a single waw to *and when*; rule 55's counting rule was followed here in preference to that precedent, and both verses were decided the same way.
- The resumptive שָׁם/שָׁמָּה inside the אֶל/עַל אֲשֶׁר relative is `unrepresented`, the fused *whither* (1.12) and *Whithersoever* (1.20) carrying the direction once under rule 22; both fusions head on the relative. The main-clause שָׁמָּה of 1.20 is a different token and keeps its own *thither* with its directive he under rule 32.
- The four-faces and four-wings formula לְאַרְבַּעְתָּם/לְאַרְבַּעְתָּן is partitioned the same way at 1.8, 1.10, 1.16 and 1.18: לְ takes *had*, the numeral construct takes *four*, and the suffix takes *they*, discontinuous where the ASV reorders them. At 1.15 the same numeral construct in לְאַרְבַּעַת פָּנָיו instead takes *each of the four*, the ASV's *of* there being the distributive's partitive rather than rule 19's construct relation, which stays with פָּנָיו.
