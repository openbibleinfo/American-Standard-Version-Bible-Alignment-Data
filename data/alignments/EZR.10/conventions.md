# Ezra 10 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **EZR.10.1** — In בֵית הָאֱלֹהִים, the article on God owns the single fronted English 'the'; the construct keeps house and any separately licensed relation.
- **EZR.10.4** — Wholly supplied lexical predicates in verbless expansions are added: belongeth in verse 4 and be despatched in verse 14; a source preposition keeps only an independently realized relation or is unrepresented.
