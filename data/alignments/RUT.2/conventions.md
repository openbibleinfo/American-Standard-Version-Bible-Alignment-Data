# Ruth 2 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- `נָכַר` hiphil with a bare object suffix is rendered *take knowledge of X* at RUT.2.10 and RUT.2.19. In both the supplied *of* is the preposition the English light-verb phrase needs to reach that suffix and joins the verb's unit under rule 44, the suffix keeping only its pronoun and the unit heading on *knowledge*. Rule 19's *of + pronoun* suffix-rectum sentence governs a relation between two nominals, as `כֻּלָּם` → *all of them* is, and does not reach an *of* an English verb's valency licenses; the state MACULA tags the governing word in — infinitive construct at v.10, participle in construct state at v.19 — is not what decides it.
- In the first RUT.2.23 harvest phrase, `עַד־כְּלוֹת קְצִיר־הַשְּׂעֹרִים` → *unto the end of barley harvest*, the article on barley takes the chain's sole *the* under current rule 29. The nominally rendered infinitive retains *end*, harvest retains the discontinuous *of harvest*, and barley retains its attributive rendering. Both independent scoped judgments supersede the former supplied-*the* treatment on *end*: the infinitive's nominal rendering does not bypass the present article, and the multiple-*the* qualification does not apply to the sole written determiner. This amendment covers only that first phrase; the second harvest phrase is not reopened, and the original convention is preserved in the deferred-investigation audit.
- At both RUT.2.19 occurrences, `הַיּוֹם` → *to day* is one aligned article-plus-noun temporal unit, headed on the noun and *day*. Both independent scoped judgments apply current rule 30's expressly named current-day rendering to each occurrence. This supersedes the former optional article-exclusion analysis under rules 29 and 33 for these two occurrences only; the original convention is preserved in the deferred-investigation audit.
