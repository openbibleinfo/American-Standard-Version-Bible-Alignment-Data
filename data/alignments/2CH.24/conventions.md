# 2 Chronicles 24 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Periphrastic הָיָה + participle (וַיִּהְיוּ שֹׂכְרִים 24.12 → they hired; וַיִּהְיוּ מַעֲלִים 24.14 → they offered): the finite הָיָה takes the English subject pronoun and any auxiliary the ASV writes, the participle takes the lexical verb, and the pair is not fused. Alternative considered and rejected: fusing both morphemes into one unit under rule 30.
- Hiphil בּוֹא rendered bring in / brought in (24.6, 24.9, 24.10) keeps the particle in with the verb's unit, headed on bring/brought, on the ground that coming-in is the lexeme of בּוֹא and the particle restates it rather than supplying a direction the Hebrew never wrote (rule 44's first phrasal-particle sentence, not its bring-back surplus case). The alternative, marking in added, was considered in each verse and rejected uniformly.
