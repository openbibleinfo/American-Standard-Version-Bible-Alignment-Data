# Acts 1 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Periphrastic εἰμί + participle is decided by the count of English verb words the ASV wrote. Where it writes an auxiliary alongside the participle the pair splits, the auxiliary and any supplied subject going to εἰμί and the participle keeping its own word: ACT.1.10 ἦσαν ἀτενίζοντες → *they were* and *looking stedfastly*, ACT.1.13 ἦσαν καταμένοντες → *they were* and *abiding*, ACT.1.17 ἦν κατηριθμημένος → *he was* and *numbered*. Where it collapses the periphrasis into a single finite verb, the two tokens are one `aligned` unit headed on that verb: ACT.1.14 ἦσαν προσκαρτεροῦντες → *continued stedfastly*, no English word standing for the auxiliary and the past tense of *continued* carrying what ἦσαν contributes. That is rule 39's test and rule 94's count, one analysis and not two. Leaving εἰμί `unrepresented` in the collapsed case remains defensible and is named in the ACT.1.14 unit's note; the corpus was not queried for settled practice on collapsed periphrasis, so a later chapter meeting this shape should check it rather than treat ACT.1.14 as precedent.
- δεῖ at ACT.1.21 s001 is `unrepresented` and the *must* that renders it is `added` at ACT.1.22 t019: the ASV places the English of the verse-21 verb in verse 22 (rule 37). Both ends carry the matching note and neither reaches across, so do not stretch *must* back into verse 21 and do not spread a verse-21 verb phrase over the orphaned token. The BSB comparison reports δεῖ as a disagreement, and that report is not a correction.
