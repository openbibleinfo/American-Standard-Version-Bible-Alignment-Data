# Luke 8 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The Lukan narrative frame 'egeneto de ... kai': the 'kai' that introduces the main clause is realized by the ASV's complementizer 'that' and is aligned to it (8.1, 8.22), while the 'egeneto' unit keeps 'it came to pass' whole and heads on 'came'. The participial equivalent 'gegonos' keeps 'had come to pass' whole on the same head (8.34, 8.35).
- Rule 94's precondition - a compound unit only where the ASV wrote a single English word - was applied to any adjacent source pair, not only to a preposition and the word it governs. 'en mesoi' to 'amidst' (8.7) and 'pollois chronois' to 'oftentimes' (8.29) are each one aligned unit under rule 39's test; 'en toi kathexes' to 'soon afterwards' (8.1) is not joined, the preposition and the nominalizing article each standing as their own unrepresented unit and the adverb taking both English words.
- Supplied English lexical material that completes the English expression of a single source word is folded onto that word as a rule 7 helper rather than marked added: 'with water' on 'syneplerounto' (8.23), 'of age' on 'eton' (8.42), 'house' on 'archisynagogou' (8.49). Rule 103's added is scoped to material whose source token has already spent its English elsewhere in the verse, and none of these has; the added status is reserved here for the rule 97 case at 8.45, where no source token exists in the projection at all.
- Supplied copula in a verbless clause: it goes to the subject where the subject is a pronoun or a substantival article and the predicate a full nominal (8.13, 'those ... are they who'), and to the predicate where the predicate is an adverb or where the ASV's English is a cleft (8.25 'Where is your faith', 8.45 'Who is it that touched me'). The cleft copula belongs to the cleft frame on the interrogative, not to the expletive 'it' the article supplies under rule 32; both verses carry the competing reading in a note.
