# Proverbs 5 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- פָּלַס is rendered in this chapter by an English light verb plus the complement *level* — v.6 *she findeth ... not the level path*, v.21 *he maketh level all his paths*. In both, *level* is the word carrying the root's lexeme: it stays inside the verb's unit rather than going to the noun standing beside it or being marked added, and it is the target head. The v.6 colon is the harder of the two, because *level* there stands as an attributive on *path* and the ASV's own verb *findeth* renders nothing of פלס; v.21, where the same root is written as a plain predicate, is what settles it.
