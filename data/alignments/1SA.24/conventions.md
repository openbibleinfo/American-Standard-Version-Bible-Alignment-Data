# 1 Samuel 24 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- אֲשֶׁר rendered by the compound complementizer *how that* (אֵת אֲשֶׁר after a verb of seeing or declaring, v10 and v18) is one unit on the relative headed on *how*; *that* is the competing head and is named in each note.
- Where יָצָא is rendered *went out* or *is come out* and a מִן follows, the *out* is the verb's lexeme-completing particle under rule 44 and the מִן takes only *of* (v8); where the verb already writes its whole lexeme — קוּם → *rose up*, יָצָא → *cometh forth* — the מִן takes *out of* whole, headed on *out* (v7, v13, v15, v21).
- אֲשֶׁר לְ + proper name rendered as a single English genitive (*Saul's*, v4 and v5) is one three-morpheme unit headed on the name under rules 29 and 49; the article on the noun the possessive stands before is `unrepresented` (v4).
