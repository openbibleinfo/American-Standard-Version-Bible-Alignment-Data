# Psalms 113 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The short divine name יָהּ (Np) in the Hallelujah formula (vv. 1, 9) is its own unit aligned to 'Jehovah'; the ASV margin 'Hebrew Hallelujah' is read and set aside (rule 38), and the imperative keeps 'Praise ye' as in the long-form 'Praise ye Jehovah'.
- Verses 7 and 9 open with a bare Hiphil participle (מְקִימִי, מוֹשִׁיבִי) whose subject is the יְהוָה of v. 5, outside the verse. The ASV's supplied 'He' is added in both (rules 10, 20, 25; 1SA.2.8 precedent for this very phrase); the participle takes only its own lexical verb and any phrasal particle or supplied preposition rule 44 gives it. The ASV's 'And' between the two cola of vv. 4, 7 and 9 answers to no waw and is added on the same terms (rule 14).
