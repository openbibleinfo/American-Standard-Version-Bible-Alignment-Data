# Ezekiel 7 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Every one-English-word fusion in this chapter is recorded `aligned`, never `noncompositional`: waw+lo -> *neither* (7.4, 7.9, 7.13, 7.19) and -> *nor* (7.11, twice), waw+al -> *nor* (7.12), min+qarob -> *shortly* (7.8), be+chuts -> *without* and min+bayit -> *within* (7.15), be+suffix -> *therein* and al-ken -> *therefore* (7.20). Rules 6, 30 and 35 do not settle the status, and the corpus splits almost evenly on the identical constructions (waw+lo -> *neither*: 88 aligned against 98 noncompositional; -> *nor*: 126 against 54; be+suffix -> *therein*: 27 against 24), so the chapter chose one and held it throughout. Heads follow rule 35: the negative for a fused negative, otherwise the morpheme carrying the lexeme.
- The frozen first-person suffix of adonai is `unrepresented` (7.2 s008, 7.5 s004): the ASV writes *the Lord Jehovah* with no pronoun answering to it, and the supplied *the* belongs to adonai's own unit under rule 15 rather than to the suffix. BSB counts this suffix represented in both verses; that is a convention difference and not a correction.
- ASV italics are placed in this chapter by asking what the italicized word completes, not by rule 7 alone. An italic that completes the English phrase rendering one substantive joins that substantive's unit and is not added: *a day of* tumult and *of* joyful shouting (7.7 s015, s018) and secret *place* (7.22 s010), which is how the unitalicized *an unclean thing* (7.19 s011, 7.20 s025), *detestable things* (7.20 s013) and *holy places* (7.24 s016) are placed too. An italic that instead supplies a predicate for a clause whose Hebrew has no verb (*shall remain*, 7.11, rule 20) or a connective standing between two nominals the Hebrew joins asyndetically (*and*, 7.20, rule 42) answers to no morpheme and is `added`. These are the chapter's only two added units.
