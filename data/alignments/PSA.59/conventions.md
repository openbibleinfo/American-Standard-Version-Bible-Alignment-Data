# Psalms 59 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **PSA.59.6** — Refrain (vv.6, 14): the identical Hebrew is aligned identically morpheme by morpheme; the jussive frame 'let them' the ASV writes in v.14 belongs to each verb under rule 17 and nothing else moves. לָעֶרֶב → 'at evening' leaves the assimilated article unrepresented and gives 'at' to the לְ, while לַבֹּקֶר → 'in the morning' (v.16) gives the assimilated article the written 'the'. יְסוֹבְבוּ → 'go round about' heads on round (competing head go).
- **PSA.59.9** — מִשְׂגָּב → 'high tower' heads on high throughout the chapter (vv.9, 16, 17), high carrying the שׂגב lexeme under rule 31; the competing head is tower. Where it is the predicate of a verbless clause (vv.9, 17) the copula joins that unit under rule 20 and the 1cs suffix takes only 'my'; where it is the complement of הָיִיתָ with לִי (v.16) the לְ is unrepresented under rule 40 and the suffix takes 'my'.
