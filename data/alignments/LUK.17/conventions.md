# Luke 17 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- LUK.17 carries three prepositional phrases the ASV renders by a single English word, and divides them by rule 93's test on the governed word's own corpus gloss rather than by the shape of the Greek. εἰς τὰ ὀπίσω → *back* at 17:31 takes the pleonastic arm: ὀπίσω is glossed *back* by itself in five corpus units (JHN.20.14, JHN.6.66, LUK.9.62, MAT.24.18), so the adverb takes the word alone and εἰς and τά are each their own unrepresented unit. ἐπὶ τὸ αὐτό → *together* at 17:35 and μετὰ ταῦτα → *afterward* at 17:8 are joined as one noncompositional unit under rule 45, neither αὐτό nor ταῦτα spelling those words alone, with the intervening article of 17:35 unrepresented under rule 93. JHN.18.6's εἰς ὀπίσω → *backward* is not counter-precedent: there the ASV wrote a *-ward* for the preposition and here it wrote none. The BSB report of εἰς and τά at 17:31 as represented follows from this choice and is not a correction.
