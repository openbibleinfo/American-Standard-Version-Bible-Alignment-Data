# Romans 15 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- An expressed accusative subject of an infinitive is its own aligned unit on the English subject word, standing outside the infinitive's own unit: Christon -> Christ (15.8), hymas -> ye (15.13), me -> I (15.16, 15.19). Rule 74's fold is scoped in terms to a subject Greek leaves unexpressed, and this chapter writes none, so do not read the English nominative form as evidence that the accusative belongs inside the verb's unit.
- A supplied demonstrative determiner and a supplied generic nominal go to the article where one stands and to the noun where the Greek is anarthrous, the modifier staying bare in both: ta polla -> these ... times on the article with a bare 'many' (15.22, rules 60, 102), against apo pollon eton -> these ... years on the noun with a bare 'many' (15.23, rules 51, 58). Where the article takes the nominal it also takes whatever English preposition the ASV writes in front of the phrase, headed on the nominal: 'of things' and 'things' at 15.27, and 'in things' at 15.17, where the article is an adverbial accusative whose own case no English preposition realizes under rule 106. That last placement is the chapter's one open call and its unit note names the competing reading, which puts the 'in' on pros.
