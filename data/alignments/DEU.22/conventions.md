# Deuteronomy 22 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- A subordinator the Hebrew writes once and the ASV rewrites at the head of a coordinated clause is that morpheme's own second realization, and the waw opening that clause takes only the coordinator written for it: 'im takes both 'if's at DEU.22.2 and lema'an both 'that's at DEU.22.7, while their waws keep 'or' and 'and'. Rule 55's coordinator-and-subordinator pair is read only where no written subordinator's scope reaches the clause, as at DEU.22.14, where wa-eqrav rendered 'and when I came nigh' stands under no ki or 'im.
- The consequent wa-met / wa-metah of the execution formula is rendered by the resultative 'to death' at DEU.22.21 and DEU.22.24: that verb takes 'to death' and its own waw is unrepresented under rule 14, while the stoning verb keeps only its subject, auxiliary and lexical verb. Where the ASV instead writes a finite clause for the same verb, as at DEU.22.22 'then they shall die', the waw takes the 'then' and the verb its subject and predicate.
- sim 'alilot devarim is rendered by the English idiom 'lay ... to charge', and 'to ... charge' belongs to the verb's own discontinuous unit under rule 25. Where a dative le- stands in the clause it takes the 'to' and its suffix the pronoun (DEU.22.14); where none stands, the verb holds 'to ... charge' whole and the ASV's italic pronoun answers to no morpheme and is added under rule 44 (DEU.22.17). The ASV italicizes all three words in the second case, which by rule 7 corroborates nothing about where the division falls.
- betulim is rendered 'the tokens of virginity' throughout the chapter (DEU.22.14, 15, 17 twice, 20). 'the tokens of' is the supplied completion under rule 31 and the unit heads on 'virginity'; where the word stands in construct, the following na'arah or bat keeps the chain's own 'of' under rule 19, so the completion's own 'of' is not written twice.
