# Psalms 122 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- לְמַעַן takes the whole English compound the ASV writes for it — 'For ... sakes' (v8, discontinuous around its object) and 'For the sake of' (v9) — headed on sake(s) under rule 24; the 'of' closing 'For the sake of' is the compound's own and never the following construct noun's, which keeps only its supplied 'the' (rule 29) while its rectum takes its own 'of' (rule 19).
