# Numbers 27 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The possessive formula runs through this chapter in four shapes, and each is read by what stands in the clause (rule 43). v. 3 writes הָיוּ, so the verb takes the *had*, the preposition לוֹ is unrepresented under rule 40, the suffix takes the subject *he*, and the clause's לֹא takes the determiner *no* under rule 15. vv. 4, 9, 10 are verbless: אַיִן takes *no* under rule 50, the לְ takes *have* or *had*, and the suffix takes *he*. v. 8 is the same verbless clause, but the ASV writes no subject pronoun there — the subject carries over from *a man* — so the suffix is unrepresented under rule 22; that is not a split with vv. 9-10, and the difference is the ASV's, not the analysis's. v. 11 writes the possessor אָבִיו, which takes the English subject noun *his father* while the suffix keeps only *his* and the לְ still carries the *have*. v. 17 is a relative possessive: the written אֲשֶׁר supplies the English subject, the לְ takes *have*, אַיִן takes *no*, and the resumptive suffix takes nothing.
- *Take thee* (v. 18) and *Get thee up* (v. 12) are placed by what the Hebrew writes rather than by the English they share. v. 18 writes לְךָ, so the preposition is unrepresented under rule 40 and the suffix takes the *thee* under rule 18. v. 12 writes no dative at all, and its *thee* is kept inside the imperative's own unit as part of the ASV's fixed rendering of the verb of motion, on the ground that it supplies no argument the Hebrew lacks but is coreferential with the imperative's own subject; the competing reading marks it `added` under rule 44's supplied-object sentence, whose stated licence is the source verb's voice or elative sense. Nothing else in the chapter folds a supplied pronoun into a verb, and this convention is not authority to do so elsewhere.
