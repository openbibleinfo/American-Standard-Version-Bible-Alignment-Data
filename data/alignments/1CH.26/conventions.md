# 1 Chronicles 26 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- בֵּית אָבוֹת written elliptically as אָבוֹת: where the chapter writes only אָבוֹת and the ASV supplies *houses* for it to stand on as a possessive — *the heads of the fathers houses*, *by fathers houses* — the supplied noun joins the אָבוֹת unit under rule 31 and the unit heads on *fathers*; it is never `added`, and the ASV's italic corroborates the supply without deciding it. Where the Hebrew writes בֵּית itself, as at 26.13 לְבֵית אֲבוֹתָם → *their fathers houses*, rule 2 splits the pair and *houses* is that noun's own word. Settled at reconciliation against 24 corpus units, among them 1CH.24.6 and 1CH.24.31.
- The chapter's directional phrases — לַמִּזְרָח, לַמַּעֲרָב, לַצָּפוֹנָה, לַנֶּגְבָּה, מִזְרָחָה, צָפוֹנָה, מַעְרָבָה, מֵעֵבֶר, בְּשֶׂכֶל — are each rendered by one English word, so the preposition fuses with its noun under rule 40 and a directive he in the phrase joins the same unit under rules 30 and 32, headed on the noun carrying the lexeme. The assimilated [הַ] MACULA segments inside such a phrase is its own `unrepresented` unit, because the one English word carries no determination; where the fused phrase is the predicate of a verbless clause the construction's copula joins that one unit (26.17 *Eastward were*), and a preposition the fusion leaves nothing to say goes `unrepresented` beside it (26.30 מֵעֵבֶר לַיַּרְדֵּן → *beyond the Jordan*).
