# 2 Samuel 2 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- מֵאַחֲרֵי rendered *from following* (2.19, 2.21, 2.22, 2.26, 2.30) splits: מִן takes *from* and אַחֲרֵי takes the verbal *following*. Where the ASV writes a single word for the pair — *followed* (2.27), *behind* (2.23) — or for הָיוּ אַחֲרֵי (*followed*, 2.10), the morphemes fuse under rule 30 headed on אַחַר, which carries the content.
- Pleonastic יָסַף + עוֹד rendered by one adverbial expression — *again* (2.22), *any more* (2.28) — fuses under rule 30 rather than leaving עוֹד unrepresented beside a rendered verb; the unit heads on the finite verb as source head, and the subject pronoun the ASV writes for that verb (*they*) joins it. Where לֹא is rendered *no* onto a following עוֹד (*no more*, 2.28) each morpheme takes its own word.
- נְעָרִים → *young men* (2.14, 2.21) heads on *young* under rule 31, since the ASV does not write bare *men* for נַעַר; the competing head *men* is named in each note.
- Rule 45 with a casus pendens: in 2.23 (וַיְהִי כָּל־הַבָּא … וַיַּעֲמֹדוּ → *it came to pass that as many as came … stood still*) the apodosis opens with its fronted subject, so the waw on the verb is resumptive rather than apodosis-opening; the frame keeps its *that* and that waw is unrepresented. Where the waw itself opens the apodosis (2.1) it takes the *that*.
