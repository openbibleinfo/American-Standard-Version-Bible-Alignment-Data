# 1 Kings 17 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **1KI.17.3** — Where עַל־פְּנֵי is rendered by a single English word realizing the compound as a whole (*before*), it is one unit headed on פָּנֶה, the morpheme carrying the lexeme, not on the syntactic head עַל. Settled against 1KI.17.5, which has the identical clause. Where instead the English word is the ordinary rendering of עַל alone (*upon the earth*, 1KI.17.14), עַל takes it and פְּנֵי is its own `unrepresented` unit.
- **1KI.17.23** — Where מִן is rendered by a fused out of inside one unit, the target head is out, the token carrying the ablative lexeme, not the connective of. Settled against 1KI.17.19.
