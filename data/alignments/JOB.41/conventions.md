# Job 41 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Where the ASV renders one comparative preposition by an elliptical or correlative English expression, the whole expression is that preposition's single rendering under rule 30 and no part of it is `added` or handed to the noun or predicate beside it: כַּ → 'as with' (41.5), כְּ → 'As of' (41.20), and כְּמוֹ → 'as ... as' (41.24), the last discontinuous around the predicate participle under rule 25 and headed on the occurrence governing the noun. The competing reading, which marks the second English word `added`, was live in 41.5 and 41.20 and was declined because no other morpheme stands for it. This does not reach a degree word the ASV writes with no comparative preposition in the clause: the 'so' of 41.10 and 41.16 belongs to the predicate it modifies.
- A supplied English attributive standing beside a Hebrew noun joins that noun's unit under rule 31 where it elaborates the noun's own lexeme — 'burning torches' for לַפִּידִים (41.19), 'the pointed shaft' for שִׁרְיָה (41.26), 'the bands of fishermen' for חַבָּרִים (41.6), 'a pot of ointment' for מֶרְקָחָה (41.31) — and is its own `added` unit under rule 20's supplied-lexical-verb boundary where it instead predicates of the noun something the Hebrew does not write and the noun's own rendering is already complete: the italic 'burning' before 'rushes' in 41.20, where אַגְמֹן is fully rendered by 'rushes' and the coordination's one participle נָפוּחַ has already been spent on 'boiling'. Ask which of the two the English word is doing before folding it in; the ASV's italic corroborates either way and decides neither (rule 7).
