# Daniel 4 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **DAN.4.14** — A separately segmented Aramaic determined-state ending takes a written ASV article; the noun retains only its lexical English.
- **DAN.4.23** — The fixed עַד דִּי expression rendered by one “till” or “until” is a single noncompositional unit.
- **DAN.4.24** — The title “Most High” heads on “High”; “Most” is the competing degree-word anchor, but the corpus favors “High.”
- **DAN.4.23** — The verb נְחֵת rendered “came/coming down” heads on the lexical verb; “down” is its phrasal particle.
