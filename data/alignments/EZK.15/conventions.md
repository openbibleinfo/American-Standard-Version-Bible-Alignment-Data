# Ezekiel 15 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה → *the Lord Jehovah* (15.6 s005-s007, 15.8 s011-s013): the ASV writes *the Lord*, not *my Lord*, so the `י` of אֲדֹנָי has no English of its own and is `unrepresented` under rules 18 and 22, while the supplied *the* joins the noun's unit under rule 15, no article morpheme being present. The same holds whether the formula follows כֹּה אָמַר (15.6) or נְאֻם (15.8). BSB represents that suffix; the difference is conventional and neither project is in error on it.
- עֵץ before a determined noun is aligned two ways in this chapter because the ASV renders it two ways, and the two are not to be reconciled to each other. עֵץ־הַגֶּפֶן → *the vine tree* is attributive under rule 28: the construct keeps *tree*, the rectum keeps *vine*, and the rectum's own article morpheme takes the single written *the* fronted to the phrase's left edge (rules 29, 47) — 15.2 s005-s007, 15.6 s010-s012. עֲצֵי/עֵץ הַיַּעַר → *the trees of the forest* is rule 29's arthrous clause: the ASV writes a *the* before each word, so nothing is fronted, the construct takes a supplied *the*, the article takes the *the* standing before its own word, and the rectum takes the relational *of* — 15.2 s016-s018, 15.6 s014-s016. Morphological number (Ncmsc in 15.6, Ncmpc in 15.2) does not enter into it.
