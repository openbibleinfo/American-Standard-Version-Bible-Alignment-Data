# Exodus 16 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **EXO.16.24** — When ASV renders כַּאֲשֶׁר as the single conjunction as, keep the R+Tr pair in one aligned fusion headed on כְּ; the comparative preposition supplies a defensible internal head, so the expression is not noncompositional.
