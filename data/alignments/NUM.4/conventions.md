# Numbers 4 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **NUM.4.3** — In NUM.4, מַעְלָה rendered 'upward' and מִלְמַעְלָה rendered 'over' or 'above' are aligned multi-morpheme fusions headed on מַעַל, not noncompositional units.
- **NUM.4.15** — In NUM.4.15 and NUM.4.20, the waw of וָמֵתוּ licenses the ASV clause connective 'lest'; it is not unrepresented and 'lest' is not added.
