# Job 22 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- A construct epithet naming a class of person by a body part — נְשׂוּא פָנִים (22.8) → 'the honorable man', שַׁח עֵינַיִם (22.29) → 'the humble person' — is fused into the single English adjective under rule 30, headed on the construct (rule 23), and the supplied 'the ... man/person' joins that unit under rule 46; the rectum is not marked unrepresented beside it. Both ASV Hebrew-gloss margins corroborate the fusion and decide nothing (rule 38).
- A stative qal or a hiphil the ASV renders as copula + adjective — יִסְכָּן 'be profitable' (22.2), תִצְדָּק 'thou art righteous' (22.3), רָמּוּ 'high they are' (22.12), רָחֲקָה 'is far' (22.18), יִשְׂמָחוּ 'are glad' (22.19) — keeps the whole phrase in the verb's unit and heads on the adjective, which carries the lexeme (rule 44's adjective complement); the copula is never treated as rule 20's construction-supplied one.
