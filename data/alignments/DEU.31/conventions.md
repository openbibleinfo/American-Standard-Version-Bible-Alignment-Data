# Deuteronomy 31 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The come-to-pass frame of DEU.31.24 has its apodosis in DEU.31.25: waw + yehi in v24 takes 'And it came to pass' with no complementizer, and the verse-initial 'that' of v25 is the apodosis waw's rendering under rule 45's apodosis-waw sentence, not an added subordinator under rule 55. A pass seeing v25 alone would find a clause-initial 'that' over a bare waw and could mark it added; the frame that licenses it is in the preceding verse. Compare v21, where the same frame and its apodosis waw stand inside one verse.
- **DEU.31.11** — In lera'ot 'et-penei YHWH the object marker is its own unrepresented unit and the construct noun penei holds the whole of 'before', because 'before' renders paneh's own lexeme rather than the object relation, which is the condition rule 16 sets. This was chosen over the fused reading even though the corpus aligns 'et + paneh as one 'before' unit in twelve places, including one whose 'et is tagged as an object marker; rule 16 is the rule specific to H853 and rule 30's one-word condition does not reach a morpheme whose sense the word carries nothing of. Recorded so the choice is not re-litigated from the corpus table alone.
