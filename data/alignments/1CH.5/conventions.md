# 1 Chronicles 5 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- יָחַשׂ (hithpael, 'enrol by genealogy') rendered with a supplied English noun that writes its lexeme — 'the genealogy be reckoned' (5.1), 'the genealogy was reckoned' (5.7), 'were reckoned by genealogies' (5.17) — is one unit on the verb under rule 44's lexeme-carrying-noun clause, headed on genealogy/genealogies, not a supplied subject noun marked added under rule 17. The competing reading (noun added, head on reckoned) is named in each note; a later chapter that meets this verb should read the corpus for it before departing.
- The specifying לְ of וַיַּגְלֵם לָראוּבֵנִי וְלַגָּדִי וְלַחֲצִי (5.26) takes the ASV's appositional 'even' under rule 11, since it stands on the very phrase the word introduces; the repeated לְ on each later member, for which the ASV writes only the waw's 'and', is unrepresented under rule 40.
- Two-token proper names (Baal | meon, Baal | hermon, Tilgath | pilneser) are split token to English word; where the name is a rectum the chain's 'of' joins the first token (5.26 'of Tilgath'), and where the ASV writes no 'of' both tokens are bare (5.6).
