# Hebrews 7 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- Versification: the ASV translates 7:20's closing clause οἱ μὲν γὰρ χωρὶς ὁρκωμοσίας εἰσὶν ἱερεῖς γεγονότες at the head of 7:21. Under rule 37 s007-s014 of 7:20 are unrepresented and t001-t010 of 7:21 are one added unit, each side carrying the cross-reference and neither reaching across. A reference comparison reports all eight tokens as disagreements and that is not a correction.
- Where a single Greek verb is rendered by a supplied light verb plus a noun complement, this chapter heads on the complement throughout: ἀφομοιόω → made like (7:3), ἀποδεκατόω and δεκατόω → tithes (7:5, 7:6, 7:9), προσέχω → attendance (7:13), τελειόω → perfect (7:19), ἐντυγχάνω → intercession (7:25), and νομοθετέομαι → law (7:11). None of made, take, paid, given or received glosses its lexeme; the corpus spends receive on λαμβάνω, which is separately aligned three times in this chapter.
- The two draw-nigh/draw-near phrases are headed by lexeme and not by the shared English. ἐγγίζομεν (7:19) heads on nigh, the ἐγγ- family taking the proximity word in all six corpus units of ἐγγίζω and in every ἐγγύς unit rule 71 lists; προσερχομένους (7:25) heads on draw, προσέρχομαι resting on ἔρχομαι, whose own sense is the verb word, as HEB.10.1 and HEB.10.22 read it against HEB.4.16 alone. Do not level these two to one word.
- Supplied subjects: where the Greek copula is expressed, a wholly supplied English subject rides with it (7:15, what we say on ἐστιν, the expressed predicate κατάδηλον keeping only evident); where the clause is verbless, the predicate carries the supplied subject and copula together (7:14 πρόδηλον → it is evident, 7:11 χρεία → need was there, 7:20 χωρίς → it is without). The difference is the expressed copula, not English word order.
- καθ᾽ ὅσον at 7:20 is kept as one aligned unit on the lexicalized connective inasmuch as, under rule 94's clause for an English adverbial that is one lexeme spelt apart, the -much answering to ὅσον. Every other κατά in the chapter (7:5, 7:11 twice, 7:15, 7:16 twice, 7:17, 7:22) stands as its own unit; the compound is licensed by the fused English alone, and splitting inasmuch from as remains defensible.
