# Psalms 26 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **PSA.26.2** — Where כִּלְיָה (reins) and לֵב stand together, the ASV renders כִּלְיָה as heart, with the margin 'Heb. reins' on that word, and לֵב as mind, keeping the Hebrew order (so also PSA.7.9, JER.11.20, JER.17.10, JER.20.12). Align heart to כִּלְיָה and mind to לֵב; do not read the English as a reordering of the two nouns.
