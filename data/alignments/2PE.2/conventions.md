# 2 Peter 2 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **2PE.2.16** — Where the ASV converts a Greek circumstantial participle into a coordinate finite clause, the supplied juncture 'and' is a helper on the finite verb it INTRODUCES, not on the participle it follows, and it is never 'added'. 2PE.2.4 'and committed them' and 2PE.2.16 'and stayed' read the same construction the same way. The corpus places such a juncture on the verb it introduces in 34 units across 21 chapters, against REV.1.1 alone on the participle.
- **2PE.2.2** — For a complex prepositional frame on an expressed proper preposition, where rule 67 leaves the head open, this chapter follows the neighbour reading and heads on the prepositional word: 2PE.2.2 'by reason of' heads on 'by' with 1PE.1.14, JHN.12.11, REV.9.2, REV.18.19 and 2CO.9.14. Where the carrier is an article rule 67 is settled rather than divided, so 2PE.2.22 'according to the' heads on 'the' at high confidence.
