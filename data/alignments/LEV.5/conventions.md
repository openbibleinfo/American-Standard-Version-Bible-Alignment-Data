# Leviticus 5 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The sin-formula relative takes the whole of the ASV's *where-* frame and heads on the *where-* word, whether or not the Hebrew writes the resumptive preposition and suffix. אשר יטמא בה → *wherewith he is unclean* (5.3) and אשר חטא עליה → *that wherein he hath sinned* (5.5) are three-morpheme fusions under rules 22 and 30; the bare אשר חטא of 5.7 and 5.11 takes *for that wherein* and the bare אשר שגג of 5.18 takes *wherein*, each on the relative alone, since no preposition or suffix stands there to share the word. Any supplied *for* or *that* introducing that clause joins the same unit under rule 46's rider and is never added. The unit sizes differ because the Hebrew differs; this is not a split.
- Compound offering names are headed by rule 31's subtraction test and its tie-break, and the two do not fall the same way for every lexeme. חטאת → *a sin offering*, אשם → *a trespass offering* and עלה → *a burnt offering* head on the modifier (*sin*, *trespass*, *burnt*), which carries the lexeme; מנחה → *the meal offering* (5.13) and אשה → *the offerings of Jehovah made by fire* (5.12) head on *offering*/*offerings*, because there the noun is what realizes the lexeme and the modifier is the ASV's specifier. Do not regularize the second pair onto the modifier: the divergence is the tie-break working, and each of those two units names the competing reading.
- Where the casuistic protasis writes more conditional morphemes than the ASV writes subordinators, the morpheme standing at the clause head, where the English word stands relative to the clause's other renderings, takes it, and the other is unrepresented under rule 22: ואם־נפש כי תחטא → *And if any one sin* (5.17) gives *if* to אם and leaves כי with nothing. Where a relative is the only subordinating morpheme in the protasis, that relative takes the *if*: נפש אשר תגע → *if any one touch* (5.2), a present morpheme taking its English before rule 55's added reading does.
