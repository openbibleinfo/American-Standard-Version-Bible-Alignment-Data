# Numbers 25 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- המדינית is aligned three ways in this chapter and all three are licensed, so do not level them. NUM.25.6 renders the phrase indefinitely (a Midianitish woman), so the article is unrepresented and the supplied a and woman join the adjective's unit; NUM.25.14 writes the Midianitish woman, so the article takes the the and the supplied woman still joins the adjective; NUM.25.15 writes אשה in the Hebrew and a single the for the whole phrase, so the noun's article takes that the, the gentilic's agreement article is unrepresented under rule 47, and the adjective keeps only Midianitish because the noun it would have been expanded with is written.
- בעל פעור is one MACULA lemma spanning two source tokens, and this chapter splits it into two units, one per token, in NUM.25.3 and NUM.25.5, because the ASV writes two English words (Baal peor). Rule 30's one-English-word condition is what decides it; a single lemma across two tokens is not itself a reason to fuse.
- Rule 30 fusions in this chapter are recorded with status aligned rather than noncompositional: מעל to from (NUM.25.8, NUM.25.11), בתוך to among (NUM.25.11), תחת אשר to because (NUM.25.13), לכן to Wherefore (NUM.25.12). The corpus records both statuses for such fusions, so this is a chapter-wide choice and not a rule; noncompositional was reserved, per rule 6, for phrases that are genuinely indivisible rather than merely fused.
