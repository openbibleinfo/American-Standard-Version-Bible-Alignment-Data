# Jeremiah 34 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **JER.34.1** — Fixed multi-source Hebrew expressions rendered by one indivisible English word are noncompositional rather than aligned in this chapter; this covers מֵאֵת/from, לִפְנֵי/before, אַחֲרֵי כֵן/afterwards, מֵעִם/from, לָכֵן/therefore, וְ לֹא/neither, הַיּוֹם/now, and מֵאֵין/without.
