# 2 Chronicles 9 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The title הַמֶּלֶךְ שְׁלֹמֹה rendered 'king Solomon' leaves its article its own unrepresented unit (9.9, 9.12, 9.15, 9.20, 9.22); where the ASV writes 'the king' alone the article takes the 'the' as usual.
- מִזֶּה וּמִזֶּה follows the ASV's word count: one noncompositional unit headed on the first מִן where the ASV writes 'on either side' (9.18), and five units — each מִן → 'on', each זֶה taking 'the one side' / 'the other', the waw 'and' — where it writes a word for each member (9.19).
