# Genesis 47 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- **GEN.47.6** — Causative ישב rendered with a light verb plus dwell heads on dwell.
- **GEN.47.18** — A possessive determiner replacing an explicit article aligns to that article when no possessive morpheme is present.
- **GEN.47.20** — The compositional fusion of לְ plus Pharaoh as English Pharaoh's is aligned rather than noncompositional.
