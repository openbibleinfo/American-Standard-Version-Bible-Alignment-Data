# Ezra 4 alignment conventions

Chapter-wide judgments, recorded as they were made. Read this before continuing an interrupted chapter or starting a review range. Conventions that generalize belong in the testament contract under `config/` once the chapter is finished.

- The title בְּעֵל־טְעֵם → 'the chancellor' is one noncompositional unit (4.8, 4.9, 4.17): no English word writes either lexeme, so the first token בְּעֵל is the display anchor under rule 6, the target head is 'chancellor', and the supplied 'the' joins the unit under rule 15.
- The province name עֲבַר־נַהֲרָה: עֲבַר (tagged Np) takes 'beyond' together with whatever the ASV supplies to expand it — the italic 'the country' under rule 31, and the 'of' of a preceding construct (שְׁאָר עֲבַר) under rule 19 — headed on 'beyond' (4.10, 4.17, 4.20); נַהֲרָה keeps 'River' and its determined ending the 'the'. Where a preposition stands before it (בַּעֲבַר, 4.16) the two fuse as one 'beyond' under rule 30, headed on עֲבַר, as the corpus fuses מֵעֵבֶר/בְּעֵבֶר in over forty units.
- The epistolary closer וּכְעֶנֶת / וּכְעֶת → 'and so forth' (4.10, 4.11, 4.17): the waw takes 'and' and the adverb takes 'so forth' headed on 'forth'. The compound עַל־דְּנָה is fused whatever English it takes — 'therefore' (4.14), 'for which cause' (4.15), 'herein' (4.22) — with the source head on the governing עַל in every case under rule 23, since the English word written does not choose a source head.
- Periphrastic הֲוָה + participle: the auxiliary is unrepresented where an explicit subject noun stands and the ASV writes a simple finite verb on the participle (4.4 וַיְהִי ... מְרַפִּים → 'weakened'); where the ASV writes a subject pronoun for it (4.24 וַהֲוָת בָּטְלָא → 'it ceased') that pronoun realizes the finite auxiliary's person and number under rule 17 and the participle takes the lexical verb.
